ENGLISH:
Your crop has Maize fall armyworm. Use Emamectin benzoate 5% SG (sold as Proclaim). Mix 0.4 g in one litre of water. For one acre, use 80 g in 200 L of water. Do not harvest for 7 days after applying. The label has a yellow triangle. Cover your mouth and hands. Do not spray against the wind.
