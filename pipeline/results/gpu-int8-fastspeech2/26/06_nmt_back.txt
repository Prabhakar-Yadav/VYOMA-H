ENGLISH:
Your crop has Potato late blight. Use Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP for potato late blight (sold as Curzate M8). Mix 2 to 3 g in one litre of water. For one acre, use 600 g in 200 to 300 L of water. Do not harvest for 10 days after applying. Check the coloured triangle on the container before you use it. Cover your mouth and hands. Do not spray against the wind.
