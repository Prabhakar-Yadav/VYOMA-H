This could be Wheat yellow rust, which is a disease, or it could be Wheat interveinal chlorosis (iron or manganese), which means the soil is short of food. The treatment is completely different, and a spray will not fix a shortage in the soil. Please show the plant to your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra before buying anything.
