This looks like Okra yellow vein mosaic. I do not have a verified medicine to give you for it, so please do not spray anything on guesswork. There are things you can do today. 1. Grow tolerant varieties — Parbhani Kranti, Arka Anamika, Arka Abhay, Varsha Uphar, Janardhan, Haritha. 2. Avoid susceptible varieties in the summer crop. 3. Uproot and destroy diseased plants as soon as the yellow vein pattern shows, from the seedling stage onward. For a spray, ask your Krishi Vigyan Kendra.
