# Dose

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Per kg of seed | **3 g** (registered figure) |
| Per quintal (100 kg) of seed | **300 g** |
| Slurry water | **10 ml per kg seed**, i.e. 1 litre per 100 kg |
| Waiting period | not applicable, this is applied at sowing |

# Farmer-facing instruction

> Loose smut cannot be sprayed. The fungus lives inside the seed, so it is stopped before sowing or not at all. Mix 3 g of the product per kg of seed, using 10 ml of water per kg to make a slurry. For one quintal of seed that is 300 g of product in 1 litre of water. Treat the seed in shade, dry it in shade, and sow within a few days.

# Safety

- **Never feed treated seed to people or to animals.** This is the most common fatal
  pesticide accident pattern with treated seed in India. Keep treated seed in clearly
  marked bags, away from grain meant for eating.
- Do not mix a chemical seed dressing with Rhizobium, PSB or Trichoderma in the same
  slurry. If both are wanted, apply the fungicide first, dry the seed fully, then apply
  the biofertiliser immediately before sowing.

# A free alternative that also works

Solar heat treatment controls loose smut and costs nothing: soak the seed in water for
four hours in the morning, then spread it in strong summer sun for the rest of the day.
