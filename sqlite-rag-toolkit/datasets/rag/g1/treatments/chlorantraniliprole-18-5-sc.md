# Farmer-facing instruction

> Mix 0.3 millilitres of Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC in one litre of water.
> For one acre, use 60 millilitres in 200 litres of water.
> **Direct the spray into the whorl** — the funnel at the top of the plant.
> Do not pick the crop for **10 days** after spraying.

# Safety

- **Blue triangle** on the label — slightly hazardous.
- Low toxicity to bees and beneficial insects — preferred where pollinators are active.
- Cover mouth, nose, and hands. Wash with soap and water after spraying.
- Keep animals and children out of the field for **12 hours**.

See [handling and protective measures](/safety/spray-safety.md).

# Constraints

- **Pre-harvest interval: 10 days.**
- Maximum 2 sprays per season. Alternate with
  [Emamectin benzoate](/treatments/emamectin-benzoate-5-sg.md) — do not use the
  same mode of action twice in a row against fall armyworm.
- The dose is small. Measure with a proper measuring cup, not by eye.
