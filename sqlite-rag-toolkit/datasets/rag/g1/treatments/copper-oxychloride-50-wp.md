# Farmer-facing instruction

> Mix 3 grams of Copper oxychloride 50% WP in one litre of water.
> For one acre, use 1 kilogram in 200 litres of water.
> Do not harvest for **15 days** after spraying.
>
> **This will slow the disease. It will not cure it.** Stopping nitrogen fertiliser
> and draining the field matter more than this spray.

# Safety

- **Blue triangle** on the label — slightly hazardous.
- Cover mouth, nose, and hands. Wash with soap and water after spraying.
- Toxic to fish — significant in rice, where fields connect to water channels.
  Do not drain treated water directly into ponds used for fish.
- Keep animals and children out of the field for **24 hours**.

See [handling and protective measures](/safety/spray-safety.md).

# Constraints

- **Pre-harvest interval: 15 days.**
- **Suppressive only, not curative.** Copper reduces bacterial spread on the leaf
  surface. It cannot clear an established infection inside the plant.
- Repeated copper use accumulates in soil. Do not exceed 2 sprays.
- Do not tank-mix with acidic materials.
