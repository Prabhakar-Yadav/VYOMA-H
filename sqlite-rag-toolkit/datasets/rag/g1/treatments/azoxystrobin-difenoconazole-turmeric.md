# Farmer-facing instruction

> Mix 1 ml in one litre of water. For one acre use 202 ml in about 202 L of water. Do not harvest for 60 days after spraying.

# Safety

- Registered on turmeric only. Do not use this figure on ginger, which has a different registered product and a very different waiting period.

# What actually controls rhizome rot

- Raised beds with sharp drainage. Rhizome rot is a waterlogging disease above all.
- Disease free seed rhizomes taken from a healthy field.
- Remove infected clumps together with the surrounding soil.
