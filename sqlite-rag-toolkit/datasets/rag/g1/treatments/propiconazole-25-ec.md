# Farmer-facing instruction

> Mix 1 millilitre of Propiconazole 25% EC in one litre of water.
> For one acre, use 200 millilitres in 200 litres of water.
> Spray as soon as the first yellow stripes appear. Do not wait.
> Cover the whole plant, including lower leaves.
> Do not harvest for **35 days** after spraying.

# Safety

- **Yellow triangle** on the label — moderately hazardous.
- Toxic to fish. Do not wash equipment near ponds, canals, or streams.
- Cover mouth, nose, and hands. Do not spray against the wind.
- Wash with soap and water immediately after spraying.
- Keep animals and children out of the field for **24 hours**.

See [handling and protective measures](/safety/spray-safety.md).

# Constraints

- **Pre-harvest interval: 35 days — the longest in this bundle.** Late-season
  spraying near harvest is not permitted. If the crop is within 35 days of
  harvest, this product must not be used.
- Do not exceed 2 sprays per season.
