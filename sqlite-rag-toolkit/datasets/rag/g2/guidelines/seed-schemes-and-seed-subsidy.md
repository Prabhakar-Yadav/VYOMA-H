**What the farmer gets**
- Subsidy on certified seed of notified varieties, given through the National Food Security Mission and other crop missions. The subsidy is a fixed amount per quintal, or a percentage of the seed cost.
- Seed Village Programme: foundation and certified seed is supplied at subsidised cost to a group of farmers in a village, along with training in seed production and seed treatment. Support is also given for seed storage bins.
- Seed minikits of new and high-yielding varieties, distributed free during campaign drives, especially for pulses and oilseeds.
- Free replacement or relief seed after a notified natural calamity.

**SATHI — Seed Traceability portal**
seednet.gov.in and the SATHI portal carry a QR code trail for certified seed lots.
Scanning the QR code on the seed bag shows the source, the lot and the certification, so the farmer can confirm the seed is genuine.

**Who qualifies**
Farmers cultivating the notified crop in the district qualify.
Seed Village Programme groups need about 50 to 150 farmers in one village.

**How to apply**
1. Ask the block agriculture office or the KVK which subsidised seed is available for the coming season.
2. Book seed through the state agriculture portal, the state seed corporation outlet, the cooperative society or the KVK.
3. Buy from a licensed dealer and collect a proper cash memo showing the lot number.
4. Scan the QR code on the bag to check the lot.
5. Keep the cash memo and the empty bag tag until harvest, so a complaint about germination can be made.

**Documents needed**
- Aadhaar
- Land record
- Bank passbook for DBT of subsidy
- Cash memo from the licensed dealer

⚠ Seed subsidy rates, crop lists and distribution channels are notified separately by each state every season.
