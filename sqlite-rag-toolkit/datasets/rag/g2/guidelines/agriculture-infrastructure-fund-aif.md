**What the farmer gets**
A medium and long term loan to build post-harvest and community farming infrastructure — warehouses, cold stores, grading and sorting units, pack houses, primary processing units, ripening chambers, assaying units, custom hiring centres and farm machinery banks.

On loans up to Rupees 2 crore:
- 3 percent interest subvention for up to 7 years
- Interest is capped at 9 percent per year
- Credit guarantee under CGTMSE, with the guarantee fee paid by the Government

**Who qualifies**
- Individual farmers
- Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs)
- Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)
- Self Help Groups and Joint Liability Groups
- Marketing cooperative societies, multipurpose cooperative societies
- Agri-entrepreneurs and start-ups
- Central and State agencies, and Public Private Partnership projects

**How to apply**
1. Go to agriinfra.dac.gov.in.
2. Register with mobile number and Aadhaar number. An OTP creates the login.
3. Prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) for the proposed asset.
4. Fill the application form on the portal and upload the DPR.
5. Choose the lending bank from the list on the portal.
6. The Department verifies the project. The bank appraises and sanctions the loan.
7. The interest subvention is credited against the loan account each year.

**Documents needed**
- Aadhaar and PAN
- Detailed Project Report with cost estimate
- Land ownership or lease document for the project site
- Bank account details
- Registration certificate for an FPO, SHG, cooperative or company
- Quotations for machinery and civil work

**Deadline pattern**
Applications are open on the portal.
⚠ The scheme's sanction window and eligible activity list have been widened over time. Confirm the current eligible activity list on the portal before preparing a DPR.
