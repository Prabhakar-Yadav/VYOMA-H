The mission runs in two parts.

### 12a. NMEO-Oilseeds
Approved for 2024-25 to 2030-31 with an outlay of Rupees 10,103 crore.
Focus crops: rapeseed-mustard, groundnut, soybean, sunflower, sesamum, safflower, niger, linseed and castor.
Also covers cottonseed, rice bran, coconut and tree-borne oilseeds.

**What the farmer gets**
- High quality certified seed of improved varieties, supplied through the SATHI seed traceability portal
- Cluster-based Value Chain Partnerships, where 65 oilseed clusters are developed with FPOs, cooperatives and industry
- Training on Good Agricultural Practices
- Advisory on weather, sowing window and pest management
- Support for intercropping oilseeds and for growing oilseeds on rice fallow land
- Assured procurement backing through the Minimum Support Price for oilseed crops

**How to apply**
Approach the district or block agriculture office, the KVK, or the FPO working in an oilseed cluster.
Seed is booked through the SATHI portal at seednet.gov.in or through the state seed corporation.

### 12b. NMEO-Oil Palm
Support for planting material, maintenance during the gestation years, drip irrigation, borewells and harvesting tools for oil palm growers.
A price assurance formula protects the grower on fresh fruit bunch prices.
Approach the state horticulture department or the assigned oil palm processing company.

⚠ Component rates, cluster locations and the assistance table are notified state by state each year.
