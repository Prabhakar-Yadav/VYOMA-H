## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Tamil Nadu's agricultural framework is powered by a separate, dedicated State Agriculture Budget tailored to the agro-climatic dynamics of the Cauvery Delta basin, dryland rainfed tracts, and coastal belts:
1. **Unprecedented Institutional Debt Recovery:** Comprehensive state exchequer commitment deploying over ₹6,220 Crore to waive outstanding short-term agricultural crop loans with Primary Agricultural Cooperative Credit Societies (PACCS)—providing 100% complete waivers for loans up to ₹75,000 and tiered reliefs for larger amounts.
2. **Universal Grassroots Convergence (KAVIADP):** Operating across all 12,525 village panchayats, the *Kalaignarin All Villages Integrated Agriculture Development Programme* brings together 15+ departments to transform barren fallow land into productive multi-cropping clusters, financing 100% free community borewells, coconut saplings, and 100% subsidized micro-irrigation for SC/ST dryland farmers.
3. **Ecological Soil Restoration & Seed Multiplication:** The *Mannuyir Kaathu Mannuyir Kaapom Scheme* restores soil organic carbon through free *Dhaincha* and *Sunnhemp* green manuring, while the *Seed Village Scheme* delivers a direct ₹2/kg cash incentive bonus for multiplying certified paddy seed varieties.

## Section 1: Flagship Institutional Debt Alleviation & Grassroots Convergence

### [TN_SCHEME_001] Co-operative Farm Loan Waiver Scheme
* **Category:** Institutional Debt Alleviation & Credit Recovery
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers across Tamil Nadu holding outstanding short-term agricultural crop loans with cooperative credit societies.
* **Benefit Details:** Landmark debt relief initiative totaling over ₹6,220 Crore to restore creditworthiness for more than 13.34 Lakh farmers:
  * **100% Complete Waiver:** Disbursed for all cooperative crop loans up to ₹75,000.
  * **Tiered Assistance:** For loans between ₹75,000 and ₹1 Lakh, a maximum waiver of ₹75,000 is applied. For loans exceeding ₹1 Lakh, a fixed relief of ₹35,000 is credited directly into the loan account.
  * Clears accumulated interest and principal liabilities directly from the State Treasury into the lending banks.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active resident cultivators with verified crop loan accounts managed under registered Primary Agricultural Cooperative Credit Societies (PACCS).
* **How to Apply:** Eligible loan profiles are automatically matched through cooperative banking core databases; farmers can verify waiver certificates at their local cooperative society branch.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, original Kisan Credit Card (KCC) / Loan Passbook, and land ownership title extracts (Patta/Chitta).

---

### [TN_SCHEME_002] Kalaignarin All Villages Integrated Agriculture Development Programme (KAVIADP)
* **Category:** Grassroots Agricultural Convergence & Fallow Land Reclamation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farming families, smallholders, and Adi Dravidar (SC/ST) community cultivators across all 12,525 village panchayats.
* **Benefit Details:** Flagship multi-departmental convergence mission to bring fallow lands under active cultivation:
  * Groups scattered fallow plots into contiguous fallow land clusters to establish collective farming blocks.
  * Sinks 100% state-subsidized community borewells, constructs farm ponds, and clears dense thorny bushes.
  * Distributes input packages: free high-yielding coconut seedlings, home garden vegetable seed kits, micro-sprayers, and a ₹5,000 cash incentive bonus for expanding vegetable cultivation.
  * **100% Full Infrastructure Subsidy for SC/ST Farmers:** Provides 100% full subsidies specifically to Adi Dravidar and tribal farmers for sinking dryland wells and installing precision micro-irrigation systems.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active farming families located in designated rural panchayat batches.
* **How to Apply:** Handled locally at the village level through joint inspection camps organized by the Agriculture, Horticulture, and Rural Development Departments.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Community Certificate (mandatory for 100% SC/ST subsidies), Pattadar Passbook / Patta-Chitta copy, and cluster layout verification maps.

## Section 2: Soil Health, Ecological Farming & Precision Water Security

### [TN_SCHEME_003] Mannuyir Kaathu Mannuyir Kaapom Scheme (Soil Health Mission)
* **Category:** Ecological Agriculture Transition & Bio-Input Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Cultivators facing declining soil organic matter, salinity, and micro-nutrient depletion.
* **Benefit Details:** State-wide biological soil restoration mission:
  * 100% state-funded free distribution of certified green manure seeds (*Dhaincha* / *Sesbania aculeata* and *Sunnhemp* / *Crotalaria juncea*) to enhance organic carbon and natural biological nitrogen fixation.
  * Extends capital financial assistance to build permanent on-farm vermicompost sheds.
  * Subsidizes localized production units for liquid bio-fertilizers (*Azospirillum*, *Phosphobacteria*, *Potash Releasing Bacteria*).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered small, marginal, and progressive farmers committed to chemical-reduction farming layouts.
* **How to Apply:** Distributed prior to the Northeast and Southwest monsoons through local Agricultural Extension Centres (AECs) / Uzhavan Seva desks.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID code, and land title extract copy.

---

### [TN_SCHEME_004] Micro Irrigation Scheme (Per Drop More Crop Module)
* **Category:** Precision Water Engineering & Energy Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and general category farmers cultivating horticultural crops or water-intensive commercial crops.
* **Benefit Details:** Precision water infrastructure deployment covering 4 Lakh hectares:
  * Up to an 85% capital financial subsidy for small and marginal farmers (100% for SC/ST farmers under KAVIADP) to install drip and sprinkler networks.
  * **Solar Pumping Integration:** Dedicated line item funding 1,000 on-farm standalone solar pump sets (70% cumulative subsidy) to ensure daytime irrigation without grid power fluctuations.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers with an operational borewell, open well, or river lift source holding clean land titles.
* **How to Apply:** Digital registration via the unified state micro-irrigation tracking portal (`tnhorticulture.tn.gov.in/tnhorti/mims`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, recent land Jamabandi documents, pump electricity or solar yield certificate, and vendor layout maps.

## Section 3: Input Subventions, Mechanization & Sowing Security

### [TN_SCHEME_005] Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM Tamil Nadu)
* **Category:** Farm Machinery & Custom Hiring Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholders, youth agri-entrepreneurs, and registered agricultural cooperative societies.
* **Benefit Details:** Drives large-scale farm mechanization across rural blocks:
  * 40% to 75% capital investment subsidy directly on individual machinery purchases: power tillers, rotavators, paddy transplanters, laser land levelers, and power weeders.
  * Finances the establishment of block-level Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) enabling smallholders to rent heavy machinery at state-regulated concessional hourly rates.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers or registered youth collectives with active operational field layouts.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online via the national [AgriMachinery Portal](https://agrimachinery.nic.in/) under the Tamil Nadu state nodal desk.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID, proforma invoice quote from an empanelled machinery dealer, and land possession records.

---

### [TN_SCHEME_006] Seed Village & Paddy Multiplication Incentive Scheme
* **Category:** Core Sowing Input Provision & Price Incentives
* **Target Beneficiary:** Paddy, pulse, and oilseed farmers establishing seasonal seed production blocks.
* **Benefit Details:** Alleviates input strain and promotes village-level multiplication of certified seed varieties:
  * 50% direct cost subsidy on the procurement of foundational and certified seeds for pulses, groundnut/oilseeds, and millets.
  * **Direct Cash Incentive:** Provides a direct production incentive bonus of ₹2 per kilogram specifically for producing certified paddy seeds, disbursed via DBT.
  * Grants upfront subsidies covering up to 33% of the cost (capped up to ₹3,000) for procuring modern metal scientific storage bins to eliminate post-harvest grain losses.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active landholders with clear crop booking entries verified by localized agricultural officers.
* **How to Apply:** Distributed directly before the sowing season via block-level Agricultural Extension Centres (AECs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Pattadar Passbook, and active seasonal Crop Booking log receipt.
