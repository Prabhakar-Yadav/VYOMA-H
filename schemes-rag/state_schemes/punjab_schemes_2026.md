## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Punjab's state-level agricultural framework focuses heavily on tackling three structural challenges:
1. **Paddy Stubble Burning (Parali):** Mitigated via the In-Situ Crop Residue Management (CRM) machinery subsidy (50% individual, 80% Custom Hiring Centres).
2. **Critical Groundwater Depletion:** Addressed through the Paani Bachao, Paise Kamao DBT electricity scheme, Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) cash incentive (₹1,500/acre), and canal solar lift irrigation networks in dark zones.
3. **Crop Diversification & Value Addition:** Promoted via Kinnu citrus processing, poplar agroforestry chipping subsidies, and dairy farm modernization.

## Section 1: Stubble Burning Prevention, Clean Energy & Groundwater Conservation

### [PUN_SCHEME_001] Punjab In-Situ Crop Residue Management (CRM Blueprint)
* **Category:** Stubble Burning Prevention & Farm Mechanization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual landholders, registered farmer groups, Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS), and Panchayats across all Punjab districts.
* **Benefit Details:** Provides heavy capital subventions for specialized CRM machinery to eliminate winter paddy straw burning:
  * **Individual Farmers:** 50% direct capital subsidy on Super Seeders, Happy Seeders, Smart Seeders, paddy straw choppers, reversible MB ploughs, and heavy-duty straw balers.
  * **Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) / Farmer Collectives:** 80% capital grant to set up common farm machinery banks for affordable equipment rentals to small and marginal farmers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Operational landholders within Punjab. Sourced machinery must come from state-empanelled manufacturers with unique laser-engraved chassis numbers.
* **How to Apply:** Applications and machinery selection are filed online on the unified agricultural mechanization portal (`agrimachinerypb.com`). Physical verification is conducted post-delivery by the Block Agriculture Officer.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Jamabandi records, bank account passbook, PAN card, and proforma invoice quote from an empanelled vendor.

---

### [PUN_SCHEME_002] Punjab State Solar High-Efficiency Lift Irrigation Project
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Groundwater Preservation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farming clusters and Water User Associations (WUAs) operating in critical groundwater over-exploited "dark zones".
* **Benefit Details:** Replaces deep, grid-dependent tubewells with surface water lift systems drawing from regional canal distributaries:
  * **Canal Pipeline Infrastructure:** 90% state funding to lay underground High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) pressure pipe networks directly to farm boundaries.
  * **Solar Pump Component:** 75% financial grant to install standalone solar photovoltaic pumping units (7.5 HP to 15 HP) powering micro-sprinkler and drip crop lines.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Joint farming groups forming a contiguous cluster representing a minimum cumulative farm footprint of 10 Hectares.
* **How to Apply:** Proposals are formulated and submitted through the local office of the Department of Soil and Water Conservation, Punjab.
* **Documents Required:** Group resolution document signed by all clustered landowners, land mutation extracts, layout blueprints, and joint bank account parameters.

---

### [PUN_SCHEME_003] Paani Bachao, Paise Kamao Scheme (DBT for Electricity)
* **Category:** Groundwater Conservation & Resource Subvention
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers holding active, metered agricultural electricity tubewell connections.
* **Benefit Details:** Incentivizes groundwater conservation by setting a fixed monthly electricity consumption quota for agricultural tubewells:
  * If the farmer consumes less electricity than the assigned entitlement threshold, the Punjab Government transfers a direct cash bonus payout of ₹4 per electricity unit (kWh) saved directly via DBT into their bank account.
  * Electricity continues to be supplied free of cost; the bonus is an added cash incentive for efficiency.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must have operational micro-irrigation lines or laser-leveled surfaces. The power connection must be equipped with a functional PSPCL smart energy meter.
* **How to Apply:** Enrollment is completed through the local sub-division office of Punjab State Power Corporation Limited (PSPCL) or via the dedicated agricultural power management gateway.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, recent PSPCL agricultural power bill showing Consumer Account (CA) number, Land Jamabandi copy, and bank passbook.

## Section 2: Water-Smart Cultivation, Input Quality & Rainwater Management

### [PUN_SCHEME_004] Punjab State Seed Subvention Mission (Uttam Beej Portal)
* **Category:** Crop Input Quality & Production Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal farmers cultivating certified wheat, basmati, and oilseed varieties.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidizes high-quality certified and foundation seeds to protect growers from spurious inputs:
  * Provides an instant digital discount voucher of 50% of the seed cost (capped at ₹1,000 per quintal).
  * Vouchers are redeemed instantly at authorized cooperative societies or certified state seed dealers, eliminating middlemen and black marketing.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to registered farmers who map their seasonal crop cycles on the state registry. Maximum subsidy allocation is capped at 5 Acres per farming household.
* **How to Apply:** Digital seed vouchers are generated online through the Uttam Beej tracking gateway (`seedsubvention.punjab.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID, Land Jamabandi records, and active mobile number for OTP validation.

---

### [PUN_SCHEME_006] Punjab Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) Cash Incentive Scheme
* **Category:** Water-Smart Precision Cultivation Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers adopting Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) technology instead of traditional puddled puddle transplanting.
* **Benefit Details:** Combats rapid water table depletion by providing a direct cash incentive transfer of ₹1,500 per acre:
  * Sowing is verified via satellite remote sensing data combined with physical ground-truthing by block-level agriculture teams.
  * The incentive offsets weed-management herbicides and tractor fuel costs during early growth stages.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland fields located within Punjab and officially certified as operational DSR plots during seasonal field inspections.
* **How to Apply:** Registration is completed during the early kharif sowing window via the central Anaaj Kharid / DSR portal (`anaajkharid.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Jamabandi parcel references, bank passbook, and a self-declaration sowing certificate countersigned by the village Lambardar.

---

### [PUN_SCHEME_007] Mukhya Mantri Sanjhi Chat Yojana (Community Rainwater Harvesting)
* **Category:** Decentralized Water Resource Management
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder farming clusters, village Panchayats, and Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS).
* **Benefit Details:** Recharges severely depleted aquifers in groundwater dark zones:
  * Fully funds 85% of the capital cost to build community rooftop rainwater harvesting structures on public and cooperative buildings.
  * Includes gravity filtration units and underground storage sumps to feed localized sprinkler systems during dry spells.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered farmer collectives or village committees covering a minimum cumulative farm layout of 5 Hectares surrounding the installation point.
* **How to Apply:** Handled offline by routing formal project proposals through the local Block Office of the Soil and Water Conservation Department.
* **Documents Required:** Group resolution log, blueprint layout plans of the storage facility, and land clearance certificates.

## Section 3: Diversification, Agroforestry, Dairy & Allied Enterprises

### [PUN_SCHEME_005] Kamdhenu Dairy Enterprise Upgradation Scheme
* **Category:** Allied Sector Livestock Infrastructure & Micro-Credit
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural youth, women dairy farmers, and progressive cattle breeders establishing commercial dairy units.
* **Benefit Details:** Promotes commercial milk production infrastructure:
  * **10 to 20 Milch Animal Units (HF / Jersey Cross / Murrah):** 25% capital subsidy (escalated to 33% for SC/ST beneficiaries) for constructing modern cattle sheds, automatic milking machines, and Total Mixed Ration (TMR) feed mixers.
  * **Interest Subvention:** 5% interest subsidy on bank loan repayments for a continuous tenure of 5 years.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Punjab aged 18 to 45 years possessing at least 0.5 Acres of dedicated green fodder land.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted online via the Punjab Animal Husbandry Department portal and verified by the local Block Veterinary Officer.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, dairy farming training certificate (minimum 2-week course from a state-recognized institute), land title or 5-year registered lease deed, and bank details.

---

### [PUN_SCHEME_008] Punjab Agri-Export Logistics Support Scheme
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Value Addition & Global Market Linkages
* **Target Beneficiary:** Registered Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and agri-exporters processing high-value or perishable crops (e.g., Kinnow citrus, seed potatoes, organic Basmati).
* **Benefit Details:** Executed in coordination with the Punjab Agro Industries Corporation (PAIC):
  * Provides a direct 30% financial transport subsidy (capped at ₹15 Lakh per enterprise per cycle) covering refrigerated container truck rentals, air freight, and ocean freight from Punjab inland container depots (ICDs) to international ports.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered FPOs, cooperatives, or individual enterprises holding an active Import Export Code (IEC) and an APEDA registration certificate.
* **How to Apply:** Upload export consignment invoices, bills of lading, and inspection proofs directly to the PAIC single-window portal (`punjabagro.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Commercial PAN card, IEC license copy, APEDA registration, actual Bill of Lading (BL), and certified freight billing receipts.

---

### [PUN_SCHEME_009] Kinnu and Agro-Forestry Value Addition Mission
* **Category:** Crop Diversification & Secondary Agriculture Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Cultivators diversifying out of traditional wheat-paddy monoculture into Kinnow orchards or commercial poplar/eucalyptus agroforestry.
* **Benefit Details:** Provides a 60% capital grant (capped at ₹8 Lakh per cluster) for setting up primary processing units:
  * Automated fruit washing, waxing, and electronic sizing/grading lines for Kinnow growers.
  * High-capacity mobile wood-chipping and debarking machines for agroforestry clusters.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland acreage registered under diversification tracking on the state agriculture portal.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted through the Punjab State Horticulture Department portal or at the District Horticulture Office.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Jamabandi records, crop conversion certificate issued by the District Horticulture Officer, and vendor quotes.

## Section 4: Rural Welfare & Mandi Labor Social Security

### [PUN_SCHEME_010] Punjab Mandi Board Labor Social Security Framework
* **Category:** Social Security & Hazard Protection for Market Laborers
* **Target Beneficiary:** Registered manual market laborers (Arhtiya helpers, palledars, loaders, weighing scale operators, and cleaners) working across Punjab Mandi Board yards.
* **Benefit Details:** Provides state-absorbed social security and accidental insurance:
  * **Accidental Death or Permanent Total Disability:** Flat financial compensation of ₹4 Lakh paid to the legal nominee family.
  * **Serious Occupational Injury or Amputation:** Cash assistance of ₹1 Lakh to ₹2 Lakh for emergency hospitalization and surgical care.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Manual laborers holding a valid, non-expired digital identity registration card issued by the Punjab State Agricultural Marketing Board (Punjab Mandi Board).
* **How to Apply:** Claims are processed on a fast-track basis by submitting accident documentation to the local Market Committee office within 30 days of the incident.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, active Mandi Labor ID Card, police FIR / General Diary entry copy, civil hospital medical board certificate, and nominee bank passbook.

## Section 5: Ground Application Workflows & State Lexicon

| Regional Term | Operational Context & Significance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Jamabandi** | Official state Record of Rights (RoR) proving land ownership, mandatory for all subsidy verification. |
| **Lambardar** | Village revenue head whose physical signature/countersign validates self-declarations for DSR and crop conversion. |
| **Custom Hiring Centre (CHC)** | Machinery bank set up by PACS or farmer groups receiving 80% subsidy under CRM for village rentals. |
| **Anaaj Kharid Portal** | Core state procurement and farmer registry system (`anaajkharid.in`) used for DSR tracking and MSP DBT. |
| **Arhtiya** | Commission agents operating in Mandi yards; their registered laborers are covered under `PUN_SCHEME_010`. |
| **PAIC** | Punjab Agro Industries Corporation, the nodal agency managing freight subsidies for Kinnow and Basmati exports. |
| **PSPCL** | Punjab State Power Corporation Limited, managing agricultural power metering and ₹4/unit DBT payouts under Paani Bachao scheme. |
