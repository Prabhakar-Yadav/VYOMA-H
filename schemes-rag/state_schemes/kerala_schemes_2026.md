## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Kerala's agricultural framework is shaped by distinct coastal wetlands, below-sea-level polders, and high-value plantation ecosystems:
1. **Wetland & Below-Sea-Level Agro-Ecology:** Preservation of unique *Pokkali* (organic saline paddy-prawn rotational farming in Alappuzha/Ernakulam/Thrissur) and *Kole lands* (below-sea-level rice polders) with ₹20,000/ha de-watering subsidies and 100% organic buyback.
2. **Social Security for Aging Farm Laborers:** The *Karshaka Pension Scheme* provides a direct, lifelong monthly pension of ₹1,600 to elderly agricultural workers aged 60+ with family incomes under ₹1 Lakh.
3. **High-Value Horticultural Risk & Plantation Protection:** State crop insurance covering ₹35,000/ha for paddy and ₹300 per fallen Nendran banana plant, paired with *Keragramam* coconut rejuvenation clusters and *Subhiksha Keralam* fallow land input grants.

## Section 1: Direct Livelihood Support, Food Security & Social Welfare

### [KER_SCHEME_001] Subhiksha Keralam (Hi-Tech Value Chain Module)
* **Category:** Food Security & High-Value Agricultural Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual cultivators, Joint Liability Groups (JLGs), and Kudumbashree women collectives bringing fallow land back under cultivation.
* **Benefit Details:** Promotes statewide self-reliance in vegetables, tubers, and food grains:
  * Direct cash input subsidy of ₹40,000 per Hectare to clear wild vegetation and execute initial plowing on fallow plots.
  * 75% matching capital investment subsidy to construct urban-edge terrace polyhouses, rain-sheltered cultivation sheds, and micro-drip networks.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland located within Kerala. Collective and group farming actions must be registered and validated by the local Krishi Bhavan.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are filed online via the Agriculture Information Management System (`aims.kerala.gov.in`) or processed directly through the Agricultural Officer (AO) at the village Krishi Bhavan.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Revenue tax receipt, Krishi Bhavan Registration Slip, and bank passbook.

---

### [KER_SCHEME_004] Karshaka Pension Scheme (State Agri-Labor Welfare Fund)
* **Category:** Agricultural Worker Social Security & Retirement Relief
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder farmers and traditional landless manual agricultural laborers who have reached retirement age.
* **Benefit Details:** Administered by the Kerala Agricultural Workers Welfare Fund Board:
  * Transfers a direct, lifelong monthly pension payout of ₹1,600 per month via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into the bank accounts of aging agricultural workers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Kerala aged 60 years or older. Total annual household income from all sources must be under ₹1 Lakh, and cumulative land ownership must not exceed 2.5 Acres (1 Hectare). The applicant must have performed verified manual farm labor for a minimum continuous period of 10 years.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are verified at the grassroots level by the Gram Panchayat office and registered through local Akshaya Centres or the village Krishi Bhavan.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, proof of age (School Certificate / Voter ID), certified Annual Income Certificate from the Village Officer, agricultural labor verification certificate signed by the Agricultural Officer (AO), and bank passbook.

## Section 2: Climate-Resilient Coastal Wetlands & Crop Risk Insurance

### [KER_SCHEME_005] Pokkali & Kole Land Climate-Resilient Cultivation Support
* **Category:** Sustainable Coastal Wetland Farming & Ecological Safeguards
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farming collectives, traditional Padasekhara Samithis, and individual cultivators in waterlogged coastal saline wetlands (Pokkali lands) and below-sea-level flood basins (Kole lands).
* **Benefit Details:** Protects fragile coastal wetland agricultural ecosystems:
  * Direct operational cash grant of ₹20,000 per Hectare to execute seasonal land preparation, de-watering pumping operations, and outer ring-bund reinforcement.
  * Free distribution of specialized salt-tolerant organic seed varieties.
  * 100% buyback guarantee of organic Pokkali rice grain harvest at premium state-notified procurement floor rates.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must fall within officially demarcated Pokkali or Kole wetland boundaries. Applications must be routed through a registered collective farming unit or Padasekhara Samithi.
* **How to Apply:** File applications on the AIMS portal (`aims.kerala.gov.in`) or submit through the managing committee of the local Padasekhara Samithi.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Padasekhara Samithi membership register citation, Land Revenue tax receipt, and group bank account passbook.

---

### [KER_SCHEME_002] Kerala State Crop Insurance Scheme (Paddy & Cash Crop Component)
* **Category:** High-Risk Crop Insurance & Climate Disaster Mitigation
* **Target Beneficiary:** All cultivators growing perishable cash crops (Paddy, Banana, Coconut, Arecanut, Pepper, Tapioca, Rubber, Cardamom) vulnerable to heavy monsoon storms and coastal flooding.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive state-funded crop insurance requiring nominal farmer premium contributions (e.g., ₹2 per banana plant or ₹100 per hectare of paddy):
  * **Paddy Crop Loss (Flood/Submergence):** Flat statutory compensation payout of ₹35,000 per Hectare.
  * **Nendran Banana Crop Loss (Wind storm/Lodging/Flood):** Flat compensation payout of ₹300 per individual damaged plant.
  * **Coconut Tree Uprooting:** Direct cash relief of ₹1,000 per bearing palm.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to both landowning cultivators and verified tenant farmers. Enrollment must occur within 30 days of seasonal crop planting.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online through `aims.kerala.gov.in` or registered directly at the village Krishi Bhavan.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Tax receipt or signed tenancy declaration countersigned by the local Ward Member, and e-crop confirmation entry.

## Section 3: Plantation Rejuvenation & Allied Livestock Enterprises

### [KER_SCHEME_003] Keragramam Yojana (Integrated Coconut Development)
* **Category:** Commercial Plantation Preservation & Disease Control
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder coconut farmers and village coconut farming clusters facing severe yield declines from Root (Wilt) and Red Palm Weevil diseases.
* **Benefit Details:** Establishes intensive, contiguous 25-Hectare coconut cultivation clusters (*Keragramam*):
  * Cluster-level grant of ₹50,000 per 25-Hectare block for establishing community processing and pest monitoring units.
  * Free supply of premium high-yielding hybrid dwarf coconut seedlings (TXD or West Coast Tall varieties).
  * 50% subsidy on organic bio-fertilizers, neem cake, and soil acidity correcting dolomite application.
  * Direct compensation grant of ₹2,000 per tree to fell and remove incurably disease-infected senile palms.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Landholders possessing a minimum of 10 standing coconut palms on their property. Farmers must agree to clear-cut infected palms under department supervision.
* **How to Apply:** Enroll at the regional Krishi Bhavan or through the local Padasekhara Samithi / Coconut Cluster Coordinator.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Krishi Bhavan registration ID, Land Possession Certificate (LPC), and bank passbook.

---

### [KER_SCHEME_006] Kerala Agri-Allied Hi-Tech Micro-Poultry & Goat Breeding Venture
* **Category:** Livestock Allied Enterprise & Livelihood Upgrades
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural youth, landless agricultural workers, and women self-help groups (Kudumbashree units) establishing micro-livestock enterprises.
* **Benefit Details:** Credit-linked capital subsidies for clean, modern backyard mini-farms:
  * **Goat Rearing Units (5 Does + 1 Buck baseline):** 50% to 60% direct capital subsidy to purchase elite indigenous breeds (Malabari or Attappady Black) and construct raised-slat wooden sheds.
  * **Poultry Units (50 Layer Chicks package):** 70% financial grant covering high-yield layer strains (BV380 or Gramapriya), automated feeding trays, and electric brooders.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Kerala aged 18 to 55 years holding an animal health registration certificate from a government veterinary clinic.
* **How to Apply:** Handled offline by registering with the block-level Veterinary Extension Centre or village Krishi Bhavan.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Kudumbashree membership identification card (for women group subsidies), land/backyard space possession certificate, and bank passbook.

## Section 4: Ground Application Workflows & Malayalam Lexicon

| Regional Term | Operational Context & Significance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AIMS Portal** | Agriculture Information Management System (`aims.kerala.gov.in`), the unified digital window for all state agricultural schemes, insurance, and subsidies. |
| **Krishi Bhavan** | Village-level agriculture office headed by the Agricultural Officer (AO), serving as the primary nodal touchpoint for all farmer services. |
| **Padasekhara Samithi** | Registered collective farming committee of paddy landholders managing common irrigation, de-watering, and mechanization across polders. |
| **Pokkali Lands** | Coastal saline marshlands using a natural, organic cyclic rotation: Pokkali salt-tolerant rice during monsoons (June–Oct), followed by filtration prawn rearing (Nov–April). |
| **Kole Lands** | Low-lying wetland polders situated below sea level in Thrissur and Malappuram, requiring heavy de-watering pumps to enable paddy cultivation. |
| **Kudumbashree** | State poverty eradication and women empowerment mission running Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) for collective fallow land farming. |
| **Akshaya Centres** | Kerala's statewide e-governance service centers providing public access to AIMS scheme registration and Karshaka Pension applications. |
| **Land Possession Certificate (LPC)** | Revenue document issued by the Village Office verifying undisputed possession of agricultural land parcels. |
