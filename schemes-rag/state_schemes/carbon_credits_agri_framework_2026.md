# Farmer-Facing Agricultural Carbon & Green Credit Schemes (2026 Edition)

This document contains the 6 direct, actionable agricultural carbon and green credit programs where individual farmers and smallholders receive direct financial cash transfers (DBT) for adopting climate-smart farming practices.

---

### [CARB_SCHEME_001] Uttar Pradesh Zero-Stubble & Agroforestry Reward (UP Carbon Project)
- Category: Carbon Credits
- Target Beneficiary: Small, marginal, and tenant farmers in Uttar Pradesh (Saharanpur, Bareilly, Ayodhya, and surrounding districts) who stop crop residue burning and plant boundary trees.
- Benefit Details: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) cash payments into the farmer's bank account from a Rs 202 Crore state carbon fund. Farmers receive an estimated Rs 2,500 to Rs 4,800 per acre per year across a 5-year project commitment.
- Eligibility Criteria: Stop burning paddy and wheat stubble after harvest. Plant rows of fast-growing indigenous trees (Poplar, Neem, Melia Dubia) along field boundaries. Non-burning is verified every 5 days via Sentinel satellite imagery.
- How to Apply: Bring your land revenue documents (Khatauni / 7/12) and Aadhaar card to your local Block Farmer Producer Organization (FPO) office or apply on the UP Krishi Portal under the Carbon Offset tab.
- Documents Required: Aadhaar card, Khatauni / land title records, bank passbook seeded with Aadhaar, and FPO registration ID.
- Contact Details: UP Agriculture Department Carbon Desk, Krishi Bhavan, Lucknow / Block Agriculture Extension Office.

### [CARB_SCHEME_002] Punjab & Haryana Water-Saving Paddy Bonus Project
- Category: Carbon Credits
- Target Beneficiary: Rice and paddy farmers in Punjab (Ludhiana, Patiala, Sangrur, Amritsar) and Haryana (Karnal, Kaithal, Kurukshetra) adopting water-saving techniques.
- Benefit Details: Extra cash incentive of Rs 1,200 to Rs 2,800 per acre per year sent directly to the farmer's bank account via DBT, completely separate from standard MSP crop sale income.
- Eligibility Criteria: Stop continuous deep flooding of rice fields. Adopt Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) or Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) using perforated field water pipes (pani pipes). Zero open-field stubble burning.
- How to Apply: Contact your local FPO Field Executive or registered project partner (Varaha, Grow Indigo, Boomitra). Field teams map farm boundaries using mobile GIS applications for free.
- Documents Required: Aadhaar card, land record (Jamabandi / Fard), PM-KISAN linked bank passbook, and field survey confirmation.
- Contact Details: Punjab & Haryana State Agriculture Departments / Local Block FPO Extension Hub.

### [CARB_SCHEME_003] Farm Boundary Tree Subsidy (National Sub-Mission on Agroforestry)
- Category: Agroforestry Carbon
- Target Beneficiary: Small, marginal, and landholding farmers across all Indian States planting commercial and timber trees along farm bunds and boundaries.
- Benefit Details: 50% direct government subsidy to purchase tree saplings from state nurseries, plus an additional cash reward of Rs 150 to Rs 300 per tree per year as the trees grow from year 3 to year 7.
- Eligibility Criteria: Ownership or recorded tenancy of cultivable farmland (7/12, Patta, Jamabandi, or Khatauni). Planting a minimum of 50 saplings (Poplar, Eucalyptus, Melia Dubia, Shisham, Bamboo) along field boundaries without displacing food crops.
- How to Apply: Apply at your village Gram Panchayat office, nearest Common Service Centre (CSC), or online through the National Agroforestry Portal.
- Documents Required: Aadhaar card, land title record, bank passbook, and sapling purchase receipt from government nursery.
- Contact Details: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (MoA&FW), New Delhi / Local Forest Range Office. Portal: agroforestry.dac.gov.in.

### [CARB_SCHEME_004] Central & South India Natural Farming Multiplier (Andhra & Telangana)
- Category: Carbon Credits
- Target Beneficiary: Smallholders, marginal farmers, and recorded tenant cultivators (CCRC card holders) in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana practicing chemical-free agriculture.
- Benefit Details: Direct cash bonuses paid every 18 months because high-biodiversity Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) commands premium prices ($12 to $15 per credit) on international voluntary carbon markets.
- Eligibility Criteria: 100% elimination of synthetic chemical fertilizers and urea. Regular application of organic formulations like Jeevamrutha and Beejamrutha. Practice Pre-Monsoon Dry Sowing (PMDS) to maintain 365-day green soil cover.
- How to Apply: Register your name with your local village Rythu Sadhikara Samstha (RySS) cluster coordinator or at the nearest Rythu Bharosa Kendra (RBK).
- Documents Required: Aadhaar card, CCRC tenant card or Pattadar Passbook, bank passbook, and APCNF natural farming enrollment ID.
- Contact Details: Rythu Sadhikara Samstha (RySS), Department of Agriculture, Andhra Pradesh. Website: apcnf.in.

### [CARB_SCHEME_005] Central India Regenerative Cotton Cluster (Madhya Pradesh & Gujarat)
- Category: Carbon Credits
- Target Beneficiary: Cotton and soybean farmers in black soil regions across Madhya Pradesh (Khargone, Khandwa, Dhar) and Gujarat (Rajkot, Amreli, Surendranagar).
- Benefit Details: Direct cash bonus of Rs 3,500 to Rs 5,200 per hectare every year paid out directly before the Kharif sowing season, funded by global sustainable cotton supply chains.
- Eligibility Criteria: Eliminate chemical pesticides in cotton cultivation. Intercrop cotton with legumes and pulses (pigeon pea / green gram) to naturally restore soil nitrogen and organic carbon.
- How to Apply: Register directly at your local block Cotton Ginning Cooperative Center or FPO carbon desk partnering with the Better Cotton Initiative (BCI) / Grow Indigo.
- Documents Required: Land revenue record (7/12 / Khasra), Aadhaar card, bank passbook, and cotton sowing declaration.
- Contact Details: MP Department of Farmer Welfare / Gujarat State Cooperative Cotton Federation.

### [CARB_SCHEME_006] North-East Bamboo Plantation Sink (Assam, Mizoram, Nagaland)
- Category: Carbon Credits
- Target Beneficiary: Tribal and hill farmers in Assam (Karbi Anglong), Mizoram (Mamit), Nagaland (Dimapur), and Meghalaya managing sloping or fallow lands.
- Benefit Details: Cash payouts of Rs 4,000 to Rs 7,500 per acre every year starting from the fourth year of bamboo plantation, due to bamboo's rapid biomass carbon sequestration rate.
- Eligibility Criteria: Cultivation of high-yield bamboo species (Bambusa balcooa, Tulda) on hill contours or empty shifting cultivation (jhum) fallow land. Complete ban on burning vegetation during land preparation.
- How to Apply: Enrol through your local Village Community Council or State Bamboo Development Agency (BDA) under the North Eastern Council (NEC).
- Documents Required: Community land possession certificate, Aadhaar card, Village Council endorsement letter, and GPS boundary survey.
- Contact Details: North Eastern Council (NEC), Shillong / State Bamboo Development Agencies. Website: necouncil.gov.in.
