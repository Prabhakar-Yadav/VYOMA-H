## Executive Summary & National Operational Context

### National Policy Landscape
The Indian agricultural decarbonization ecosystem operates under two primary national architectures:
1. **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS):** Regulated by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) and the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), establishing the formal Indian Carbon Market (ICM). The CCTS features an "Offset Mechanism" where voluntary sectors (including agriculture, agroforestry, and methane abatement) generate Carbon Credit Certificates (CCCs) tradable to obligated domestic industrial entities.
2. **Green Credit Programme (GCP):** Governed by the Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education (ICFRE), creating a non-carbon market trading architecture. Under the GCP, environmental activities like agroforestry and water conservation generate tradable Green Credits through an online portal tracking ecosystem services rather than pure emissions offsets.

### State-Level Initiatives
* **Uttar Pradesh:** The UP Mandi Parishad and dedicated Agroforestry Carbon Desks have institutionalized an Agroforestry Policy aimed at boosting state green cover from 9.23% to 15%. In collaboration with technical entities like TERI, the state monitors carbon accumulation across millions of boundary trees, channeling structured financial inflows to smallholders.
* **Punjab & Haryana:** State initiatives promote Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) and Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) to limit methane emissions from flooded paddies, leveraging machinery subsidies (Happy Seeders, laser levelers) to establish the necessary electronic compliance logs.
* **Andhra Pradesh:** Through Andhra Pradesh Community-Managed Natural Farming (APCNF), the state runs the world's largest agro-ecological transition program, generating high-premium soil carbon offsets by eliminating synthetic inputs across millions of acres.
* **Madhya Pradesh & Maharashtra:** Focus centers on Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) enhancement via regenerative tillage, crop residue retention, and multi-cropping systems linked to international voluntary carbon registries.
* **Ladakh:** The High-Altitude Carbon-Neutral Ladakh initiative incentivizes passive greenhouses, organic farming, and cold-desert agroforestry to lock carbon in fragile alpine soils.
* **Coastal Regions:** Blue Carbon frameworks in West Bengal (Sundarbans), Gujarat, and Odisha monetize mangrove restoration and marine fisheries/open-sea cage culture under specialized coastal ecosystem credits.

### FPO Aggregation, MRV, and Economic Metrics
Because individual smallholder farms average less than 2 hectares, direct market access is barred by transaction costs. The operational framework relies entirely on Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) and private agritech developers acting as aggregators. These entities bundle contiguous or distributed smallholdings into single project pipelines of at least 500 to 1,000 hectares.

Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (MRV) has shifted from costly manual soil cores to digital MRV models. These systems fuse high-resolution satellite remote sensing (Sentinel-2 NDVI, radar backscatter) with AI-driven biophysical models and localized stratified soil testing to verify carbon sequestration and methane reductions down to individual field boundaries.

## Individual Scheme Profiles

### [CARB_SCHEME_001] Uttar Pradesh Agroforestry Carbon Credit Project
* **Category:** State Government Scheme / Public-Private Initiative
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and tribal farmers practicing boundary or block plantation across Uttar Pradesh.
* **Benefit Details:** Generates 3.5 to 5.5 $tCO_2e$ per acre annually based on tree biomass accumulation. Estimated cash payout ranges from ₹1,800 to ₹4,200 per acre per year via direct benefit transfer (DBT). Technical practice requires planting and maintaining native timber and fruit-bearing trees (Mango, Neem, Shisham, Poplar) on farm bunds or fallow patches.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Minimum cluster size of 500 hectares aggregated via an FPO or local project desk. Baseline requirement dictates that the land must have had low tree canopy cover over the preceding 3 years. Clear land tenure with verified land revenue records is mandatory.
* **How to Apply:** Onboarding occurs through local FPO desks or UP Mandi Parishad technical counters. Field agents perform GPS plot geofencing using handheld mobile applications to record field boundaries. Digital MRV audits execute canopy density and health analytics via Sentinel-2 NDVI monitoring data.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, Khatauni (land record extract), geo-tagged boundary coordinates (KML or Shapefiles), passport size photograph, active bank passbook copy for Aadhaar-enabled payment system (AePS) linkage.
* **Contact Details & Official Portals:** Department of Forest, Environmental and Climate Change, Government of Uttar Pradesh. Portal: `upforest.gov.in`

### [CARB_SCHEME_002] National CCTS Agricultural Offset Mechanism
* **Category:** Central Government Scheme
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs), cooperative agricultural societies, and unified village clusters nationwide.
* **Benefit Details:** Issues Carbon Credit Certificates (CCCs) tracking actual metric tonnes of $CO_2$ equivalent reduced. Estimated monetization ranges from ₹1,200 to ₹3,500 per acre per year depending on market clearing prices within the Indian Carbon Market (ICM). Technical practices include transitioning to zero-tillage, implementing biomass mulching, and adopting multi-cropping agronomy.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Aggregated project footprint must cross a threshold of 1,000 continuous or distributed hectares under a single management entity. Farmers must commit to a minimum 5-year project permanence cycle without shifting back to intensive residue burning or deep inversion tillage.
* **How to Apply:** FPOs apply directly through the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) National Carbon Registry Portal. The application requires upload of aggregated digital maps. The digital MRV audit process matches field crop logs against satellite-derived land surface disturbance metrics.
* **Documents Required:** FPO registration certificate, consolidated land records index (7/12 extract, Patta, or Jamabandi matching state jurisdiction), signed farmer consent deeds for carbon property rights transfer, verified baseline agricultural log.
* **Contact Details & Official Portals:** Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), Ministry of Power, Government of India. Portal: `beeindia.gov.in`

### [CARB_SCHEME_003] Green Credit Programme (GCP) Agroforestry Framework
* **Category:** Central Government Scheme
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers, primary agricultural credit societies (PACS), communities, and FPOs.
* **Benefit Details:** Grants tradable Green Credits computed per tree established and sustained over a 2-year verification lifecycle. Estimated monetization ranges from ₹800 to ₹2,500 per acre per year via trading on the domestic GCP market platform. Technical practice requires tree plantation on degraded lands, farmlands, or common property resources.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to all land sizes; smallholders can register parcels as small as 0.1 hectares. Land must be free from legal encumbrances. Baseline practices must show absence of existing dense vegetation prior to enrollment.
* **How to Apply:** Applicants register directly on the MoEFCC Green Credit Portal. The location is geo-tagged via the mobile app interface. Independent validation is conducted by the Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education (ICFRE) through designated local verification agencies.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, land ownership deed or community land usage allocation certificate (Patta), high-resolution GPS boundary coordinates, baseline site photographs.
* **Contact Details & Official Portals:** Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education (ICFRE), Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change. Portal: `mofecc-gcp.nic.in`

### [CARB_SCHEME_004] Northwest India Paddy Methane Reduction Project
* **Category:** Public-Private Aggregated Market
* **Target Beneficiary:** Paddy-cultivating farmers in Punjab, Haryana, and Western Uttar Pradesh.
* **Benefit Details:** Yields 1.5 to 2.5 $tCO_2e$ per acre per year by avoiding anaerobic methane generation. Estimated cash payout ranges from ₹1,200 to ₹2,800 per acre per year paid out by the private developer post-issuance. Technical practice requires shifting from continuous submergence to Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) using field water tubes, or adopting Direct Seeded Rice (DSR).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Aggregation via registered agritech channels with a minimum cluster size of 400 hectares. Farmers must possess functional irrigation access under their direct operational control to alternate watering cycles accurately.
* **How to Apply:** Registration is conducted via partner FPOs or agritech field agents. Field boundaries are mapped using mobile GPS tracking. Digital MRV audits utilize radar satellite imagery (Sentinel-1) to detect surface water metrics, validating that field drying cycles occurred according to protocol.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, Jamabandi or 7/12 land record, active tube-well electricity account or canal water allocation receipt, signed carbon asset rights agreement, digital baseline agricultural log.
* **Contact Details & Official Portals:** Joint Agritech Project Registry Counters / Partnered State Agriculture Extensions. Portal: `growindigo.co.in` / `boomitra.com`

### [CARB_SCHEME_005] APCNF Regenerative Agriculture Carbon Program
* **Category:** State Government Scheme / Public-Private Initiative
* **Target Beneficiary:** Marginal, small, and tenant farmers enrolled under the Andhra Pradesh Community-Managed Natural Farming network.
* **Benefit Details:** Yields 0.8 to 2.0 $tCO_2e$ per acre per year through soil carbon building. Estimated cash payout ranges from ₹900 to ₹2,200 per acre per year, delivered alongside premium organic market access. Technical practice requires 100% elimination of synthetic fertilizers and pesticides, application of microbial bio-inoculants (*Jeevamrutham*), and year-round permanent ground cover.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active enrollment in the APCNF field register. Tenant farmers must present valid tenancy certificates (*Batai-dar* / *CCRC* verification). Baseline monitoring must show history of conventional chemical farming practices.
* **How to Apply:** Onboarding occurs through the local Rythu Bharosa Kendras (RBKs) and APCNF village-level cadres. Field mapping is executed via GIS-based land parcel identification systems. Verification incorporates stratified soil sampling combined with remote sensing verification models (Verra VM0042 methodology).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, Webland RoR (Record of Rights) printout or CCRC (Crop Cultivator Rights Card) for tenant farmers, geo-tagged plot photos, historical 3-year input purchase log self-declaration.
* **Contact Details & Official Portals:** Rythu Sadhikara Samstha (RySS), Government of Andhra Pradesh. Portal: `apcnf.in`

### [CARB_SCHEME_006] Central India Soil Organic Carbon Restoration Initiative
* **Category:** Public-Private Aggregated Market
* **Target Beneficiary:** Dryland and rainfed farmers growing cotton, soybean, and pulses in Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Chhattisgarh.
* **Benefit Details:** Generates 0.7 to 1.8 $tCO_2e$ per acre per year via stable humus accumulation. Estimated cash payout ranges from ₹700 to ₹1,900 per acre per year. Technical practices include zero-till or reduced-till mechanical seeding, multi-species cover cropping, and in-situ crop residue incorporation via shredders instead of field burning.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Managed under an FPO cluster model spanning a minimum threshold of 800 hectares. Land tenure must be clear of active ownership disputes.
* **How to Apply:** Farmers apply through their local FPO management committee. Agritech technical field teams execute baseline core soil testing down to 30 cm depth. Digital MRV involves continuous satellite monitoring of crop residue retention index and fractional vegetation cover.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, 7/12 extract or Khasra-Khatauni document, FPO membership passbook, GPS boundary coordinate data file, signed validation consent form.
* **Contact Details & Official Portals:** National Sustainable Agriculture Carbon Registry Cell / Partnered Carbon Developers. Portal: `agricoop.nic.in`

### [CARB_SCHEME_007] High-Altitude Carbon-Neutral Ladakh Framework
* **Category:** State Government Scheme
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder high-altitude farmers and nomadic agricultural cooperatives in Ladakh and alpine regions.
* **Benefit Details:** Yields 1.2 to 3.0 $tCO_2e$ per acre per year inside specialized micro-climate farming units. Estimated cash payout ranges from ₹1,500 to ₹3,200 per acre per year funded via regional carbon compensation allocations. Technical practices require building passive solar greenhouses, cultivating localized Seabuckthorn agroforestry blocks, and practicing organic composting.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farming units must be located above 3,000 meters altitude. Land must be clearly registered within local LAHDC revenue records.
* **How to Apply:** Application forms are submitted to the local Executive Councilor (Agriculture) office or Block Development Officer. GPS plot coordinates are cataloged by the Ladakh Remote Sensing Application Centre. Digital verification cross-references thermal satellite data to assess greenhouse efficiency and biomass yield.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, Local Resident Certificate, revenue land allotment document (Patta), geo-fenced site coordinates, cooperative membership certificate.
* **Contact Details & Official Portals:** Ladakh Autonomous Hill Development Council (LAHDC) / Department of Agriculture Ladakh. Portal: `ladakh.nic.in`

---

### [CARB_SCHEME_008] Coastal Mangrove Blue Carbon and Estuarine Fisheries Program
* **Category:** State Government Scheme / Public-Private Initiative
* **Target Beneficiary:** Coastal communities, marine fishing cooperatives, and smallholders in estuarine buffer zones (Sundarbans, Mahanadi, Gulf of Khambhat).
* **Benefit Details:** Yields high-value blue carbon assets tracking 4.5 to 8.0 $tCO_2e$ per acre per year due to deep anaerobic sediment carbon trapping. Estimated cash payout ranges from ₹2,500 to ₹6,500 per acre per year. Technical practices require mangrove sapling restoration along farm borders, maintaining tidal water channels, and shifting to integrated silvo-aquaculture (shrimp/fish cultivation inside mangrove canopies).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Lands must fall within Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) buffer boundaries. Group aggregation requires a minimum of 200 hectares of contiguous tidal mudflats or low-lying brackish water plots under community management.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are routed through the local Marine Fishing Cooperative Society or Gram Panchayat to the State Forest Department Coastal Carbon Cell. Verification combines multi-spectral satellite canopy assessments with regular sediment core testing by accredited Validation and Verification Bodies (VVBs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, Coastal land occupancy license or traditional fishing rights certificate, village panchayat resolution document, KML geo-fenced boundary coordinates.
* **Contact Details & Official Portals:** State Forest Departments (Coastal Wetland Divisions) / National Centre for Sustainable Coastal Management (NCSCM). Portal: `ncscm.res.in`

## Ground Application Lexicon

| Term / Acronym | Functional Operational Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD)** | An irrigation methodology where paddy fields are allowed to dry out for a period of days before re-flooding, halting anaerobic methane production without reducing grain yield. |
| **Verra VM0042** | The internationally recognized methodology framework for "Methodology for Agricultural Land Management," defining how soil organic carbon sequestration projects are measured, audited, and credited. |
| **CCTS Indian Carbon Market** | The national compliance architecture designed to allow domestic heavy industries to buy carbon credit certificates from voluntary sectors like agriculture to meet their emission reduction targets. |
| **Green Credit GCP** | A market mechanism that monetizes environmental activities (such as water harvesting and afforestation) via non-carbon tradeable units, distinct from greenhouse gas reduction metrics. |
| **Digital MRV** | Automated Measurement, Reporting, and Verification utilizing remote sensing, satellite data feeds, cloud computing, and machine learning models to track farm compliance without physical onsite audits. |
| **Sentinel-2 NDVI** | Normalized Difference Vegetation Index derived from the European Space Agency's Sentinel-2 satellites, used to calculate canopy density, biomass, and chlorophyll absorption metrics on the ground. |
| **Soil Organic Carbon (SOC)** | The measurable carbon component of soil organic matter, acting as the core metric for tracking carbon sequestration under regenerative farming practices. |
| **VVB Auditor** | Validation and Verification Body; an independent, accredited third-party agency (such as TÜV, S&P Global, or EPIC) tasked with verifying field data before credits can be minted. |
| **FPO Aggregator** | A Farmer Producer Organisation that collects the physical land assets and compliance logs of thousands of smallholder farmers into a single entity to meet registry size thresholds. |
| **Jamabandi / Khatauni / 7-12** | State-specific vernacular land tenure registers and Records of Rights (RoR) used to verify legal land ownership and cultivation rights during the baseline audit stage. |
