## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

West Bengal's agricultural governance framework addresses the high-density smallholder and tenant farming realities across the lower Gangetic delta, the salinity-prone coastal Sundarbans, and the northern sub-Himalayan Terai-Dooars and Darjeeling hills:
1. **Universal Income Protection & Zero-Premium Risk Cushion:** The landmark twin-pillar *Krishak Bandhu* initiative delivers guaranteed cash assistance (up to ₹10,000/year with a ₹4,000 minimum floor) and ₹2 Lakh ex-gratia death compensation covering both title-holding landholders and recorded tenant sharecroppers (*Bargadars*). The *Bangla Shasya Bima (BSB)* framework provides 100% state-funded, zero-premium crop insurance for all major food grains, pulses, oilseeds, and jute.
2. **Rainwater Retention, Storage Silos & Soil Restoration:** *Jal Dharo Jal Bharo* subsidizes on-farm rainwater harvesting ponds integrated with pond-bank horticulture and inland pisciculture, *Amar Fasal Amar Gola* finances on-farm grain silos and onion storage houses (up to ₹25,000 grants) to stop distress sales, and *Matir Swasthya Suraksha* delivers a 75% subsidy for *Dhaincha* green manuring to reclaim saline coastal soils.
3. **Multi-Tier Mechanization & Grassroots Outreach:** Heavy machinery automation through the *Financial Support to Farm Mechanisation (FSSM)* (40%–50% capital subsidies), ₹20 Lakh grants for cooperative *Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs)*, and the *OTA-CHY* small-tool program (50% subsidy up to ₹10,000 for knapsack sprayers, brush cutters, and weeders), processed seamlessly via decentralized *Duare Sarkar* camps and the *Matir Katha* digital portal.

## Section 1: Flagship Direct Income Support, Social Security & Zero-Premium Insurance

### [WB_SCHEME_001] Krishak Bandhu (Assured Income & Death Benefit Scheme)
* **Category:** Direct Agri-Investment Support & Social Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** All landowning farmers, recorded sharecroppers (*Bargadars*), and agricultural laborers across West Bengal.
* **Benefit Details:** Landmark twin-component state financial safety net:
  * **Krishak Bandhu Assured Income:**
    * Provides a guaranteed financial grant of ₹10,000 per year to farmers possessing 1 acre or more of cultivable land.
    * Cultivators owning less than 1 acre receive a prorated financial grant with a guaranteed minimum statutory floor of ₹4,000 per year.
    * Disbursed in two equal seasonal tranches (Kharif and Rabi cycles) directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
  * **Krishak Bandhu Death Benefit:**
    * Delivers a direct lump-sum ex-gratia financial compensation of ₹2 Lakh to the legal nominee/family upon the death of the registered farmer (due to natural or accidental causes) between the ages of 18 and 60 years.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active resident cultivators registered on the state land records database (RoR / Porcha), including legally verified *Bargadars* (sharecroppers).
* **How to Apply:** Handled digitally via the unified [Krishak Bandhu Portal](https://krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in/) or through physical enrollment desks at localized block *Duare Sarkar* (Government at your Doorstep) camps.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID Card (mandatory), Pattadar Passbook / RoR (Record of Rights / Porcha) extract, Barga registration certificate (for sharecroppers), bank passbook, and declaration form. For Death Benefit: Death Certificate and Age Proof of the deceased farmer.

---

### [WB_SCHEME_002] Bangla Shasya Bima (BSB Crop Insurance)
* **Category:** Comprehensive Crop Risk Insurance (Zero-Premium Model)
* **Target Beneficiary:** All category farmers cultivating notified crops in designated administrative blocks across West Bengal.
* **Benefit Details:** 100% state-subsidized crop risk insurance framework protecting against cyclones, floods, unseasonal rains, and pest outbreaks:
  * **100% Premium Waiver for Food Crops:** The State Government completely covers the entire insurance premium liability for major food grains, pulses, oilseeds, and commercial fiber crops (Paddy/Rice, Maize, Jute, Mustard, and Pulses).
  * The insurance coverage is provided 100% free of cost to the farmer (zero premium deduction).
  * Financial compensation payouts are calculated using satellite remote sensing indices and localized Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) certified by Krishi Projukti Sahayaks (KPS).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators (both title-holding landowners and tenant sharecroppers) cultivating notified seasonal crops in notified blocks. Mandatory for farmers availing institutional seasonal crop loans.
* **How to Apply:** Processed seasonally at block Agriculture Offices, through Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies (PACS), or during *Duare Sarkar* outreach windows.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Land possession note / RoR extract, Sowing Certificate verified by the local Krishi Projukti Sahayak (KPS), and bank passbook.

## Section 2: Water Security & Precision Irrigation Subsidies

### [WB_SCHEME_003] Jal Dharo Jal Bharo Yojana (Farm Pond Harvesting Module)
* **Category:** Rainwater Harvesting & On-Farm Water Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and general category farmers constructing on-farm storage reserves in drought-prone blocks (Purulia, Bankura, Paschim Medinipur) and delta zones.
* **Benefit Details:** Structural subvention framework to arrest surface runoff and recharge groundwater:
  * Provides a direct state-sponsored financial grant to construct or re-excavate permanent rainwater harvesting farm ponds and multi-tier storage tanks.
  * Promotes ecological convergence by integrating farm pond water storage with allied inland pisciculture (fish fingerling stocking) and commercial vegetable cultivation on pond embankment bunds.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must have a clear, contiguous layout suitable for pond construction that does not obstruct natural drainage channels.
* **How to Apply:** Validation dossiers are routed through the regional Block Development Officer (BDO) or submitted to the Water Resources Investigation & Development Department (WRIDD) desk.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, recent Land RoR (Porcha) extract, sketch layout design of the proposed farm pond, and an environmental clearance note from the Gram Panchayat.

---

### [WB_SCHEME_004] Bangla Krishi Sech Yojana (Micro-Irrigation Module)
* **Category:** Precision Micro-Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Vegetable growers, betel vine farmers, orchardists, and multi-crop smallholders in water-stressed or coastal saline tracts.
* **Benefit Details:** Precision water management subvention optimizing water-use efficiency and lowering pumping costs:
  * Delivers up to an 85% capital financial subsidy for small and marginal farmers to install complete drip and sprinkler irrigation networks.
  * Minimizes evaporation losses, prevents soil salinity buildup in coastal zones, and ensures uniform root-zone moisture delivery.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators possessing an active, independent water lift source (open well, borewell, or solar/electric pump connection) with valid land records.
* **How to Apply:** Apply directly at the regional District Agriculture Office (DAO) or via the state micro-irrigation monitoring index.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Patta / RoR Jamabandi papers, pump connection certificate, and vendor micro-irrigation design blueprints.

## Section 3: Farm Mechanization, Custom Hiring & Implement Subsidies

### [WB_SCHEME_005] Financial Support to Farm Mechanisation (FSSM)
* **Category:** Farm Automation Capital Subsidy
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual progressive farmers, smallholder collectives, and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).
* **Benefit Details:** Accelerates the deployment of heavy agricultural machinery to counter critical seasonal agricultural labor shortages:
  * Provides a direct 40% to 50% capital investment subsidy (capped at state-prescribed maximum financial limits) on individual purchases of heavy machinery: tractors, power tillers, rotavators, combined harvesters, paddy transplanters, and laser land levelers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered cultivators or farmer collectives listed on the state agricultural database holding operational land records.
* **How to Apply:** Applications open dynamically during announced booking windows on the [Matir Katha Portal](https://matirkatha.net/).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID, machinery dealer proforma invoice quotation, and land mutation records.

---

### [WB_SCHEME_006] One Time Assistance for Agri-Implements (OTA-CHY)
* **Category:** Small-Scale Farm Tool Subvention
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders, small vegetable growers, and family farming setups.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial assistance to make manual and semi-mechanized micro-implements accessible:
  * Grants a direct 50% capital subsidy capped at ₹10,000 to purchase essential small-scale farm implements.
  * Equipment covered: manual rotary weeders, brush cutters, pedal-operated paddy threshers, battery-operated knapsack sprayers, seed drills, and specialized wheel hoes.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Restricted to small and marginal category farmers listed on the centralized state database.
* **How to Apply:** Handled through block Agriculture Extension nodes or via localized *Duare Sarkar* mobile registration counters.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Land ownership card / KPS verification receipt, and bank passbook.

---

### [WB_SCHEME_007] Custom Hiring Centre (CHC) Establishment Subvention
* **Category:** Collective Modern Mechanisation & Asset Creation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS), registered Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and rural youth self-help group collectives.
* **Benefit Details:** High-impact engineering subvention eliminating machinery procurement barriers for smallholders:
  * Extends a massive 40% direct capital financial subsidy capped at ₹20 Lakh to establish a village-level Custom Hiring Centre (CHC).
  * The subsidy funds a comprehensive heavy machinery bank containing high-powered tractors, combined harvesters, laser land levelers, and power tillers.
  * Ensures that small and marginal farmers can rent advanced mechanized equipment at fixed, low state hourly hiring rates.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered cooperative societies or farm collectives operating inside verified rural blocks with adequate space for machinery sheds.
* **How to Apply:** Handled digitally through the Matir Katha Portal during open scheduling windows, followed by physical inspection of the storage yard.
* **Documents Required:** Group Registration Certificate, PACS Resolution Book copy, land allocation / long-term lease papers for the storage yard, bank loan sanction letter, and proforma machinery quotes.

## Section 4: Post-Harvest Storage, Soil Care, Polyhouses & Coastal Fisheries

### [WB_SCHEME_008] Amar Fasal Amar Gola Scheme (On-Farm Storage Subvention)
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Asset Creation & Storage Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders looking to prevent immediate post-harvest distress sales and grain spoilage.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial support to construct scientific, climate-resilient storage units right at the farm gate:
  * Provides a tiered financial capital subsidy ranging from ₹5,000 up to ₹25,000 directly into the farmer's account.
  * Systematically earmarked to construct: Scientific Grain Silos / Golas (capacity up to 5 MT), low-cost ventilated Onion Storage structures, and high-altitude potato curing lofts.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Smallholders holding verified land titles with active crop-booking logs verified by local field personnel.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online via the state agricultural marketing desk or physically compiled during block-level *Duare Sarkar* outreach camps.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Land Record (RoR) extract copy, design sketch of the proposed storage unit, and bank passbook.

---

### [WB_SCHEME_009] Matir Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (State Soil Care & Green Manuring)
* **Category:** Sustainable Soil Health & Organic Inputs
* **Target Beneficiary:** Cultivators facing heavy soil salinity in coastal Sundarbans or declining organic carbon indices from intensive mono-cropping.
* **Benefit Details:** Biological soil reclamation and chemical reduction drive:
  * Fully state-funds block-level deployment of mobile soil-testing vans to issue customized digital Soil Health Cards.
  * Delivers a 75% state-funded input subsidy for sourcing certified *Dhaincha* (*Sesbania*) seeds and high-grade bio-fertilizer strains to run early-season green manuring and fix atmospheric nitrogen.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to all registered resident farmers in West Bengal who complete a seasonal crop booking registry.
* **How to Apply:** Distributed prior to the Kharif monsoon through local Agricultural Extension Centres (AECs) or Krishi Projukti Sahayak (KPS) networks.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Unique Farmer ID, and land possession tracking receipts.

---

### [WB_SCHEME_010] High-Tech Protected Horticulture and Floriculture Mission
* **Category:** High-Value Horticultural Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers, vegetable growers, and floriculturists across northern hill sectors (Darjeeling/Kalimpong) and peri-urban grids.
* **Benefit Details:** Climate protection shielding premium cash crops from heavy monsoon downpours and summer heatwaves:
  * 50% to 65% capital investment subsidy to build naturally ventilated polyhouses, shade-net structures, and automated misting systems.
  * Sourcing grants for tissue-cultured planting materials for off-season capsicums, cherry tomatoes, and exotic cut flowers (Orchids, Anthuriums, Gerberas).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators possessing clear land titles with an operational micro-drip irrigation system installed on the plot.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural blueprints and agronomic proposals directly to the District Horticulture Officer (DHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, certified land mutation copy, vendor structural design quotes, and soil-water chemical test profiles.

---

### [WB_SCHEME_011] Jalyatra Fishery Module (Inland & Saline Aquaculture Expansion)
* **Category:** Allied Sector Fisheries & Multi-Income Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional fishermen, rural youth collectives, and families in coastal Sundarbans or delta lowlands owning water-bodies.
* **Benefit Details:** Leverages West Bengal's immense fish consumption and export potential to create profitable alternative livelihoods:
  * 50% to 60% capital financial subsidy to construct new shrimp/fish ponds or re-excavate old, underutilized village tanks.
  * Supplies high-quality fish fingerlings (elite carp and air-breathing fish), automated aerator machinery setups, and subsidized high-protein fish feed kits.
  * Direct institutional linkage with the State Fisheries Development Corporation (SFDC) to ensure fair, middleman-free marketing.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of West Bengal aged 18 to 55 years holding absolute ownership or long-term lease over a minimum water expanse of 0.05 Hectares.
* **How to Apply:** File physical application profiles directly with the regional District Fisheries Officer (DFO) or during scheduled block outreach camps.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, land revenue sheets proving water-body layout permissions, and bank passbook details.
