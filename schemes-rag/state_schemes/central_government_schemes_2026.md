# Central Government Schemes Master Database (2026 Edition)

*Comprehensive validation against 2026 Union Budget parameters, NITI Aayog framework, and AgriStack integration rules.*

---

## SECTION 1: DIRECT INCOME SUPPORT & PENSION

### [SCHEME_001] PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
* **Category:** Agriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landholding farmer families (husband, wife, and minor children) who own cultivable land as per updated state land records.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct income support of ₹6,000 per financial year, kept unchanged by the Union Budget. Disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts in three equal installments of ₹2,000 across fixed 4-month cycles (April–July, August–November, and December–March).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Ownership of cultivable agricultural land registered in the state database. Institutional landholders, institutional professionals, high-income earners, government employees, and active income-tax payers are strictly excluded.
* **How to Apply:** Register via `pmkisan.gov.in` or at the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC). Prerequisites include completing e-KYC (OTP-based or Face Authentication/biometric). *Crucial Note:* Farmer ID registration is now mandatory for new applicants under the central AgriStack framework.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, proof of Aadhaar-seeded bank account linked to NPCI mapper, original land ownership records (Khata/Khasra), and unique Farmer ID.

---

### [SCHEME_002] PM-KMY (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana)
* **Category:** Financial Inclusion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal farmers owning up to 2 hectares of cultivable land.
* **Benefit Details:** A guaranteed minimum monthly pension of ₹3,000 after attaining 60 years of age. Operates on a 50:50 voluntary contributory model where the Central Government matches the farmer’s monthly premium (ranging from ₹55 to ₹200 based on entry age) rupee-for-rupee. Fund managed by LIC. If the subscriber passes away post-retirement, the spouse receives a 50% family pension (₹1,500/month).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small and marginal farmers aged between 18 and 40 years at entry. Applicants must not be enrolled in any other statutory social security schemes (e.g., NPS, EPFO, PM-SYM). Income-tax payers are excluded.
* **How to Apply:** Enrollment is completed through a CSC or self-registration on the `maandhan.in` portal. The initial premium is payable in cash, with subsequent iterations handled via auto-debit.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, savings bank account passbook/cancelled cheque, and an active mobile number.

## SECTION 2: CREDIT, INSURANCE & FINANCIAL INCLUSION

### [SCHEME_003] KCC (Kisan Credit Card)
* **Category:** Financial Inclusion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Owner cultivators, tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers, individual fishers, and animal husbandry farmers.
* **Benefit Details:** Access to revolving short-term institutional credit for crop cultivation and working capital needs of allied sectors. Base lending rate is 7% per annum. Timely repayment triggers a 3% Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) along with a 2% interest subvention, dropping the effective borrowing rate to 4% p.a. Collateral-free borrowing extends up to ₹2 lakh. Card structural validity spans 5 years.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active farmers with cultivable land holdings, or individuals legally engaged in dairy, poultry, piggery, or marine/inland fisheries. No minimum landholding barrier applies.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications route via the unified JanSamarth portal (`jansamarth.in`) or commercial banks. PM-KISAN beneficiaries can utilize an expedited, simplified one-page form.
* **Documents Required:** Completed application form, passport photos, identity proof (Aadhaar or Voter ID), address proof, land parcel data/cropping pattern records, and land validation documents.

---

### [SCHEME_004] PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)
* **Category:** Insurance
* **Target Beneficiary:** Owner farmers, tenant cultivators, and sharecroppers growing notified crops within state-notified geographical zones.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive yield insurance covering crop losses from localized natural calamities, post-harvest losses, pests, and epidemics. Premium responsibilities for farmers are strictly capped at 2% for Kharif crops, 1.5% for Rabi crops, and 5% for commercial/horticultural crops. Coverage extends to wild-animal attacks and paddy inundation.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers growing certified crops in notified regions. Valid for age brackets between 18 and 70 years with an active bank account linked to an Aadhaar card.
* **How to Apply:** Enrollment is finalized via the PMFBY portal (`pmfby.gov.in`), authorized banks, or CSC centers prior to seasonal cut-off dates. In the event of crop loss, formal claims must be registered within 72 hours of the damaging event.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, certified land records or a valid tenancy certificate, bank passbook, and a self-certified crop sowing declaration.

---

### [SCHEME_005] PMJDY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana)
* **Category:** Financial Inclusion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Any unbanked citizen aged 10 years and above. Acts as the base node for downstream DBT disbursements.
* **Benefit Details:** A zero-balance basic savings bank account with no account opening or maintenance fees. Includes a complimentary RuPay debit card packed with an integrated ₹2 lakh accidental death insurance cover. Accounts showing healthy transactions over a 6-month period become eligible for an overdraft facility up to ₹10,000 (restricted to one member per household, with preference for women).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Must be an Indian national without any pre-existing commercial bank account. The overdraft feature is limited to the working age group of 18 to 65 years.
* **How to Apply:** Accounts can be opened instantly via any commercial bank branch or local Bank Mitra (Business Correspondent) outlet utilizing Aadhaar-based e-KYC.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card serves as the primary document. If unavailable, alternative Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) like Voter ID, PAN card, or NREGA job card are accepted.

---

### [SCHEME_006] SVAMITVA
* **Category:** Financial Inclusion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Inhabitants of rural populated villages owning residential properties within the "Abadi" (inhabited) zones.
* **Benefit Details:** Free provision of a formal, legally validated Property Card (Record of Rights). Updates village land ownership data using high-resolution drone mapping and GIS survey technology. The generated cards enable property owners to treat rural residential assets as financial collateral for institutional loans and drastically mitigate local boundary disputes.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Residents holding residential structures within surveyed village Abadi limits, with proven possession established prior to September 25, 2018.
* **How to Apply:** No separate individual online application is required. Executed system-wide by the state revenue department. Residents must actively participate in marking boundaries during village "Chuna Markan" exercises and file adjustments during the public Gram Sabha review window. Final cards are downloadable via `svamitva.nic.in` or DigiLocker.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, existing traditional property proofs or tax receipts (optional but helpful for resolving local cross-claims).

## SECTION 3: MODERNIZATION & SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE

### [SCHEME_007] PM-KUSUM (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan)
* **Category:** Energy
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers, water user associations, and clusters looking to transition away from diesel/grid dependency.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital subsidy for setting up standalone solar photovoltaic water pumping systems (3 HP to 10 HP capacities under Component B) and solarizing existing grid-tied agricultural pumps. The Central Financial Assistance (CFA) provides a fixed 30% subsidy, while state governments offer matching top-ups, bringing the cumulative subsidy relief to between 60% and 90% depending on state-level allocations.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Ownership of cultivable land holding an identifiable, operational water source. For Component B, the field must not possess an active grid-connected electric pump connection.
* **How to Apply:** Applications must be filed directly through respective State Nodal Renewable Energy Agencies (e.g., MEDA, RRECL) or regional DISCOM portals. National entry links can be cross-checked at `pmkusum.mnre.gov.in`.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, current land ownership documents (Khasra/Khatauni), bank account details, and proof of a continuous water source.

---

### [SCHEME_008] PM-DDKY (PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana)
* **Category:** Agriculture / Modernization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and traditional farmers residing across 100 low-performing agricultural districts across India.
* **Benefit Details:** A macro-convergence scheme spanning 6 years (2025-2031) with a total outlay of ₹1.44 lakh crore (₹24,000 crore annually) to overhaul districts with low crop yields and below-average credit availability. It consolidates 36 agricultural and allied schemes across 11 ministries to enhance crop yields by 20-30%, expand solar irrigation, optimize credit distribution, and set up block-level post-harvest storage facilities.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers cultivating holdings (< 2 hectares) within the 100 targeted districts identified by NITI Aayog's monitoring dashboard.
* **How to Apply:** Operates under a saturation-based governance model. District-level development plans are drafted directly by District Collectors in coordination with regional Agricultural Universities. Farmers receive benefits directly at the block/panchayat level.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, land parcel records, unique Farmer ID, and banking data.

---

### [SCHEME_009] SMAM (Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization)
* **Category:** Machinery
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers and rural entrepreneurs, with enhanced subsidy distributions for women, SC/ST, and small/marginal categories.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital procurement subsidies for purchasing modern agricultural machinery (including tractors, power tillers, rotavators, laser land levelers, and drones). General category applicants receive a 40% to 50% subsidy. Small, marginal, SC, ST, and women farmers receive an elevated 50% to 80% subsidy. The program also funds the establishment of Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) to facilitate equipment rental for smallholder farmers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Any individual farmer holding verifiable titles. Specific states impose a restrictive timeline preventing applicants from claiming machinery subsidies if a similar purchase occurred within the last 7 years.
* **How to Apply:** Registration is executed through state-specific integrated DBT portals (e.g., MahaDBT in Maharashtra, Agripurv in UP). High-demand machine models like tractors are frequently allocated via computerized lottery draws.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, land title records, bank account validation, passport photo, and caste certificate (where applicable for higher subsidy bands).

---

### [SCHEME_010] PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop
* **Category:** Irrigation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers, farming cooperatives, and registered Water User Associations.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial subsidy focused on installing water-efficient micro-irrigation systems, specifically drip and sprinkler networks. Small and marginal farmers receive a 55% financial subsidy, whereas general category farmers receive a 45% subsidy. The structural funding arrangement follows a 60:40 split between Center and State resources, scaling to 90:10 for North Eastern and Himalayan states.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Any farmer possessing functional agricultural land suitable for micro-irrigation deployment.
* **How to Apply:** Digital entry via `pmksy.gov.in`, the designated state horticulture department portal, or offline processing by submitting forms to the local Block Agriculture Officer or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card (linked to an active mobile number), updated bank passbook, verified land records showing complete mutation titles, passport photo, and an SC/ST category certificate if claiming priority access.

---

### [SCHEME_011] AIF (Agriculture Infrastructure Fund)
* **Category:** Financial Inclusion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers, Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS), FPOs, Self Help Groups (SHGs), and agri-startups.
* **Benefit Details:** A specialized loan interest mitigation framework providing a 3% per annum interest subvention on commercial bank loans up to a maximum cap of ₹2 crore per project. The subvention holds a maximum operational tenure of 7 years. Projects valuing up to ₹2 crore receive full collateral exemption, with the government directly absorbing the CGTMSE credit guarantee fee. Eligible structures include post-harvest warehouses, cold chains, assaying labs, sorting/grading hubs, and primary processing units.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Any individual or institutional entity submitting a bankable, viable business plan for post-harvest infrastructure development. A single borrower can execute up to 25 distinct localized projects.
* **How to Apply:** Online registration and submission of a Detailed Project Report (DPR) through the designated portal at `agriinfra.dac.gov.in` using Aadhaar OTP verification.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, comprehensive Detailed Project Report (DPR), land clear titles/lease deeds for the project site, PAN card, and banking records.

---

### [SCHEME_012] e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)
* **Category:** Agriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and local traders.
* **Benefit Details:** A free digital agricultural trading network integrating over 1,300+ APMC mandis across India into a singular virtual marketplace. The system allows electronic bidding and open price discovery across state boundaries, removing dependencies on localized commission agents. Trade liquidations are settled within 1 business day directly into the farmer's bank account via integrated DBT.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers with verified landholdings operating within participating e-NAM states.
* **How to Apply:** Self-registration is available online at `enam.gov.in` or via the official e-NAM mobile application by registering as a "Farmer." Alternatively, farmers can register in-person at any integrated APMC mandi gate.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card or alternative photo ID, updated bank passbook or cancelled cheque leaf, land title records or Mandi registration slip, and mobile number.

---

### [SCHEME_013] SHC (Soil Health Card Scheme)
* **Category:** Agriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** All categories of landholding farmers (owner cultivators, tenants, and sharecroppers).
* **Benefit Details:** Free structural analysis of soil quality across 12 distinct macro, micro, and physical health parameters (including N, P, K, Sulphur, Zinc, Iron, pH, and Organic Carbon). The printed Soil Health Card is issued periodically every 2 to 3 years and includes crop-specific fertilizer and organic manure dosage recommendations to optimize crop yields and prevent chemical overuse.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to all Indian farmers cultivating agricultural plots across any State or Union Territory.
* **How to Apply:** Registration is accessible via `soilhealth.dac.gov.in`. Field officers or designated village technical assistants visit plots to collect soil samples, which are analyzed at regional laboratories. The finalized card is delivered through the local Panchayat within 30 to 45 days.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, basic land reference data (Khata/Khasra numbers), and PM-KISAN ID (optional).

---

### [SCHEME_014] PKVY (Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana)
* **Category:** Agriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers organized into clusters, prioritizing small, marginal, and traditional communities.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial support amounting to ₹31,500 per hectare over a continuous 3-year transition cycle to adopt certified organic farming practices. Of this total allocation, a component of ₹15,000 per hectare is routed via direct DBT to farmers specifically to procure organic inputs (including bio-fertilizers, vermicompost, and botanical extracts). Organic product certification is validated through the community-based Participatory Guarantee System (PGS-India).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers willing to form cohesive organic clusters spanning a minimum of 20 hectares. Individual landholder benefits are structurally capped at 2 hectares per applicant.
* **How to Apply:** Farmers must enroll collectively via regional clusters managed by District Agriculture Officers, or register individual details on the unified organic commerce site `jaivikkheti.in`.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, land ownership records, group cluster declaration form, and active bank account details.

## SECTION 4: EMPLOYMENT, ALLIED AGRI & FOOD PROCESSING

### [SCHEME_015] MGNREGA (Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act)
* **Category:** Employment
* **Target Beneficiary:** Adult members of rural households seeking livelihood security.
* **Benefit Details:** Statutory legal guarantee providing up to 100 days of unskilled manual wage employment per fiscal year per household. Wages are state-notified, updated periodically, and disbursed via DBT within 15 days of muster roll closure. If the administration fails to provide suitable work within 15 days of receiving a written demand, the applicant becomes entitled to a mandatory daily unemployment allowance. Equal wages are enforced for male and female workers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Adult Indian nationals residing permanently within the jurisdiction of the local Gram Panchayat. Both Above Poverty Line (APL) and Below Poverty Line (BPL) families are eligible.
* **How to Apply:** Registration for a permanent Job Card is processed through the local Gram Panchayat office or online via `nrega.nic.in`. Once the card is issued, a separate written demand for work must be submitted to the Panchayat Secretary.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, Ration card, passport size photograph, and an Aadhaar-linked savings bank passbook.

---

### [SCHEME_016] PMFME (Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises)
* **Category:** Agriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual micro-food processors, Self Help Groups (SHGs), Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and cooperatives.
* **Benefit Details:** Providing a credit-linked capital subsidy of 35% on eligible project costs for upgrading or setting up micro-food processing units, with a maximum cap of ₹10 lakh per individual enterprise. The subsidy is back-ended and credited to the lending bank account after loan sanction. The applicant must contribute a minimum of 10% of the project cost. Common infrastructure projects run by FPOs or SHGs can receive subsidy support capped at ₹3 crore.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** New or existing food processing units (including pickles, spice grinding, flour mills, fruit pulping, or bakery items) operating as micro-scale ventures.
* **How to Apply:** Digital project profiles and applications must be filed online through the PMFME portal. Following initial screening, applications are forwarded to commercial banks for loan appraisal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, PAN card, Udyam Registration certificate, passport photographs, and a detailed project report/business plan.

---

### [SCHEME_017] PMMSY (Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana)
* **Category:** Agriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Fishers, fish farmers, fish workers, coastal communities, SHGs, and specialized Fisheries Cooperatives.
* **Benefit Details:** Credit-linked subsidies to build fisheries infrastructure, covering items like pond construction, hatcheries, deep-sea fishing vessels, bio-floc systems, cold storage, and refrigerated transport vehicles. General category beneficiaries receive a 40% financial subsidy, while women and SC/ST candidates receive an elevated 60% subsidy.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individuals or groups actively engaged in aquaculture, marine capture, or fish retail operations. Individual fishers must possess a valid regional fishing license or regulatory certificate.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted online via the National Fisheries Digital Platform (NFDP) portal, supported by submitting a physical Detailed Project Report (DPR) to the local District Fisheries Officer.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, Detailed Project Report (DPR), community caste certificate (if applying for the 60% subsidy band), and active fishing license/boat registration documents.

---

### [SCHEME_018] NLM-EDP (National Livestock Mission - Entrepreneurship Development)
* **Category:** Agriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual entrepreneurs, FPOs, SHGs, JLGs, and Section 8 companies.
* **Benefit Details:** A one-time back-ended capital subsidy providing 50% of the total project cost for establishing specialized livestock farming and breeding units. Maximum subsidy caps are structured as follows: up to ₹25 lakh for rural poultry parent farms, up to ₹50 lakh for sheep and goat breeding farms (minimum scale of 500 ewes/does plus 25 rams/bucks), and up to ₹30 lakh for modern piggery breeding ventures.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individuals or legal groups possessing adequate land holdings or structural sheds to construct livestock units, along with training credentials in animal husbandry.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications must be submitted directly through the unified NLM portal at `nlm.udyamimitra.in` for scrutiny and technical evaluation.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, comprehensive project proposal, land title ownership or long-term lease deeds for the project site, bank statements, and training certificates.

---

### [SCHEME_019] RGM (Rashtriya Gokul Mission)
* **Category:** Agriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual dairy farmers, cattle breeders, and rural entrepreneurs focused on indigenous cattle development.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial support focused on conserving and genetically enhancing indigenous bovine breeds (such as Gir, Sahiwal, and Tharparkar). Key benefits include a direct subsidy of ₹5,000 per successful IVF pregnancy achieved in cattle. It also offers a 50% capital subsidy (capped at ₹2 crore) to establish automated Breed Multiplication Farms that supply high-genetic-merit heifers to local farmers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active dairy farmers or entrepreneurs with sufficient land resources to support large cattle herds. General access to sex-sorted semen or Artificial Insemination (AI) services is open to all dairy cattle holders.
* **How to Apply:** Applications for large infrastructure components (like Breed Multiplication Farms) are processed through the District Animal Husbandry Department. Local breeding benefits can be accessed through village Artificial Insemination technicians.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, land ownership details, dairy registration data, and standard application forms.

---

### [SCHEME_020] ACABC (Agri-Clinics and Agri-Business Centres Scheme)
* **Category:** Agriculture / Livelihood Entrepreneurship
* **Target Beneficiary:** Unemployed agricultural graduates, post-graduates, and diploma holders in agriculture or allied subjects (veterinary, dairy, fisheries).
* **Benefit Details:** A system that funds the creation of decentralized agricultural service centers to support farmers. It provides a 45-day residential entrepreneurship training course. Post-training, it offers a credit-linked back-end subsidy on institutional loans up to ₹20 lakh for individual projects and up to ₹100 lakh for group ventures. The baseline subsidy rate is 36% for general category candidates and up to 44% for women, SC/ST, and hill-state candidates.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Applicants aged between 18 and 60 years holding recognized qualifications in agriculture or allied domains. *Note:* Retired government employees or active farmers without technical agricultural degrees are strictly excluded from the subsidy infrastructure.
* **How to Apply:** Digital registration is processed via `acabc.gov.in` (or `acabcmis.gov.in`). Candidates must pass a screening test, complete mandatory training at an assigned nodal institute, and submit their business venture profile to NABARD for subsidy processing.
* **Documents Required:** Degree/Diploma certificate in agriculture or allied sciences, Aadhaar card, detailed Bankable Project Report (DPR), and training completion certificate.

## SECTION 5: HEALTHCARE, HOUSING & RURAL UTILITIES

### [SCHEME_021] PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat)
* **Category:** Healthcare
* **Target Beneficiary:** Economically vulnerable rural families identified via SECC-2011 parameters, along with a universal inclusion for all senior citizens aged 70 years and above.
* **Benefit Details:** Free health insurance cover providing up to ₹5 lakh per family per financial year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization. The scheme operates on a cashless and paperless delivery model across all empanelled public and private hospitals, covering over 1,949 distinct medical, surgical, and post-hospitalization procedures. Senior citizens aged 70+ receive an independent, dedicated card with a distinct ₹5 lakh top-up cover.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Households listed in the SECC-2011/Awaas+ deprivation data, or any individual citizen who has reached 70 years of age, regardless of socio-economic status.
* **How to Apply:** Eligibility can be checked via `mera.pmjay.gov.in` or the Ayushman mobile app. To generate the Ayushman Card, beneficiaries must complete e-KYC validation at a local CSC or any empanelled hospital helpdesk.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card, active Ration Card or state-validated family identification document, and a verified mobile number.

---

### [SCHEME_022] JJM (Jal Jeevan Mission / Har Ghar Jal)
* **Category:** Utilities
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural households lacking reliable, clean drinking water access.
* **Benefit Details:** Free provision and structural installation of a Functional Household Tap Connection (FHTC) delivering a minimum supply of 55 litres of potable water per person per day directly inside the domestic premises. The water must meet strict BIS 10500 quality standards. The initial infrastructure installation is completely free, though the local Village Water and Sanitation Committee (VWSC) may charge a nominal community maintenance fee (typically ₹30 to ₹100 per month) post-connection.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Any rural household located in a village where water supply pipeline infrastructure is active or under deployment.
* **How to Apply:** No individual application needs to be filed on an online portal. Residents should contact their local Gram Panchayat or Village Water and Sanitation Committee (VWSC) to include their home in the pipeline layout. Connection status maps can be cross-verified at `ejalshakti.gov.in/jjm`.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card of the head of the household for internal system entry.

---

### [SCHEME_023] PMUY (Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana)
* **Category:** Utilities
* **Target Beneficiary:** Adult women belonging to low-income households.
* **Benefit Details:** Provides a clean cooking energy transition by funding a free initial LPG connection, a cash assistance component of ₹1,600, a complimentary first gas cylinder refill, and a free hotplate/stove. Beneficiaries also receive a dedicated targeted subsidy of ₹300 per cylinder refill for up to 12 refills per fiscal year.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** The applicant must be a woman aged 18 years or older, with no other active domestic LPG connection in her household. She must belong to a vulnerable socio-economic category (such as SC/ST, Antyodaya Anna Yojana, PMAY-G list, or identified Most Backward Classes).
* **How to Apply:** Online applications can be submitted through `pmuy.gov.in`, or offline by submitting forms directly to the nearest LPG distributor (Indane, HP, or Bharat Gas).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar card of the applicant, Aadhaar of all adult family members, active Ration Card or regional family composition certificate, bank account details, and an address proof.

---

### [SCHEME_024] PMAY-G (Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin)
* **Category:** Housing
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural households living in dilapidated structures, kutcha homes, or without permanent shelter.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct financial assistance for constructing a permanent pucca house with a minimum area of 25 square meters (including dedicated cooking space). Beneficiaries receive a financial grant of ₹1,20,000 in plain geographic areas and ₹1,30,000 in hilly, difficult terrain, or Integrated Action Plan (IAP) districts. The grant is disbursed via DBT in installments linked to physical construction progress, verified with geo-tagged photographs. This scheme converges with MGNREGA to provide around 90–95 days of construction labor wages, along with a ₹12,000 grant for toilet construction under the Swachh Bharat Mission.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Families listed on the SECC-2011 or Awaas+ survey databases, verified by the local Gram Sabha. Automatic exclusion applies if any family member owns a motorized two-wheeler, three-wheeler, four-wheeler, or agricultural tractor, regardless of income.
* **How to Apply:** Individuals cannot apply directly on an open portal. Beneficiary selections are drawn from verified Awaas+ tracking lists. Local block development officials upload data through the dedicated AwaasSoft data entry portal following Gram Sabha approvals.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar cards of family members, active bank account details, MGNREGA Job Card number, and official survey verification receipt.
