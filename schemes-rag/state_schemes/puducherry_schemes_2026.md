## Executive Summary & Territory Operational Context

The Union Territory of Puducherry operates across a unique quad-enclave coastal geography spanning three different Indian states: the Coromandel coast enclaves of Puducherry and Karaikal (encircled by Tamil Nadu), the Malabar coast enclave of Mahé (along the Arabian Sea in Kerala), and the deltaic enclave of Yanam (along the Godavari River in Andhra Pradesh):
1. **Enclave-Adapted Input Cushioning & Social Security:** Offsetting high urban-industrial land and input costs through 50% to 75% direct subsidies on certified seeds (Paddy, Pulses, Oilseeds) and *Dhaincha* green manure disbursed via DBT through localized *Uzhavar Udhaviyagams* (Farmers' Help Centres), backed by a ₹5 Lakh ex-gratia accidental death social security cushion.
2. **Coastal Aquifer Protection & Daytime Solar Pumping:** Mitigating heavy groundwater extraction and stopping seawater intrusion into coastal freshwater aquifers through 90% capital subsidies for precision drip/sprinkler grids, paired with 60% to 80% subsidies for 2 HP to 7.5 HP standalone solar water pumps managed by the Renewable Energy Agency Puducherry (REAP).
3. **Marine Fishery Mechanization & Allied Diversification:** Capitalizing on the extensive coastline of all four regions by providing 40% to 60% subsidies for boat motorization, insulated transport vehicles, fish-landing ice plants, open-sea cage culture, and 75% subsidies for climate-resilient backyard poultry and goat breeding units.

## Section 1: Flagship Direct Support, Sowing Subsidies & Social Security

### [PY_SCHEME_001] Scheme for Development of Agriculture through Input Subsidies
* **Category:** Essential Sowing Input Support & Sustainable Cultivation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and tenant cultivators operating across Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahé, and Yanam.
* **Benefit Details:** Prioritized cost-reduction framework cushioning early seasonal sowing expenses:
  * Delivers a direct 50% to 75% financial subsidy on the procurement of certified Paddy/Rice seeds, pulses, oilseeds, and regional vegetable varieties.
  * 50% concession on specialized bio-inputs, organic compost blocks, *Dhaincha* green manure seeds, and biological pest controls to restore soil biology.
  * Disbursed dynamically via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into the farmer's validated bank account.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active resident cultivators registered under the verified land records database of the UT Administration.
* **How to Apply:** Handled physically through regional Agriculture Extension Centres (AECs) or block-level *Uzhavar Udhaviyagams* (Farmers' Help Centres).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Patta extract papers / Lease Deed (for tenant farmers), active crop-booking verification logs, and bank passbook.

---

### [PY_SCHEME_002] PM-KISAN (Puducherry Implementation Node)
* **Category:** Direct Financial Income Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landowning smallholder families across all four distinct enclaves.
* **Benefit Details:** Tightly monitored cash-transfer safety net providing baseline liquidity:
  * Delivers a guaranteed income support of ₹6,000 per year, disbursed in three equal seasonal installments of ₹2,000 each.
  * Synchronized with central databases via biometric Aadhaar validation to clear fast, middleman-free DBT credits.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small and marginal landholders with operational land records registered under active verified status on the UT land revenue index.
* **How to Apply:** Digital registration online via the central PM-KISAN portal or through local Common Service Centres (CSCs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, digitized Land Records (Patta), active Aadhaar-seeded mobile number, and bank passbook.

---

### [PY_SCHEME_010] Farmers Welfare Social Security & Accidental Insurance Scheme
* **Category:** Farmer Social Security & Risk Insulation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Land tilling farmers, recorded tenants, and agricultural laborers aged 18 to 70 years.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial cushion protecting farming families from occupational hazards:
  * Direct financial ex-gratia compensation of ₹5 Lakh to the nominee in the event of the farmer’s accidental death while performing farm operations (tractor rollovers, lightning strikes, machinery injuries, or well drowning).
  * Tiered financial compensation of up to ₹2 Lakh for permanent disability or loss of limbs.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident farmers or landless laborers actively working on agricultural fields in the UT. Claims must be submitted within 45 days.
* **How to Apply:** Verified by the local Revenue Officer and routed to the District Magistrate (DM) for direct DBT release.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card of victim and nominee, Post-Mortem Report / Disability Certificate, Police FIR copy, and Land Patta details (if landowning).

## Section 2: Aquifer Security & Precision Daytime Solar Irrigation

### [PY_SCHEME_003] Micro-Irrigation Scheme (Per Drop More Crop Module)
* **Category:** Precision Water Engineering & Seawater Intrusion Control
* **Target Beneficiary:** Vegetable growers, fruit orchardists, and sugarcane planters facing groundwater salinity and seasonal dry spells.
* **Benefit Details:** Crucial water management intervention stopping seawater from seeping into vulnerable freshwater aquifers:
  * Direct state-sponsored 90% capital financial subsidy for smallholders to install complete drip and sprinkler irrigation networks.
  * Minimizes evaporation losses, prevents coastal soil salinity buildup, and ensures uniform root-zone moisture delivery.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators possessing an active groundwater lift source (open well or borewell) with absolute land title passbooks.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically at your regional District Agriculture Office (DAO) or submit verification profiles through field extension agents.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, recent land Patta records, pump electricity status certificate, and empanelled vendor design maps.

---

### [PY_SCHEME_004] PM-KUSUM Component-B (Solar Water Pump Yojana)
* **Category:** Clean Energy Daytime Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Off-grid smallholders, vegetable clusters, and coastal farming blocks breaking fuel dependency.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital cost reduction for standalone solar water pumping installations:
  * 60% to 80% cumulative capital investment subsidy (leveraging UT-specific subvention rules) on standalone solar water pumps.
  * Supports surface and submersible configurations ranging from 2 HP to 7.5 HP, ensuring stable daytime irrigation power without erratic electrical grid lines.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must have a verified underground well or borewell connection without an active grid-connected electric pump.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications open during announced booking windows on the official UT administration tracking portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, land ownership records, water source technician yield certificate, and a zero-grid affidavit.

## Section 3: Farm Mechanization & Marine Fisheries Modernization

### [PY_SCHEME_005] Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM Puducherry)
* **Category:** Precision Farm Mechanization Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder farming groups, young agri-entrepreneurs, and registered agricultural cooperative societies.
* **Benefit Details:** Reduces heavy manual labor costs and accelerates cropping timelines:
  * 40% to 50% capital subsidies for individual general category farmers.
  * Up to 80% higher subsidy rates for Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) and Farm Machinery Banks established by cooperatives.
  * Equipment supported: specialized rice transplanters, high-powered tillers, rotavators, and mechanized harvesters adapted for paddy and narrow coastal fields.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual farmers or village collectives owning registered land titles or recognized community lease plots.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online via the national [AgriMachinery Portal](https://agrimachinery.nic.in/) selecting the Puducherry state node.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer Registration Code, dealer proforma price quote, and block verification log.

---

### [PY_SCHEME_006] Marine Fisheries Modernization and Fishermen Welfare Scheme
* **Category:** Allied Sector Fisheries & Coastal Livelihood Expansion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional coastal fishermen, boat owners, and marine-allied cooperative societies across all four enclaves.
* **Benefit Details:** Multi-sector marine aquaculture diversification:
  * 40% to 60% capital financial subsidy to buy modernized marine safety equipment, GPS tracking tools, and insulated fish-transport vehicles.
  * Extends heavy subventions to convert traditional fishing boats into motorized units, alongside financing modern ice plant installations near fish landing centers.
  * Fully funds the setup of open-sea Cage Culture systems and brackish-water mud-crab fattening loops along calm coastal bays.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent resident fishermen holding a valid Biometric Fisher ID Card issued by the Department of Fisheries and Fishermen Welfare.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural site or boat modification proposals directly to the regional District Fisheries Officer (DFO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Fisher Identity Card, boat registration certificates (if applicable), and bank passbook details.

## Section 4: Protected Horticulture, Coconut Rejuvenation & Allied Livestock

### [PY_SCHEME_007] Integrated Horticulture Development and Protected Cultivation Mission
* **Category:** Commercial Horticulture Acceleration & Multi-Tier Inputs
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers, vegetable growers, and orchardists across all four districts.
* **Benefit Details:** High-impact asset creation maximizing per-acre income:
  * 50% to 75% capital financial subsidy to build naturally ventilated polyhouses, shade-net structures, and automated misting systems.
  * Distributes certified fruit saplings including Mango, Sapota, Guava, and Banana clones at highly subsidized rates.
  * Fully funds on-farm concrete vermicompost units (up to ₹50,000 grant per family) to scale up organic inputs.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators possessing clear land titles with an operational micro-irrigation layout.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural design and plantation proposals directly to the District Horticulture Officer (DHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, certified land mutation copy, vendor structural design quotes, and soil-water suitability profiles.

---

### [PY_SCHEME_008] Coconut Rejuvenation and Solar Copra Dryer Initiative
* **Category:** High-Value Cash Crop Commercialization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional plantation owners, self-help groups (SHGs), and smallholders maintaining active coconut groves.
* **Benefit Details:** Protects and modernizes the territory's primary horticultural economic base:
  * Distributes high-yielding, dwarf, disease-free Coconut saplings at a 75% state-funded financial subsidy.
  * Grants direct capital subventions of up to ₹25,000 per unit to install Modern Solar Copra Dryers to replace unhygienic wood-fired smoky pits, boosting market valuation for coconut oil.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators owning or holding legally recorded land deeds or village-approved long-term cultivation leases.
* **How to Apply:** Route applications through block help centers (*Uzhavar Udhaviyagams*) or directly to the Department of Agriculture.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Patta extract papers, and dealer nursery bills.

---

### [PY_SCHEME_009] Integrated Backyard Poultry & Goat Breeding Initiative
* **Category:** Allied Sector Livestock & Alternative Income Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landless rural laborers, marginalized women-led self-help groups (SHGs), and lower-income families.
* **Benefit Details:** Shields vulnerable families from nutritional insecurity and single-crop failures:
  * 75% direct capital investment subsidy to build biosecure, climate-resilient animal pens and poultry coops.
  * Supplies elite dual-purpose backyard poultry breeds (*Vanaraja* chicks) along with high-yielding goat breeding pairs.
  * Fully covers first-year veterinary feed kits, deworming tracking cards, and electronic animal tagging.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident livestock owners holding valid Aadhaar-linked accounts who pass an initial animal husbandry training assessment.
* **How to Apply:** Apply directly to the regional Chief Veterinary Officer (CVO) or during scheduled block outreach camps.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, EWS entitlement card (if applicable), space availability certificate, and bank passbook details.
