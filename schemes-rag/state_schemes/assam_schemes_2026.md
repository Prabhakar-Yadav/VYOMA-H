## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Assam's agricultural transformation focuses on the Brahmaputra and Barak river valleys, addressing recurrent monsoon flooding, tea plantation economics, and heavy mechanization deficits:
1. **CMSGUY Revenue Village Saturation Model:** Flagship mega-mission allocating one shared heavy tractor asset unit (70% state subsidy + 20% bank loan + 10% group equity) to a registered group of 8–10 farmers in every single revenue village across Assam.
2. **Underground Pressurized Piped Irrigation (₹4,000 Crore):** Complete shift away from open-channel canals to underground river-fed pressurized pipe networks across major river basins (Barak, Subansiri, Manas), eliminating water conveyance losses and avoiding private land acquisition.
3. **Small Tea Grower (STG) Relief & Youth Agri-Excellence:** Expansion of the agricultural income tax exemption threshold to ₹10 Lakh for over 1.5 lakh small tea families, paired with ₹1 Lakh *Krishi Utsav* cash grants and ₹2 Crore AIF credit lines for educated young farmers.

## Section 1: Flagship Farm Mechanization & Village Saturation

### [ASSAM_SCHEME_002] Chief Minister’s Samagra Gramya Unnayan Yojana (CMSGUY Tractor Unit)
* **Category:** Mega Farm Mechanisation Subvention & Saturation Model
* **Target Beneficiary:** Registered groups of 8 to 10 actual adult farmers residing within the exact same revenue village. Individual applications are blocked.
* **Benefit Details:** Flagship program designed to place a complete tractor unit in every revenue village across Assam:
  * **70% Government Subsidy:** State covers 70% of the total cost of a tractor unit and matching accessories (cultivators, rotavators, hydraulic trailers), capped at ₹5.5 Lakh.
  * **20% Institutional Bank Loan:** Financed through commercial bank linkages.
  * **10% Group Margin Share:** Upfront capital contribution shared among group members.
  * **Shared Rental Mandate:** The beneficiary group is legally bound to provide the tractor and accessories to other small, non-member farmers in the village on affordable state-capped rental terms for at least 5 to 6 years.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Group of 8–10 farmers from the same revenue village. Strictly one member per family inside a single group to prevent nepotism. Must hold clear land records in the village.
* **How to Apply:** Digital proposals are submitted via `mmscmsguy.assam.gov.in` or `diragri.assam.gov.in`. Scrutinized by the District Level Committee (DLC) headed by the Deputy Commissioner (DC).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Cards of all 8–10 members, Land Patta / Dag numbers, Group Bank Account Passbook, Group Resolution deed, and dealer proforma invoice.

## Section 2: Climate-Resilient Underground Piped Irrigation

### [ASSAM_SCHEME_006] Chief Minister’s Pressurized Piped Irrigation Network Project
* **Category:** Climate-Resilient Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual fields and small farming clusters dependent on erratic rainfed irrigation across the Brahmaputra and Barak command areas.
* **Benefit Details:** Brand new ₹4,000 Crore state mega-project spanning 2026–2031:
  * Completely replaces obsolete open-channel earth canals with underground pressurized piped distribution networks fed by major perennial rivers (Barak, Subansiri, Manas, Brahmaputra).
  * Delivers automated, high-efficiency lift irrigation directly to farm boundaries, preventing evaporation/seepage losses and eliminating private land acquisition issues.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivable farmland mapped under the state's command area irrigation survey, prioritizing double-cropping paddy and rabi oilseed (mustard) belts.
* **How to Apply:** Coordinated through local Water User Associations (WUAs) and the State Irrigation Department. Farmers register plot coordinates at the block development desk.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Patta / Dag number, and registration certificate with the local village Water User Association (WUA).

## Section 3: Small Tea Grower Tax Relief & Youth Agri-Excellence

### [ASSAM_SCHEME_007] Small Tea Growers (STG) Agricultural Income Tax Exemption
* **Category:** Financial Relief & Cash Crop Protection
* **Target Beneficiary:** Independent, small-scale tea plantation owners (*Small Tea Growers / STG*).
* **Benefit Details:** Historic state tax shield enacted in the 2026 State Budget:
  * Quadrupled the agricultural income tax exemption threshold, raising it from ₹2.5 Lakh up to ₹10 Lakh of annual agricultural income.
  * Provides complete tax immunity to over 1.5 Lakh small tea farming families, preserving critical working capital for buying bio-fertilizers, organic pesticides, and paying leaf-plucking wages.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Must be registered as a "Small Tea Grower" with the Tea Board of India, with individual tea plantation acreage capped at 10.12 Hectares (approx. 25 Acres). Corporate estates are excluded.
* **How to Apply:** Claimed during annual agricultural income tax filings on the state revenue department portal by providing the STG Smart Card number.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Tea Board of India STG Smart Card, Land Revenue Patta documents, and annual agricultural income self-declaration ledger.

---

### [ASSAM_SCHEME_008] Chief Minister’s Krishi Utsav / Agriculture Excellence Award
* **Category:** Agri-Entrepreneurship Livelihood Support & Incubation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Educated, unemployed rural youth and women implementing modern, self-reliant farming practices.
* **Benefit Details:** Annual state honors for 1,000 progressive educated young farmers:
  * Direct cash recognition grant of ₹1 Lakh each.
  * Fast-tracked institutional linkage to automated cold storage and post-harvest processing loans of up to ₹2 Crore (with a 3% interest subvention) under the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Rural residents aged 18 to 40 years holding a minimum educational qualification of Class 10/Matriculation, with verified field innovations (organic clusters, polyhouses, high-tech sorting/grading, drone spraying).
* **How to Apply:** Apply online via the state agriculture excellence portal prior to the annual *Krishi Utsav* convention.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Educational Marksheet/Certificate, Unique Farmer ID, and video/photographic documentation of the innovative farm enterprise.
