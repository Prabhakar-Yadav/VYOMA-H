## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Uttar Pradesh's agricultural policy governs India's largest agrarian economy, spanning the hyper-fertile Gangetic plains, the flood-prone Purvanchal basin, and the water-scarce rocky plateau of Bundelkhand:
1. **Perimeter Defense & Universal Social Security:** Protects standing crops from stray cattle and Nilgai through *Mukhyamantri Khet Suraksha* (60% subsidy up to ₹1,43,000/ha for 12-volt pulsed solar fencing) and provides ₹5 Lakh direct ex-gratia death compensation (*Krishak Durghatna Kalyan*) covering title-holding farmers, sharecroppers (*Batai-dars*), and landless agricultural laborers.
2. **Daytime Solarization & Arid-Zone Water Harvesting:** Rapid deployment of 2 HP to 10 HP standalone solar water pumps under *PM-KUSUM Component-B* (60% cumulative subsidy via digital token bookings on `upagriculture.com`) paired with ₹1,05,000 grants for plastic-lined *Khet Talab* rainwater ponds across Bundelkhand and Purvanchal.
3. **Decentralized Knowledge, Zero-Interest Credit & Processing:** Free smart-class agricultural training across all 75 districts under the *Million Farmers School (Kisan Pathshala)*, 0% effective interest short-term crop loans via PACS, and 50% capital grants for agro-processing units under the *Nivesh Mitra* single window for export-grade GI crops like *Kalanamak Rice* and *Malihabadi Mangoes*.

## Section 1: Flagship Crop Protection, Social Security & Zero-Interest Credit

### [UP_SCHEME_001] Mukhyamantri Khet Suraksha Yojana (Solar Fencing Scheme)
* **Category:** Perimeter Security & Crop Yield Protection
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders losing standing crops to stray cattle, wild boars, and Nilgai (blue bulls).
* **Benefit Details:** Non-lethal physical barrier and predator deterrent system:
  * Extends a 60% state financial subsidy capped at ₹1,43,000 per Hectare to install low-intensity solar-powered electric fencing along farm perimeters.
  * Delivers a mild, non-lethal 12-volt pulsating electric current that shocks and turns back intruding animals without causing permanent injury, sounding an automatic siren alarm upon contact.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Smallholders recorded on the state land revenue database (Khatauni). Adjacent farmers can pool boundaries to claim higher community fencing allocations.
* **How to Apply:** Digital application via the unified [UP Agriculture Department Portal / Krishi Kosh](http://upagriculture.com/) followed by physical inspection by localized Agriculture Supervisors.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Revenue Khatauni extracts, Khasra field maps issued by the Lekhpal/Patwari, and vendor proforma quotation.

---

### [UP_SCHEME_002] Mukhyamantri Krishak Durghatna Kalyan Yojana
* **Category:** Farmer Social Security & Risk Insulation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landowning farmers, sharecroppers (*Batai-dars*), and landless agricultural laborers aged 18 to 70 years.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive financial cushion protecting farming households from fatal occupational hazards:
  * Delivers a direct financial ex-gratia compensation of ₹5 Lakh to the designated nominee in the event of accidental death occurring during agricultural operations (tractor rollovers, snake bites, lightning strikes, drowning in irrigation wells, or thresher machine injuries).
  * Tiered compensation of up to ₹2 Lakh for permanent disability, loss of vision, or loss of limbs.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident cultivators and farm laborers actively working in Uttar Pradesh. Incident claim dossiers must be submitted within 45 days.
* **How to Apply:** Application packets are verified by the local Tehsildar and forwarded to the District Magistrate (DM) for direct DBT release from the State Treasury.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card of victim and nominee, Post-Mortem Report / Disability Certificate, Police FIR / Panchnama copy, and Land Khatauni details (if landowning).

---

### [UP_SCHEME_008] UP Short-Term Interest Subvention Crop Loan Scheme
* **Category:** Institutional Micro-Finance Credit Subvention
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landowning small and marginal cultivators holding active Kisan Credit Cards (KCC).
* **Benefit Details:** Institutional crop credit subvention through cooperative banking channels:
  * Deploys state funds to top up central prompt-repayment interest relief frameworks.
  * **0% Effective Interest Rate:** The State Government bears the remaining interest burden, ensuring that farmers pay zero net interest if the short-term crop loan principal is repaid promptly within the 12-month seasonal deadline.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident farmers holding clear land titles registered under regional Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) or District Cooperative Banks (DCBs).
* **How to Apply:** Processed online during standard seasonal renewal cycles directly at local PACS bank counters with biometric Aadhaar validation.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, active KCC Passbook, and recent Land Khatauni revenue copy.

## Section 2: Clean Energy Daytime Irrigation & Drought Mitigation

### [UP_SCHEME_003] PM-KUSUM Component-B (Solar Pump Yojana - UP Node)
* **Category:** Clean Energy Daytime Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and general category farmers breaking dependency on costly diesel generators or erratic grid power.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital cost reduction for standalone solar water pumping installations:
  * 60% cumulative financial subsidy (30% Central allocation + 30% State top-up) on standalone solar pumps.
  * Farmer contributes a token 40% margin share.
  * Supports 2 HP, 3 HP, 5 HP, 7.5 HP, and 10 HP surface and submersible solar pump configurations.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must have a verified underground borewell or open well. The applicant must not possess an active grid-connected electric pump on the same plot.
* **How to Apply:** Digital registrations open dynamically during announced booking windows on `upagriculture.com`. Farmers book online tokens backed by a standard online security deposit.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, land Khatauni records, tube-well water yield feasibility certificate, and a zero-grid electricity meter affidavit.

---

### [UP_SCHEME_004] Bundelkhand & Purvanchal Khet Talab Yojana (Farm Pond Scheme)
* **Category:** Rainwater Harvesting & Drought Mitigation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers in water-stressed blocks, particularly across the 7 districts of Bundelkhand and drought-prone pockets of Purvanchal.
* **Benefit Details:** On-farm water harvesting to capture monsoon runoff:
  * Direct state-sponsored capital financial subsidy of 50% capped at ₹1,05,000 for constructing a standard 22m x 20m x 3m farm pond (*Khet Talab*).
  * Fully covers the integration of plastic geomembrane lining sheets to stop water loss via seepage in porous and rocky terrains.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators possessing minimum contiguous agricultural land deeds of 0.5 Hectares.
* **How to Apply:** Online registration via the UP Agriculture portal (`upagriculture.com`) prior to monsoon onset.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, recent Land Khatauni documents, sketch design layout of the pond, and bank passbook.

## Section 3: Farm Mechanization, Sowing Inputs & Capacity Building

### [UP_SCHEME_005] Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM UP Node)
* **Category:** Farm Machinery & Custom Hiring Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual smallholders, rural youth entrepreneurs, and registered agricultural cooperative societies.
* **Benefit Details:** Automates farming operations to save time and manual labor:
  * 40% to 50% capital investment subsidy on individual equipment: laser land levelers, rotavators, power tillers, happy seeders, and multi-crop threshers.
  * **Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs):** Grants 40% to 80% capital subsidies (capped at ₹4 Lakh to ₹12 Lakh) to establish Village Machinery Banks, enabling marginal farmers to rent heavy machinery at state-capped hourly rates.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers or registered youth collectives with active operational field layouts.
* **How to Apply:** Processed entirely online through the national [AgriMachinery Portal](https://agrimachinery.nic.in/) selecting the Uttar Pradesh nodal desk.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID code, dealer proforma invoice quotes, and land verification records.

---

### [UP_SCHEME_006] Certified Seed Distribution & Crop Diversification Scheme
* **Category:** Core Sowing Input Provision & Crop Top-ups
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders setting up seasonal Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid sowing operations.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidizes foundational sowing inputs:
  * Delivers high-yielding varieties (HYV) and certified quality seeds for major staples (Paddy, Wheat, Pulses, Mustard/Oilseeds) at a direct 50% state-funded financial subsidy.
  * Disbursed directly using the DBT framework post-purchase from state-authorized seed stores (*Beaj Godam*).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active landholders with clear crop booking entries verified by localized agricultural extension officers.
* **How to Apply:** Distributed directly before the sowing season via block-level Agriculture Extension Centres (AECs) and regional seed stores.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Khatauni Passbook, and active seasonal verification log receipt.

---

### [UP_SCHEME_007] Million Farmers School (Kisan Pathshala Framework)
* **Category:** Technological Agricultural Literacy & Decentralized Training
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholders, women farmers, and rural multi-crop collectives across all 75 districts.
* **Benefit Details:** High-impact rural capacity-building and technology-transfer mission:
  * Conducts free seasonal smart-class training modules organized at the Gram Panchayat level (hosted at primary schools or Panchayat Bhavans).
  * Audio-visual training on precision drone spraying, zero-budget natural farming, balanced fertilizer application (NPK + micro-nutrients), and climate-resilient crop rotation.
  * Free distribution of baseline bio-fertilizer trial kits, certified seed minikits, and comprehensive farming literature handbook.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Universal access open to all resident farmers registered on the central state database.
* **How to Apply:** Walk-in enrollment managed seasonally by block Agriculture Extension Officers (AEOs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card and unique Farmer ID.

## Section 4: Agro-Processing, Protected Polyhouses & Indigenous Dairy

### [UP_SCHEME_009] UP Agri-Business, Agro-Processing, and Agri-Marketing Promotion Policy
* **Category:** Supply Chain Logistical Security & Post-Harvest Value Addition
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), registered agricultural cooperatives, and young rural agro-entrepreneurs.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive post-harvest industrialization policy:
  * Up to a 50% capital financial grant to construct primary sorting/grading pack-houses, fruit ripening chambers, solar crop dryers, and multi-commodity cold storage units.
  * 5% interest subvention for 5 years on commercial bank loans availed for processing plant machinery.
  * **Export Transport Subsidy:** 25% road and air freight subsidy for exporting specialty GI crops (such as GI *Kalanamak Rice* from Siddharthnagar and GI *Malihabadi Dussehri Mangoes* from Lucknow).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered FPOs, cooperatives, or individual agro-entrepreneurs holding valid industrial or converted agricultural land titles.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online through the unified [Nivesh Mitra Single Window Portal](https://niveshmitra.up.nic.in/).
* **Documents Required:** FPO Registration Certificate, Land Title deeds / long-term registered lease, bank loan sanction letter, and detailed engineering DPR.

---

### [UP_SCHEME_010] Advanced Protected Cultivation Greenhouse & Polyhouse Subsidy
* **Category:** High-Tech Precision Horticulture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers transitioning into year-round climate-controlled vegetable, medicinal plant, and floriculture lines.
* **Benefit Details:** Infrastructure climate engineering protecting crops against intense summer heatwaves (up to 46°C) and winter frost:
  * 50% to 70% front-ended capital investment subsidy to build naturally ventilated polyhouses, shade-net structures, and automated misting lines.
  * Sourcing grants for tissue-cultured planting materials for Dutch Roses, Gerberas, and off-season colored capsicums.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators possessing clear land titles with an operational micro-drip irrigation system installed on the plot.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural designs and agronomic plans directly to the District Horticulture Officer (DHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, certified land mutation copy, vendor design quotation, and soil-water chemical test profiles.

---

### [UP_SCHEME_011] Nand Baba Milk Mission (UP Cow Breeding & Dairy Module)
* **Category:** Allied Sector Dairy Expansion & Indigenous Breed Protection
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural families, livestock rearers, and women-led dairy self-help groups.
* **Benefit Details:** State-wide mission to enhance indigenous milk yields and expand village-level processing:
  * Direct financial incentive grant of up to ₹40,000 for purchasing elite indigenous milch cow breeds (Sahiwal, Gir, and Tharparkar) from outside states.
  * Fully covers the construction cost of modern biosecure animal sheds + 50% subsidy on automated milking machines and chaff cutters.
  * Integrates village milk collection points with computerized Data Processing Milk Collection Centres (DPMCC) to guarantee transparent pricing based on computerized fat and SNF testing.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident livestock owners holding active Aadhaar-linked accounts who complete a basic livestock management orientation.
* **How to Apply:** Apply directly to the regional Chief Veterinary Officer (CVO) or during scheduled block dairy cooperative enrollment camps.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Animal Health Ear-Tag Certificate issued by a veterinary officer, and bank credentials.
