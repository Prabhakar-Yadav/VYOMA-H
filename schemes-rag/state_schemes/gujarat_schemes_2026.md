> **Comprehensive State-Level Agricultural & Environmental Policy Framework**  
> **State Code:** `GJ` / `GUJ`  
> **Key State Focus Areas:** Single-Window Digital Farmer Delivery (*i-Khedut Portal*), Zero-Premium Climate Disaster Relief (*Mukhya Mantri Kisan Sahay Yojana / MMKSY*), Grid-Tied Solar Irrigation & Power Export (*Suryashakti Kisan Yojana / SKY* - ₹7/unit Feed-in Tariff), On-Farm Storage Godowns (*Pak Sangrah*), Crop Wildlife Protection (*Kaantali Taar & Zhatka Machines*), Micro-Irrigation & Mulching (*GGRC*), Indigenous Cattle Dairy (*Gir & Kankrej*), Farmer Smartphone Sahay

## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Gujarat's state agricultural policy is driven through the centralized **i-Khedut Portal** (`ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in`), tailored to meet the challenges of arid and semi-arid tracts (Kutch, Saurashtra, and North Gujarat) as well as fertile central/southern cash-crop zones (cotton, groundnut, castor, cumin, banana, and sugarcane):
1. **Zero-Premium Climate Risk Protection (MMKSY):** A 100% state-funded disaster relief mechanism providing up to ₹25,000/ha without requiring any farmer insurance premium payment for drought, flood, or unseasonal rainfall losses.
2. **Clean Energy & Farmer Power Monetization (SKY):** The *Suryashakti Kisan Yojana* connects farm solar pumps to the power grid, allowing farmers to irrigate crops during the day and sell surplus power back to DISCOMs at a guaranteed ₹7 per unit rate for 7 years.
3. **Storage, Fencing & Precision Infrastructure:** Includes on-farm *Pak Sangrah* godowns (up to ₹5 Lakh), barbed wire *Kaantali Taar* fencing (₹300/m), 40% smartphone subsidies for digital inclusion, and 100% drone subsidies for FPOs.

## Section 1: Climate Risk Management & Clean Energy Irrigation

### [GUJ_SCHEME_001] Mukhya Mantri Kisan Sahay Yojana (MMKSY)
* **Category:** Weather and Climate Risk Management & Input Assistance
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and large-scale landholding farmers cultivating across drought, flood, or unseasonal rain-affected talukas in Gujarat.
* **Benefit Details:** 100% state-funded, zero-premium disaster compensation framework:
  * **Crop Yield Loss between 33% and 60%:** Flat financial relief of ₹20,000 per Hectare (capped at a maximum of 4 Hectares per farming family).
  * **Crop Yield Loss exceeding 60%:** Elevated financial relief of ₹25,000 per Hectare (capped at a maximum of 4 Hectares per farming family).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to all registered landholders. Loss must result from extreme weather triggers: severe drought (< 10 inches of seasonal rain), flash flooding/inundation, or heavy unseasonal rain (> 50mm within 48 hours during harvesting windows).
* **How to Apply:** Applications are logged digitally via the i-Khedut Portal (`ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in`). Field loss assessments are executed by the local Talati-cum-Mantri and Agriculture Extension Officer.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card (linked to mobile), digital copies of 7/12 and 8-A Utara from AnyRoR, a Talati-certified Crop Sowing Verification Form, and an Aadhaar-seeded bank passbook.

---

### [GUJ_SCHEME_002] Suryashakti Kisan Yojana (SKY - Solar Grid Tie Module)
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Renewable Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers and farmer clusters holding active grid-connected agricultural electricity connections.
* **Benefit Details:** Grid-tied solar irrigation with guaranteed feed-in tariffs:
  * 60% capital subsidy (shared between state and central funds) to install on-farm solar photovoltaic arrays. The farmer contributes 5% upfront, with the remaining 35% provided as a low-interest institutional loan.
  * **Feed-in Tariff Guarantee:** Farmers use solar energy for day-time irrigation and sell all surplus electricity back to the local DISCOM (PGVCL/UGVCL/MGVCL/DGVCL) at a guaranteed rate of ₹7 per unit (₹3.50 base tariff + ₹3.50 state incentive bonus) for the first 7 years.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active, non-defaulted agricultural power connection with an operational tubewell or borewell pump.
* **How to Apply:** Digital registration on the i-Khedut Portal during active booking cycles. Technical appraisals are co-managed by GEDA and DISCOM engineers.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, electricity bill showing Consumer ID, 7/12 Land Utara, bank passbook, and NOC from the local power substation.

---

### [GUJ_SCHEME_006] Gujarat Agri-Plastic Mulching & Drip Irrigation Top-Up
* **Category:** Precision Agriculture & Water Conservation Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Cultivators growing high-value cash crops (cotton, groundnut, potato, banana, vegetables) in arid zones (Kutch, Saurashtra, Banaskantha).
* **Benefit Details:** Implemented in coordination with the Gujarat Green Revolution Company (GGRC):
  * 50% state matching top-up subsidy for plastic mulching sheets (capped at ₹20,000 per Hectare).
  * 70% to 85% total subsidy to install precision micro-irrigation lines (drip and micro-sprinkler arrays).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Landholders with operational irrigation sources. Mulching subventions are capped at 2 Hectares per farming household per fiscal cycle.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online via the GGRC network system (`ggrc.co.in`) or routed through the i-Khedut portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, 7/12 Land Utara, water pump power certificate, itemized billing invoices from a GGRC-empanelled plastic manufacturer, and bank passbook.

## Section 2: Post-Harvest Storage, Processing & Value Addition

### [GUJ_SCHEME_007] Mukhya Mantri Pak Sangrah Structure Yojana (On-Farm Godown Subsidy)
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Storage & Infrastructure Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual landholding farmers and smallholder groups looking to protect harvested crops from moisture, unseasonal rain, and storage pests.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital cash subsidy to build permanent on-farm storage godowns (*Pak Sangrah*):
  * **Individual Farmers:** Direct capital cash subsidy of ₹30,000 per farmer to construct an on-farm storage structure.
  * **FPOs & Farmer Groups (5+ Farmers):** Subsidies automatically scale up to a maximum ceiling of ₹5,00,000 for centralized community storage hubs.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Building center height must meet the minimum 10–12 feet specification for proper ventilation. The structure is strictly restricted to agricultural produce storage.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are filed online during seasonal booking windows on the i-Khedut Portal (`ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, 7/12 and 8-A land Utara extracts, structural blueprint layout, and bank passbook.

---

### [GUJ_SCHEME_004] Scheme for Financial Assistance to Agro-Processing Units
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Infrastructure & Market Value Addition
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), young agri-entrepreneurs, and rural cooperative societies.
* **Benefit Details:** Administered through the Gujarat Agro Industries Corporation (GAIC):
  * 35% matching capital investment subsidy (up to a massive ceiling of ₹50 Lakh) to construct cold-chain networks, value-added packaging lines (for mangoes, onions, groundnuts), sorting/grading lines, and processing mills.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered business entities or farmer collectives holding non-agricultural (NA) cleared land or a minimum 10-year registered industrial lease.
* **How to Apply:** Detailed Project Reports (DPR) and financial appraisal documents are uploaded online via the GAIC portal (`gaic.gujarat.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, commercial PAN card, company registration documents, bank loan sanction letter, NA land title deeds, and architectural blueprints.

## Section 3: Crop Protection & Indigenous Livestock Enterprises

### [GUJ_SCHEME_008] Gujarat Barbed Wire & Kaantali Taar Fencing Assistance Scheme
* **Category:** Crop Protection & Field Safeguards
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers facing crop destruction from wild boars, blue bulls (*Nilgai*), or stray animals.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidizes physical and electronic farm boundary protection:
  * Direct financial assistance of ₹300 per running meter (up to a maximum perimeter boundary of 400 meters) to erect high-tensile barbed wire fencing (*Kaantali Taar*).
  * Alternatively, provides a flat 50% matching financial subvention for solar-powered energized shock fence packages (*Zhatka Machines*).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Minimum contiguous farmland cluster footprint of 2 Hectares (adjacent smallholders can cluster plots to qualify).
* **How to Apply:** Submitted online via the i-Khedut platform before the monsoon cycle.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, joint cluster verification forms signed by all clustered landowners, and a Talati certificate validating boundaries.

---

### [GUJ_SCHEME_005] Kamdhenu Desi Gau Palan Protsahan Yojana
* **Category:** Livestock Enterprise & Allied Sector Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural unemployed youth, women dairy farmers, and traditional cattle breeders establishing modern dairy units.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital subsidy and interest subvention for mini-dairy breeding farms using elite indigenous cattle breeds (Gir or Kankrej cows):
  * **12-Cow Mini-Dairy Setup:** 50% capital financial subsidy (capped at ₹3.5 Lakh) to construct modern concrete cattle sheds, buy automated milking machines, and setup heavy-duty chaff cutters.
  * **Interest Relief:** 5% interest subvention on commercial bank loans for a continuous tenure of 5 years.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Gujarat aged 18 to 50 years holding or leasing at least 0.5 Acres of dedicated green fodder land.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted digitally on the i-Khedut Portal under the Animal Husbandry tab.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, land availability verification layout / 5-year lease deed, animal husbandry training clearance certificate, PAN card, and bank passbook.

## Section 4: Farm Mechanization, Digital Precision & Smartphone Sahay

### [GUJ_SCHEME_003] i-Khedut Agro-Mechanization & Tractor Subsidy Support
* **Category:** Farm Mechanization & Automation Subvention
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder farmers, women cultivators, and registered agricultural cooperative societies.
* **Benefit Details:** Upfront capital subsidies to modernize field operations:
  * **Tractors (up to 40 PTO HP):** 40% subsidy (capped at ₹45,000) for general categories; 50% subsidy (capped at ₹60,000) for small, marginal, SC, ST, and women farmers.
  * **Power Tillers, Rotavators, and Multi-Crop Threshers:** 50% to 60% direct subsidy.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Restricted to one subsidized tractor unit per unique farming family identity every 10 years.
* **How to Apply:** Apply online via `ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in`. Allotments are decided through a randomized computerized lottery system.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, 7/12 & 8-A land papers, caste certificate (if claiming priority subsidy bands), and dealer proforma invoice.

---

### [GUJ_SCHEME_009] Gujarat Agriculture Drone (Agri-Bot) Subvention Program
* **Category:** High-Tech Digital Precision Farming
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers, agricultural graduates, cooperatives, and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).
* **Benefit Details:** Capital grants for procuring DGCA-certified agricultural drone kits for aerial pesticide spraying and nano-urea application:
  * **FPOs & Cooperatives:** 100% financial grant (capped at ₹5 Lakh) for acquiring agricultural drone packages.
  * **Individual Agri-Graduates / SC-ST Smallholders:** 50% capital subsidy (capped at ₹4 Lakh).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Drones must be sourced from DGCA-approved empanelled manufacturers. Individual operators must hold an active Remote Pilot License.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are logged under the high-tech mechanization tab of the i-Khedut dashboard.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, DGCA Remote Pilot License copy, Drone Unique Identification Number (UIN), and equipment invoice quotes.

---

### [GUJ_SCHEME_010] Scheme for Financial Assistance on Purchase of Smartphones by Farmers (Smartphone Sahay)
* **Category:** Digital Inclusion & Agri-Extension Services
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landholding cultivators who do not currently own digital mobile smartphone hardware.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct cash subvention to help farmers buy data-enabled smartphones to monitor weather alerts, use regional e-mandi trackers, check pest advisory alerts, and apply for state subsidies:
  * The state covers 40% of the smartphone purchase price or up to ₹6,000 (whichever is lower) as a single Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Restricted to a maximum of one smartphone purchase subsidy per unique 8-A landholding identity across the state.
* **How to Apply:** Handled online via `ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in`. The generated subsidy voucher is presented at any authorized commercial retailer within the district for instant billing adjustments.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, 7/12 and 8-A Land Utara extracts, and the itemized commercial GST tax invoice of the phone showing its IMEI tracking digits.
