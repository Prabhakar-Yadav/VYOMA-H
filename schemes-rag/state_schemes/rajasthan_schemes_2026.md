## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Rajasthan's agricultural framework is engineered to overcome the harsh agro-ecological realities of the Thar Desert, severe water table depletion, extreme evaporation losses, and stray animal intrusion across arid and semi-arid tracts:
1. **Direct Income Enhancement & Zero-Interest Credit:** Augments the central PM-Kisan grant with a dedicated ₹3,000 state top-up via *Mukhyamantri Kisan Samman Nidhi* (delivering ₹9,000/year via Jan Aadhaar-linked DBT) alongside 100% interest-free seasonal crop loans through Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS).
2. **Arid-Zone Water Conservation & Solarization (90%):** Complete elimination of open-channel evaporative water losses through 60% subsidies for underground PVC/HDPE pipelines, up to ₹3,40,000 grants for geomembrane-lined *Diggi* rainwater storage tanks, and 90% subsidies on standalone solar pumps under *PM-KUSUM*.
3. **Perimeter Defense & Agro-Processing Value Chains:** *Tarbandi* wire fencing (70% subsidy) protects crops against wild boars and Nilgai, while the *Agro-Processing & Agribusiness Promotion Policy* grants 50% capital subventions linked to specialized RIICO Agro Food Parks in Kota, Jodhpur, Sriganganagar, and Alwar.

## Section 1: Direct Income Support, Animal Insurance & Zero-Interest Credit

### [RJ_SCHEME_001] Mukhyamantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana
* **Category:** Direct Agri-Investment Support & Income Supplement
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landowning farmers registered across all 50 districts of Rajasthan.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial top-up operating alongside the central PM-Kisan platform:
  * Delivers an additional state-funded direct cash transfer of ₹3,000 per year.
  * Combined with the central PM-Kisan allocation (₹6,000), beneficiaries receive a cumulative financial grant of ₹9,000 annually via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small and marginal farmers recorded on the state land revenue database (Apna Khata / Jamabandi). Mandatory e-KYC and centralized Krishi Stack Farmer ID mappings are required.
* **How to Apply:** Managed digitally via the unified [RajKisan Sathi Portal](https://rajkisan.rajasthan.gov.in/) and authenticated using Jan Aadhaar biometric verification.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Jan Aadhaar Card, Land Jamabandi / Pattadar Passbook, linked mobile number, and bank passbook.

---

### [RJ_SCHEME_002] Rajasthan Mukhyamantri Mangala Pashu Bima Yojana
* **Category:** Allied Sector Risk Insurance & Livestock Welfare
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small-scale dairy farmers, livestock rearers, and rural pastoral families.
* **Benefit Details:** Cattle and bovine risk protection framework:
  * 100% premium-free insurance coverage for up to two elite milch cattle (cows or buffaloes) per family.
  * Direct financial payout of ₹40,000 per animal in the event of death due to disease outbreaks, natural disasters, or accidental trauma.
  * The State Government pays the entire annual insurance premium on behalf of the livestock owner.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident cattle owners with active Jan Aadhaar registrations, prioritizing families below the poverty line (BPL).
* **How to Apply:** Enrolled during seasonal veterinary camps or submitted via the local Animal Husbandry block office.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Jan Aadhaar, Ear-Tagging Certificate issued by a Government Veterinary Officer, and bank passbook.

---

### [RJ_SCHEME_008] Short-Term Interest-Free Crop Loan Scheme
* **Category:** Institutional Agricultural Credit Subvention
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders holding active Kisan Credit Cards (KCC).
* **Benefit Details:** Institutional credit relief disbursed through cooperative banking channels:
  * Provides short-term seasonal crop loans at an effective 0% annual interest rate.
  * The State Government bears the entire interest subvention burden, provided the borrowing farmer repays the loan principal promptly within the 12-month seasonal deadline.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident cultivators with clear land titles registered under regional Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) or Central Cooperative Banks (CCBs).
* **How to Apply:** Applied and renewed online through the Cooperative Department Portal with biometric Jan Aadhaar validation.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Jan Aadhaar, active KCC Passbook, and recent Land Jamabandi revenue extract.

## Section 2: Arid-Zone Water Security & Renewable Irrigation Infrastructure

### [RJ_SCHEME_003] Farm Pond & Diggi Construction Scheme
* **Category:** Rainwater Harvesting & Arid Irrigation Engineering
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers in water-stressed desert districts (Jodhpur, Bikaner, Barmer, Jaisalmer, Nagaur) constructing on-farm storage units.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital subvention to capture monsoon runoff in desert belts:
  * Direct financial subsidy of up to ₹3,40,000 for constructing a 1,200 cubic meter plastic-lined farm pond (*Diggi*).
  * Fully covers the procurement and welding of 500-micron High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) geomembrane plastic lining sheets to prevent water seepage into porous desert sand.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers owning a minimum contiguous landholding of 0.5 Hectares (unirrigated) or 0.3 Hectares (irrigated).
* **How to Apply:** Apply online on the RajKisan Sathi Portal prior to the monsoon season.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar, Jan Aadhaar, recent Land Jamabandi extract, and vendor structural design quotation.

---

### [RJ_SCHEME_004] PM-KUSUM Solar Pump Scheme (Rajasthan Module)
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Daytime Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Off-grid farmers, tube-well owners, and desert cultivation clusters replacing costly diesel generator sets.
* **Benefit Details:** Massive capital subsidy on standalone solar water pumping installations:
  * Delivers a cumulative 90% subsidy structure (60% Central Share + 30% State Top-Up) on standalone solar pumps.
  * The farmer contributes a token margin share of only 10% upfront.
  * Supports 3 HP, 5 HP, and 7.5 HP surface and submersible solar pump configurations.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must have a verified open well, borewell, or active *Diggi* catchment. Applicant must not have an active grid-connected electric pump connection.
* **How to Apply:** Online application during dynamic booking windows on the [RajKisan Sathi Portal](https://rajkisan.rajasthan.gov.in/).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar, Jan Aadhaar, land mutation records, tube-well water yield certificate, and zero-grid electrical meter affidavit.

---

### [RJ_SCHEME_005] Underground Irrigation Pipeline Subsidy
* **Category:** Precision Water Management & Evaporation Control
* **Target Beneficiary:** Cultivators optimizing water transport from tubewells/diggis to crop fields.
* **Benefit Details:** Eliminates high evaporative and seepage losses characteristic of earthen open channels:
  * 60% capital financial subsidy on the procurement and installation of underground PVC / HDPE pressure irrigation pipelines (capped at ₹18,000 for small/marginal farmers).
  * Increases farm-level water conveyance efficiency by up to 30%, conserving crucial groundwater reserves and electricity.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers owning an operational well, borewell, or solar/electric pump connection holding clear land titles.
* **How to Apply:** Apply through regional E-Mitra kiosks or online via the RajKisan Sathi Portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar, Jan Aadhaar, Jamabandi revenue receipt, and pipeline manufacturer invoice from empanelled dealers.

## Section 3: Crop Protection, Arid Horticulture & High-Tech Polyhouses

### [RJ_SCHEME_006] Wire Fencing (Tarbandi) Yojana
* **Category:** Crop Yield Protection & Animal Intrusion Control
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders suffering crop destruction from stray cattle, wild boars, and Nilgai (blue bulls).
* **Benefit Details:** Mitigates the 15% to 25% annual yield loss caused by animal intrusion:
  * 70% state subsidy capped at ₹48,000 for erecting barbed wire fencing (*Tarbandi*) along a 400-meter farm perimeter.
  * Adjacent smallholders can pool contiguous land boundaries to claim higher community fencing grants.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Minimum contiguous plot size of 1.5 Hectares per family (or pooled across 2–3 adjacent farming families).
* **How to Apply:** Digital application on the RajKisan Sathi Portal followed by physical post-installation verification by the local Agriculture Supervisor.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar, Jan Aadhaar, Khasra-map layout coordinates from the Patwari, and vendor material bills.

---

### [RJ_SCHEME_007] Naye Fal Bagicho Ki Sthapana (Orchard Establishment)
* **Category:** Horticultural Diversification & Arid Cash Crops
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive cultivators transitioning from low-value mono-cropping (Pearl Millet/Guar) to high-margin arid fruit orchards.
* **Benefit Details:** Long-term asset creation for climate-resilient fruit crops:
  * Direct capital subsidies covering certified nursery saplings and planting costs for Pomegranate (Bhagwa), Citrus (Kinnow/Lime), Date Palm (Tissue-Culture Medjool/Barhee), and Guava.
  * **Mandatory Micro-Drip Integration:** Subsidies are sanctioned and fruit saplings released only after the farmer installs an operational micro-drip irrigation network.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators with suitable soil and water test profiles holding clean land title deeds.
* **How to Apply:** Submit orchard blueprints and soil reports to the District Horticulture Officer (DHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar, Jan Aadhaar, Land mutation proof, drip installation certificate, and government nursery bills.

---

### [RJ_SCHEME_011] Advanced Protected Cultivation Green/Polyhouse Subsidy
* **Category:** High-Tech Precision Horticulture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers cultivating off-season vegetables (capsicum, cucumbers) and commercial flowers under extreme desert weather.
* **Benefit Details:** Climate engineering infrastructure protecting crops from scorching desert heatwaves (up to 48°C) and winter ground frosts:
  * 50% to 75% front-ended capital subsidy on the complete construction of naturally ventilated polyhouses, shade nets, and anti-insect netting structures.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Landowners holding verified water sources with an integrated micro-drip irrigation system installed on the target plot.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural blueprints and cropping plans directly to the District Horticulture Officer (DHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar, Jan Aadhaar, certified land mutation copy, vendor design quote, and water testing report.

## Section 4: Soil Restoration, Millet Missions & Agro-Processing

### [RJ_SCHEME_009] RajKVY Green Manure (Dhaincha) Minikit Distribution
* **Category:** Soil Health Restoration & Organic Inputs
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers facing declining organic carbon, desertification, and nitrogen depletion in sandy soils.
* **Benefit Details:** State-wide biological soil conditioning:
  * 100% free distribution of certified *Dhaincha* (*Sesbania bispinosa*) seed minikits.
  * Encourages early-season green manuring to boost natural nitrogen fixation, microbial biomass, and moisture-holding capacity in arid soils.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small, marginal, and cooperative farming households registered on the central state database.
* **How to Apply:** Distributed seasonally before Kharif rains through local Gram Panchayats and Agriculture Supervisors.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Jan Aadhaar, and unique Krishi Stack Farmer ID.

---

### [RJ_SCHEME_010] PM Dhana-Dhanya Krishi Yojana (Millet Promotion Module)
* **Category:** Staple Food Security & Dryland Nutri-Cereals
* **Target Beneficiary:** Dryland farmers cultivating low-water-consuming nutri-cereals (*Bajra* / Pearl Millet and *Jowar* / Sorghum).
* **Benefit Details:** Leverages Rajasthan's position as India's top millet producer:
  * Free distribution of certified high-yielding hybrid millet seed minikits.
  * Funds pilot decentralized millet processing lines (destoners, dehullers, flour mills) to supply nutri-cereal products to public Mid-Day Meals and Anganwadi centers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Landholders located in state-notified dryland millet priority districts (Alwar, Barmer, Nagaur, Jodhpur, Jalore).
* **How to Apply:** Administered online through the [RajKisan Sathi Single Window Hub](https://rajkisan.rajasthan.gov.in/).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar, Jan Aadhaar, crop booking tracking log, and bank details.

---

### [RJ_SCHEME_012] Agro-Processing, Agro-Marketing, and Agri-Business Promotion Policy
* **Category:** Supply Chain Logistical Security & Post-Harvest Value Addition
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), registered agricultural cooperatives, and rural agro-entrepreneurs.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive post-harvest agro-industrial framework:
  * Up to a 50% capital financial subsidy (up to ₹1 Crore for FPOs / ₹50 Lakh for individual entrepreneurs) to construct primary sorting/grading pack-houses, multi-commodity cold storage, and solar crop dryers.
  * Direct integration with designated state Agro Food Parks developed by RIICO (in Kota, Jodhpur, Sriganganagar, and Alwar) to eliminate middleman margins.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered farmer collectives or individual agro-business operators holding valid converted agricultural or industrial land titles.
* **How to Apply:** Applications and DPRs are processed online via the state's Business Regulations Action Plan (BRAP) Single Window Clearance System.
* **Documents Required:** FPO Registration Certificate, Land Title deed / long-term registered lease, bank loan sanction letter, and detailed engineering project report.
