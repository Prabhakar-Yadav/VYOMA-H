## Executive Summary & Island Operational Context

The agricultural governance of the Andaman & Nicobar Islands operates under the strict constraints of a fragile tropical island ecosystem: limited land mass, heavy equatorial monsoons with severe soil erosion, high vulnerability to coastal salinity, and complete logistical isolation from the Indian mainland:
1. **100% Organic Island Agriculture:** Phasing out synthetic agrochemicals across all island clusters to shield delicate mangrove forests and marine coral reef sanctuaries, supported by direct transition cash grants (₹10,000/ha) and 100% administration-funded Participatory Guarantee System (PGS-India) organic certifications.
2. **Plantation Value Addition & Decentralized Logistics:** Modernizing primary coconut and arecanut groves via high-density multi-tier intercropping (black pepper, clove, nutmeg, ginger) and building decentralized processing infrastructure at the village gate—deploying solar copra drying tunnels and cold-pressed Virgin Coconut Oil (VCO) extraction plants to eliminate costly shipping of raw produce to the mainland.
3. **Marine-Agri Livelihood Convergence & Rainwater Retention:** Overcoming acute post-monsoon dry spells through 90% subsidized HDPE geomembrane-lined rainwater harvesting ponds, paired with alternative coastal livelihoods—deep-sea *Kappaphycus* seaweed cultivation, brackish-water mud crab fattening, and climate-resilient indigenous *Nicobari fowl* breeding.

## Section 1: Flagship Organic Inputs, Plantation Subventions & Spices

### [AN_SCHEME_001] Island Organic Cultivation & Bio-Input Incentive Scheme
* **Category:** Ecological Agriculture Transition & Input Assistance
* **Target Beneficiary:** Registered landholders, tribal farmers, and active vegetable/fruit cultivators across all island districts.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial and structural assistance to eliminate synthetic chemical fertilizer dependency:
  * Delivers a direct cash assistance grant of ₹10,000 per Hectare (spread across a 3-year transition cycle) via DBT.
  * Supplies 100% free or heavily subsidized certified bio-fertilizers, vermicompost starter sets, earthworm colonies (*Eisenia fetida*), and biological pest management kits.
  * Fully covers group organic certification expenses under the Participatory Guarantee System (PGS-India) to clear island produce for premium eco-tourist market channels.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active resident island farmers committing to long-term chemical-free agricultural protocols. Priority given to remote southern islands and tribal council settlements.
* **How to Apply:** Handled through the state agriculture digital node or via physical validation files verified by local Agriculture Extension Officers (AEOs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Island Resident / Tribal Pass Certificate, unique Farmer ID card, Land revenue possession certificate, and bank passbook.

---

### [AN_SCHEME_002] Coconut & Arecanut Rejuvenation Programme
* **Category:** High-Value Cash Crop Commercialization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional plantation owners, tribal councils, and smallholders maintaining active coconut groves.
* **Benefit Details:** Modernizes the islands' primary agricultural economic base:
  * Distributes high-yielding, dwarf, and disease-resistant Coconut and Arecanut saplings at a 75% administration-funded financial subsidy.
  * Direct capital subvention grant of up to ₹25,000 per unit to install modern Solar Copra Dryers to replace unhygienic wood-fired pits, preserving essential volatile oil content and commanding premium market valuations.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators owning or holding legally recorded land deeds or village-approved long-term cultivation leases.
* **How to Apply:** Route applications through block Agriculture Development Officers (ADOs) or directly to the Department of Agriculture, Port Blair.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Island Domicile proof, Land Patta extract papers, and dealer nursery bills.

---

### [AN_SCHEME_007] Multi-Tier High-Density Plantation Scheme
* **Category:** Agroforestry Expansion & Intercropping Livelihoods
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder plantation owners, home-garden cultivators, and tribal settlement clusters.
* **Benefit Details:** Land-use optimization model stacking crops vertically beneath tall coconut and arecanut canopies:
  * 75% direct administration subsidy on the distribution of elite shade-loving cash crop planting materials.
  * Supplies high-yielding varieties of Black Pepper (*Panniyur* lines), Clove, Nutmeg, Cinnamon, and high-value Ginger/Turmeric rhizomes.
  * Provides specialized vertical support grids, bio-fertilizer starter sets, and non-chemical growth boosters.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active landholders or tribal council communities maintaining operational, well-spaced coconut or arecanut groves.
* **How to Apply:** Routed via block Agriculture Extension Centres (AECs) or regional Zonal Agriculture Offices (ZAOs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Island Resident / Tribal Certificate, Land Patta revenue passbook, and a multi-tier farm layout map.

---

### [AN_SCHEME_008] High-Yielding Black Pepper & Spice Cultivation Subsidy
* **Category:** Specialty Cash Crop Commercialization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholders and plantation farmers scaling up indigenous island spice production.
* **Benefit Details:** Capitalizes on the warm, humid equatorial island climate:
  * Direct financial incentive grant of ₹40,000 per Hectare spread across a 3-year maintenance cycle.
  * Fully funds disease-free rooted pepper cuttings from empanelled state nurseries, shade-net mesh fabrics, and bamboo support stakes to prevent waterlogging root damage.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators holding clear land titles or recognized long-term tribal land allocation rights.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically at your regional District Agriculture Office (DAO) or through block extension counters.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Local Resident Certificate, updated Land Revenue Records (RoR), and certified nursery invoices.

## Section 2: Island Water Security & Groundwater Lift Subsidies

### [AN_SCHEME_003] PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop (Andaman Module)
* **Category:** Precision Water Management & Rainwater Harvesting
* **Target Beneficiary:** Vegetable growers, orchardists, and terrace step farmers experiencing severe freshwater scarcity during the dry post-monsoon months.
* **Benefit Details:** Captures equatorial monsoon runoff before it drains into the ocean:
  * 90% capital financial subsidy to construct permanent rainwater harvesting retention ponds (Farm Ponds).
  * Fully covers the installation of High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) geomembrane plastic lining sheets to prevent freshwater from seeping into sandy coastal soils.
  * Completely funds the physical layout and installation of precision drip-irrigation networks and micro-sprinklers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small and marginal island farmers holding valid land revenue documents with verified catchment layouts.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically at your regional District Agriculture Office (DAO) or via block agriculture extension agents.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Patta ownership receipt, sketch layout blueprint of the micro-irrigation grid, and water source certificate.

---

### [AN_SCHEME_010] Provision of Agri-Input Kits for Vegetables & Micro-Tubewell Infrastructure
* **Category:** Vegetable Sowing Security & Groundwater Lift Subsidies
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and tenant vegetable growers cultivating low-lying coastal valleys.
* **Benefit Details:** Reduces upfront seasonal sowing costs while securing dry-season water pumping:
  * 100% free High-Yielding Variety (HYV) Vegetable Seed Minikits (Tomato, Brinjal, Okra, Chili, Gourds) along with organic neem-cake bio-inputs.
  * 50% capital subsidy capped at ₹25,000 to construct small-diameter micro-tubewells or open ring-wells.
  * Supplies lightweight, energy-efficient kerosene or solar water pump sets to ensure continuous irrigation delivery.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active smallholder vegetable cultivators with clear field bookings verified by local extension teams.
* **How to Apply:** Organized seasonally through block Agriculture Extension Centres (AECs) or at regional Gram Panchayat offices.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Island Resident Card, Land revenue receipt copies, and active crop-booking verification logs.

## Section 3: High-Tech Protected Horticulture & Farm Automation

### [AN_SCHEME_004] High-Tech Protected Horticulture and Polyhouse Subvention
* **Category:** High-Tech Precision Horticulture & Floriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers cultivating year-round climate-controlled vegetable, exotic fruit, and cut-flower lines.
* **Benefit Details:** Protects premium cash crops from equatorial heatwaves, heavy downpours, and pest outbreaks:
  * 50% to 85% front-ended capital investment subsidy (leveraging UT-specific subvention guidelines) to construct naturally ventilated polyhouses, insect-proof netting, and shade-net fabrics.
  * Directly links island growers to luxury eco-tourism resorts to supply high-value off-season crops (cherry tomatoes, colored bell peppers, orchids, and anthuriums) without mainland transport delays.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators possessing clear land titles with an operational micro-drip irrigation system installed on the plot.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural designs and agronomic proposals directly to the District Horticulture Officer (DHO) in Port Blair.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Island Resident Certificate, certified land title passbook, vendor structural design quotes, and soil-water test suitability profiles.

---

### [AN_SCHEME_005] Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM Andaman Node)
* **Category:** Precision Farm Mechanization Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual smallholders, youth collectives, and registered island cooperative societies.
* **Benefit Details:** Alleviates acute agricultural labor shortages in remote island blocks:
  * 50% to 80% capital investment subsidy specifically on lightweight farm machinery engineered for island soils and coconut groves.
  * Equipment supported: mini power tillers, hand-held brush cutters, mechanical palm tree climbers, portable coconut de-huskers, and power knapsack sprayers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual island cultivators or registered rural collectives possessing land usage clearances certified by local revenue authorities.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online via the national [AgriMachinery Portal](https://agrimachinery.nic.in/) by selecting the Andaman & Nicobar state node.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID code, proforma invoice from an empanelled machinery manufacturer, and land passbook extracts.

## Section 4: Marine Aquaculture, Copra Processing & Allied Livestock

### [AN_SCHEME_006] Integrated Island Aquaculture and Seaweed Livelihood Project
* **Category:** Allied Sector Fisheries & Multi-Income Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Coastal fishermen, rural youth groups, and tribal families owning low-lying brackish-water lines.
* **Benefit Details:** Multi-sector marine aquaculture expansion:
  * 60% capital financial subsidy to construct biosecure Brackish-water Ponds or coastal floating cages for mud crab fattening and high-value tiger shrimp culture.
  * Fully funds the sourcing of bamboo rafts, monofilament ropes, and elite seed lines to establish Deep-Sea Seaweed (*Kappaphycus alvarezii*) cultivation in calm island bays.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents or recognized tribal council members aged 18 to 45 with access to coastal zones cleared by Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) authorities.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural proposals directly to the regional District Fisheries Officer (DFO) or during scheduled block outreach camps.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Local Resident Certificate, CRZ clearance / Panchayat land allocation logs, and bank passbook details.

---

### [AN_SCHEME_009] Primary Processing and Value Addition of Coconut Products
* **Category:** Supply Chain Logistical Security & Post-Harvest Assets
* **Target Beneficiary:** Micro-entrepreneurs, self-help groups (SHGs), and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) across island clusters.
* **Benefit Details:** Overcomes mainland shipping bottlenecks by processing produce right at the village gate:
  * 50% upfront capital financial grant to construct decentralized agro-processing units near plantation clusters.
  * Funds modern machinery lines: cold-pressed Virgin Coconut Oil (VCO) extraction plants, coconut shell charcoal carbonization kilns, and automated coir fiber processing looms.
  * Connects processing units directly to mainland export terminals via administration-subsidized shipping cargo lines.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered collective groups, FPOs, or individual entrepreneurs possessing land matching regional industrial zoning rules.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online via the unified Single Window Clearance System or at the Directorate of Industries, Port Blair.
* **Documents Required:** FPO / Group Registration Certificate, absolute Land Title deeds or valid 10-year registered Lease Agreement, bank credit clearance logs, and detailed engineering DPR.

---

### [AN_SCHEME_011] Integrated Backyard Poultry & Goat Breeding Initiative
* **Category:** Allied Sector Livestock & Alternative Income Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landless rural laborers, women-led dairy groups, and tribal households across remote islands.
* **Benefit Details:** Shields island families from nutritional insecurity and single-crop failure:
  * 75% direct capital investment subsidy to construct biosecure, climate-resilient animal pens and poultry coops.
  * Supplies elite, disease-resistant backyard poultry breeds (indigenous *Nicobari fowl* and *Vanaraja* chicks) along with high-yielding *Nicobari* or *Malabari* dual-purpose goat breeding pairs.
  * Fully covers first-year veterinary feed kits, deworming tracking cards, and electronic animal tagging.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident livestock owners holding valid Aadhaar-linked accounts who pass an initial animal husbandry orientation.
* **How to Apply:** Apply directly to the regional Chief Veterinary Officer (CVO) or during scheduled block outreach camps.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Tribal Status Certificate (if applicable), space availability certificate signed by the Pradhan, and bank passbook details.
