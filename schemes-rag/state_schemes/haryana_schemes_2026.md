## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Haryana's agricultural and rural policy framework addresses critical challenges across the intensive Indo-Gangetic wheat-paddy belt, semi-arid southern districts, and waterlogged saline plains:
1. **Water Conservation & Crop Diversification:** Addressed through *Mera Pani Meri Virasat* (₹7,000/acre direct incentive to shift paddy land to maize, cotton, pulses, and vegetables) and 75% subsidized HAREDA solar pumping systems.
2. **Market Price Risk Elimination (Bhavantar Bharpayee Yojana):** Comprehensive price deficit compensation guaranteeing that if APMC Mandi wholesale prices drop below floor prices for vegetables and fruits, the state automatically transfers 100% of the price gap via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
3. **In-Situ Stubble Management (SB-82):** Combats winter air pollution with a direct ₹1,200/acre operational reward for zero-burning stubble management paired with 50%–80% subsidies on Super Seeders and straw balers.

## Section 1: Crop Diversification & Market Deficit Floor Price Protection

### [HARYANA_SCHEME_001] Mera Pani Meri Virasat Yojana
* **Category:** Crop Diversification & Water Conservation Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landowning farmers voluntarily diversifying away from water-guzzling paddy (rice) fields in notified groundwater-stressed blocks.
* **Benefit Details:** Promotes groundwater conservation by compensating farmers for shifting out of paddy:
  * Flat direct cash grant of ₹7,000 per acre to transition paddy land into alternative low-water crops (maize, cotton, pulses, oilseeds, or horticultural crops).
  * 100% free comprehensive crop insurance coverage for diversified acreage during Year 1.
  * 40% state capital subvention for procuring specialized pneumatic precision seed planters.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must have been verified under active paddy cultivation on the state registry during the preceding Kharif cycle. Acreage must be mapped on the Meri Fasal Mera Byora (MFMB) portal.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications are logged via the Meri Fasal Mera Byora Portal (`fasal.haryana.gov.in`). Field tracking and crop switch verifications are co-managed by the local Patwari and Agriculture Development Officer (ADO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID / PPP), updated Jamabandi land ownership extracts, and bank passbook.

---

### [HARYANA_SCHEME_007] Bhavantar Bharpayee Yojana (BBY - Horticultural Price Protection)
* **Category:** Market Price Risk Mitigation & Deficit Compensation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers cultivating state-notified vegetable and fruit crops (Potatoes, Onions, Tomatoes, Cauliflower, Carrots, Peas, Kinnow, Guava).
* **Benefit Details:** Protects horticultural growers against unviable market price crashes:
  * The state sets fixed guaranteed base incentive floor prices for each notified crop.
  * If the wholesale price in the local APMC Mandi falls below the floor price, the state government automatically calculates the price deficit and transfers 100% of the difference directly into the farmer's bank account via seasonal DBT lines.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators must complete mandatory crop acreage registration on the Meri Fasal Mera Byora (MFMB) registry before harvesting windows close.
* **How to Apply:** Automated tracking triggers when a farmer logs their crop yield sale inside any integrated state APMC Mandi electronic auction terminal against an authorized J-Form.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Family ID (PPP), active MFMB seasonal registration slip, and J-Form market trading invoices.

## Section 2: In-Situ Crop Residue Management & Clean Energy Irrigation

### [HARYANA_SCHEME_008] State Scheme for Crop Residue Management (SB-82 Operational Incentive)
* **Category:** In-Situ Environmental Protection & Stubble Burning Mitigation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Paddy harvesting farmers using scientific mechanical implements to manage standing straw residue without open-field burning.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive operational reward and machinery subsidy framework:
  * Direct cash reward incentive of ₹1,200 per acre paid to farmers who execute in-situ (mixing into soil) or ex-situ (baling for biomass plants) stubble management.
  * 50% capital machinery subsidy for individual farmers and 80% grant for Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) to purchase Super Seeders, Happy Seeders, Straw Choppers, and heavy-duty Balers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must be verified via satellite remote sensing data to confirm zero open-field crop residue burning plots.
* **How to Apply:** Digital verification logs are submitted through the agricultural mechanization portal (`agriharyana.gov.in`) immediately after Kharif harvesting.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID), GPS-tagged field photo uploads, and machinery serial tracking numbers.

---

### [HARYANA_SCHEME_004] Haryana Solar Water Pumping Scheme (HAREDA Module)
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Renewable Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers requiring off-grid daytime irrigation power who have opted out of conventional electrical grid connections.
* **Benefit Details:** Implemented through the Haryana Renewable Energy Development Agency (HAREDA):
  * Deploys standalone DC/AC solar water pumping systems (3 HP, 5 HP, 7.5 HP, and 10 HP surface or submersible configurations).
  * **Subsidy Structure:** Combined central (PM-KUSUM) and state subvention covers up to 75% of the total capital cost. The farmer contributes only a 25% token margin share upfront.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must possess an active, verified underground borewell or water catchment layout. The applicant must not hold any pre-existing grid-connected agricultural electric tubewell meter in their name.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications are logged through the state service gateway (`saralharyana.gov.in`) during active seasonal booking windows.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID), current Land Jamabandi extract, borewell depth certificate, and a declaration confirming zero grid power meters.

## Section 3: Agricultural Family Social Security, Welfare & Livestock Insurance

### [HARYANA_SCHEME_002] Mukhyamantri Kisan Evam Khet Mazdoor Jeevan Suraksha Yojana
* **Category:** Agricultural Social Security & Accident Insurance
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers, tenant cultivators, and manual agricultural laborers aged between 15 and 65 years.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct financial compensation for fatal or disabling injuries caused while operating agricultural machinery (tractors, threshers, cane crushers, tubewells, power sprayers) or lightning strikes during field duties:
  * **Accidental Death:** Flat statutory compensation payout of ₹5 Lakh to the registered legal nominee.
  * **Total Permanent Disability (Loss of both limbs or both eyes):** Compensation payout of ₹2.5 Lakh.
  * **Partial Permanent Disability (Loss of one hand/foot or serious injury):** Compensation payout of ₹1 Lakh to ₹1.5 Lakh.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Accident must occur during active farm duties in Haryana. Claims must be submitted within a strict 2-month window from the incident date.
* **How to Apply:** Handled offline by submitting itemized claim folders to the local Market Committee office of the Haryana State Agricultural Marketing Board (HSAMB).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, certified Post-Mortem Report or Government Medical Board Disability Certificate, Police FIR / Daily Diary Entry, and a verification note signed by the Gram Panchayat Sarpanch.

---

### [HARYANA_SCHEME_005] Mukhya Mantri Parivar Samridhi Yojana (MMPSY - Agri-Panchayat Link)
* **Category:** Financial Stabilization & Social Safety Net
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder farming families and landless agricultural workers holding low cumulative household incomes.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct annual income support of ₹6,000 per family per financial year delivered via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT):
  * The fund is structurally calibrated to automatically absorb and pay the farmer's annual premium contributions for major central social security plans (PM-JJBY life insurance, PM-SBY accidental insurance, and PM-KMY farmer pension).
  * The remaining cash balance is credited directly into the bank account as an input support fund.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Verified annual family income from all sources must be under ₹1.80 Lakh, and cumulative land ownership must not exceed 5 Acres (2 Hectares), verified through the Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) database.
* **How to Apply:** Form profiles are submitted through local Common Service Centres (CSCs) or processed digitally on the Antyodaya Saral Portal (`saralharyana.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, active Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID), land Jamabandi extracts, and an Aadhaar-seeded bank passbook.

---

### [HARYANA_SCHEME_009] Haryana Pashudhan Bima Yojana (Livestock Security Module)
* **Category:** Allied Sector Livestock Asset Protection
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional dairy farmers, pastoralists, and rural livestock breeders owning cattle, buffaloes, or small ruminants.
* **Benefit Details:** Highly subsidized comprehensive livestock insurance:
  * **Large Animals (Cows, Buffaloes, Camels, Horses):** Premium is capped at a token cost of just ₹100 per animal for a comprehensive 3-year policy cover (100% free for SC beneficiaries).
  * **Small Animals (Sheep, Goats, Pigs):** Premium is capped at a token cost of just ₹25 per animal for a 3-year policy cover.
  * Provides full market valuation cash compensation if an insured animal dies due to disease, natural calamity, or lightning strike.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to all permanent resident animal rearers holding an official animal health certificate. Maximum allocation is capped at 5 large animals per unique household ID.
* **How to Apply:** Enrolled at local government veterinary hospitals or via special tagging drives conducted by block livestock extension officers.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Family ID (PPP), formal RFID ear-tag tracking code, and veterinary fitness assessment certificate.

## Section 4: Input Quality, Saline Aquaculture & Precision Drones

### [HARYANA_SCHEME_006] Haryana Uttam Beej Subvention Mission
* **Category:** High-Yield Input Quality Maximization
* **Target Beneficiary:** All active landholding cultivators procuring certified high-yield seed varieties for Kharif and Rabi crops.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidizes the retail purchase price of certified wheat, paddy, mustard, barley, and pulse seeds by 20% to 50%:
  * Farmers generate dynamic digital vouchers to claim immediate price discounts directly at authorized retail dealer counters.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers must have completed active registration on the Meri Fasal Mera Byora (MFMB) portal. Support is capped at a maximum of 5 acres per farming family per season.
* **How to Apply:** Voucher applications are processed online through the unified Uttam Beej Portal (`uttambeej.haryana.gov.in`) prior to sowing.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, active MFMB Registration ID slip, and mobile number linked for OTP confirmation.

---

### [HARYANA_SCHEME_010] Integrated Aquaculture and Inland Saline Fisheries Project
* **Category:** Blue Revolution Allied Livelihood Upgrades
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers owning waterlogged or high-salinity underground fields (primarily in Rohtak, Jhajjar, Jind, Hisar, and Bhiwani districts).
* **Benefit Details:** Transforms unproductive saline land into commercial aquaculture hubs:
  * 60% capital financial grant to excavate inland fish/shrimp ponds, install high-efficiency surface aerators, and construct nursery tanks for White Leg Shrimp (*Litopenaeus vannamei*).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must have a verified soil/water analysis report indicating high salinity or permanent waterlogging that impedes standard cereal crop cultivation.
* **How to Apply:** Project development proposals are submitted online through the dedicated fisheries portal (`harfish.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Jamabandi ownership papers, chemical water salinity test card, and itemized project cost estimations.

---

### [HARYANA_SCHEME_003] Haryana Agri-Drone Sahayata Scheme
* **Category:** Precision High-Tech Farming Tech Subvention
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), women SHGs, and agricultural graduates.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidies for deploying DGCA-certified agricultural drones for aerial nano-urea application and precision pesticide spraying:
  * **FPOs & Cooperatives:** 100% financial grant (capped at ₹5 Lakh) for acquiring agricultural drone packages.
  * **Individual Agri-Graduates / SC-ST Smallholders:** 50% capital subsidy (capped at ₹4 Lakh).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Drones must be sourced from DGCA-approved empanelled manufacturers. Individual operators must hold an active Remote Pilot License.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted digitally through the high-tech mechanization tab on the state agriculture portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Family ID (PPP), DGCA Remote Pilot License, Drone UIN, and vendor proforma quotes.
