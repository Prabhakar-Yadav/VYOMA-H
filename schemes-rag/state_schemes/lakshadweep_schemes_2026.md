## Executive Summary & Island Operational Context

Lakshadweep's agricultural governance operates across 10 inhabited coral atolls under fragile ecological realities: porous coral sand soils with low water-holding capacity, complete absence of surface rivers, small fragmented land parcels, and extreme vulnerability of its shallow underground freshwater lens (which floats immediately above saline seawater):
1. **100% Certified Organic Territory Mandate:** Enforcing a complete ban on synthetic chemical fertilizers and pesticides to protect the shallow freshwater lens and delicate lagoon coral reefs. The administration provides 100% free/90% subvented bio-inputs (bone meal, neem cake, Azotobacter), ₹20,000 household vermicompost grants, and 100% state-funded Participatory Guarantee System (PGS-India) organic certifications.
2. **Decentralized Coconut Value Addition (VCO):** Offsetting steep mainland maritime freight costs by establishing on-island processing micro-units—providing 50% to 65% capital grants for cold-pressed Virgin Coconut Oil (VCO) plants, desiccated coconut mills, and modern solar tunnel copra dryers that eliminate traditional smoke-drying.
3. **Sustainable Blue Economy & Soil-Less Food Security:** Supporting eco-friendly, dolphin-safe "Pole and Line" skipjack tuna fishing through 50% to 60% subsidies for fiberglass boats, baitfish tanks, and solar-powered ice plants, paired with 100% state-sponsored lagoon *Kappaphycus* seaweed rafts (with guaranteed buybacks via LDCL) and 75% subsidized nutrient-film technique (NFT) vertical hydroponics to overcome soil fertility deficits.

## Section 1: Flagship 100% Organic Inputs & Coconut Rejuvenation

### [LD_SCHEME_001] Comprehensive Island Organic Bio-Input Replacement Program
* **Category:** Ecological Agriculture Transition & Fertilizer Substitution
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual landholders, island farming families, and local coconut grower cooperatives across all 10 inhabited atolls.
* **Benefit Details:** Cushions islanders against the financial cost of maintaining 100% chemical-free farming:
  * Distributes 100% free or 90% subvented organic bio-inputs: certified city compost, bone meal, neem cakes, and bio-fertilizer cultures (*Azotobacter*, *Phosphotika*).
  * Direct financial grant of ₹20,000 per household to construct permanent concrete Vermicompost Processing Units, complete with earthworm starter colonies (*Eisenia fetida*).
  * Fully covers the annual audit and tracking protocols for international organic group certification (PGS-India mapping) to clear island produce for premium organic export channels.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active resident island farmers registered on the territory's unified agricultural index with zero synthetic chemical input usage.
* **How to Apply:** Distributed seasonally before the monsoons through local island Agriculture Extension Centres (AECs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Island Resident Certificate, unique Farmer ID card, land possession note, and bank passbook.

---

### [LD_SCHEME_002] Coconut Plantation Rejuvenation & Solar Copra Dryer Initiative
* **Category:** High-Value Cash Crop Commercialization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional coconut grove owners, smallholders, and youth micro-enterprises.
* **Benefit Details:** Upgrades the islands' primary agricultural crop while improving hygienic processing:
  * Supplies high-yielding, dwarf, disease-free Coconut seedlings at a 75% direct financial subsidy to replace old, unproductive palms.
  * Direct capital subvention grant of up to ₹30,000 per unit to install Modern Solar Tunnel Copra Dryers. This replaces unhygienic wood-fired smoke-drying, ensuring copra dries cleanly to command premium sulfur-free market valuations.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators owning or holding legally recorded land deeds or village-council approved long-term cultivation leases.
* **How to Apply:** Submit physical registration profiles directly at the local Island Agriculture Office (IAO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Lakshadweep Domicile proof, Land Patta extract papers, and dealer nursery bills.

---

### [LD_SCHEME_008] Organic Rodent & Rhinoceros Beetle Eco-Safe Mitigation Scheme
* **Category:** Crop Protection & Biological Input Delivery
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional coconut plantation owners experiencing heavy yield drops due to palm canopy pests.
* **Benefit Details:** Completely non-toxic biological protection matrix matching the territory's 100% organic status:
  * 100% free distribution of Biological Protection Kits to eliminate Rhinoceros Beetles and coconut rodents without chemicals.
  * Supplies automated Pheromone Traps (*Oryctalure* lines), biological fungal agents (*Metarhizium anisopliae* kits), and botanical bio-sprays.
  * Fully covers specialized training for safe tree-top trap placement without contaminating adjacent lagoon water borders.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active landholders maintaining operational coconut groves listed on the territory's land registry.
* **How to Apply:** Distributed directly through island Agriculture Extension Centres (AECs) or via local field extension teams.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Unique Farmer Registration Code, and land possession records.

## Section 2: Freshwater Lens Protection & Post-Harvest VCO Processing

### [LD_SCHEME_003] Rainwater Harvesting & Micro-Irrigation Subvention Grid
* **Category:** Precision Water Management & Freshwater Lens Protection
* **Target Beneficiary:** High-yield vegetable growers, intercrop farmers, and backyard homesteaders.
* **Benefit Details:** Specialized water engineering to capture monsoon runoff while protecting the shallow underground freshwater lens from over-extraction:
  * Upfront 90% capital financial subsidy to install comprehensive on-farm rainwater harvesting structures equipped with specialized storage tanks.
  * Fully covers precision drip-irrigation networks, micro-sprinklers, and automated soil moisture sensors to ensure zero freshwater wastage on porous sandy soils.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Island farmers holding valid land revenue documents with a verified roof-catchment or ground-catchment layout.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically at your regional Zonal Agriculture Office (ZAO) or via local island field extension agents.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, land mutation proof, sketch design blueprint of the micro-irrigation layout, and a water source clearance note.

---

### [LD_SCHEME_004] Island Virgin Coconut Oil (VCO) Processing & Micro-Enterprise Grants
* **Category:** Supply Chain Logistical Security & Post-Harvest Asset Creation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs), registered Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and young island entrepreneurs.
* **Benefit Details:** Eliminates heavy maritime freight costs by converting raw coconuts into high-value, lightweight commodities right on the islands:
  * 50% to 65% upfront capital investment grant to construct micro-processing units near plantation groves.
  * Funds modern processing lines: Cold-pressed Virgin Coconut Oil (VCO) extraction plants, automated desiccated coconut powder mills, and coconut vinegar fermentation setups.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered collective groups or individuals owning land or holding a long-term registered lease deed.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online via the unified UT Single Window Clearance System or at the Directorate of Industries in Kavaratti.
* **Documents Required:** Group Registration Certificate / FPO Code, absolute Land Title deeds or Lease Agreement, bank credit clearance logs, and detailed engineering DPR.

## Section 3: Sustainable Marine Fisheries & Blue Economy Seaweed

### [LD_SCHEME_005] Pole and Line Tuna Fishing Modernization & Ice-Plant Subvention
* **Category:** Allied Sector Fisheries & Marine Livelihood Expansion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional island fishermen, mechanized boat owners, and marine cooperative societies.
* **Benefit Details:** Modernizes Lakshadweep's globally recognized, dolphin-safe sustainable skipjack tuna fishing technique:
  * 50% to 60% capital financial subsidy for marine safety gear, GPS fish-finders, and automated live-baitfish holding tanks.
  * Heavy capital subventions to procure specialized fiberglass mechanized boats alongside decentralized Solar-Powered Ice Plants across all islands to stop catch spoilage.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent resident fishermen holding a valid Biometric Fisher ID Card issued by the Department of Fisheries.
* **How to Apply:** Submit boat modification or equipment procurement proposals directly to the regional District Fisheries Officer (DFO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Fisher Identity Card, boat registration certificates, and bank passbook details.

---

### [LD_SCHEME_006] Sustainable Lagoon Seaweed Cultivation Infrastructure Grant
* **Category:** Blue Economy & Sustainable Marine Aquaculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Fishermen collectives, island youth groups, and women-led marine Self-Help Groups (SHGs).
* **Benefit Details:** Alternative marine livelihood expansion inside calm lagoons without disrupting the coral reef perimeter:
  * 100% state-sponsored training and infrastructure to construct floating bamboo seaweed rafts inside calm atoll lagoons.
  * Distributes premium, fast-growing seed stocks of *Kappaphycus alvarezii*, monofilament ropes, life-jackets, and high-density tracking floats.
  * Guaranteed institutional buyback agreement with the Lakshadweep Development Corporation Limited (LDCL) for commercial bio-stimulant production.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of the UT aged 18 to 50 years with water-space clearance approved by the island village council.
* **How to Apply:** Submit applications physically to the regional island Fisheries Extension Officer (FEO) during early calm-sea windows.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Island Native / Resident Certificate, valid Biometric Fisher ID Card, and bank passbook.

## Section 4: Soil-Less Hydroponics & Indigenous Backyard Poultry

### [LD_SCHEME_007] High-Density Vertical Hydroponic & Roof Horticulture Drive
* **Category:** Soil-Less Urban-Adjacent Agriculture & Food Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** Island households, school Mid-Day Meal units, and local vegetable growers looking to bypass low coralline sand fertility.
* **Benefit Details:** Solves the territory's fresh vegetable deficit while preventing chemical runoff into the underground freshwater lens:
  * 75% direct financial subsidy to procure automated vertical nutrient-film technique (NFT) hydroponic arrays and lightweight roof shading setups.
  * Supplies premium soil-less grow bags filled with nutrient-enriched coco-peat, high-yielding vegetable seed kits (Tomato, Mint, Coriander, Chili), and micro-sprayers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Residents across notified island sectors with clear, unshaded backyard or roof space.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically at the island's Zonal Agriculture Office (ZAO) or during scheduled block layout inspections.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile proof, space availability declaration note, and bank account credentials.

---

### [LD_SCHEME_009] Indigenous Backyard Poultry & Poultry Feed Extension
* **Category:** Allied Sector Livestock & Alternative Income Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landless rural laborers, women-led SHGs, and marginalized households across the atolls.
* **Benefit Details:** Reduces reliance on expensive mainland egg/meat shipments:
  * 100% free distribution of low-input Backyard Poultry Units consisting of 10 to 30 disease-resistant chicks per family.
  * Supplies elite tropical breeds (such as indigenous *Nicobari fowl* or *Vanaraja*) naturally adapted to foraging beneath coconut groves in high island humidity.
  * Fully covers first-year veterinary feed kits and health tracking cards via regional veterinary dispensaries.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents across notified island sectors passing basic resource screening.
* **How to Apply:** Managed by block-level outreach teams linked to the Department of Animal Husbandry.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Resident Certificate, EWS card (if applicable), and a basic poultry enclosure declaration note.
