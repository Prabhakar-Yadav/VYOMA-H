## Executive Summary & UT Operational Context

Jammu & Kashmir's agricultural policy framework addresses the unique agro-climatic demands of temperate mountain valleys, sub-tropical Jammu plains, and high-altitude pastoral rangelands:
1. **Holistic Agri-Economy Overhaul (HADP):** A massive ₹5,012 Crore mission encompassing 29 tech-driven projects to modernize temperate horticulture, cold chains, protected polyhouses, and dairy lines across all 20 districts.
2. **High-Value GI Cash Crops & High-Density Horticulture:** Revitalization of world-renowned Pampore saffron corms (₹25,000/kanal grant) and 50% capital subsidies to transition orchards to dwarf clonal rootstocks (Apple, Cherry, Walnut) protected with anti-hail trellis frameworks.
3. **Mountain Logistics & Pastoral Welfare:** The *PARVAZ Scheme* offsets mountain transport bottlenecks by providing a 25% air cargo freight subsidy on perishable fruits, while the *ISDS Scheme* provides zero-interest biological credit units (25 ewes + 1 ram) for sheep farmers.

## Section 1: Flagship Agri-Economy Restructuring & High-Density Horticulture

### [JK_SCHEME_001] Holistic Agriculture Development Programme (HADP)
* **Category:** Comprehensive Agri-Economy & Value Chain Restructuring
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual progressive farmers, smallholders, Self Help Groups (SHGs), Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and youth agri-entrepreneurs across all 20 districts of J&K.
* **Benefit Details:** A landmark integrated agricultural initiative spanning 29 specialized sectoral projects:
  * 50% to 70% front-ended capital investment subsidy to establish controlled atmosphere (CA) cold storage facilities, commercial grading/sorting pack-houses, high-density fruit orchards, and climate-controlled polyhouses.
  * Includes seed security, precision micro-irrigation, and decentralized post-harvest food-processing machinery grants.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident landholders or registered youth cooperatives with verified land revenue records registered under active status on the centralized HADP tracking index.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications and project profiles are submitted through the unified HADP single-window portal (`hadp.jk.gov.in`). Field verification is conducted jointly by the Block Agriculture Officer (BAO) and Horticulture Development Officer (HDO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile Certificate, updated Land Khasra Girdawari title sheets, detailed project cost estimates from empanelled vendors, and bank passbook.

---

### [JK_SCHEME_004] J&K High-Density Orchard Transition Subvention
* **Category:** Horticultural Commercialization & Technology Adaptation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal fruit growers transitioning traditional senile apple, pear, cherry, and walnut orchards to high-density rootstocks.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital subvention for planting dwarf clonal rootstocks (e.g., M9 rootstocks for apples):
  * 50% front-ended capital subsidy covering the procurement of certified imported/tissue-cultured plants, micro-drip irrigation setups, galvanized iron trellis wire frameworks, and anti-hail netting structures.
  * Maximum physical layout ceiling is capped at 20 Kanals (approx. 1 Hectare / 2.5 Acres) per farming family.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Landowners holding clear revenue titles with certified soil and irrigation water suitability parameters. Planting layout must adhere to strict row-to-row spacing standards.
* **How to Apply:** Online registration via the Directorate of Horticulture portal or offline submission directly to the regional Horticulture Development Officer (HDO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile Certificate, certified Land Mutation copy from the Revenue Department, soil testing report, and empanelled nursery proforma invoices.

## Section 2: Heritage GI Cash Crops & Mountain Clean Energy Irrigation

### [JK_SCHEME_002] Jammu & Kashmir Saffron Production & Rejuvenation Mission
* **Category:** GI-Tagged Cash Crop Protection & Upgrades
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional saffron growers in notified saffron cultivation belts (primarily Pampore, Srinagar, Budgam, and Kishtwar plateaus).
* **Benefit Details:** Soil health restoration and productivity enhancement for GI-tagged Kashmiri Saffron:
  * Direct cash subsidy grant of ₹25,000 per Kanal (approx. ₹2 Lakh per Hectare) for deep machine plowing, soil sterilization, and replacing low-yielding degraded corms with certified healthy seed corms.
  * Installation of automated micro-sprinkler mist irrigation networks to supply critical protective moisture during the blooming window (September–October).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Land parcels situated within demarcated saffron zoning grids. The land must hold a valid mutation clearance signature from the revenue Patwari.
* **How to Apply:** Handled offline by routing applications through the Chief Agriculture Officer (CAO) or submitting directly at the Advanced Saffron Development Office, Pampore.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique AgriStack Farmer ID, updated Land Khasra Girdawari extracts, and bank passbook.

---

### [JK_SCHEME_005] PM-KUSUM Component-B (J&K JAKEDA Module)
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Renewable Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Off-grid farmers, orchardists, and smallholder clusters requiring dependable daytime irrigation in non-electrified or erratic mountain power belts.
* **Benefit Details:** Implemented through the Jammu & Kashmir Energy Development Agency (JAKEDA):
  * Deploys standalone DC/AC solar photovoltaic water pumping installations (2 HP, 3 HP, 5 HP, and 7.5 HP surface or submersible configurations).
  * **Subsidy Structure:** The UT Administration and MNRE provide a massive cumulative 80% capital subsidy, requiring the farmer to contribute a token margin share of only 20% upfront.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must hold a verified underground borewell, open well, or continuous perennial gravity spring/stream catchment. The applicant must not have an active grid-connected electric agricultural connection.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications open dynamically during scheduled booking windows on the JAKEDA portal (`jakeda.jk.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile Certificate, updated land revenue documents, water source yield certificate from an irrigation engineer, and an affidavit confirming zero grid power meters.

## Section 3: High-Altitude Livestock & Mountain Allied Diversification

### [JK_SCHEME_003] Integrated Sheep Development Scheme (ISDS Module)
* **Category:** Livestock Allied Enterprise & Pastoral Economy
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural unemployed youth, traditional Chopan/Bakarwal pastoralists, and women entrepreneurs expanding commercial mutton and wool lines.
* **Benefit Details:** Unique biological credit loop model:
  * The UT Government provides 100% free deployment of a starter breeding farm unit comprising 25 purebred ewes and 1 elite breeding ram (Merino cross / Corriedale varieties).
  * **Repayment Terms:** The beneficiary pays zero cash interest; instead, they return 5 healthy ewe lambs annually to the department over a 5-year period. These returned lambs are then used to seed new starter units for other rural youths.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of J&K aged 18 to 40 years with adequate land or shed infrastructure to shelter livestock during harsh winter snowfall months.
* **How to Apply:** Digital profiles are submitted through the single-window portal or filed directly with the District Sheep Husbandry Officer (DSHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile Certificate, unemployment self-declaration affidavit, winter shed availability sketch map, and bank passbook.

---

### [JK_SCHEME_007] J&K Mission on Apiculture & National Beekeeping Upgrades
* **Category:** Allied Sector Diversification & Non-Timber Mountain Products
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder farmers, landless rural youth, and women self-help groups establishing commercial honey production units in mountain pastures.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial and technical assistance to harness rich wild flora (Acacia, Mustard, Saffron, Apple blossoms):
  * **Starter Kits:** 50% to 60% direct capital subsidy to purchase 10 to 50 active bee colony boxes containing elite *Apis mellifera* hives, honey extractors, and smoker gear.
  * **Cluster Processing Hubs:** 80% capital grant for registered FPOs and cooperatives to establish regional honey testing labs, processing machinery, and bottling lines.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of J&K aged 18 to 50 years. Preference is given to candidates completing a 7-day beekeeping training course at a Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK).
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted through the Directorate of Agriculture portal or directly to the Chief Agriculture Officer (CAO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile Certificate, KVK training certificate, and bank passbook.

## Section 4: Post-Harvest Air Cargo Logistics & Mountain Connectivity

### [JK_SCHEME_006] PARVAZ Market Linkage Scheme (Air Cargo Freight Subsidy)
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Logistics & Export Value Chain Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual fruit growers, registered Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and high-value agri-exporters handling perishable fruits (Cherry, Strawberry, Plum, Pear, high-grade Apple).
* **Benefit Details:** Overcomes severe highway transit delays caused by winter landslides on the NH-44 highway:
  * The UT Administration provides a direct 25% freight financial subsidy on total air cargo shipping costs for transporting perishable horticultural crops from Srinagar or Jammu international airports to any major domestic terminal in India.
  * Subsidy reimbursements are credited directly via DBT to the freight invoices of registered growers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to registered fruit orchardists in J&K packing produce through government-notified cold-chain pack-houses or APEDA-registered pack-houses.
* **How to Apply:** Digital claims and invoice logs are submitted through the J&K Horticultural Produce Marketing and Processing Corporation (JKHMPC) portal (`jkhmpc.jk.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Farmer ID code, actual Air Way Bill (AWB) copy, commercial pack-house tax invoice, and bank passbook.
