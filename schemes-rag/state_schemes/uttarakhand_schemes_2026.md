## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Uttarakhand's agricultural policy is designed to address the stark division between the fertile Terai plains and the fragile, steep-slope terrain of the mountain districts. The state's strategy focuses on stopping rural depopulation ("ghost villages") by transitioning vulnerable slopes from wildlife-prone food grains to high-value organic cash crops, aromatic plants, and high-density horticulture:
1. **Aromatic Value Chains & Organic Export Shields:** Shifting farmland borders exposed to wild animals toward deer-resistant essential oil crops (Damask Rose, Lemongrass, Cinnamon) supported by the *Centre for Aromatic Plants (CAP, Selakui)* with community Field Distillation Units (FDUs), paired with 100% state-funded international organic certification under the *Uttarakhand Organic Commodity Board (UOCB)*.
2. **Mountain Water Engineering & Solar River Lift Pumping:** Overcoming the mountain challenge of "water in the valley, thirst on the hill" through 70% to 85% subsidies for *Saur Swarojgar* solar lift pumping installations managed by UREDA, alongside 90% subsidies on plastic-lined *Chalk-Khal* rainwater storage ponds.
3. **Indigenous Nutri-Cereals, 0% Credit & High-Altitude Allied Lines:** Guaranteed state MSP buyback for indigenous millets (*Mandua* and *Jhangora*), 0% interest farm credit up to ₹3 Lakh (individual) / ₹5 Lakh (groups) under *Deendayal Upadhyaya Sahakarita*, 5-year MGNREGA-converged organic tea estates (*UTDB*), and concrete mountain *Raceways* for high-value Coldwater Trout aquaculture.

## Section 1: Flagship Mountain Support, Aromatic Missions & Organic Chains

### [UK_SCHEME_001] Mukhyamantri Aromatic Planter Incentive Scheme
* **Category:** Value-Chain Expansion & Alternative Cash Crop Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder hill cultivators, tribal farmers, and active cluster cooperatives in hilly blocks looking to diversify from traditional crops.
* **Benefit Details:** Flagship economic transition model engineered by the Centre for Aromatic Plants (CAP, Selakui):
  * Converts wildlife-vulnerable farm borders into high-return aromatic clusters resistant to monkey and wild boar destruction.
  * Distributes certified, elite planting materials (Lemongrass, Damask Rose, Cinnamon, Mint, and Chamomile) at a 50% to 75% direct state subsidy.
  * Grants upfront capital subventions to establish community-operated Field Distillation Units (FDUs) to extract high-value essential oils right at the farm gate.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident cultivators holding valid land titles or long-term village-council lease permissions in notified aromatic clusters.
* **How to Apply:** Submit applications digitally via the unified State Agriculture Portal (`agriculture.uk.gov.in`) or route files through regional block Aromatic Plant Cluster Officers.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC), land ownership Jamabandi extract, and bank passbook.

---

### [UK_SCHEME_002] Uttarakhand Organic Crop Multiplication and Certification Drive
* **Category:** Ecological Agriculture Transition & Quality Control
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual hill farmers, traditional grower groups, and registered Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) transitioning into verified chemical-free setups.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive long-term support framework managed by the Uttarakhand Organic Commodity Board (UOCB):
  * Fully state-funds the expensive protocol of international organic group certification (PGS-India and NPOP mapping) to capture premium export markets.
  * Delivers a direct input incentive grant of ₹10,000 per Hectare spread across a 3-year transition cushion to cover certified bio-fertilizers and bio-pesticides.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active farming clusters located within state-notified organic blocks who agree to undergo periodic soil chemical residue testing.
* **How to Apply:** Handled at the cluster level via regional Agriculture Extension Officers (AEOs) or by registering through localized FPO channels.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Group registration logs, soil profile analysis baseline logs, and field tracking history.

---

### [UK_SCHEME_006] State Millet Promotion & Minimum Support Price (MSP) Support
* **Category:** Staple Nutri-Cereal Preservation & Price Incentives
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional hill farmers cultivating indigenous low-water nutri-cereals (*Mandua* / Finger Millet and *Jhangora* / Barnyard Millet).
* **Benefit Details:** Protects indigenous biodiversity and enhances dryland mountain profit margins:
  * Establishes a guaranteed State Minimum Support Price (MSP) framework to buy back raw millets directly from farmers, eliminating middleman exploitation.
  * Delivers a direct cash incentive bonus via DBT for meeting quality harvest targets, alongside free distribution of hybrid millet minikits to smallholders.
  * Connects village clusters directly to state-funded localized processing lines to supply de-hulled nutri-cereals to public Mid-Day Meals and Anganwadi centers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active hill landholders with clear crop booking entries verified by localized agricultural extension officers.
* **How to Apply:** Automated tracking applies during harvest drop-offs at government-notified regional grain procurement stations or cooperative hubs.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Jamabandi revenue receipt copies, and Agriculture Market Committee (AMC) sale logs.

## Section 2: High-Altitude Water Security & Solar Lift Pumping

### [UK_SCHEME_003] Mukhyamantri Saur Swarojgar Yojana (Solar Lift Irrigation Module)
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Mountain Water Resource Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** Hill-terrace vegetable growers and fruit orchard clusters lacking dependable gravity water lines.
* **Benefit Details:** Structural climate engineering solving the mountain challenge of accessing deep valley river waters:
  * Upfront 70% to 85% capital investment subsidy to install high-powered standalone solar water pumping units paired with river lift mechanisms.
  * Enables smallholders to lift water from low-lying perennial rivers or deep ravines up to multi-tiered step terraces, ensuring dependable daytime irrigation power without diesel generator expenses.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must have a verified surface water source or perennial spring located below the plot with clear land usage rights.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications open dynamically during scheduled token booking windows on the [Uttarakhand Renewable Energy Development Agency (UREDA)](https://ureda.uk.gov.in/) portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID code, land mutation records, technical water lift elevation feasibility report, and zero-grid electricity pump affidavit.

---

### [UK_SCHEME_004] PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop (Uttarakhand Hill Module)
* **Category:** Precision Micro-Irrigation & Water Harvesting
* **Target Beneficiary:** Hilly terrace vegetable growers, fruit orchardists, and commercial flower nurseries.
* **Benefit Details:** Adapts precision micro-irrigation to steep slopes:
  * 90% capital financial subsidy to construct permanent rainwater harvesting retention ponds (*Chalk-Khals* / Farm Ponds) equipped with High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) geomembrane plastic lining sheets.
  * Fully covers the layout and installation of gravity-fed precision drip-irrigation networks, micro-sprinklers, and water delivery tube grids.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small and marginal hill farmers holding valid land revenue documents with verified catchment layouts.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically at your regional District Horticulture Office (DHO) or via block horticulture extension officers.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, land mutation proof, sketch design blueprint of the micro-irrigation grid, and a water source availability certificate.

## Section 3: Mountain Mechanization, Zero-Interest Credit & High-Density Orchards

### [UK_SCHEME_005] Uttarakhand State Horticultural Mission (Apple & Kiwi Fruit Expansion)
* **Category:** Commercial Horticulture Acceleration
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers transitioning traditional low-yield hill plots into commercial high-density orchards.
* **Benefit Details:** High-impact asset creation scaling export lines for mountain fruits:
  * 50% to 75% capital financial subsidy to source imported, certified high-yielding high-density Apple clone rootstocks (M9, MM106) and Kiwi fruit saplings (Allison, Hayward).
  * Fully covers the installation cost of specialized anti-hail nets, perimeter barbed-wire fencing, and integrated micro-drip grids on the plot.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators working on active farm plots positioned within state-notified altitudinal elevation zoning tiers (above 1,500m for high-density apples).
* **How to Apply:** Submit plantation proposals directly to the Directorate of Horticulture, Chaubattia (Ranikhet), or regional DHO lines.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Hill Land Revenue Title Passbook, soil profile health cards, and empanelled nursery vendor quotes.

---

### [UK_SCHEME_007] Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali Hill Farm Mechanisation Scheme
* **Category:** Specialized Mountain Mechanisation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal hill landholders, youth collectives, and women's self-help groups (SHGs) facing extreme labor shortages.
* **Benefit Details:** Overcomes steep terrace terrain constraints where conventional tractors cannot operate:
  * 50% to 80% capital investment subsidy specifically on lightweight, low-gravity farm machinery engineered for slope traction.
  * Distributed equipment targets mountain terraces: mini mountain power tillers, hand-held hill-climbing weeders, mechanical brush cutters, portable grain threshers, and manual seed-drills.
  * Funds the creation of Village Machinery Banks under rural cooperatives to allow remote micro-landholders to rent gear at fixed, low state hourly rates.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual hill cultivators or registered rural collectives possessing land usage clearances certified by local revenue Patwaris or Village Councils.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online via the national [AgriMachinery Portal](https://agrimachinery.nic.in/) selecting the Uttarakhand state node.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID code, proforma invoice from an empanelled machinery manufacturer, and land mutation passbook extracts.

---

### [UK_SCHEME_008] Deendayal Upadhyaya Sahakarita Kisan Kalyan Yojana
* **Category:** Short-Term Institutional Agricultural Credit Subvention
* **Target Beneficiary:** Marginal hill cultivators, standalone women farmers, and landless sharecroppers operating through cooperative societies.
* **Benefit Details:** Institutional credit relief framework insulating high-altitude cultivators from private moneylenders:
  * **0% Net Interest Rate:** Provides 100% interest-free agricultural loans up to ₹3 Lakh for individual farmers and up to ₹5 Lakh for registered Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) or Self-Help Groups (SHGs), provided the loan principal is repaid within 12 months.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident cultivators with active membership inside the regional Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACS) or District Cooperative Bank network.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online during scheduled seasonal credit deployment cycles directly at local PACS bank counters with biometric validation.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, active Kisan Credit Card (KCC) or PACS Loan Passbook, and recent Land Jamabandi revenue documents.

## Section 4: Agro-Processing, Organic Tea Estates & Coldwater Aquaculture

### [UK_SCHEME_009] Chief Minister’s Mountain Agro-Processing & Integrated Pack-house Scheme
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Logistics & Niche Market Value Addition
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), village cooperative fruit unions, and mountain agro-entrepreneurs handling delicate temperate yields.
* **Benefit Details:** Overcomes severe highway transit bottlenecks and landslide transit delays:
  * Up to a 70% upfront capital financial grant to establish solar-powered, off-grid micro-cold storage rooms (5 MT to 20 MT capacities) and computerized sorting/grading pack-houses near mountain production hubs.
  * Finances specialized primary processing units for high-value organic hill outputs: ginger washing beds, garlic peeling arrays, and vacuum-packing infrastructure for certified *Mora* (local wild honey) and *Musha* (mountain kidney beans / Rajma).
  * Direct linkage to state marketing distribution channels under the Uttarakhand State Agriculture Produce Marketing Board (Mandi Parishad).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered collective groups or individuals aged 18 to 50 owning land or holding a minimum 10-year registered lease deed.
* **How to Apply:** Complete project blueprints are processed online via the integrated [State Single Window Portal](https://investuttarakhand.uk.gov.in/) or at the Directorate of Horticulture.
* **Documents Required:** Group Registration Certificate/FPO Code, absolute Land Title deeds or 10-year Lease Agreement, bank loan sanction records, and detailed engineering DPR.

---

### [UK_SCHEME_010] Uttarakhand Tea Development Board (UTDB) High-Altitude Plantation Scheme
* **Category:** Agroforestry Expansion & Sustainable Mountain Livelihoods
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landless hill laborers, smallholders, and community clusters in high-altitude temperate belts (elevations between 1,000m to 2,500m).
* **Benefit Details:** Converts abandoned, degraded hillsides and porous rainfed lands into long-term organic tea estates:
  * 100% free distribution of premium tea clones (*Camellia sinensis* varieties suited for cold climates) alongside soil-enriching organic bio-inputs.
  * Daily cash labor wage co-payments via MGNREGA integration for up to 5 consecutive years to handle initial terracing, contour plowing, weeding, and fencing.
  * Guaranteed long-term institutional buyback linkage through state-managed tea processing factories located in Almora, Bageshwar, and Champawat.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers with verified plots located in notified tea priority zones, or community land blocks cleared by the local Gram Panchayat.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically through the regional manager offices of the Uttarakhand Tea Development Board (UTDB).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC), land possession certificate, or Gram Panchayat lease validation note.

---

### [UK_SCHEME_011] Mukhyamantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (Coldwater Aquaculture Module)
* **Category:** Allied Sector Fisheries & Mountain Income Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Youth entrepreneurs, traditional fishermen, and hill families owning land near cold perennial mountain streams.
* **Benefit Details:** High-altitude coldwater aquaculture expansion:
  * Direct 50% to 60% capital financial subsidy to construct permanent concrete *Raceways* engineered specifically for high-value Coldwater Trout or Mahseer breeding.
  * Supplies elite trout ova/fingerlings, automated aerator machinery setups, and high-protein specialized fish feed kits.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Uttarakhand aged 18 to 45 years with access to a clean perennial water source (mountain springs or gravity channels) where water temperatures remain strictly below 20°C year-round.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural site proposals directly to the regional District Fisheries Officer (DFO) or during scheduled block outreach camps.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, PRC copy, land revenue sheets proving water-body layout permissions, and bank passbook details.
