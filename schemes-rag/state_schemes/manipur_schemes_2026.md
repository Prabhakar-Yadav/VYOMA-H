## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Manipur's agricultural policy is shaped by its distinct dual topography: the fertile, densely cultivated central Imphal Valley and the surrounding high-altitude hill districts. State interventions prioritize climate-resilience, economic recovery, seed self-reliance, and input security across conflict-affected and disaster-hit areas:
1. **Targeted Input Cushioning & Disaster Relief:** The *Krishi Input Subsidy & Oorja Initiative* delivers a 50% subsidy on certified seeds (paddy, maize, vegetables, hill ginger) and supplies premium organic bio-fertilizers at a heavily capped concessional rate of ₹250 per bag through 150 regional distribution centers, organizing specialized distribution camps for flood/drought-affected blocks and internally displaced persons (IDPs).
2. **Multi-Sector Economic Diversification (IFS):** Breaking single-crop dependency and shifting cultivation (*Jhum*) through the *Integrated Farming System (IFS)*, co-financing micro-fisheries, backyard poultry, apiculture (bee boxes), and organic horticulture on a single plot.
3. **Organic Certification & GI Value Chains (MASOCA):** Standardizing chemical-free farming across 400 hectares in all 16 districts via the *Manipur Seed and Organic Certification Agency (MASOCA)* in partnership with the Central Agricultural University (CAU, Imphal), while securing price stability for indigenous GI *Chakhao* (black rice).

## Section 1: Flagship Input Relief & Direct Income Support

### [MN_SCHEME_001] Manipur Krishi Input Subsidy & Oorja Module
* **Category:** Essential Sowing Input Support & Sustainable Cultivation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders across both valley paddy belts and hill cultivation zones.
* **Benefit Details:** Alleviates upfront seasonal sowing and crop nutrition expenses:
  * **50% Seed Subsidy:** Delivers a 50% state-funded financial subsidy on certified seeds (Paddy, Maize, local Vegetables, and high-value Hill Ginger).
  * **Oorja Organic Initiative:** Provides premium organic bio-fertilizers at a capped concessional rate of ₹250 per bag (against an open market price of ₹480) to phase out synthetic chemical fertilizers.
  * **IDP & Relief Focus:** Deploys specialized mobile input distribution camps for flood/drought-impacted blocks, law-and-order affected areas, and farmland tilled by internally displaced persons (IDPs).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered farmers listed on the centralized state agriculture index, with prioritized allocations for hill district ginger cultivators and relief camps.
* **How to Apply:** Distributed seasonally across 150 regional Urea and Fertilizer Distribution Centres managed by the State Agriculture Department.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, verified Pattadar title extract / Village Authority letter, and active seasonal Crop Booking slip.

---

### [MN_SCHEME_002] PM-KISAN (Manipur Implementation Node)
* **Category:** Direct Financial Income Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder and marginal landowning farming families across all 16 districts of Manipur.
* **Benefit Details:** Fully state-monitored cash-transfer safety net providing baseline financial security:
  * Delivers a guaranteed direct income support of ₹6,000 per year, disbursed systematically in three equal seasonal installments of ₹2,000 each.
  * Deposited directly through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the farmer's validated Aadhaar-linked bank account.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small and marginal landholders with operational land records registered under active verified status on the state land revenue platform.
* **How to Apply:** Digital registration via the central PM-KISAN portal or through local block-level Common Service Centres (CSCs) linked to the Manipur Agriculture Department tracker.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, recent land mutation papers / Pattadar Passbook, and active Aadhaar-linked bank account passbook.

## Section 2: Valley & Hill Water Supply & Farm Mechanization

### [MN_SCHEME_003] PMKSY Water Pump Subsidy Scheme
* **Category:** Irrigation Infrastructure Expansion & Pumping Grants
* **Target Beneficiary:** Valley rice farmers and hill-terrace cultivators lacking dependable gravity irrigation networks.
* **Benefit Details:** Targeted on-farm water security to counter seasonal dry spells:
  * Direct state-sponsored capital financial subsidy of ₹10,000 on the purchase of approved kerosene/diesel/electric water pump sets.
  * Extends concessional assistance to install precision micro-sprinklers and drip irrigation pipelines, cutting water and energy wastage.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small and marginal category farmers owning or leasing plots with proximity to continuous mountain streams, springs, tubewells, or open catchment ponds.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically at the designated regional District Agriculture Office (DAO) or through line department field extension agents.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile certificate, land ownership/lease record, and a water source yield clearance certificate.

---

### [MN_SCHEME_004] Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM Manipur)
* **Category:** Precision Farm Mechanization Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder farming groups, cooperative rice cultivation clubs, and young rural agri-entrepreneurs.
* **Benefit Details:** Reduces heavy manual labor costs and accelerates cropping timelines:
  * 40% to 50% capital investment subsidy for individual farmers (scaling up to 80% for registered cooperative Custom Hiring Centres).
  * Equipment specifically engineered for valley paddies and hill terraces: specialized rice transplanters, high-powered tillers, rotavators, power weeders, and localized grain harvesters.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Priority is assigned to valley rice-growing belts (such as Bishnupur, Imphal West, Thoubal) and registered tribal youth farming cooperatives in hill sectors (Churachandpur, Ukhrul, Senapati).
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications and dealer invoices are uploaded via the national [AgriMachinery SMAM Portal](https://agrimachinery.nic.in/) by selecting the Manipur state nodal office.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer Registration Code, dealer proforma price quote from an empanelled manufacturer, and Village Council validation log.

## Section 3: Integrated Farming Systems (IFS) & Organic Certification

### [MN_SCHEME_005] Integrated Farming System (IFS) Initiative
* **Category:** Multi-Sector Livelihood Diversification & Economic Recovery
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional farmers, shifting (*Jhum*) cultivators, and smallholders looking to broaden single-crop income avenues.
* **Benefit Details:** Flagship state economic revival framework aimed at driving agricultural profitability and mitigating risk:
  * Finances the co-establishment of complementary agricultural and allied enterprises on a single contiguous plot.
  * Supports capital installation for localized micro-fisheries ponds, backyard poultry/duckery sheds, apiculture (bee colony boxes), and organic horticulture to insulate farmers from single-crop failures.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active landholders with a strong focus on enhancing sustainability, particularly across the state's remote hill districts.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are verified and processed through joint cluster camps organized by the State Planning and Agriculture Departments.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Tribal Status Certificate, Village Council land allotment clearance, and an IFS farm layout blueprint.

---

### [MN_SCHEME_006] Manipur Seed and Organic Certification Agency (MASOCA)
* **Category:** Quality Control & Certified Natural Farming
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual progressive cultivators, cluster farmers, and registered Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) shifting to chemical-free agriculture.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive institutional standardization framework managed in partnership with the Central Agricultural University (CAU, Imphal):
  * Issues formal *Natural Farming Scope Certificates* enabling local organic crops to command premium domestic and export market valuations.
  * Dedicates approximately 400 hectares of farmland across all 16 districts under verified organic production grids.
  * Promotes local certified seed multiplication for oilseeds and pulses (mustard, soybean, sunflower) to eliminate reliance on external seed imports.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active farming clusters or FPOs executing natural, chemical-free agricultural rotations under strict verification rules.
* **How to Apply:** Enroll at regional Model Organic Villages or submit validation dossiers directly to the MASOCA Certification Unit.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, FPO group registration certificate, soil chemical-clearance logs, and field diary tracking history.
