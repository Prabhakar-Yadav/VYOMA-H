## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Madhya Pradesh's state agricultural policy framework is designed around the demands of India's primary soybean, wheat, and pulse-producing agrarian landscape:
1. **Direct Income Security (MKKY):** Provides an unconditional state matching cash top-up of ₹6,000/year to PM-KISAN beneficiaries, delivering a cumulative ₹12,000 annually per farming family via the SAARA portal.
2. **Digital Market & Price Risk Protection (e-Uparjan):** End-to-end digital slot booking and automated SMS tokens for orderly MSP crop procurement at Mandi yards and 90-day buffer warehousing.
3. **Farm Security, Water & Logistics:** All-weather farm-to-market road construction (*Khet Sadak*), 90% subsidized standalone solar pumps via MPUVNL, on-farm *Balram Talaab* pond grants, and solar electric fencing (*Zhatka Machines*) to prevent Nilgai/wildlife damage.

## Section 1: Direct Income Support & Market Price Deficit Guarantee

### [MP_SCHEME_001] Mukhyamantri Kisan Kalyan Yojana (MKKY)
* **Category:** Direct Income Support & Financial Stabilization
* **Target Beneficiary:** All landowning smallholder and marginal farming households resident in Madhya Pradesh.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct state matching cash top-up tied to the central PM-KISAN framework:
  * The Madhya Pradesh Government disburses a flat grant of ₹6,000 per financial year delivered via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each.
  * Installments synchronize with seasonal input purchasing cycles (Kharif sowing, Rabi sowing, and Zaid/summer crops).
  * Combined with PM-KISAN's ₹6,000, each eligible landowning family receives a total annual income support of ₹12,000 directly into their Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active landholding farming families verified on the state's land records registry who are enrolled as active beneficiaries of the central PM-KISAN scheme.
* **How to Apply:** Handled digitally through the Smart Application for Revenue Administration portal (`saara.mp.gov.in`). Ground checks and beneficiary locking are executed by the local Patwari.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Samagra Family ID Code, unique PM-KISAN Registration ID, updated Land Khasra/Khatauni extracts, and NPCI-mapped bank passbook.

---

### [MP_SCHEME_006] MP e-Uparjan Price Deficit Guarantee & Procurement Scheme
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Market Risk Management & Floor Price Protection
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual landholders and registered sharecroppers cultivating food grains (Wheat, Paddy) and pulses (Gram, Lentils, Mustard) in Madhya Pradesh.
* **Benefit Details:** End-to-end digital harvest procurement and price assurance framework:
  * Automates slot booking and issues computerized SMS tokens directly to farmers, specifying a designated date, time, and Mandi procurement center for crop delivery.
  * Eliminates Mandi yard congestion and distressed fire-sales below Minimum Support Price (MSP).
  * Guarantees matching cash compensation for market deficits or provides emergency buffer storage in state civil supply warehouses for up to 90 days.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators must complete mandatory crop acreage tracking entries (*Phori / Girdawari*) on the e-Uparjan portal before seasonal harvesting windows close.
* **How to Apply:** Registration is processed online via the integrated e-Uparjan Portal (`mpeuparjan.nic.in`) or at authorized village Kiosk/CSC counters.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Samagra ID, updated Land Khasra extract from SAARA, and an Aadhaar-seeded bank passbook.

## Section 2: Farm Infrastructure, Logistics & Water Harvesting

### [MP_SCHEME_003] Mukhyamantri Khet Sadak Yojana
* **Category:** Agricultural Logistics & Rural Infrastructure Development
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural farming communities, village Panchayats, and farming clusters requiring all-weather farm-to-market connectivity.
* **Benefit Details:** 100% state-funded construction of permanent gravel or non-bituminous all-weather farm roads (*Khet Sadak*):
  * Links isolated agricultural field clusters directly to major village arterial roads, Mandi yards, and district highways.
  * Significantly reduces transit damage and transport costs for perishable horticultural crops, grains, and tractor implements.
  * 100% of construction materials and machinery costs are absorbed through state infrastructure funds converged with MGNREGA labor.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** The proposed road route must serve a minimum contiguous agricultural field command boundary of 20 Hectares owned by multiple farming households.
* **How to Apply:** Handled offline by submitting a formal resolution passed by the local Gram Sabha directly to the Janpad Panchayat or Block Development Officer (BDO).
* **Documents Required:** Gram Panchayat resolution copy, cadastral revenue maps showing road alignment, voluntary land donation consent forms from landowners along the route, and technical survey report from an Assistant Engineer.

---

### [MP_SCHEME_004] MP Mukhyamantri Solar Pump Yojana
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Irrigation Infrastructure Development
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual landholders or farming groups needing off-grid water pumping solutions for day-time irrigation in non-electrified or erratic power belts.
* **Benefit Details:** Administered in coordination with the Madhya Pradesh Urja Vikas Nigam Limited (MPUVNL):
  * Deploys standalone DC/AC solar water pumping systems (1 HP, 2 HP, 3 HP, and 5 HP surface or submersible configurations).
  * **Subsidy Structure:** Provides up to a 90% financial subvention for small, marginal, and tribal farmers. The beneficiary pays only a 10% token margin share of the benchmark equipment cost.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must hold a verified, operational open dugwell or deep borewell with adequate water discharge. The plot must not possess an active grid-connected electrical pump connection.
* **How to Apply:** Digital profile entries are submitted on the unified MP Solar Pump Portal (`cmsolarpump.mp.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Samagra ID, Land Khasra/Khatauni tracking records, water source certificate signed by an MPUVNL field engineer, and bank passbook.

---

### [MP_SCHEME_005] Mukhya Mantri Balram Talaab Yojana
* **Category:** Water Harvesting & Groundwater Recharge Asset Creation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders constructing on-farm rainwater catchment ponds (*Balram Talaab*).
* **Benefit Details:** Financial assistance to excavate standardized farm ponds to capture monsoon runoff and recharge groundwater:
  * **SC / ST Beneficiaries:** 75% financial capital subsidy capped at ₹1,00,000.
  * **General Category Beneficiaries:** 50% financial subsidy capped at ₹80,000.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators must hold clear land titles. The scheme is open exclusively to farmers who have already installed micro-irrigation systems (drip or sprinkler arrays) on their land.
* **How to Apply:** Applications open seasonally ahead of the monsoon cycle via the MP Agriculture Department portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Samagra ID, updated Land Khasra entry details from SAARA, micro-irrigation installation verification certificate, and bank passbook.

## Section 3: Livestock, Dairy Enterprises & Crop Protection Fencing

### [MP_SCHEME_002] Mukhyamantri Krishi Pashu Palan Protsahan Yojana
* **Category:** Allied Sector Livestock & Dairy Enterprise
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural youth, smallholder farmers, and traditional cattle breeders establishing modern milch animal units.
* **Benefit Details:** Credit-linked capital investment subsidy for mini-dairy breeding farms utilizing elite indigenous cattle breeds (Sahiwal, Gir, Tharparkar) or high-yield Murrah buffaloes:
  * **4 to 10 Milch Animal Units:** 75% capital subsidy for SC/ST applicants; 50% capital subsidy for general category applicants to construct modern concrete cattle sheds, install automatic milking machines, and acquire heavy-duty chaff cutters.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Madhya Pradesh aged 18 to 50 years holding at least 0.5 Acres of dedicated land to cultivate green fodder.
* **How to Apply:** Project proposals are filed online under the Animal Husbandry module on the state agriculture portal and verified by the local Veterinary Assistant Surgeon.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Samagra ID, land possession document or 5-year registered lease deed, animal husbandry training completion certificate, and bank PAN details.

---

### [MP_SCHEME_007] Mukhyamantri Khet Suraksha Yojana (Solar Fencing Subvention)
* **Category:** Crop Protection & Asset Infrastructure Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landholding farmers and smallholder clusters facing severe crop destruction from Nilgai (blue bulls), wild boars, and stray cattle.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital subsidy for installing solar-powered energized electric fencing (*Zhatka Machines*):
  * Delivers a non-lethal, high-voltage pulsating electrical shock that safely deters wild animals without causing injury or mortality.
  * **Subsidy Structure:** 60% financial capital subsidy (capped at ₹50,000 per applicant) covering up to a maximum fence perimeter of 400 running meters.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual farmers with a minimum contiguous plot of 0.5 Hectares, or adjacent smallholders clustering their land plots to reach a minimum contiguous area of 2 Hectares.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted online through the SAARA portal or the state agriculture mechanization dashboard.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Samagra ID, joint land parcel verification forms signed by all clustered landowners, and proforma invoice from an empanelled fencing manufacturer.

## Section 4: Seed Multiplication & Farm Mechanization

### [MP_SCHEME_008] Madhya Pradesh Beej Gram Yojana (Certified Seed Distribution)
* **Category:** High-Yield Input Quality & Production Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and tribal farmers cultivating certified high-yield seed varieties for Kharif and Rabi crops.
* **Benefit Details:** Promotes village-level seed self-sufficiency by converting entire village clusters into specialized seed production hubs (*Beej Gram*):
  * Supplies foundation seed minikits of wheat, soybean, gram, and maize at a 75% to 85% subsidy off retail market prices.
  * Farmers receive technical training to multiply foundation seeds into certified seeds, which are then distributed to surrounding community members at affordable state-regulated rates.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered small and marginal farmers on the state agriculture portal. Benefits are capped at a maximum of 1 Hectare per farming family per season.
* **How to Apply:** Seed voucher applications are processed online through the unified MP Agriculture portal prior to sowing windows.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Samagra ID, unique Farmer ID, and active mobile number for OTP verification.

---

### [MP_SCHEME_009] MP Krishi Yantra Anudan Yojana (Mechanization Vouchers)
* **Category:** Farm Mechanization & Automation Subvention
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers, women cultivators, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and agricultural graduates.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct capital grants for acquiring modern agricultural machinery suited for black cotton and alluvial soils:
  * **Heavy Machinery (Tractors, Combine Harvesters, Laser Land Levelers):** 40% to 50% direct subsidy (capped at ₹2.5 Lakh per individual project tranche).
  * **Handheld Tools (Power Tillers, Rotavators, Multi-Crop Threshers):** 50% to 60% direct subsidy for women, SC, and ST applicants.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Restricted to one tractor or heavy agricultural machine per farming family ID every 7 years. Clear land titles required.
* **How to Apply:** Digital registration and machine selection are completed on the unified agricultural engineering portal (`dbt.mpdage.org`). Beneficiaries are selected through a computerized randomized lottery system.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Samagra ID, Khasra/Khatauni land extracts, and formal proforma invoice from an empanelled machinery manufacturer.
