## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Meghalaya's agricultural framework is tailored to the steep, undulating terrains of the Khasi, Jaintia, and Garo Hills, maximizing high-value niche agro-climatic commodities while solving complex mountain transport constraints:
1. **High-Value GI Cash Crops & Floriculture:** Strategic commercialization of world-renowned *Lakadong Turmeric* (7.5%+ curcumin content) in Jaintia Hills, exotic *Shiitake/Button Mushrooms*, commercial *Lilium/Orchid* floriculture (₹7 Lakh subsidy packages), and value-added *Jackfruit* processing across Garo Hills.
2. **Grassroots Cash Transfers & Livestock (FOCUS & CM-ELEVATE):** The *FOCUS and FOCUS+* programs disburse direct cash assistance (₹5,000 per member in Producer Groups + ₹5,000 per family), while the *CM-ELEVATE* window delivers 50% capital subsidies for commercial piggery farms and 75% grants for weather-proof crop warehouses.
3. **Pioneering Mountain Logistics (1917 iAMP & MARVS):** Award-winning centralized digital freight aggregation via toll-free helpline `1917` paired with subsidized *Agri-Truck* response vehicles (*MARVS* - 50% vehicle subsidy) connecting remote hill collection points directly to regional wholesale markets.

## Section 1: Flagship Value-Added Cash Crop Missions & Floriculture

### [ML_SCHEME_001] Mission Lakadong
* **Category:** Value-Chain Expansion & GI-Tagged Crop Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder turmeric farmers, tribal cultivators, and specialized FPOs in Jaintia Hills (Laskein, Thadlaskein) and notified production belts.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive value-chain development for world-renowned Lakadong Turmeric (famous for exceptionally high 7.5%–9% curcumin content):
  * 50% to 75% state subsidy on organic, disease-free certified seed rhizomes.
  * Capital grants to construct community-managed solar drying yards, washing stations, and localized powder pulverizing machinery units.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident tribal cultivators with valid land tenure clearance in notified turmeric-growing blocks.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are routed through the Directorate of Agriculture or submitted at the District Agriculture Office (DAO) and block Agriculture Development Offices (ADOs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Scheduled Tribe (ST) Certificate, Land Possession Certificate (LPC) from village council heads (*Doloi / Sirdar*), and bank passbook.

---

### [ML_SCHEME_002] Meghalaya Mushroom Mission
* **Category:** Allied Horticulture & Alternative Livelihoods
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs), landless laborers, and unemployed youth establishing indoor cultivation units.
* **Benefit Details:** Input and technical deployment scaling Button, Oyster, and high-value Shiitake mushrooms:
  * 60% to 80% direct state subsidy on starter cultivation packs (sterilized spawn, pasteurized substrate bags, mist sprayers, temperature controllers).
  * Establishes fully funded regional mushroom spawning laboratories and spawn banks to secure supply lines for remote hill blocks.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Meghalaya aged 18+ who have completed a mandatory 3-day training module from a state-recognized center.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted through the state horticulture portal or directly at the District Horticulture Office (DHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile Certificate, mushroom training certificate copy, and bank passbook.

---

### [ML_SCHEME_008] Meghalaya Floriculture Mission 1.0
* **Category:** Commercial Horticulture Acceleration
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholders and progressive floriculturists entering commercial flower production.
* **Benefit Details:** High-impact investment framework to build Meghalaya into an export-oriented floriculture hub:
  * ₹7 Lakh financial subsidy package per farmer to completely cover initial installation and infrastructure costs.
  * Supports precision infrastructure setups for high-demand, high-value temperate flowers (Lilium, Anthuriums, and Orchids) backed by guaranteed state buyback arrangements.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual growers or SHGs located in cold temperate altitudinal zones (primarily East Khasi Hills and West Khasi Hills).
* **How to Apply:** Register via plain-paper application through the block Horticulture Development Officer (HDO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Possession Certificate (LPC), and soil-elevation suitability profile.

---

### [ML_SCHEME_011] Mission Jackfruit
* **Category:** Agro-Forestry & Value-Added Food Processing
* **Target Beneficiary:** Village-level nano-entrepreneurs, self-help groups, and tribal fruit gatherers across the Garo Hills and lower ridge basins.
* **Benefit Details:** Commercializes underutilized wild jackfruit trees to stop post-harvest fruit spoilage:
  * Finances the creation of 300 Nano Jackfruit Processing Enterprises at the village level through targeted startup capital grants.
  * Establishes regional Techno-Incubation Centres (TICs) providing processing machinery, vacuum packaging, and training to produce jackfruit flour, chips, and vacuum-sealed freeze-dried chunks.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Rural households and local processing collectives located in dense jackfruit catchment areas.
* **How to Apply:** Apply through the local Agriculture Development Officer (ADO) or the Directorate of Food Processing (DOFP) center.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile proof, and processing group membership record.

## Section 2: Direct Financial Support & Livestock Diversification

### [ML_SCHEME_006] FOCUS & FOCUS+ Program
* **Category:** Direct Financial Income & Collective Farming Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural farming families, marginalized agricultural households, and active Producer Groups (PGs) across Meghalaya.
* **Benefit Details:** Flagship grassroots financial empowerment framework:
  * **FOCUS Module:** Direct cash grant of ₹5,000 per member to individuals grouped into village Producer Groups (PGs) to fund collective input procurement and farm equipment.
  * **FOCUS+ Expansion:** Adds an additional ₹5,000 direct cash grant per farming household to help expand family farm assets or settle seasonal production liabilities.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Smallholders and multi-crop farmers registered under formal government-verified village Producer Groups.
* **How to Apply:** Village cluster enrollment drives managed by block development officials and Village Community Facilitators (VCFs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, active Producer Group (PG) registration code, and bank passbook.

---

### [ML_SCHEME_007] Meghalaya Piggery Development Scheme (CM-ELEVATE Module)
* **Category:** Allied Animal Husbandry & Commercial Livestock
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural youth, traditional farming families, and livestock collectives (FPOs and Cooperatives).
* **Benefit Details:** Executed under the state's flagship bank-linked *CM-ELEVATE* program:
  * 50% direct capital financial subsidy to construct modern commercial piggery farms.
  * Fully covers climate-resilient concrete shed construction, purchasing elite-breed parental stock piglets, veterinary healthcare kits, and automatic feeding troughs.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Meghalaya aged 18 to 58 years with suitable land layouts. Bank credit linkages are channeled through Meghalaya Rural Bank (MRB) and State Bank of India (SBI).
* **How to Apply:** Digital proposals and credit requests are submitted through the integrated CM-ELEVATE Portal (`cmelevate.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile certificate, Land title deed / 10-year registered lease, and Detailed Project Report (DPR).

## Section 3: Organic Conversion & Precision Polyhouse Horticulture

### [ML_SCHEME_003] Meghalaya State Organic Mission
* **Category:** Ecological Agriculture Transition
* **Target Beneficiary:** Fruit growers, spice farmers, and village cluster cooperatives shifting to 100% chemical-free organic farming.
* **Benefit Details:** Long-term transition cushion to unlock export value:
  * Direct financial incentive grant of ₹10,000 per Hectare spread across a 3-year structural transition phase to cover organic bio-fertilizers, neem cake, and bio-pesticide kits.
  * The state government completely funds the expensive international organic group certification process (PGS-India and NPOP standards) for entire village clusters.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators working on contiguous land plots within declared organic clusters who agree to undergo periodic soil chemical residue testing.
* **How to Apply:** Routed through Agriculture Extension Officers (AEOs) or registered cluster FPOs.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, village council land certificate, soil analysis baseline log, and bank passbook.

---

### [ML_SCHEME_004] Greenhouse & Polyhouse Protected Cultivation Subvention
* **Category:** High-Tech Precision Horticulture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers transitioning into year-round exotic floriculture (Orchids, Anthuriums) and high-value off-season vegetable production.
* **Benefit Details:** Protects high-value crops against torrential mountain downpours and hailstorms:
  * 50% to 75% front-ended capital investment subsidy on the fabrication and erection of modern naturally ventilated polyhouses, shade net structures, and integrated micro-drip irrigation systems.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers with verified perennial gravity springs, open wells, or borewell connections holding clear land titles.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural designs and cropping plans to the Directorate of Horticulture or local DHO.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, certified Land Title deed / long-term lease clearance, vendor structural quote, and water testing report.

## Section 4: Mountain Supply Chain, Warehousing & Fleet Logistics

### [ML_SCHEME_005] 1917 Integrated Agriculture Market Platform (1917 iAMP)
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Digital Logistics & Market Aggregation
* **Target Beneficiary:** All active farmers, livestock rearers, and FPOs looking to ship perishable produce without middleman exploitation.
* **Benefit Details:** Centralized digital market linkage network:
  * Accessible via the dedicated toll-free helpline number `1917`, allowing farmers to log harvest inventory, check daily nationwide wholesale prices, and request state-subsidized transport vehicles.
  * Dispatches specialized subsidized logistics vans (*Agri-Trucks*) to remote mountain collection points to pick up perishables and drop them at major commercial mandis.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to all resident agriculturalists and allied operators in Meghalaya.
* **How to Apply:** Direct voice dial into the `1917` toll-free network or register on `1917iamp.gov.in`.
* **Documents Required:** None for phone consults; Aadhaar Card and Farmer ID for hauling truck bookings.

---

### [ML_SCHEME_009] Meghalaya Agriculture Warehouse Scheme (CM-ELEVATE)
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Infrastructure & Market Stabilization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual progressive farmers, registered Cooperatives, and Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs).
* **Benefit Details:** Administered by the Meghalaya Basin Management Authority (MBMA) to eliminate post-harvest wastage:
  * **75% Upfront Government Subsidy:** State funds three-fourths of the overall design and construction cost to build weather-proof local crop storage warehouses.
  * The entrepreneur contributes a token 5% margin share, with the remaining 20% funded via institutional bank credit.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Applicants aged 18 to 58 years owning land or holding a registered lease valid for at least 10 years.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications submitted on `cmelevate.in` or the unified myScheme portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Title deeds or 10-year registered Lease Agreement, and bank credit clearance letter.

---

### [ML_SCHEME_010] Meghalaya Agriculture Response Vehicle Scheme (MARVS)
* **Category:** Farm-to-Market Supply Chain Logistics
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural youth entrepreneurs, village logistics cooperatives, and smallholder transport groups.
* **Benefit Details:** Funds commercial mountain cargo fleets:
  * Upfront 50% financial subsidy and capital grant to buy small commercial pickup trucks, utility transport vehicles, or refrigerated crop vans.
  * Bank interest subvention on EMIs to reduce loan repayment pressure on rural transport operators.
  * Vehicles are integrated with `1917 iAMP` GPS dispatch systems to match local farm pickup requests automatically.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Unemployed rural youth or registered agrarian cooperatives based in remote unconnected blocks.
* **How to Apply:** Submitted online through the Department of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Commercial Driving License (for individuals), cooperative registration certificate, and bank loan sanction letter.

## Section 5: Ground Application Workflows & Meghalaya Lexicon

| Regional Term | Operational Context & Significance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Doloi / Sirdar / Nokma** | Traditional community village chieftains issuing certified Land Possession Certificates (LPC) in the absence of formal revenue deeds. |
| **1917 iAMP** | State flagship toll-free digital logistics network connecting remote mountain farmers to buyers and subsidized Agri-Trucks. |
| **FOCUS / FOCUS+** | Flagship cash transfer scheme providing ₹5,000/member in Producer Groups + ₹5,000/family for agricultural investments. |
| **CM-ELEVATE** | Umbrella entrepreneurship portal (`cmelevate.in`) offering 50%–75% subsidies for piggery, warehouses, and logistics vehicles. |
| **IVCS / FPO** | Integrated Village Cooperative Societies and Farmer Producer Organizations acting as grassroots credit and input distribution hubs. |
| **MBMA** | Meghalaya Basin Management Authority, administering rural infrastructure, warehouses, and value chains. |
| **PGS / NPOP** | Participatory Guarantee System and National Programme for Organic Production certification funded 100% by the State Organic Mission. |
