## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Andhra Pradesh's agricultural framework is internationally recognized for grassroots digital decentralization through **Rythu Bharosa Kendras (RBKs)** and sustainable agro-ecology:
1. **Consolidated Direct Benefit Transfer & Tenant Inclusivity:** Unified cash support of ₹13,500 per family per year (*YSR Rythu Bharosa* across three seasonal phases: ₹7,500 Kharif, ₹4,000 Rabi, ₹2,000 Sankranti). Uniquely covers landless tenant cultivators holding a Crop Cultivator Rights Card (CCRC) with 100% state funding.
2. **Decentralized RBK Touchscreen Infrastructure:** Over 10,700 village RBK kiosks equipped with digital ordering screens for subsidized certified inputs delivered within 48–72 hours, integrated with free constituency soil/pesticide testing labs.
3. **Zero-Premium Risk Mitigation & Climate Agro-Ecology:** Automatic ₹1 token crop insurance enrollment via seasonal *e-Crop* digital registry entries, alongside the state-wide *APCNF* natural farming mission (providing ₹8,000/acre grants and certified premium market channels).

## Section 1: Consolidated Income Support & Zero-Interest Credit

### [AP_SCHEME_001] YSR Rythu Bharosa
* **Category:** Consolidated Income & Input Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landowning farmer families, alongside landless Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), Backward Class (BC), and Minority tenant cultivators across Andhra Pradesh.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct financial income support of ₹13,500 per family per year:
  * **Phase 1 (May):** ₹7,500 disbursed directly ahead of the Kharif sowing cycle (includes PM-KISAN matching tranche).
  * **Phase 2 (October):** ₹4,000 transferred during crop harvesting and Rabi field preparation.
  * **Phase 3 (January):** ₹2,000 disbursed around the Sankranti festival.
  * **Tenant Inclusivity:** Landless tenant farmers from SC, ST, BC, and Minority communities receive the full ₹13,500 annual grant funded 100% from state resources.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Landholders must have acreage verified on the official AP Webland Digital Portal. Tenant farmers must hold a valid Crop Cultivator Rights Card (CCRC) with an active lease of at least 11 months.
* **How to Apply:** Handled at village Rythu Bharosa Kendras (RBKs). Village Agriculture Assistants (VAAs) upload physical verification logs to `ysrrythubharosa.ap.gov.in`.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card (linked to mobile), Pattadar Passbook / Webland digital printout, CCRC Card signed jointly by tenant and landowner (for tenants), and an Aadhaar-seeded bank passbook.

---

### [AP_SCHEME_003] YSR Sunna Vaddi Panta Runalu (Zero-Interest Crop Loans)
* **Category:** Institutional Agricultural Credit & Interest Subvention
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal farmers, alongside tenant cultivators securing short-term seasonal crop borrowings.
* **Benefit Details:** 100% interest subvention making short-term crop loans completely interest-free (0% interest):
  * The state government directly reimburses the 4% annual interest charge on commercial and cooperative bank borrowings up to a maximum credit limit of ₹1 Lakh.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** The crop loan must be fully repaid within the stipulated 12-month tenure. Overdue accounts or loan defaulters revert to standard commercial rates.
* **How to Apply:** Crop financing is processed through commercial banks, District Cooperative Central Banks (DCCBs), or PACS linked to RBK farmer credentials.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, KCC Bank Account details, CCRC Tenant Card (if applicable), and Webland land records.

## Section 2: 1-Rupee Crop Insurance, RBK Kiosks & Soil Testing Labs

### [AP_SCHEME_002] YSR Free Crop Insurance (YSR Uchita Fasal Bima Yojana)
* **Category:** Agricultural Risk Mitigation & Insurance
* **Target Beneficiary:** All farmers cultivating notified crops in notified areas across Andhra Pradesh (both landowners and tenant cultivators).
* **Benefit Details:** Complete state takeover of farmer insurance premiums:
  * Farmers pay only a token fee of ₹1, while the state government absorbs the entire remaining premium liability.
  * Claims are settled transparently based on automated satellite remote sensing, weather indices, and Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Mandatory crop acreage registration on the state's digital *e-Crop (e-Pekata)* registry before seasonal cut-off dates.
* **How to Apply:** Auto-enrolled dynamically upon completing the seasonal e-Crop entry at the local Rythu Bharosa Kendra (RBK).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, e-Crop digital confirmation slip number, and NPCI-seeded bank passbook.

---

### [AP_SCHEME_004] YSR Rythu Bharosa Kendras (RBK Input Subsidy Model)
* **Category:** Input Quality & Decentralized Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Every active cultivator requiring certified seeds, fertilizers, chemical inputs, and testing gear.
* **Benefit Details:** One-stop rural input and advisory network spanning over 10,700 villages:
  * Interactive digital touchscreens allow farmers to order certified foundation seeds, bio-fertilizers, and chemical pesticides at government-subsidized rates.
  * Inputs are delivered directly to the village RBK within 48 to 72 hours with a state quality guarantee stamp.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to all operational cultivators with a digital Farmer ID in Andhra Pradesh.
* **How to Apply:** Visit the village RBK kiosk, complete biometric/facial authentication, and order inputs via the digital inventory terminal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card and unique e-Crop ID / Farmer Registration Number.

---

### [AP_SCHEME_006] Dr. YSR Agri Testing Labs Scheme
* **Category:** Modernization & Quality Control
* **Target Beneficiary:** Active cultivators and local agri-input dealers.
* **Benefit Details:** Free chemical and physical testing services across 147 constituency-level and 13 district-level integrated laboratories:
  * Farmers drop off soil samples, irrigation water samples, fertilizers, or pesticides to check nutrient profiles and detect adulterated chemical products.
  * Digital soil health cards and test reports are sent via SMS to the farmer's mobile within 7 business days.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to all farmers in Andhra Pradesh prior to input application or sowing.
* **How to Apply:** Hand over samples to the Village Agriculture Assistant (VAA) at the local RBK.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card and sample tracking voucher generated at the RBK desk.

## Section 3: Free Irrigation Borewells, Farm Ponds & Agroforestry

### [AP_SCHEME_005] YSR Jala Kala (Free Borewells Scheme)
* **Category:** Irrigation & Asset Creation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal farmers operating in groundwater-accessible arid belts without existing irrigation access.
* **Benefit Details:** 100% state-funded drilling and installation of agricultural borewells:
  * Covers scientific hydrogeological surveys, drilling, casing pipes, and the supply/installation of an electric submersible motor pump set completely free of charge.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small/marginal farmers owning a contiguous land parcel of at least 2.5 Acres (adjacent smallholders can form a joint cluster to hit the 2.5-acre minimum). Hydrogeologist survey must confirm high groundwater feasibility.
* **How to Apply:** Submitted online via `ysrjalakala.ap.gov.in` or through the village volunteer and Grama Sachivalayam network.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Pattadar Passbook, clear Title Deeds, and Small/Marginal Farmer Certificate issued by the Mandal Revenue Officer (MRO).

---

### [AP_SCHEME_009] YSR Rainwater Harvesting & Farm Pond Bund Agroforestry Project
* **Category:** Irrigation & Water Conservation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholders in drought-prone rainfed belts (primarily Rayalaseema and dry upland coastal mandals).
* **Benefit Details:** Full state funding to excavate 1.55 Lakh on-farm rainwater harvesting ponds:
  * State absorbs all heavy earthmoving machinery excavation costs under MGNREGS/CAMPA convergence.
  * Free distribution of high-yield fruit-bearing and medicinal tree saplings (Lemon, Guava, Pomegranate, Banana) to plant on pond bunds, creating a secondary multi-tier agroforestry income stream.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland lacking existing borewell or river lift irrigation access.
* **How to Apply:** Coordinated through Grama Sachivalayam MGNREGS desks and Village Revenue Officers (VROs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land tax receipts, and active MGNREGA Job Card.

## Section 4: Community Natural Farming & Women Livelihood Diversification

### [AP_SCHEME_008] Andhra Pradesh Community Managed Natural Farming (APCNF Value Chain)
* **Category:** Sustainable Agriculture & Climate-Resilient Production
* **Target Beneficiary:** Cultivators transitioning completely from synthetic chemicals to 100% organic and natural biological inputs.
* **Benefit Details:** Backed by the global Food Planet Prize framework:
  * Direct cash incentive grant of ₹8,000 per acre distributed over 3 years to construct on-farm bio-input preparation tanks (*Jeevamrutham*).
  * Specialized state organic brand certification enabling farmers to sell produce at a 20% to 30% price premium.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators dedicating 0.5 to 5 Acres to chemical-free natural farming, validated via surprise micro-soil analysis checks at RBKs.
* **How to Apply:** Enrolled through Village Natural Farming Cluster Coordinators or at the local RBK kiosk.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Farmer ID, and baseline soil health card verifying zero synthetic chemical residues.

---

### [AP_SCHEME_007] YSR Cheyutha (Agri-Allied Livelihood Expansion Component)
* **Category:** Allied Sector & Livelihood Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Women belonging to SC, ST, BC, and Minority communities aged 45 to 60 years expanding into dairy, sheep/goat rearing, or poultry.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct financial assistance of ₹75,000 distributed over 4 years (₹18,750 per year):
  * State provides market linkage partnerships (with Amul, Reliance, etc.) to set up small commercial dairy units, bulk milk chilling points, and mini-retail outlets.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Women aged 45–60 years from SC/ST/BC/Minority communities holding a valid BPL White Ration Card.
* **How to Apply:** Verified by Grama Sachivalayam (Village Secretariat) staff and processed online through the state welfare portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Integrated Community/Caste Certificate, Age Proof (Voter ID/Birth Certificate), and bank passbook.
