## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Telangana's agricultural governance framework addresses the agrarian conditions of the semi-arid Deccan plateau, combining extensive surface lift irrigation projects (Kaleshwaram, Sitarama) with aggressive direct investment support and risk insulation:
1. **Direct Income Injection & Full Debt Relief:** Large-scale financial deployment via *Rythu Bharosa* (₹12,000/acre/year in two equal tranches) and *Rythu Runa Mafi* (clearing up to ₹2 Lakh of outstanding institutional crop loans per farming family directly from the State Treasury).
2. **Comprehensive Social Security & 24/7 Free Utility:** 100% government-funded *Rythu Bima* group life insurance (partnered with LIC, providing ₹5 Lakh within 10 days of a farmer's death regardless of cause), paired with uninterrupted, round-the-clock free electrical power for all agricultural borewells.
3. **Targeted Micro-Irrigation & Production Incentives:** Up to 100% capital subsidies for drip and sprinkler systems via *TGMIP*, ₹500/quintal cash bonus above MSP for *Sanna Biyyam* fine rice, 75% subsidies for sheep units (Yadava/Kuruma pastoralists), and village *Rythu Vedika* agricultural extension platforms.

## Section 1: Flagship Investment Support, Loan Waivers & Life Risk Insulation

### [TG_SCHEME_001] Rythu Bharosa Scheme
* **Category:** Direct Agri-Investment Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual landowning farmers and registered cultivators across all 33 districts of Telangana.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct investment assistance to fund farm inputs and land preparation ahead of sowing:
  * Delivers a direct financial cash transfer of ₹12,000 per acre per year.
  * Disbursed systematically via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) in two equal seasonal installments of ₹6,000 each for the Kharif and Rabi cycles.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small, marginal, and large landholding farmers with clear, updated land titles recorded on the state's central land registry.
* **How to Apply:** Automated digital linkage occurs through the state land management database. New registrations, parcel additions, or bank detail corrections are routed through the local Mandal Agriculture Officer (MAO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Pattadar Passbook, linked mobile number, and Aadhaar-seeded bank passbook copy.

---

### [TG_SCHEME_002] Rythu Runa Mafi (Crop Loan Waiver)
* **Category:** Institutional Agricultural Debt Relief
* **Target Beneficiary:** Distressed farming families holding outstanding short-term agricultural crop loans.
* **Benefit Details:** Complete institutional debt clearing mechanism:
  * Waives formal institutional crop loans up to a maximum financial limit of ₹2 Lakh per farming family unit.
  * Direct clearings are disbursed from the State Treasury straight into empanelled commercial banks, Regional Rural Banks (APGVB / TGB), and District Cooperative Central Banks (DCCBs) to restore institutional credit eligibility.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Crop loans must be issued by institutional banks within Telangana and mapped under a single family-unit ration card log.
* **How to Apply:** Eligible profiles are auto-pulled via banking data matching. Grievance applications for missing accounts can be submitted at local MeeSeva centers or through the Mandal Agriculture Office.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Pattadar Passbook, Loan Account Statement / KCC details, and Family Ration Card.

---

### [TG_SCHEME_003] Rythu Bima Scheme (Farmers' Group Life Insurance)
* **Category:** Social Security & Life Risk Insurance
* **Target Beneficiary:** Registered landholding farmers to insulate families from catastrophic economic distress following the death of the primary breadwinner.
* **Benefit Details:** 100% state-funded group life insurance model partnered with the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC):
  * Provides a fixed, fast-tracked financial payout of ₹5 Lakh directly into the designated nominee's bank account.
  * Settled within 10 business days of the farmer's death, irrespective of whether the cause was natural or accidental.
  * The state government bears the entire annual insurance premium on behalf of the farmer.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active registered landholders aged between 18 and 59 years.
* **How to Apply:** Handled at the village level by submitting physical nomination and consent forms to the local Agriculture Extension Officer (AEO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card of the farmer, Age Proof document (Voter ID / Birth Certificate), Nominee’s Aadhaar Card, and Nominee’s Bank Details.

## Section 2: Production Incentives, Free 24/7 Power & Credit Subvention

### [TG_SCHEME_004] Fine Paddy Crop Incentive Bonus (Sanna Biyyam Bonus)
* **Category:** Output Crop Pricing Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Paddy farmers cultivating fine-grain rice varieties (*Sanna Biyyam*) across Telangana.
* **Benefit Details:** Production-boosting fiscal bonus to incentivize quality cereal cultivation:
  * Delivers an additional cash incentive bonus of ₹500 per quintal over and above the government-notified Minimum Support Price (MSP).
  * Disbursed directly via DBT into farmers' bank accounts upon selling their fine paddy yields at state-authorized Agricultural Market Committee (AMC) procurement centers or Paddy Procurement Centres (PPCs).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers cultivating verified fine rice varieties certified during seasonal crop booking inspections by the local AEO.
* **How to Apply:** Automatically calculated and processed upon weighing and logging produce at government grain procurement PPC scales.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Pattadar Passbook, Unified Crop Booking Certificate, and AMC Sale Receipt (J-Form equivalent).

---

### [TG_SCHEME_005] 24/7 Free Agricultural Power Supply
* **Category:** Essential Input Utility Subsidy
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers operating electric agricultural borewells and pump sets across rural belts.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive energy infrastructure support:
  * 100% free, high-quality, uninterrupted 24-hour agricultural power supply across dedicated rural electricity feeders.
  * Eradicates daytime power rationing, eliminating hazardous nighttime irrigation duties for farmers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Valid, registered agricultural pump set connections managed under state power distribution companies (TSSPDCL / TSNPDCL).
* **How to Apply:** Automatic deployment across dedicated agricultural feeder networks; no separate billing or registration is required once an agricultural connection is sanctioned.
* **Documents Required:** Electricity Consumer Number and Land Ownership documents (only required for fresh connection applications).

---

### [TG_SCHEME_008] Vaddi Leni Runalu & Pavala Vaddi Scheme
* **Category:** Institutional Low-Interest Crop Credit
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal farmers maintaining active crop credit accounts with prompt repayment discipline.
* **Benefit Details:** Tiered interest subvention framework:
  * **Vaddi Leni Runalu (0% Interest):** 100% interest waiver (effective 0% interest rate) on seasonal crop loans up to ₹1 Lakh, provided the principal is repaid within the 12-month due date.
  * **Pavala Vaddi (3% Concessional Interest):** Concessional 3% annual interest rate (*Pavala interest*) for crop loans ranging between ₹1 Lakh and ₹3 Lakh.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Crop loan accounts linked to a valid Kisan Credit Card (KCC) showing clean, on-time repayment records.
* **How to Apply:** Administered automatically through the lending institutional bank branch at the time of loan renewal or seasonal repayment.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, KCC Token Card, Bank Loan Clearance Statement, and Pattadar Passbook.

## Section 3: Water Conservation, Micro-Irrigation & Polyhouses

### [TG_SCHEME_006] Telangana Micro Irrigation Project (TGMIP)
* **Category:** Water Resource Preservation & Precision Agriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and general category farmers constructing drip or micro-sprinkler irrigation systems.
* **Benefit Details:** High capital subvention scaling up to a ceiling of 12.5 Acres per farming household:
  * **100% Full Subsidy:** For Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) farmers.
  * **90% Subsidy:** For Backward Classes (BC) and all small/marginal farmers.
  * **80% Subsidy:** For general category landholders.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers holding clear title deeds and verified water sources (wells/borewells) across all 32 eligible rural districts.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications submitted via the TGMIP portal (`tgmip.telangana.gov.in`) or routed through the District Horticulture Officer (DHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Pattadar Passbook, Borewell Water Feasibility Certificate, caste certificate (for SC/ST/BC), and equipment layout plan from empanelled vendors.

---

### [TG_SCHEME_007] Polyhouse & Protected Greenhouse Cultivation Project
* **Category:** High-Value Controlled Environment Horticulture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers transitioning into climate-controlled commercial floriculture (roses, gerberas, carnations) and exotic vegetable cropping (capsicum, cherry tomatoes).
* **Benefit Details:** Capital infrastructure financing:
  * Up to 75% capital financial subsidy on the total project cost to construct naturally ventilated polyhouses, shade nets, and high-tech greenhouse structures.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Landowners possessing dependable perennial water sources and uninterrupted electricity connections. Preference is given to clusters located within peri-urban transport corridors.
* **How to Apply:** Submit a Detailed Project Report (DPR) through the State Horticulture Department portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile Proof, soil and water chemical analysis reports, manufacturer structure blueprints, and Land Revenue extracts.

---

## Section 4: Allied Pastoral Livestock, Rythu Vedika & Seed Security

### [TG_SCHEME_009] Telangana Subsidized Sheep Distribution Scheme
* **Category:** Allied Sector & Animal Husbandry Enterprise
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional shepherd communities (Yadava and Kuruma families) aged 18 years and above.
* **Benefit Details:** Livestock asset distribution to boost rural meat and wool production:
  * Provides a complete starter livestock unit of 20 healthy ewes and 1 purebred breeding ram.
  * **75% Government Subsidy:** The state funds three-fourths (75%) of the unit cost; the beneficiary contributes only a 25% token margin share upfront.
  * Includes integrated animal health insurance coverage and free subsidized pasture fodder seed minikits.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered members of local Primary Sheep Breeders Cooperative Societies (PSBCS).
* **How to Apply:** Offline registration vetted by a joint block committee comprising the Mandal Revenue Officer (MRO), Mandal Development Officer (MDO), and an authorized Government Veterinary Doctor.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Caste Certificate, PSBCS Cooperative Membership ID, and bank passbook.

---

### [TG_SCHEME_010] Telangana Rythu Vedika Project
* **Category:** Agricultural Extension & Grassroots Platforming
* **Target Beneficiary:** All regional farmers requiring institutional knowledge, market intelligence, weather updates, and extension interactions.
* **Benefit Details:** State-wide agricultural infrastructure platforming:
  * Built dedicated Farmer Service Centres (*Rythu Vedikas*) across village clusters to bring farming communities under one institutional roof.
  * Serves as a central venue for organizing technical extension seminars, farmer training programs, coordinating MSP grain procurement drives, conducting soil testing campaigns, and streamlining subsidized input distributions.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open universal access to all resident cultivators and landholders in Telangana.
* **How to Apply:** No application required for physical walk-in access. Farmers participate in regular cluster meetings organized by their assigned Agriculture Extension Officer (AEO).
* **Documents Required:** None for baseline entry; Pattadar Passbook required for specialized testing or input allotment.

---

### [TG_SCHEME_011] Subsidized Seed Sourcing & Seed Chain Strengthening
* **Category:** Basic Sowing Input Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders setting up early seasonal sowing operations.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidizes foundational input expenditures:
  * Delivers certified high-yielding varieties (HYV) and foundation seeds (Paddy, Groundnut, Redgram/Pulses, Soyabean, Cotton) at 30% to 50% subsidized rates.
  * Distributed through authorized state channels to eliminate counterfeit or sub-standard commercial seeds.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small and marginal farmers with active agricultural plots registered on state records.
* **How to Apply:** Distributed prior to Kharif and Rabi sowing windows through regional Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies (PACS) or Agro Rythu Seva Kendras.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Pattadar Passbook, and active Crop Booking log receipt.
