## Executive Summary & Territory Operational Context

Ladakh's agricultural framework is defined by extreme high-altitude cold-desert realities: sub-zero winter temperatures reaching -20°C to -35°C, intense solar radiation, hyper-arid precipitation, and an ultra-short 4-to-5 month summer cropping window:
1. **Carbon-Neutral Green Winter Production (LGHP):** Deploying DIHAR-engineered passive solar greenhouses (mud-brick and double-walled polycarbonate) that trap high-altitude solar thermal radiation to maintain internal temperatures above 10°C during severe winter freezes, guaranteeing fresh green vegetable security year-round.
2. **Organic Value Chains & Native Mountain Fruits (MODI):** Driving a complete conversion to 100% certified organic status under the *Mission Organic Development Initiative (MODI)*, financing international PGS-India/NPOP certifications, commercializing wild *Seabuckthorn* with mobile pulping extraction units, and modernizing GI *Raktsey Karpo* sweet apricots with ₹35,000 multi-tier solar drying tunnels.
3. **Glacial Water Engineering & Nomadic Pashmina Insulation:** Lifting glacial meltwater from deep valley river beds (Indus, Suru, Nubra) up multi-tiered terrace steps via 75% to 85% subsidized off-grid solar lift pumps, paired with 75% winter-insulated biosecure shelters protecting nomadic *Changpa* pastoralists and their elite Pashmina goat herds across the Changthang plateau.

## Section 1: Flagship High-Altitude Support, Organic Value Chains & Wonder Crops

### [LK_SCHEME_001] Mission Organic Development Initiative (MODI Ladakh)
* **Category:** Ecological Agriculture Transition & Quality Control
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual progressive farmers, village crop clusters, and registered Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) across Leh and Kargil.
* **Benefit Details:** Multi-phase regional conversion program positioning Ladakh as a premier certified organic territory:
  * Fully state-funds international organic group certification (PGS-India and NPOP mapping) to capture luxury domestic and global export corridors.
  * Delivers a direct input incentive grant of ₹10,000 per Hectare (spread across a 3-year transition cycle) via DBT for certified bio-fertilizers and bio-pesticides.
  * Disburses a 75% financial subsidy on multi-crop value-addition machinery (Seabuckthorn berry pulping machines and apricot grading lines).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active farming clusters inside state-notified organic blocks undergoing mandatory soil chemical residue testing.
* **How to Apply:** Handled at the cluster level via regional Agriculture Extension Officers (AEOs) or by registering through local FPO channels.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Ladakh Resident Certificate, Land possession Passbook copy, and field tracking diary.

---

### [LK_SCHEME_007] High-Altitude Seabuckthorn & Wild Berry Expansion Scheme
* **Category:** Agroforestry Value-Chain Expansion & Alternative Cash Crops
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder farming groups, women-led self-help groups (SHGs), and youth cooperatives across Leh and Kargil.
* **Benefit Details:** Commercializes Ladakh's native "Wonder Berry" while stabilizing fragile riverbank soils against erosion:
  * 75% to 85% administration subsidy for commercial cultivation of Seabuckthorn (*Hippophae rhamnoides*).
  * Supplies high-yielding thornless cultivars, solar-powered mechanical harvesting prongs, and puncture-resistant safety gloves.
  * Links local village clusters with mobile primary processing extraction vans to extract raw berry pulp right at the farm gate, preventing rapid vitamin C degradation.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active landholders or registered village collectives with approved land allocations along the Indus, Nubra, or Suru river basins.
* **How to Apply:** Applications open during early spring booking windows through regional Zonal Agriculture Offices (ZAOs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Ladakh Resident Certificate, Land Patta revenue passbook, and village council boundary validation note.

---

### [LK_SCHEME_008] Raktsey Karpo Premium Apricot Orchard Rejuvenation
* **Category:** Specialty Horticultural Commercialization & Income Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional fruit growers, smallholders, and multi-crop farming blocks maintaining ancestral apricot groves.
* **Benefit Details:** Modernizes Ladakh's signature geographic indicator (GI) sweet white-kernel apricot (*Raktsey Karpo*):
  * Distributes certified, disease-resistant grafted Apricot saplings at a 75% direct state-funded financial subsidy.
  * Grants a direct capital subvention of up to ₹35,000 per farming family to install Modern Solar Multi-Tier Fruit Dryers. This replaces unhygienic open-ground drying, preserving fruit color, moisture, and gourmet export grade.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators owning or holding legally recorded land deeds or village-approved long-term cultivation leases.
* **How to Apply:** Route application packets through block-level Agriculture Extension Centres (AECs) or directly to the Directorate of Horticulture.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Resident Certificate, updated Land Revenue Records (RoR), and certified nursery invoices.

## Section 2: High-Altitude Greenhouses & Cold-Desert Water Engineering

### [LK_SCHEME_002] The Ladakh Green Greenhouse Project (LGHP)
* **Category:** High-Tech Precision Winter Horticulture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholders, vegetable growers, and women-led neighborhood self-help groups (SHGs).
* **Benefit Details:** Climate weather engineering developed in collaboration with the Defense Institute of High Altitude Research (DIHAR):
  * 75% capital investment subsidy to construct advanced Passive Solar Greenhouses (heavy mud-brick walls on three sides, reinforced double-layer polycarbonate glazing facing south).
  * Traps intense daytime high-altitude solar radiation, keeping internal air temperatures above 10°C even when outside ambient winter temperatures plunge below -20°C.
  * Enables off-season winter production of fresh spinach, coriander, mint, and brassicas without fossil-fuel heating.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers possessing valid land titles with a dedicated micro-drip irrigation system installed on the plot.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural design and agronomic proposals directly to the District Horticulture Officer (DHO) in Leh or Kargil.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Resident Certificate, certified land title passbook, and vendor structural design quotes.

---

### [LK_SCHEME_003] PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop (Ladakh Cold-Desert Module)
* **Category:** Precision Micro-Irrigation & Glacial Water Harvesting
* **Target Beneficiary:** Hilly terrace vegetable growers, fruit orchardists, and alfalfa forage planters experiencing acute cold-desert water scarcity.
* **Benefit Details:** Captures seasonal glacial meltwater before it drains into deep river gorges:
  * 90% capital financial subsidy to construct permanent rainwater and glacial meltwater storage ponds (*Zings* / Farm Ponds).
  * Fully covers the installation of frost-resistant High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) water delivery tubes, micro-sprinklers, and precision drip-irrigation networks.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small and marginal hill farmers holding valid land revenue documents with verified catchment layouts.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically at your regional District Agriculture Office (DAO) or via block agriculture extension agents.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, land mutation proof, sketch design blueprint of the micro-irrigation grid, and water source certificate.

---

### [LK_SCHEME_004] Solar Lift Irrigation Scheme for Carbon-Neutral Farms
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Mountain Water Resource Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** Isolated river-valley smallholders and terrace crop clusters lacking dependable grid power.
* **Benefit Details:** Overcomes the mountain barrier of accessing glacier rivers (Indus, Suru, Nubra) located hundreds of meters below agricultural terraces:
  * 75% to 85% capital investment subsidy to install high-powered standalone solar water pumping units paired with heavy-duty river lift piping.
  * Ensures zero-carbon, reliable daytime irrigation without diesel generator costs, directly supporting the Carbon-Neutral Ladakh roadmap.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland with a verified surface water source below the plot and verified land usage rights.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications open during scheduled token booking windows on the UT Renewable Energy Node Portal (`leh.nic.in` / `kargil.nic.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID code, land mutation records, and technical water lift elevation feasibility report.

## Section 3: Slope Mechanization, Post-Harvest Cold Chains & Nomadic Pashmina Livestock

### [LK_SCHEME_005] Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM Ladakh)
* **Category:** Specialized Mountain Mechanisation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual smallholders, youth collectives, and registered agricultural cooperative societies.
* **Benefit Details:** Reduces severe winter cropping labor bottlenecks:
  * 50% to 80% capital investment subsidy specifically on lightweight, low-gravity farm machinery engineered for steep step terrace traction.
  * Equipment distributed: mini mountain power tillers, hand-held hill weeders, portable alfalfa/fodder threshers, and automated fruit pickers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual hill cultivators or registered rural collectives possessing land usage clearances certified by local revenue authorities.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online via the national [AgriMachinery Portal](https://agrimachinery.nic.in/) by selecting the Ladakh UT node.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID code, proforma invoice from an empanelled machinery manufacturer, and land mutation passbook extracts.

---

### [LK_SCHEME_006] High-Altitude Livestock and Pashmina Breed Development Initiative
* **Category:** Allied Sector Livestock & Nomadic Income Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Nomadic pastoralists, *Changpa* tribal collectives, and backyard livestock rearers in high-altitude zones like Changthang (elevations above 4,000m).
* **Benefit Details:** Protects nomadic families from extreme winter blizzard mortality while maximizing premium Pashmina fiber yields:
  * 75% direct capital investment subsidy to construct biosecure, winter-insulated animal pens to shelter kids and lambs from heavy blizzards.
  * Supplies elite Chegu/Changthangi dual-purpose breeding bucks, tracking ear-tags, solar-powered shearing kits, and subsidized high-protein feed supplements during migratory months.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident livestock owners holding valid Aadhaar-linked accounts registered under recognized nomadic herder associations.
* **How to Apply:** Apply directly to the regional Chief Veterinary Officer (CVO) or during scheduled block outreach camps.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Scheduled Tribe (ST) Certificate, animal health tracking tag certificate, and bank passbook.

---

### [LK_SCHEME_009] Cold-Desert Solar-Powered Micro-Cold Storage Infrastructure
* **Category:** Supply Chain Logistical Security & Post-Harvest Assets
* **Target Beneficiary:** Registered Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), village cooperative fruit unions, and high-altitude agro-entrepreneurs.
* **Benefit Details:** Mitigates highway closures and winter transport bottlenecks:
  * 75% upfront capital investment grant to construct solar-powered, off-grid micro-cold storage rooms (5 MT to 15 MT capacities) near rural production centers.
  * Utilizes high-density insulation and integrated thermal bank storage to keep indoor temperatures stable without reliance on erratic grid power.
  * Connects processing clusters directly to regional airports (Leh/Kargil) for air-cargo transport of fresh organic produce to mainland metropolitan markets.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered collective groups or individuals owning land or holding a minimum 10-year registered lease deed.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online via the unified UT Single Window Clearance System or at the Directorate of Horticulture.
* **Documents Required:** FPO Group Registration Certificate, Land Title deeds / 10-year Lease Agreement, bank credit clearance logs, and detailed engineering DPR.

---

### [LK_SCHEME_010] Rainbow Trout Coldwater Aquaculture Raceway Scheme
* **Category:** Allied Sector Fisheries & Mountain Income Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual youth entrepreneurs, traditional fishermen, and hill families owning land near cold perennial glacial streams.
* **Benefit Details:** Multi-sector diversification scaling premium coldwater aquaculture:
  * 60% capital financial subsidy to construct permanent concrete *Raceways* designed specifically for high-value Coldwater Rainbow Trout breeding.
  * Supplies elite trout ova/fingerlings, automated oxygen-infusion setups, and high-protein specialized fish feed kits.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Ladakh aged 18 to 45 years with access to a clean perennial water source (glacial streams or gravity channels) where water temperatures remain strictly below 18°C year-round.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural site proposals directly to the regional District Fisheries Officer (DFO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Resident Certificate copy, land revenue sheets proving water-body layout permissions, and bank passbook.
