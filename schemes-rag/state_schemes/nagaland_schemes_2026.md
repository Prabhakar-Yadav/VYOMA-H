## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Nagaland's agricultural policy is structured around the unique terrain of the Naga Hills, customary community land tenure systems (governed under Article 371A), and the strategic transition from traditional slash-and-burn shifting cultivation (*Jhum*) toward permanent, high-value organic farming:
1. **The Nagaland Agriculture Policy 2025 & Organic Conversion:** Accelerating certified chemical-free agriculture through the *FOCUS Scheme* (providing direct input grants of ₹10,000 plus 100% free Participatory Guarantee System certification) and the *MOVCD-NER* corridor (allocating ₹25,000/ha for organic seed sourcing and cold-chain packhouses).
2. **Grassroots Credit & MSME Deployment (CMMFI):** The *Chief Minister's Micro Finance Initiative (CMMFI)* provides a 30% direct government subsidy, 60% bank loan, and 10% entrepreneur equity for agri-allied enterprises (cold storage, eco-hatcheries, and cargo fleets) capped up to ₹15 Lakh.
3. **High-Value GI Cash Crops & Allied Missions:** Scaling the global commercial footprint of GI-tagged *Raja Mircha* (Naga King Chili) and *Dendrobium Orchids*, modernizing traditional mountain beekeeping (*NBHM*), and deploying international biosecure breeding lines via the *Indo-French Cooperi Piggery Alliance*.

## Section 1: Ecological Farming, Organic Transition & Direct Grants

### [NL_SCHEME_001] Nagaland FOCUS Scheme (Farmers Organic Cultivation Upliftment Scheme)
* **Category:** Ecological Agriculture Transition & Direct Input Assistance
* **Target Beneficiary:** Registered landholders and active organic cultivators with an annual household income below ₹3 Lakh.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial and structural cushioning for chemical-free farming:
  * Direct cash assistance grant of ₹10,000 per farmer.
  * Earmarked to offset the cost of procuring certified organic bio-fertilizers, vermicomposting beds, and bio-control pest management kits.
  * Completely funds village group certification fees under the Participatory Guarantee System (PGS-India).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active residents of Nagaland committing to verified chemical-free agricultural practices with zero formal bank loan defaults.
* **How to Apply:** Handled digitally through the Department of Agriculture Nagaland Portal (`agriculture.nagaland.gov.in`) or via physical validation files checked by local Agriculture Extension Officers (AEO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile Proof, unique Farmer ID, Land verification sketch approved by the Village Council, and bank passbook.

---

### [NL_SCHEME_007] Nagaland Organic Value Chain Development (MOVCD-NER Module)
* **Category:** Regional Post-Harvest Value Addition & Global Market Linkage
* **Target Beneficiary:** Contiguous village crop clusters and registered Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs) cultivating high-value organic specialty items.
* **Benefit Details:** Part of the flagship Mission Organic Value Chain Development for North Eastern Region:
  * Financial allocation of ₹25,000 per Hectare across a 3-year cycle to fund organic seed sourcing, continuous field diagnostic monitoring, and international export certification.
  * Funds up to 75% capital grants to establish regional collection centers, solar crop drying racks, grading/sorting pack-houses, and high-speed cold chains to prevent harvest spoilage (specializing in Ginger, Turmeric, Pineapple, and Large Cardamom).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered FPCs operating inside designated geographical priority grids.
* **How to Apply:** Coordinated by the State Nodal Agency under the Directorate of Agriculture. FPCs file cluster project maps directly.
* **Documents Required:** FPO/FPC Registration Certificate, land contour cluster sketch signed by tribal village authorities, and baseline soil analysis logs.

## Section 2: Hill Mechanization, Micro-Irrigation & High-Value Horticulture

### [NL_SCHEME_003] Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM Nagaland)
* **Category:** Precision Farm Mechanization Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder farming groups, cooperative rice cultivation clubs, and small and marginal landholders.
* **Benefit Details:** Alleviates difficult hill-terrain labor constraints by providing customized mechanized machinery:
  * 40% to 50% capital investment subsidy for individual farmers (increasing up to 80% for registered cooperative clusters) on critical equipment.
  * Machinery specifically suited for mountain terraces: narrow-track power tillers, mechanized brush cutters, hill-climbing weeders, and localized crop harvesters.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual farmers or village collectives holding recognized community lease plots or certified land titles.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications and dealer invoices are processed online via the central [AgriMachinery Portal](https://agrimachinery.nic.in/) selecting the Nagaland state nodal office.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Farmer Registration Code, proforma price quote from an empanelled machinery dealer, and Village Council verification log.

---

### [NL_SCHEME_004] PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop (Nagaland Module)
* **Category:** Micro-Irrigation & Water Resource Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** Hill-terrace step cultivators, vegetable farmers, and commercial fruit orchard groups.
* **Benefit Details:** Essential winter water storage and conveyance support:
  * 90% capital financial subsidy to construct on-farm water harvesting and precision irrigation networks.
  * Covers the cost of installing gravity-fed drip-irrigation networks, micro-sprinklers, high-density polyethylene (HDPE) water delivery tubes, and secondary plastic-lined storage ponds (*Jalkunds*).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators possessing clear proximity to dependable perennial mountain gravity streams, springs, or active rainwater catchment cells.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically at the local District Agriculture Office (DAO) or through circle extension desks.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Possession verification note, site layout map, and water source availability clearance certificate.

---

### [NL_SCHEME_005] MIDH - Horticulture Mission (HMNEH Nagaland)
* **Category:** High-Value Cash Crop Commercialization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers transitioning plots into commercial spice, fruit, and exotic flower plantations.
* **Benefit Details:** Leverages a highly subsidized 90:10 Northeast funding setup to expand horticultural production:
  * Financial support to construct modern naturally ventilated polyhouses and green shade nets to produce premium cash crops like Kiwi fruit, Naga King Chili (*Raja Mircha*), and Dendrobium Orchids.
  * Finances high-altitude storage spaces, sorting centers, and integrated cold chains to connect produce directly to regional air cargo terminals.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Landholders positioned in designated altitudinal zones with certified soil parameters.
* **How to Apply:** Submit plantation layouts directly to the Directorate of Horticulture, Kohima, or regional line agents.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Hill Village Land revenue tracking sheet, soil health card, and technical vendor design blueprints.

## Section 3: Grassroots Credit, Agri-MSME & Apiculture Missions

### [NL_SCHEME_006] Chief Minister's Micro Finance Initiative (CMMFI)
* **Category:** Credit-Linked Capital Subsidy & Interest Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Standalone farmers, rural youth entrepreneurs, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).
* **Benefit Details:** Omnibus financing model implemented jointly with the Ministry of Finance to expand agri-allied enterprises:
  * **30% Government Subsidy:** Direct back-ended capital grant funded by the State Government.
  * **60% Bank Credit Line:** Financed through partner commercial banks with an automatic 10% beneficiary token margin requirement.
  * **Ceiling & Interest Waiver:** Hard financial ceiling of up to ₹15 Lakh per enterprise, alongside complete interest subvention support during initial moratorium periods.
  * Supports circular eco-hatcheries, zero-energy solar cold storage, hydroponic farming setups, and farm produce cargo hauling vehicles.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Smallholders or groups establishing verified agricultural value-addition units.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications are submitted through the unified [Nagaland CMMFI Credit Portal](https://credit.nagaland.gov.in/cmmfi) or processed at District Level Committees.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile profile, bank-format Detailed Project Report (DPR), Village Council land availability certificate, and bank clearance log.

---

### [NL_SCHEME_008] Nagaland Beekeeping & Honey Mission (NBHM)
* **Category:** Specialty Agro-Forestry & Apiculture Livelihoods
* **Target Beneficiary:** Forest-adjacent smallholders, landless rural youth collectives, and women-led honey gatherers.
* **Benefit Details:** Structured intervention backed by state agencies and the ICICI Foundation to scale premium wild mountain honey:
  * 50% to 60% equipment capital subsidy to purchase active bee colony boxes containing elite *Apis cerana* and stingless bee varieties, honey extractors, and protective gear.
  * Establishes fully funded regional honey-testing laboratories and centralized processing hubs to filter, test, and bottle honey for domestic and global markets.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent tribal residents completing a mandatory 5-day beekeeping and colony management module hosted at local Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVK).
* **How to Apply:** Handled physically through localized [Nagaland Honey Mission (NBHM) Desk Offices](https://nbhmnagaland.com/about/) or submitted via block ATMA extension lines.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Village Council certification note, KVK technical training certificate, and bank passbook.

## Section 4: Allied Livestock Entrepreneurship & Piggery Modernization

### [NL_SCHEME_002] National Livestock Mission – EDP (Nagaland Module)
* **Category:** Allied Sector Livestock Entrepreneurship
* **Target Beneficiary:** Unemployed rural youth, individual livestock farmers, and women's self-help groups.
* **Benefit Details:** Animal husbandry asset generation to meet high domestic meat demand:
  * Up to a 50% capital financial subsidy to establish commercial breeding mini-farms across three distinct tracks: Piggery, Poultry, and Sheep/Goat units.
  * Integrates state-backed Livestock Insurance schemes to protect capital investments from unexpected disease outbreaks.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents aged 18 to 45 years with a dedicated plot available for shed fabrication and access to clean water lines.
* **How to Apply:** Coordinated by the Department of Animal Husbandry & Veterinary Services; formal applications are processed through regional District Veterinary Officers.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Tribal Status Certificate, Village Council land clearance deed, and a bank-approved Detailed Project Report (DPR).

---

### [NL_SCHEME_009] Indo-French Scientific Piggery Modernisation Project
* **Category:** Advanced Livestock Breeding & Processing Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Commercial piggery farmers, meat processing cooperatives, and rural livestock startups.
* **Benefit Details:** Strategic international value-chain partnership launched under the updated National Livestock Mission framework:
  * Integrates advanced global breeding technology via a formal state partnership with *Cooperl* (a premier French pork processing collective) to establish centralized Scientific Breeding Nucleus Centers.
  * 50% direct capital financial subsidy to build biosecure, climate-controlled animal pens, import premium parent boar lines, and deploy computerized artificial insemination tracking kits.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Progressive livestock operators or cooperatives with verified multi-acre properties isolated from human settlements, backed by a stable water source.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural site proposals directly to the Directorate of Animal Husbandry & Veterinary Services, Kohima.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, tribal status proof, environmental clearance certificate, and a detailed engineering bank project blueprint.
