## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Arunachal Pradesh's state agricultural policy operates within the unique agro-climatic terrain of the Eastern Himalayas, focusing on high-value organic cash crops, high-altitude pastoralism, and transitioning shifting cultivation to permanent terrace farming:
1. **The Tripartite 45:45:10 Financing Architecture:** Replaces unmonitored cash handouts with an institutional structure: 45% Front-Ended Government Subsidy + 45% Zero-Interest Bank Loan (via SBI / Arunachal Pradesh Rural Bank) + 10% Farmer Equity Share. Applied uniformly across *Atmanirbhar Krishi*, *Bagwani*, and *Pashupalan*.
2. **Mega Kiwi Mission (Ziro Valley) & Organic Value Chains:** Massive expansion of organic Kiwi orchards across 14 districts, offering free tissue-cultured vines and 75% front-ended subsidies for galvanized iron-trellis vine support structures.
3. **High-Altitude Pastoralism & Terrace Ecology:** Preservation grants (60%–75% subsidy) for indigenous *Mithun* (*Bos frontalis*) and *Yak* herds with linkups to Yak Fibre Processing Centres in Dirang, alongside ₹30,000/ha incentives to convert slash-and-burn *Jhum* slopes into stone-bunded Terrace Rice Cultivation (TRC).

## Section 1: Tripartite 45:45:10 Atmanirbhar Missions

### [ARUN_SCHEME_001] Atmanirbhar Krishi Yojana (AMY)
* **Category:** Credit-Linked Subvention & Agricultural Mechanization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual progressive farmers, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs), and primary cooperative societies across Arunachal Pradesh.
* **Benefit Details:** Implements the landmark 45:45:10 financial formula for farm asset creation:
  * **45% Government Subsidy:** Disbursed directly as a front-ended capital grant.
  * **45% Interest-Free Bank Loan:** Funded through partner banks (State Bank of India and Arunachal Pradesh Rural Bank / Apex Bank) without accruing annual interest.
  * **10% Farmer Margin Share:** Upfront capital contribution paid by the beneficiary.
  * Individual projects are supported up to ₹10 Lakh; registered FPOs and SHG clusters can scale up to ₹50 Lakh.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Indigenous permanent residents or registered rural cooperatives with verified land possession rights (LPC). Must not be a willful loan defaulter.
* **How to Apply:** Digital registration on `arjansh.arunachal.gov.in`. The District Level Committee headed by the Deputy Commissioner (DC) sanctions applications and routes them to banks.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Possession Certificate (LPC) verified by District Revenue Authority, ST Certificate, bank NOC, and photos.

---

### [ARUN_SCHEME_002] Atmanirbhar Bagwani Yojana (ABY)
* **Category:** Commercial Horticulture Development
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual orchardists, tribal homestead farmers, and horticultural cooperatives expanding commercial fruit orchards.
* **Benefit Details:** Operates under the identical 45:45:10 financing model (45% state subsidy, 45% zero-interest loan, 10% farmer equity):
  * Deployed to establish commercial fruit orchards: land preparation, fencing, micro-drip irrigation layouts, and procurement of certified saplings (Kiwi, Apple, Mandarin Orange, Arecanut, Dragon Fruit, Persimmon).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident landholders with plots suitable for commercial fruit cultivation. Minimum plot size is 0.5 Hectares; financial support is capped at 2 Hectares per farming household.
* **How to Apply:** Applications open seasonally on `arjansh.arunachal.gov.in`, followed by soil suitability reports from the District Horticulture Officer (DHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Possession Certificate (LPC), ST Certificate, orchard layout map, and bank loan consent sheet.

---

### [ARUN_SCHEME_004] Atmanirbhar Pashupalan Yojana (APY)
* **Category:** Livestock Enterprise & Allied Sector Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural youth, traditional cattle rearers, and women entrepreneurs expanding animal husbandry.
* **Benefit Details:** Tripartite 45:45:10 funding formula to establish modern livestock enterprises:
  * Capital deployed to set up mini-dairy units (minimum 5 crossbred cows/buffaloes), commercial goat rearing units, semi-intensive pig breeding farms, or rural poultry broiler setups.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Residents aged 18 to 50 years with adequate shed space, having completed a basic animal husbandry training course.
* **How to Apply:** Submitted online via `arjansh.arunachal.gov.in` under the livestock header, vetted by the local Veterinary Officer.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Possession Certificate (LPC) / 5-year lease deed, animal husbandry training certificate, and bank passbook.

## Section 2: Hill Mechanization, Jhum Optimization & Terrace Rice Cultivation

### [ARUN_SCHEME_003] Chief Minister’s Scheme for Agri-Mechanization (CMSAM)
* **Category:** Farm Mechanization & Automation Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal hill-terrace farmers requiring localized equipment suited for steep terrains.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct front-ended financial subsidy of 50% to 75% on standard hill machinery:
  * Covers narrow-track power tillers, brush cutters, mini-tractors, paddy transplanters, hill-slope grain reapers, and motorized sprayers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident tribal farmers holding cultivable terrace or valley land.
* **How to Apply:** Submitted offline to the District Agriculture Officer (DAO) or circle extension office.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Possession Certificate (LPC), ST Certificate, and equipment proforma invoice from an empanelled manufacturer.

---

### [ARUN_SCHEME_005] Jhum Optimization & Terrace Rice Cultivation (TRC) Subsidy
* **Category:** Sustainable Hill Farming & Environmental Conservation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional hill farmers practicing slash-and-burn (*Jhum*) cultivation transitioning to settled agricultural plots.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct state financial grant of ₹30,000 per Hectare to offset the heavy labor costs of cutting, leveling, and constructing permanent stone-bunded terrace rice fields (TRC):
  * Includes free distribution of high-yielding organic paddy seed varieties and localized soil-health mapping.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Tribal hill farmers currently cultivating slopes under active Jhum cycles, where terrain allows water-retaining terrace rice cultivation.
* **How to Apply:** Coordinated through the Soil and Water Conservation Department. Farmers register via their Village Council Chairman or Anchal Samiti.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, ST Certificate, Village Council Recognition Letter verifying traditional land rights, and bank passbook.

## Section 3: Mega Kiwi Mission, Mithun/Yak Rearing & Agri-Startups

### [ARUN_SCHEME_006] Arunachal Pradesh Mega Kiwi Mission (2025–2035 Horizon)
* **Category:** Commercial Horticultural Clustering & GI Cultivation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Tribal hill cultivators and orchardists expanding organic cash crops across 14 target districts (anchored around Ziro Valley).
* **Benefit Details:** Landmark mission to make Arunachal India's organic Kiwi hub:
  * Free distribution of certified organic tissue-cultured Kiwi vines (Allison, Hayward, Monty varieties).
  * 75% front-ended structural subsidy to erect mandatory galvanized iron-trellis vine support structures and anti-hail nets.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Contiguous plot of at least 0.2 Hectares suitable for trellis layout, with verified soil pH tracking parameters.
* **How to Apply:** Apply directly to the District Horticulture Officer (DHO) or via `arjansh.arunachal.gov.in`.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Possession Certificate (LPC), ST Certificate, and circle soil suitability sheet.

---

### [ARUN_SCHEME_007] Indigenous Livestock Preservation Mission (Mithun & Yak Rearing Grants)
* **Category:** Animal Husbandry & Indigenous High-Altitude Breed Protection
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional livestock herders and high-altitude tribal pastoralists (Monpa, Brokpa, Nyishi, Adi communities).
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidies for preserving indigenous mountain livestock (*Mithun* / *Bos frontalis* and *Yak*):
  * 60% to 75% capital subsidy to construct semi-intensive breeding ranches and high-altitude winter protection sheds.
  * Free veterinary micro-chipping and vaccination.
  * Direct commercial linkages to Yak Fibre Processing Common Facility Centres (in Dirang and Tawang) to monetize organic wool and hides.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Indigenous tribal pastoralists holding traditional alpine grazing permits validated by mountain Village Councils.
* **How to Apply:** Submit application dossiers to the District Animal Husbandry and Veterinary Officer.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, ST Certificate, Village Council grazing authorization letter, and bank passbook.

---

### [ARUN_SCHEME_008] Arunachal Agri Start-Up & ACABC Integration Scheme
* **Category:** Livelihood Support & Agri-Entrepreneurship Incubation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Unemployed agricultural, horticultural, and veterinary graduates and rural tech innovators.
* **Benefit Details:** State financial grant funding up to ₹20 Lakh per entrepreneur:
  * Dovetailed with the central Agri-Clinic and Agri-Business Centre (ACABC) scheme to establish block-level custom hiring machinery centers, AI-powered soil testing kiosks, and drone-based precision spraying ventures.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Citizens aged 18 to 45 years holding a recognized degree/diploma in agriculture, horticulture, or veterinary science.
* **How to Apply:** Proposals submitted through the Department of Agriculture head office or online portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Educational Degree/Diploma, Detailed Project Report (DPR), commercial PAN card, and bank details.
