## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Mizoram's agricultural policy framework operates across the steep, high-altitude slopes of the Lushai Hills, prioritizing the structural migration from traditional shifting cultivation (*Jhum*) toward sustainable, high-value sedentary agriculture:
1. **Targeted Economic Modernization & Jhum Conversion (BANA & NLUP):** Shifting from broad subsidies to high-impact capital deployment under the *BANA Economic Upliftment Scheme* (spanning 10 priority rural sectors) and *NLUP Revival* (providing up to ₹1 Lakh one-time capital investment grants to establish permanent plantations and livestock assets).
2. **Specialty Cash Crop Missions & Global Export Linkages:** Aggressive commercial scaling of GI-candidate *Mizo Bird's Eye Chili* (high pungency), *Anthurium* cut flowers, *Mandarin Oranges*, and *Passion Fruit* mapped directly to APEDA export logistics networks.
3. **Hill Mechanization, Clean Irrigation & Oil Palm:** 50% to 80% subsidies on slope-adapted power tillers (*SMAM*), 90% subsidies on micro-drip irrigation to combat dry winter seasons, and insulated *Viability Price DBT* protections under the National Mission on Edible Oils – Oil Palm (*NMEO-OP*).

## Section 1: Flagship Agri-Livelihood & Jhum Conversion Missions

### [MZ_SCHEME_001] Bana (Economic Upliftment Scheme)
* **Category:** Comprehensive Agri-Economy & Alternative Livelihood Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers, Jhum cultivators transitioning to settled agriculture, youth agri-entrepreneurs, and women's self-help groups across all districts.
* **Benefit Details:** Landmark multi-sectoral flagship economic modernization framework:
  * Delivers a combination of direct capital grants, interest subventions, and input infrastructure across 10 priority sectors: Agriculture, Horticulture, Animal Husbandry & Veterinary, Fisheries, Sericulture, Land Resources, and Agro-Processing.
  * Transitions farmers from low-yield shifting cultivation into commercial clusters for bamboo crafts, high-density terraced fruit orchards, and rubber plantations.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent tribal residents of Mizoram holding valid land utilization or tenancy permissions approved by local Village Councils.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications and Detailed Project Reports (DPR) are submitted via the unified state portal or localized at respective District Agriculture Offices (DAOs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Tribal Status Certificate, Village Council Land Certificate, bank passbook, and sector-specific project summary.

---

### [MZ_SCHEME_007] Mizoram New Land Use Policy (NLUP) Revival Scheme
* **Category:** Jhum Transformation & Sedentary Agrarian Transition
* **Target Beneficiary:** Inner Line Permit (ILP) holding tribal farming families actively practicing slash-and-burn shifting cultivation (*Jhum*).
* **Benefit Details:** High-impact economic migration and environmental restoration grant:
  * Delivers a one-time direct capital investment grant of up to ₹1 Lakh per farming household.
  * Earmarked to establish permanent alternative agricultural assets: plantation cash crops (Rubber, Tea, Coffee, Cardamom), high-density terraced fruit orchards, commercial piggery modules, or community-based aquaculture ponds.
  * Includes multi-year technical handholding, land slope terracing assistance, and integration with state cooperative marketing federations.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active Jhum farming families whose land parcels pass structural slope and soil suitability assessments by the State Agriculture Department.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are compiled via Village Council recommendations and routed directly through the regional Block Development Officer (BDO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, State Inner Line Permit (ILP), Tribal Status Certificate, Village Council land boundary verification map, and bank passbook.

---

### [MZ_SCHEME_002] Mizoram Bird's Eye Chili Mission
* **Category:** Specialty Cash Crop Commercialization & GI Protection
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual smallholders and FPOs cultivating the state's signature high-pungency geographical indicator (GI) crop (*Mizo Chili*).
* **Benefit Details:** Value-chain scaling for Mizoram Bird's Eye Chili:
  * 50% to 75% direct subsidy on certified high-yielding, disease-resistant chili saplings and organic bio-inputs.
  * Upfront capital subsidies to construct community-managed solar drying racks, moisture-controlled sorting beds, and regional vacuum-packaging units.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Traditional cultivators operating land plots with suitable soil parameters within notified chili-growing clusters.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted through local Agriculture Extension Officers (AEOs) or directly at the Directorate of Horticulture, Aizawl.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Revenue Receipt or Village Council land note, soil test suitability card, and bank passbook.

## Section 2: Hill Mechanization, Precision Irrigation & Soil Restoration

### [MZ_SCHEME_003] Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM Mizoram)
* **Category:** Farm Automation & Steep-Slope Mechanization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders, youth farming groups, and rural machinery cooperatives.
* **Benefit Details:** Overcomes steep-slope terrain labor bottlenecks by providing specialized machinery:
  * 50% to 80% capital investment subsidy on specialized hill equipment: narrow-track power tillers, rotavators, hill-climbing brush cutters, mini-weeders, and manual hill paddy seeders.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Smallholder farmer clusters or individuals holding certified land revenue titles or village-approved cultivation leases.
* **How to Apply:** Online application through the national [AgriMachinery SMAM Portal](https://agrimachinery.nic.in/) selecting the Mizoram nodal department.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile certificate, proforma invoice from an empanelled machinery manufacturer, and Land Ownership/Possession document.

---

### [MZ_SCHEME_004] PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop (Mizoram Module)
* **Category:** Precision Micro-Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Hill-terrace vegetable growers, passion fruit planters, and commercial flower nurseries.
* **Benefit Details:** Addresses severe dry-season mountain water scarcity:
  * Up to a 90% state and central capital subsidy on the installation of drip irrigation lines, micro-sprinklers, water-carrying pipes, and secondary HDPE storage tanks.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators with access to a reliable water source (perennial mountain springs, rainwater harvesting ponds, or river lift pumps).
* **How to Apply:** Submit applications to the District Horticulture Office (DHO) or through circle extension officers.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, land mutation proof, sketch design of the micro-irrigation layout, and a water source availability certificate.

---

### [MZ_SCHEME_008] Khet Bachao Abhiyan (Mizoram Eco-Soil Module)
* **Category:** Sustainable Agriculture & Soil Restoration
* **Target Beneficiary:** Cultivators, horticulture growers, and orchardists battling mountain soil erosion, acidity, and nutrient depletion.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive ecological soil rehabilitation:
  * 100% state-funded block-level soil diagnostic testing via mobile soil-testing vans to map micro-nutrient and pH deficiencies.
  * 50% to 70% direct input subsidy on agricultural lime, bio-fertilizers, organic composting infrastructure, and natural bio-pesticides.
  * Direct integration with National Mission on Natural Farming guidelines to secure price premiums for chemical-free produce.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident farmers holding legal cultivation permissions on plots mapped under regional Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) grids.
* **How to Apply:** Handled at regional KVK centers or through mobile soil-testing van enrollment drives.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID, Land revenue Girdawari extract or Village Council verification certificate.

## Section 3: High-Value Commercial Horticulture & Oil Palm Expansion

### [MZ_SCHEME_005] Integrated Development of Horticulture (MIDH Mizoram)
* **Category:** High-Value Cash Crop Transitions
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers transitioning traditional plots into commercial spice, fruit, and exotic flower plantations.
* **Benefit Details:** Highly leveraged 90:10 central-to-state funding framework:
  * Capital grants to establish modern naturally ventilated polyhouses, shade nets, and tissue-culture nurseries for Anthuriums, Mandarin Oranges, Dragon Fruit, and high-grade Ginger.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual landholders located within designated climate elevation tiers with certified soil parameters.
* **How to Apply:** Submit plantation proposals directly to the Directorate of Horticulture, Aizawl, or regional DHO offices.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Hill Land Revenue Certificate, soil health card, and vendor structure designs.

---

### [MZ_SCHEME_009] Mizoram Horticulture Support Scheme (NHM Module)
* **Category:** Commercial Horticulture Acceleration & Export Linkage
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers, self-help groups, and fruit growers entering export-oriented value chains.
* **Benefit Details:** National Horticulture Mission (NHM) implementation:
  * 50% direct capital subsidy covering tissue-cultured planting materials for high-value Passion Fruit, Ginger, Turmeric, and Anthurium cut flowers.
  * Post-harvest quality training and direct linkage with the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA) to access export markets.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farming families with verified permanent plots suitable for commercial perennial cropping.
* **How to Apply:** Digital entry via the official Mizoram Horticulture Portal (`horticulture.mizoram.gov.in`) or physical filing at the DHO.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Tribal Status Certificate, certified land title passbook, and vendor nursery bills.

---

### [MZ_SCHEME_010] National Mission on Edible Oils – Oil Palm (NMEO-OP Mizoram)
* **Category:** Strategic Commercial Oilseed Expansion & Price Insulation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landowners converting low-yielding rainfed plots or abandoned shifting hillsides into commercial oil palm plantations.
* **Benefit Details:** Highly leveraged 90:10 Northeast funding setup:
  * Covers 85% of total planting material costs for certified seedlings alongside a 50% financial maintenance subsidy to fund inter-cropping inputs during the 4-year gestation period.
  * **Viability Price (VP) DBT:** If open-market Formula Prices dip below the state-notified Viability Price, the government automatically transfers 100% of the price deficit directly via DBT into the farmer's bank account.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators operating in state-notified oil palm zoning grids with access to certified processing mills.
* **How to Apply:** Apply directly at the Directorate of Agriculture, Aizawl, or through empanelled corporate partner desks in the state.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Revenue Mutation Certificate, water source feasibility layout, and bank passbook.

---

## Section 4: Post-Harvest Cold Chains & Traditional Sericulture

### [MZ_SCHEME_006] Mizoram Post-Harvest Cold Chain and Processing Scheme
* **Category:** Supply Chain Logistical Security & Storage
* **Target Beneficiary:** Registered Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs), cooperative fruit unions, and rural agro-entrepreneurs.
* **Benefit Details:** Combats highway transit delays and post-harvest spoilage:
  * 50% to 75% capital investment subsidy to establish solar-powered walk-in cold rooms, commercial washing-cum-sorting pack houses, and mechanized spice grinding units.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered collective groups or individuals aged 18 to 55 owning land or holding a minimum 10-year registered lease.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural and financial DPRs to the Directorate of Agriculture or Directorate of Horticulture.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, FPO Registration Certificate, 10-year Lease Deed or Land Title, and bank credit clearance certificate.

---

### [MZ_SCHEME_011] Mizoram Sericulture Mission (Silk Farming Module)
* **Category:** Traditional Textile Allied Enterprise & Tribal Livelihoods
* **Target Beneficiary:** Tribal farming households, traditional handloom weavers, and rural women's groups in sericulture blocks.
* **Benefit Details:** Scales production of premium traditional Eri Silk (*Puan-Eri*):
  * 100% free distribution of elite Eri silkworm seed eggs and healthy Castor/Mulberry host plant seedlings.
  * 50% direct capital subsidy to fabricate scientific silkworm rearing sheds and purchase advanced yarn motorized spinning machinery.
  * Guaranteed institutional buyback linkage through the Mizoram Handloom & Handicrafts Development Corporation (MHHDC).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** ILP-holding permanent tribal residents with adequate land available for silkworm foliage feed.
* **How to Apply:** Submit physical applications directly to the regional District Sericulture Officer (DSO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, State ILP copy, ST Certificate, shed infrastructure layout, and bank passbook.
