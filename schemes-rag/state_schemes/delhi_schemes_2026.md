## Executive Summary & UT Operational Context

The National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi operates a specialized agricultural and environmental framework tailored to peri-urban agricultural blocks (Najafgarh, Kanjhawala, Alipur, Narela, Bawana, and Mehrauli) and densely urbanized residential centers:
1. **Clean Energy Irrigation & Feed-in Incentives:** Subsidized standalone solar water pumping systems (60%–80% subsidy) with a guaranteed ₹4.30 per unit feed-in tariff for feeding surplus daytime power back to Delhi DISCOMs (TPDDL/BYPL/BRPL).
2. **Zero-Burning Stubble Decomposition & Urban Rooftop Farming:** 100% free tractor spraying of bio-enzyme Pusa Decomposer solution across all operational paddy fields, combined with a 75% subsidy on household urban farming kits under *Mukhyamantri Shahri Bagwani Yojana*.
3. **High-Tech Urban Agriculture & Peri-Urban Livestock:** 50%–60% capital subsidies for commercial hydroponics, aeroponics, and naturally ventilated polyhouses, along with 50%–60% grants for peri-urban micro-dairies and goat farming.

## Section 1: Clean Energy Irrigation, Feed-in Tariffs & Crop Disaster Relief

### [DEL_SCHEME_001] Mukhya Mantri Kisan Mitra Yojana (Solar Pump Subvention)
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Renewable Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers and landholder clusters cultivating commercial vegetables and flowers in peripheral rural zones of Delhi (e.g., Najafgarh, Kanjhawala, Alipur).
* **Benefit Details:** Replaces expensive diesel pumps with clean solar energy:
  * 60% to 80% independent capital subsidy to install standalone solar photovoltaic water pumping systems (3 HP to 7.5 HP capacities).
  * **Surplus Power Feed-in Tariff:** Farmers connecting solar setups to the grid receive an assured feed-in tariff of ₹4.30 per unit for surplus daytime clean power pumped into the DISCOM network (TPDDL/BYPL/BRPL).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must hold a verified operational groundwater borewell or tubewell. Priority is assigned to farmers adopting micro-irrigation (drip/sprinkler).
* **How to Apply:** Digital registration via the Delhi Renewable Energy Development Bureau or on the integrated state portal (`delhi.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Khatauni from the Delhi Bhoo-Lekh Land Registry, hydrogeologist water feasibility certificate, and bank passbook.

---

### [DEL_SCHEME_002] Delhi Agri-Horticulture Crop Damage Assistance Scheme
* **Category:** Crop Damage Risk Assistance & Emergency Relief
* **Target Beneficiary:** Owner cultivators and registered tenant farmers growing vegetables, flowers, and food grains across rural and peri-urban villages of Delhi.
* **Benefit Details:** 100% state-funded disaster compensation for unseasonal weather anomalies or Yamuna River flooding:
  * **Yield Loss between 20% and 50%:** Flat financial relief of ₹12,000 per acre (capped at a maximum of 5 acres per farming family).
  * **Yield Loss exceeding 50%:** Increased financial relief of ₹20,000 per acre (capped at a maximum of 5 acres per farming family).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland located within NCT of Delhi boundaries. Damage must stem from localized hailstorms, heavy unseasonal rain, or river overflow, logged within 72 hours of the event.
* **How to Apply:** Submit claim forms offline to the office of the local Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) or Tehsildar.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Girdawari crop verification sheet, Patwari verified self-declaration form, and bank passbook.

## Section 2: High-Tech Urban Agriculture & Peri-Urban Livestock

### [DEL_SCHEME_003] Delhi Polyhouse & Hydroponics Promotion Subsidy
* **Category:** High-Value Modern Urban Agriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers, rural youth, and urban-edge agri-entrepreneurs building high-density, controlled-environment vertical agricultural setups.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital development grants for modern precision farming:
  * **Naturally Ventilated Polyhouses:** 50% capital subsidy (capped at ₹12 Lakh per applicant).
  * **Commercial Hydroponics & Aeroponics Setups:** 60% financial subsidy to install precision soil-less farming lines, automated nutrient dosing controllers, and vertical growth racks for premium exotic greens and berries.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Delhi residents aged 18 to 50 years holding a technical training certificate from an authorized center (such as IARI Pusa or KVK). The plot must span at least 500 square meters.
* **How to Apply:** Upload Detailed Project Reports (DPR) to the Directorate of Agricultural Extension dashboard under the Development Department.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Khatauni documents or registered 5-year lease deed, vendor proforma invoice, and training certificate copy.

---

### [DEL_SCHEME_004] Delhi Urban Allied Micro-Dairy & Goat Farming Scheme
* **Category:** Livestock Enterprise & Allied Livelihood Expansion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural women collectives, small-scale cattle breeders, and unemployed youth residing in peripheral village tracts of Delhi.
* **Benefit Details:** Subventions to establish modern micro-dairy or goat-rearing facilities:
  * **Micro-Dairy Units (2 to 5 Murrah Buffaloes or Gir Cows):** 50% capital subsidy (capped at ₹3.5 Lakh) for modern stalls, milking machines, and motorized chaff cutters.
  * **Goat Rearing Units (10 Goats + 1 Buck):** 60% financial subsidy to build raised-slat wooden sheds and procure elite breeds (Barbari or Jamunapari).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Delhi holding adequate shed space separate from urban residential living zones.
* **How to Apply:** Register offline with the regional Animal Husbandry Department unit or district veterinary center.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, land/lease proof, veterinary fitness certificate, and bank passbook.

## Section 3: Urban Food Security & Bio-Enzyme Straw Digestion

### [DEL_SCHEME_005] Mukhyamantri Shahri Bagwani Yojana (Urban Agriculture Drive)
* **Category:** Sustainable Urban Farming & Food Security Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Urban residents, Resident Welfare Associations (RWAs), community housing societies, and peri-urban rooftop growers across Delhi.
* **Benefit Details:** 75% financial subsidy on standardized household urban farming kits (vertical grow racks, coco-peat blocks, organic bio-fertilizers, and automated capillary micro-drip mats) plus free on-site training sessions conducted by certified *Shahri Bagwani Mitras*.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Residents or RWAs within Delhi NCT possessing unshaded terrace, balcony, or courtyard space.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted online via the Delhi Environment Department’s portal (`gap.eforest.delhi.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, domestic electricity bill, and a digital photo of the proposed terrace/balcony area.

---

### [DEL_SCHEME_006] Delhi Pusa Decomposer Free Distribution Scheme
* **Category:** Crop Residue Management & Air Pollution Mitigation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Paddy cultivating farmers across rural and peri-urban agricultural belts of Delhi (Narela, Bawana, Alipur, Najafgarh, Kanjhawala, Mehrauli).
* **Benefit Details:** 100% free spraying of IARI Pusa bio-enzyme capsule microbial solution directly on farmers' fields using state-contracted tractor sprayers to digest standing paddy straw into organic manure within 15–20 days without burning.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to all operational paddy cultivators in Delhi NCT regardless of landholding size.
* **How to Apply:** Register crop details with the local Block Development Office (BDO) or local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Delhi Farmer ID code, and verified land Khatauni plot references.
