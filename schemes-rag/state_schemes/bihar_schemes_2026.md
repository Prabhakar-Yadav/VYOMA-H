> **State Code:** `BR` / `BIHAR`

## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Bihar's agricultural policy framework is structured around the fertile Indo-Gangetic alluvial plains, managing the dual challenges of severe recurrent floods in North Bihar (*Chaur waterlogged lowlands*) and seasonal dry spells in South Bihar:
1. **Ganges Organic Corridor & Chaur Land Conversion:** Direct input grants of ₹11,500/acre along the 13-district Ganges river belt, ₹15 Lakh custom hiring banks for PACS, and 80% capital grants to convert waterlogged *Chaur lands* into multi-tier fish pond and orchard embankments.
2. **Zero-Premium Risk Mitigation (BRFSY) & 70-Paise Power Tariff:** Complete replacement of commercial crop insurance with 100% state-funded *Bihar Rajya Fasal Sahayata Yojana* (up to ₹10,000/ha for >33% crop loss), paired with dedicated agricultural electrical connections charging only 70 paise per unit.
3. **Emergency Drought Mitigation & High-Value Diversification:** *Diesel Anudan* (₹750/acre per cycle) during dry spells, 50% grants for mushroom spawning and polyhouses, 60%–75% solar pump grants with ₹4.15/unit feed-in tariffs via BREDA, and 75% subsidies for *Sahiwal/Gir* dairy units.

## Section 1: Climate-Resilient Agriculture, Organic Corridor & Drought Relief

### [BIHAR_SCHEME_001] Mukhyamantri Harit Krishi Plant Yojana
* **Category:** Organic Production Support & Climate-Resilient Input Subsidy
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual smallholders, tenant cultivators, and Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) across Bihar.
* **Benefit Details:** Overhauls structural production inputs through three climate-resilient components:
  * **Organic Corridor Direct Grant:** Direct cash input subsidy of ₹11,500 per acre for transitioning to certified chemical-free farming within the state-notified Organic Corridor (13 districts along the Ganges river basin).
  * **PACS Custom Hiring Centre (CHC) Fund:** ₹15 Lakh capital development fund per selected PACS (50% state grant + 50% low-interest loan) to establish machinery banks offering laser land levelers, happy seeders, Super-SMS, and straw balers.
  * **Chaur Land Development Subvention:** 80% capital subsidy to convert waterlogged, low-lying lands (*Chaur lands*) into integrated agro-aquaculture systems (deep central fish pond surrounded by elevated fruit orchard embankments).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent resident cultivators of Bihar with valid land records. Organic input subsidies are capped at 2 Hectares (approx. 5 Acres) per household. Tenants qualify with a certificate signed by the local Krishi Samanvayak (Agriculture Coordinator) or Vikas Mitra.
* **How to Apply:** Digital registration via the Bihar DBT Agriculture Portal (`dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in`). The local Krishi Samanvayak conducts physical GPS field checks.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Possession Certificate (LPC) or recent *Malguzari* land tax receipt, Aadhaar-seeded bank passbook, and Ward Member countersigned tenancy self-declaration.

---

### [BIHAR_SCHEME_006] Bihar Diesel Anudan Yojana (Emergency Drought Mitigation)
* **Category:** Emergency Relief Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal cultivators facing delayed monsoons, erratic rainfall, or localized dry spells.
* **Benefit Details:** Emergency diesel cost reimbursement to protect standing crops:
  * Reimburses ₹750 per acre per irrigation cycle.
  * Kharif paddy is covered for up to 3 cycles (₹2,250/acre maximum relief cap); maize, pulses, and oilseeds are covered for up to 2 cycles (₹1,500/acre).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Activated when the state government officially declares a block/district as drought-affected. Fuel must be purchased from an authorized commercial fuel pump within the notified emergency window.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications submitted via `dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in` during active emergency windows.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, computerized diesel purchase receipt (printed with the farmer's unique registration number), land plot identification details, and bank passbook.

## Section 2: Zero-Premium Crop Loss Protection & Power Utilities

### [BIHAR_SCHEME_002] Bihar Rajya Fasal Sahayata Yojana (BRFSY)
* **Category:** Crop Damage Risk Assistance (Zero-Premium Alternative)
* **Target Beneficiary:** Owner cultivators and non-owner tenant sharecroppers cultivating notified Kharif and Rabi crops across all 38 districts.
* **Benefit Details:** 100% state-funded crop assistance requiring zero premium contribution from farmers:
  * **Crop Yield Losses between 20% and 33%:** Flat financial relief of ₹7,500 per Hectare (capped at a maximum of 2 Hectares per farmer).
  * **Crop Yield Losses exceeding 33%:** Elevated financial relief of ₹10,000 per Hectare (capped at a maximum of 2 Hectares per farmer).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to both landowners and tenant sharecroppers. Losses must result from natural disasters, droughts, unseasonal rainfall, or river flooding, validated via automated Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs).
* **How to Apply:** Applied online via the cooperative department portal (`pacsonline.bih.nic.in/fsy`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, residential certificate, self-declaration validated by the Ward Member/Panchayat Secretary, Cooperative Society membership ID, and land LPC/Malguzari receipt (for owners).

---

### [BIHAR_SCHEME_004] Bihar Mukhya Mantri Krishi Vidyut Sambandh Yojana
* **Category:** Agricultural Power Utility Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers requiring dedicated electrical energy connections for agricultural tubewells and irrigation pump sets.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital infrastructure expansion delivering electricity directly to farm boundaries:
  * State covers 100% of the capital expenditure for erecting dedicated agricultural electric poles, wiring conductors, and installing transformers.
  * **Subsidized Tariff Rate:** Agricultural power is supplied at a highly subsidized tariff of only 70 paise per unit, drastically lowering operational irrigation costs compared to diesel pumps.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must have an operational borewell or shallow tubewell irrigation pump with clear land ownership or long-term lease.
* **How to Apply:** Submitted online via DISCOM portals (NBPDCL / SBPDCL) or offline through the local Junior Engineer (JE) office.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Possession Certificate (LPC), passport photograph, and mobile number linked to the consumer ID.

## Section 3: High-Value Horticulture, Mushrooms & Plant Protection

### [BIHAR_SCHEME_005] Mukhyamantri Horticulture Mission (Polyhouses, Mushrooms & Fruit Orchards)
* **Category:** High-Value Diversification Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Young agri-entrepreneurs, women smallholders, and progressive horticulturists.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidies to expand high-value commercial horticulture:
  * **Naturally Ventilated Polyhouses:** 50% capital subsidy (up to 4,000 square meters per applicant).
  * **Mushroom Cultivation Units:** 50% subsidy to establish indoor button and oyster mushroom spawning units, paired with compost-making infrastructure.
  * **Fruit Orchards:** Subsidized planting material for GI-tagged Shahi Litchi, Jardalu Mango, and Malbhog Banana orchards.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual farmers or self-help groups with unencumbered land and an accessible water source.
* **How to Apply:** Digital proposals uploaded to the Directorate of Horticulture portal (`horticulture.bihar.gov.in`), inspected by the District Horticulture Officer (DHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, LPC, Detailed Project Report (DPR), training certificate from an authorized center (for mushroom units), and land maps.

---

### [BIHAR_SCHEME_003] Mukhyamantri Keetniyantran Yojana (Subsidized Plant Protection)
* **Category:** Input Quality & Pest Management Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and progressive farmers experiencing active crop pest infestations.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidizes plant protection chemicals and biological inputs:
  * 50% to 75% state subsidy on certified chemical/bio-pesticides, pheromone traps, and bio-weedicides (e.g., to control Fall Armyworm, Stem Borer, Pink Bollworm).
  * Scales to 100% free community distribution during state-declared emergency epidemic pest outbreaks.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered on the central DBT Agriculture portal; benefits capped at 2 Hectares per farming household.
* **How to Apply:** Generate an online pesticide purchase subsidy discount voucher via `dbtagriculture.bihar.gov.in` and redeem at authorized dealer counters.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, 13-digit State Farmer Registration Number, and mobile number for OTP confirmation.

## Section 4: Clean Energy Irrigation, Aquaculture & Indigenous Dairy

### [BIHAR_SCHEME_007] Bihar Saur Krishi Sanjivani Yojana (Agricultural Solarization)
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Renewable Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers, farmer cooperatives, and community tube-well boring groups.
* **Benefit Details:** Solar photovoltaic irrigation managed via the Bihar Renewable Energy Development Agency (BREDA):
  * 60% to 75% capital subsidy to install off-grid standalone solar water pumping installations (2 HP to 7.5 HP capacities).
  * **Grid-Tied Surplus Buyback:** Farmers choosing grid-tied configurations receive a guaranteed feed-in tariff purchase rate of ₹4.15 per unit for exporting surplus solar electricity back to the DISCOM (NBPDCL/SBPDCL).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Verified groundwater or surface irrigation sources free of high-voltage overhead transmission line interference.
* **How to Apply:** Apply online via `breda.bih.nic.in` or through the BREDA mobile application.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Possession Certificate (LPC), water source ownership proof, bank passbook, and photograph.

---

### [BIHAR_SCHEME_008] Mukhyamantri Talab Nirman Sahayata Yojana (Agri-Fisheries Convergence)
* **Category:** Allied Sector & Aquaculture Development
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers and landholder clusters excavating new commercial fish ponds (*Talab Nirman*).
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidizes earthmoving and pond setup costs:
  * 50% to 70% capital financial subsidy (higher slab for SC, ST, and women applicants) covering excavation costs capped at ₹5.5 Lakh per Hectare.
  * Includes free Year-1 starter fingerlings (Rohu, Catla, Mrigal) and mechanical surface aeration equipment kits.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Undisputed land ownership titles; minimum excavation footprint of 0.1 Hectares, capped at a maximum of 2 Hectares per farming family.
* **How to Apply:** Submitted online via the Directorate of Fisheries portal (`fisheries.bihar.gov.in`), vetted by the District Fisheries Officer (DFO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, updated Land Possession Certificate (LPC), project cost estimation sheet, bank statement, and caste certificate (where applicable).

---

### [BIHAR_SCHEME_009] Bihar Desi Gau Palan Protsahan Yojana (Indigenous Dairy Upgrades)
* **Category:** Livestock Enterprise & Animal Husbandry Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural unemployed youth, women dairy entrepreneurs, and smallholder cattle rearers establishing indigenous bovine units.
* **Benefit Details:** Credit-linked capital investment subsidy for indigenous dairy cattle (Sahiwal, Gir, Tharparkar):
  * **2 to 4 Cow Mini-Dairy Units:** 75% capital subsidy for SC, ST, and Extremely Backward Class (EBC) applicants; 50% subsidy for general categories.
  * **15 to 20 Cow Commercial Dairy Units:** 40% capital subsidy (capped at ₹10 Lakh) covering automated milking machines, modern sheds, and motorized chaff cutters.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Bihar aged 18 to 55 years holding or leasing 0.5 to 1.5 acres of green fodder land.
* **How to Apply:** Project proposals uploaded to `state.bihar.gov.in/ahd`, inspected by the local Veterinary Officer for bank loan sanction.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, land/lease agreement (minimum 5 years), PAN card, dairy training certificate, and bank passbook.
