## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Sikkim operates as the world's first certified 100% organic state. With synthetic pesticides and chemical fertilizers legally prohibited, state agricultural policies focus on sustaining biological soil fertility, maintaining international organic certifications, and maximizing production economics for high-value temperate crops:
1. **Production-Linked Direct Fiscal Incentives (MMKAY):** Pioneered volume-based direct incentive transfers rather than input handouts—paying direct per-kilogram cash bonuses (₹100/kg for Large Cardamom, ₹20/kg for Ginger, ₹10/kg for Turmeric, ₹20/kg for Buckwheat) and a ₹8/liter dairy top-up disbursed directly via DBT based on verified sales logs.
2. **100% State-Funded Organic Certification & Free Bio-Inputs:** The state government completely finances the expensive international organic group certification (NPOP and PGS-India standards) for entire village clusters, accompanied by 100% free distribution of certified bio-fertilizers, neem bio-pesticides, and organic compost.
3. **GI Spice Upgrades & High-Altitude Supply Chains:** Revitalizing virus-affected plantations of GI-tagged *Large Cardamom* through 75% subsidized tissue-cultured suckers and modern *Scientific Cardamom Dryers*, paired with up to 85% subsidies on climate-controlled polyhouses for *Cymbidium Orchids* and centralized buybacks via SIMFED.

## Section 1: Flagship Direct Income Incentives & Organic Certification

### [SK_SCHEME_001] Mukhya Mantri Krishi Atmanirbhar Yojana (MMKAY)
* **Category:** Production-Linked Direct Financial Incentive
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual organic farmers, dairy rearers, and horticultural cultivators across all 6 districts of Sikkim.
* **Benefit Details:** Production booster delivering direct cash assistance calculated on verified harvest sales volumes:
  * **Cash Crop Bonuses:** Direct per-kilogram financial incentive bonuses:
    * ₹100 per kg for certified organic Large Cardamom.
    * ₹20 per kg for organic Ginger.
    * ₹10 per kg for organic Turmeric.
    * ₹20 per kg for organic Buckwheat.
  * **Dairy Top-Up:** Additional incentive grant of ₹8 per liter on milk supplied to local cooperative milk producer unions.
  * Disbursed directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the farmer’s Aadhaar-seeded bank account upon submission of official purchase receipts.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident landholders or tenant organic farmers possessing an active Organic Farmer ID under the state agricultural index, with zero synthetic chemical usage defaults.
* **How to Apply:** Handled digitally through the [Sikkim Agriculture Production Department Portal](https://sikkim.gov.in/) or via block-level Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) inspection desks.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Sikkim Subject / Certificate of Identification (COI), unique Organic Farmer ID card, cooperative or AMC sale receipts, and bank passbook.

---

### [SK_SCHEME_002] Sikkim Organic Mission Certification and Input Scheme
* **Category:** Quality Control & Organic Sourcing Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Contiguous village crop clusters and registered Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) practicing chemical-free agriculture.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive institutional standardization and resource cushion:
  * The State Government completely funds the expensive process of international organic group certification (under NPOP/PGS-India standard mapping) to retain the state's 100% organic tag.
  * Provides a 100% free distribution network for certified bio-fertilizers, neem-based bio-pesticides, organic compost, and elite indigenous crop seeds to completely phase out toxic chemical inputs.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active farming clusters located inside state-notified organic conversion zones who undergo strict periodic soil-chemical testing.
* **How to Apply:** Managed automatically at the village cluster level through the Sikkim Organic Mission nodal field officers.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Group registration logs, field diary logs showing strict organic crop rotation details, and soil analysis baseline logs.

## Section 2: Specialty Crop Missions & High-Tech Floriculture Infrastructure

### [SK_SCHEME_003] National Mission on Large Cardamom (Sikkim Module)
* **Category:** Specialty Cash Crop Commercialization & GI Value Chain Protection
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional large cardamom growers across high-altitude temperate belts.
* **Benefit Details:** Protects and scales the production of Sikkim’s premier geographical indicator (GI) spice crop:
  * Distributes high-yielding, tissue-cultured, disease-free cardamom suckers at a 75% state-funded financial subsidy to replace viral *Chirke* and *Foorkey* diseased stands.
  * Grants capital financial assistance to transition traditional smoky curing sheds (*Bhattis*) to modern, energy-efficient Scientific Cardamom Dryers to preserve essential volatile oil parameters and maximize market valuation.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators owning or holding village-approved long-term cultivation leases over plots situated in designated high-humidity hill valleys.
* **How to Apply:** Route applications through localized block Agriculture Development Officers (ADOs) or directly to the Spices Board regional desk.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Certificate of Identification (COI), land possession verification note, and dealer nursery bills.

---

### [SK_SCHEME_004] Advanced Protected Cultivation Green/Polyhouse Subsidy
* **Category:** High-Tech Precision Horticulture & Floriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers transitioning into year-round climate-controlled exotic floriculture (Orchids, Cymbidiums) and high-value off-season vegetable lines.
* **Benefit Details:** Infrastructure weather engineering support to handle heavy mountain monsoon hazards and extreme high-altitude frosts:
  * Provides a direct 50% to 85% front-ended capital investment subsidy (leveraging Northeast-specific funding rules) to build naturally ventilated polyhouses, insect-proof netting, and automated drip-irrigation grids.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers possessing valid land-holding titles with a dedicated micro-drip irrigation system successfully installed on the target plot.
* **How to Apply:** Submit a comprehensive structural and agronomic proposal directly to the District Horticulture Officer (DHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, COI copy, certified land title passbook, vendor structural design quotes, and soil-water test suitability profiles.

## Section 3: Steep-Slope Mountain Mechanization & Post-Harvest Cold Chains

### [SK_SCHEME_005] Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM Sikkim)
* **Category:** Precision Mountain Farm Automation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders, youth farming groups, and rural machinery cooperatives.
* **Benefit Details:** Alleviates extreme terrain labor boundaries by providing mechanized micro-tools:
  * Grants a 50% to 80% capital investment subsidy on specialized equipment engineered specifically for steep terrace farming layouts.
  * Distributed equipment targets mountain terrains: mini power tillers, mechanized hillside brush cutters, lightweight terraced weeders, and localized maize shellers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual farmers or village collectives owning registered land titles or recognized community lease plots.
* **How to Apply:** Profiles and dealer invoices are processed online during scheduled windows through the central [AgriMachinery Portal](https://agrimachinery.nic.in/).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Farmer Registration Code, proforma price quote from an empanelled machinery dealer, and block verification log.

---

### [SK_SCHEME_006] Sikkim Post-Harvest Supply Chain and Micro-Cold Storage Project
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Logistics & Market Stabilization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Registered Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs), cooperative fruit unions, and rural agro-entrepreneurs handling highly perishable organic crops.
* **Benefit Details:** Overcomes steep mountain transport delays by financing local storage assets to stop post-harvest wastage:
  * Offers up to a 75% capital financial grant to set up solar-powered zero-energy cold storage rooms, commercial washing-cum-sorting pack houses, and spice grinding machinery near production blocks.
  * Maps local enterprises directly into specialized state marketing networks under the Sikkim State Cooperative Supply and Marketing Federation (SIMFED) to bypass middlemen.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered collective groups or individuals aged 18 to 55 who possess land matching regional zoning regulations.
* **How to Apply:** Complete project blueprints are processed through the Directorate of Horticulture or Directorate of Agriculture.
* **Documents Required:** Group Registration Certificate/FPO Code, COI copy, Land Title deeds or long-term registered lease documents, bank loan sanction records, and a detailed engineering project report.
