## Executive Summary & Territory Operational Context

The Union Territory of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (DNHDD) operates across three distinct geographic enclaves: the landlocked, forested, and tribal-dominated terrain of Dadra & Nagar Haveli (practicing rainfed paddy and ragi agriculture) alongside the coastal, marine-dependent enclaves of Daman and Diu:
1. **Targeted Tribal Subventions & Sowing Security:** Delivering 100% free agricultural input kits (HYV Paddy, Ragi, Maize, pulses, bio-pesticides, and hand tools) through Zonal Agriculture Offices (ZAO, Silvassa) to cushion tribal smallholders against industrial land pressures.
2. **Marine Fishery Mechanization & Fuel Subsidies:** Extensive operational relief for coastal fishermen in Daman and Diu—providing 50% capital subsidies for outboard marine engines (OBMs), GPS navigators, echo-sounders, 75% grants for insulated ice boxes, and direct sales tax/VAT rebates on High-Speed Diesel (HSD) via smart-card fuel terminals.
3. **Clean Energy Micro-Irrigation & Coastal Horticulture:** Overcoming coastal groundwater salinity and erratic power grids through 60% to 80% subsidized standalone solar water pumps (2 HP to 7.5 HP), corrosion-resistant micro-drip networks with sand filters, and 50% to 75% subsidies for protected polyhouses producing high-value *Kesar* and *Alphonso* mango orchards.

## Section 1: Flagship Input Relief, Tribal Subventions & Direct Income

### [DN_SCHEME_001] Free Agricultural Input Kit Distribution Scheme
* **Category:** Essential Sowing Input Support & Tribal Crop Production
* **Target Beneficiary:** Scheduled Tribe (ST), Scheduled Caste (SC), and marginal landholders across the rural blocks of Dadra and Nagar Haveli.
* **Benefit Details:** Alleviates upfront seasonal sowing expenses for rainfed cultivators:
  * Delivers 100% free agricultural input kits containing certified High-Yielding Variety (HYV) Paddy, Maize, Ragi, and pulse seeds.
  * Supplies free organic manure, neem-based bio-pesticides, and essential hand tools (sickles, weeding hooks, seeders) directly to the farm gate.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Smallholder resident farmers with verified land records (7/12 extract). Priority assigned to tribal farming blocks and women-headed households.
* **How to Apply:** Handled physically through distribution camps organized seasonally by the Zonal Agriculture Office (ZAO) in Silvassa.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, ST/SC Certificate (if applicable), updated Land Revenue Passbook (7/12 extract) copy, and linked mobile number.

---

### [DN_SCHEME_002] High-Yielding Fruit Plant Distribution and Intercropping Drive
* **Category:** Horticultural Expansion & Livelihood Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholders diversifying income beyond single-crop rainfed paddy setups.
* **Benefit Details:** Expands permanent green cover and commercial horticulture:
  * Supplies certified grafts of elite fruit plants (Mango—Alphonso/Kesar, Sapota/Chiku, Guava, and Dwarf Coconut clones) at a 75% to 90% administration financial subsidy.
  * Provides technical training on multi-tier intercropping to cultivate seasonal vegetables beneath fruit tree canopies.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators owning or holding legally recorded land deeds with access to basic watering infrastructure.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically during pre-monsoon booking windows at the nearest District Horticulture Extension Centre.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile certificate, Land Patta / 7/12 extract papers, and dealer nursery bills.

---

### [DD_SCHEME_001] Comprehensive Agri-Input and Certified Seed Subsidy
* **Category:** Essential Sowing Input Support & Sustainable Cultivation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and Scheduled Tribe (ST) cultivators across all three districts.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidizes core agricultural inputs:
  * 50% to 75% financial subsidy on the procurement of certified HYV seeds for Paddy, Ragi, Pulses, and regional vegetable varieties.
  * 50% upfront concession on bio-fertilizers, organic manure, neem bio-pesticides, and micro-nutrient formulations.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active resident farmers recorded on the centralized land revenue database of the UT Administration.
* **How to Apply:** Handled physically through regional Agriculture Extension Centres (AECs) or block Zonal Agriculture Offices (ZAOs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, ST Certificate (if claiming 75% subsidy rate), Land Revenue 7/12 extract, and bank passbook.

---

### [DD_SCHEME_002] PM-KISAN (DNHDD Implementation Node)
* **Category:** Direct Financial Income Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder and marginal landowning farming families across Dadra & Nagar Haveli, Daman, and Diu.
* **Benefit Details:** State-monitored cash-transfer safety net providing baseline liquidity:
  * Guaranteed direct income support of ₹6,000 per year, disbursed in three equal seasonal tranches of ₹2,000 each.
  * Deposited directly through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the farmer's validated Aadhaar-linked bank account.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small and marginal landholders with operational land records registered under active verified status on the UT land registry.
* **How to Apply:** Digital registration via the central PM-KISAN portal or through local Common Service Centres (CSCs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, digitized Land Records (7/12 extract), Aadhaar-seeded mobile number, and bank passbook.

## Section 2: Clean Energy Daytime Irrigation & Coastal Water Engineering

### [DN_SCHEME_003] PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop (DNHDD Coastal Module)
* **Category:** Precision Water Management & Micro-Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Vegetable growers and orchardists facing groundwater salinity and winter dry spells in Daman and Diu.
* **Benefit Details:** Precision engineering subvention tailored to coastal soil dynamics:
  * Up to an 85% to 90% capital financial subsidy for small and marginal farmers to install precision drip and sprinkler irrigation networks.
  * Fully covers the installation of corrosion-resistant inline drippers and specialized sand/gravel filters to handle saline water profiles.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers with an operational open well, borewell, or micro-tubewell holding verified land title records.
* **How to Apply:** Submit applications to the District Agriculture Office (DAO) or register via the unified micro-irrigation portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, recent land revenue 7/12 mutation documents, pump status certificate, and vendor layout designs.

---

### [DN_SCHEME_004 / DD_SCHEME_003] Solar Powered Pump Set Subsidy Scheme (PM-KUSUM Stack)
* **Category:** Clean Energy Daytime Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Off-grid smallholders, tribal farming groups, and vegetable clusters breaking fuel dependency.
* **Benefit Details:** Substantial capital cost reduction for standalone solar water pumping installations:
  * 60% to 80% cumulative financial subsidy (leveraging UT-specific subvention rules) on standalone solar water pumps.
  * Supports 2 HP, 3 HP, 5 HP, and 7.5 HP surface and submersible solar pump configurations.
  * Enables smallholders to irrigate step terrains and remote patches without recurring electricity or diesel costs.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must possess a verified underground well, borewell, or continuous surface water source without an active grid-connected electric pump.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications open during announced booking windows on the official UT administration tracking portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID, water source capability clearance from an irrigation technician, and a zero-grid affidavit.

---

### [DD_SCHEME_004] Micro-Irrigation & Farm Pond Subvention Grid
* **Category:** Precision Micro-Irrigation & Water Harvesting
* **Target Beneficiary:** Vegetable growers, fruit orchardists, and dryland farmers in water-stressed or coastal blocks.
* **Benefit Details:** Integrated on-farm water conservation:
  * Provides up to a 90% capital subsidy for drip and sprinkler irrigation grids.
  * Fixed financial grants to construct permanent rainwater harvesting farm ponds lined with High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) geomembrane plastic sheets to prevent saline water intrusion and seepage loss.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small and marginal category farmers holding valid land revenue documents with a verified water source.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically at your regional District Agriculture Office (DAO) or via local block horticulture agents.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, recent land revenue sheets (7/12 extract), sketch design layout of the micro-irrigation system, and vendor proforma quotes.

## Section 3: High-Value Coastal Horticulture & Protected Cultivation

### [DD_SCHEME_005] Integrated Horticulture Development & Protected Cultivation Mission
* **Category:** Commercial Horticulture Acceleration & Multi-Tier Inputs
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers transitioning traditional plots into high-margin fruit orchards and climate-controlled floriculture.
* **Benefit Details:** High-impact asset creation maximizing per-acre income:
  * 50% to 75% capital financial subsidy to construct naturally ventilated polyhouses, shade-net structures, and automated misting systems.
  * Distributes high-yielding, certified fruit saplings including Mango (*Alphonso*, *Kesar*), Sapota, Guava, and Coconut clones at highly subsidized rates.
  * Fully funds on-farm concrete vermicompost units (up to ₹50,000 grant per family) to scale up organic inputs.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators possessing clear land titles with an operational micro-irrigation layout.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural design and plantation proposals directly to the District Horticulture Officer (DHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, certified land mutation copy (7/12 extract), vendor design quotes, and soil-water suitability profiles.

## Section 4: Marine Fisheries Modernization, Fuel Subsidies & Cage Culture

### [DN_SCHEME_005] Mechanisation and Modernisation of Marine Fishing Boats
* **Category:** Allied Sector Marine Fishery Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional fishermen, boat owners, and fisheries cooperatives across Daman and Diu coastal belts.
* **Benefit Details:** Enhances deep-sea safety and catch preservation:
  * 50% capital financial subsidy to purchase and install internal marine engines (OBMs) on traditional wooden fishing boats.
  * Fully covers modern navigation and safety electronics: GPS units, echo-sounders, fishfinders, and two-way VHF communication handsets.
  * 75% financial subsidy for purchasing insulated ice boxes to maintain the freshness and export quality of the marine catch.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active boat owners holding valid registration logs (Vessel ID) with the Department of Fisheries, DNHDD.
* **How to Apply:** File application dossiers directly with the regional Assistant Director of Fisheries or during scheduled coastal outreach camps.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, valid Boat Registration Certificate (Vessel ID), active Fishermen Biometric Identity Card, and bank passbook.

---

### [DN_SCHEME_006] Financial Assistance on High-Speed Diesel (HSD) for Fishing Vessels
* **Category:** Operational Input Cost Relief for Marine Fisheries
* **Target Beneficiary:** Registered mechanized boat operators and fishing vessel owners in Daman and Diu.
* **Benefit Details:** Lowers operational expenditures by subsidizing vessel fuel:
  * Delivers a direct sales tax/VAT rebate or fixed cash subsidy per liter on High-Speed Diesel (HSD) purchased for marine fishing operations.
  * Disbursed dynamically via DBT into the boat owner's bank account based on verified fuel logs from authorized marine fuel outlets.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Mechanized fishing vessels holding active sea-worthiness certificates and valid fishing licenses issued by the administration.
* **How to Apply:** Automated tracking applies through smart cards issued to registered boat owners at government-notified marine fuel stations.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Fishing License copy, Sea-worthiness clearance card, and fuel log receipts.

---

### [DD_SCHEME_006] Marine Fisheries Modernization and Cage Culture Project
* **Category:** Allied Sector Fisheries & Marine Livelihood Expansion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional coastal fishermen, boat owners, and marine-allied cooperatives across Daman and Diu.
* **Benefit Details:** Multi-sector marine aquaculture diversification:
  * 40% to 60% capital financial subsidy for marine safety equipment, GPS tracking tools, and insulated fish-transport vehicles.
  * Extends heavy subventions to convert traditional fishing craft into motorized units and finance modern harbor ice plant installations.
  * Fully funds the setup of open-sea Cage Culture systems and brackish-water mud-crab fattening loops along calm coastal bays.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent resident fishermen holding a valid Biometric Fisher ID Card issued by the Fisheries Department.
* **How to Apply:** Submit structural proposals directly to the regional District Fisheries Officer (DFO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Fisher Identity Card, boat registration certificates (Vessel ID), and bank passbook details.
