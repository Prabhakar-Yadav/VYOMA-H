## Executive Summary & Territory Operational Context

As a fully planned, highly urbanized Union Territory, Chandigarh operates within an intensely constrained agricultural land footprint of approximately 912 hectares across 22 peripheral villages. As rural farmland is steadily consolidated into the Chandigarh Municipal Corporation and Chandigarh Housing Board (CHB) development plans, the territory's agrarian policy focuses on high-density urban sustainability, peri-urban dairy feeds, and pioneering digital welfare:
1. **Peri-Urban Dairy Primacy & Forage Deficits:** Maximizing limited cultivated plots to grow high-protein green fodder (Berseem, Sorghum) supported by 25% to 50% seed subsidies to sustain Chandigarh's heavy urban milch cattle and Murrah buffalo population.
2. **First-in-Nation Purpose-Bound Digital Rupee (CBDC):** Leading India by deploying the first Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) Direct Benefit Transfer mechanism—issuing encrypted, program-locked *Digital Rupee (e₹)* tokens under PMGKAY, redeemable exclusively for food grains and high-protein pulses at empanelled Fair Price Shops via secure smartphone QR codes.
3. **Mandatory Rooftop Solarization & Land Consolidation:** Legally enforcing rooftop solar installations on all residential properties measuring 500 square yards and above via the *PM Surya Ghar Chandigarh Stack* (combining ₹78,000 Central + ₹30,000 UT Administration top-ups via CPDL) and offering transparent *Structured Land Pooling* through the Chandigarh Housing Board (CHB) mapped up to ₹16 Crore per acre asset valuation.

## Section 1: Flagship Direct Inputs, CBDC Welfare & Urban Solar Stacks

### [CH_SCHEME_001] Certified Sowing Input & Seed Subsidy Module
* **Category:** Essential Input Support & Fodder Cultivation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder farming families and dairy rearers across peri-urban village clusters.
* **Benefit Details:** Directly counters high input inflation to sustain the local dairy ecosystem:
  * Delivers a direct 25% to 50% administration-funded financial subsidy on the procurement of certified foundational Wheat seeds.
  * Organizes localized input distribution camps to supply concessional green fodder seeds (Berseem, Sorghum/Chari) to bridge the territory's critical forage shortage.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active resident cultivators registered under the verified land records database of the Chandigarh District Administration.
* **How to Apply:** Distributed seasonally before the Rabi and Kharif sowing windows through the Nodal Agriculture Department Hub (open 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, verified Jamabandi / Pattadar land record passbook, and linked mobile number.

---

### [CH_SCHEME_002] CBDC Rupee-Based Food Subsidy Project (PMGKAY Node)
* **Category:** Advanced Digital Public Infrastructure & Targeted Welfare
* **Target Beneficiary:** Lower-income families, landless laborers, and welfare cardholders across Chandigarh.
* **Benefit Details:** Landmark digital welfare deployment marking Chandigarh as the first UT to launch CBDC-based Direct Benefit Transfers:
  * Disburses food security cash entitlements in the form of encrypted, purpose-bound Digital Rupee (e₹) tokens.
  * The digital funds are program-locked into the beneficiary's e₹ wallet, allowing them to be spent exclusively at government-empanelled Fair Price Shops (FPS) using a secure smartphone QR code.
  * Completely eliminates middleman leakage, cash diversion, and delay.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents listed under the Priority Household (PHH) or Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) public distribution registry.
* **How to Apply:** Handled automatically; digital wallets are activated at block civil supplies counters during ration profile synchronization.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, active digitized Ration Card, and an active Aadhaar-seeded mobile smartphone.

---

### [CH_SCHEME_003] PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (Chandigarh Stack)
* **Category:** Clean Energy Urban Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Residential property owners, urban housing societies, and independent home clusters.
* **Benefit Details:** Large-scale solar energy expansion campaign to lower municipal grid load:
  * **Stacked UT Top-Up:** In addition to the Central Government's standard ₹78,000 capital subsidy for a 3-kW residential solar array, the Chandigarh Administration provides an additional local financial subsidy of ₹30,000.
  * Drops the net cost for a standard 3-kW solar installation to an affordable out-of-pocket expense of approximately ₹48,000.
  * **Mandatory Solar Enforcement:** Rooftop solar plant installation is legally compulsory for all residential units measuring 500 square yards and above.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Residential property owners with roof structures certified safe by municipal engineers.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online via the national portal [`pmsuryaghar.gov.in`](https://www.pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) or through the nodal agency, Chandigarh Power Distribution Limited (CPDL).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, latest municipal electricity bill, property tax receipt, and roof clearance structural layout.

## Section 2: Livestock Credit, Breed Conservation & Micro-Poultry

### [CH_SCHEME_004] Pashu Kisan Credit Card (PKCC Livestock Grid)
* **Category:** Allied Sector Micro-Finance Credit
* **Target Beneficiary:** Livestock owners, peri-urban dairy farmers, and backyard animal rearers.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial liquidity buffer providing cheap working capital for dairy maintenance:
  * Extends institutional, low-interest Pashu Kisan Credit Cards (PKCC) to purchase cattle feed, veterinary medicines, and maintain biosecure dairy sheds.
  * Grants quick loans at a baseline concessional interest rate, with prompt repayment subventions reducing the net interest burden.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cattle owners possessing physical milch cows or buffaloes (such as Murrah breeds) within UT territorial limits.
* **How to Apply:** Administered physically; forms are vetted and forwarded to commercial banks by the Department of Animal Husbandry & Fisheries, UT Chandigarh.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID, veterinary health tagging certificate for the animals, and bank account credentials.

---

### [CH_SCHEME_006] Indigenous Milch Breed Conservation & Murrah Development Module
* **Category:** Livestock Breed Preservation & Dairy Incentives
* **Target Beneficiary:** Peri-urban dairy entrepreneurs, village milk pooling cooperatives, and backyard animal rearers.
* **Benefit Details:** Genetic breed upgrade and risk mitigation framework managed by the Department of Animal Husbandry & Fisheries:
  * 25% capital asset investment subsidy (topped up to 33.3% for SC/ST and women farmers) to establish micro-dairy units.
  * Covers the procurement of elite indigenous dairy cows (Sahiwal, Red Sindhi, Gir) and high-yielding Murrah buffalo lines.
  * Grants a 50% to 70% premium subvention on Livestock Insurance to protect smallholders from sudden animal mortality losses due to diseases or natural hazards.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Smallholders maintaining cattle inside designated rural sectors who hold animal medical clearance logs.
* **How to Apply:** Apply directly to the regional Veterinary Officer or during scheduled cattle health mapping campaigns.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, veterinary health-tagging charts, and active bank account credentials.

---

### [CH_SCHEME_007] Integrated Backyard Poultry & Micro-Farming Scheme
* **Category:** Alternative Livelihoods & Protein Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landless laborers, marginalized women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs), and lower-income peri-urban households.
* **Benefit Details:** Fast-yield micro-livestock assets to combat urban poverty:
  * 100% free distribution of low-input Backyard Poultry Units consisting of 20 to 50 disease-resistant, high-grade chicks per family.
  * Supplies elite dual-purpose strains (*Chabro* or *Vanaraja*) engineered for scavenging alongside local green forage.
  * Direct ex-gratia disaster assistance clause of ₹100 per bird to protect smallholders from localized environmental or weather shocks.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Residents across notified urban-periphery sectors passing income validation screenings.
* **How to Apply:** Managed by block-level outreach teams linked to the Chandigarh Municipal Corporation.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Domicile certificate, BPL/EWS entitlement card, and a basic poultry enclosure declaration note.

## Section 3: Urban Farming, SMART-PDS Logistics & Land Pooling

### [CH_SCHEME_005] Kissan Haat & Urban Organic Kitchen Gardening Campaign
* **Category:** Urban-Adjacent Horticulture & Decentralized Direct Marketing
* **Target Beneficiary:** Urban households, terrace gardening groups, and small organic producers.
* **Benefit Details:** Collaborative initiative executed alongside civil society bodies like the Kheti Virasat Mission (KVM) to transition Chandigarh into a model City of Organic Kitchen Gardens:
  * Fully funds regular hands-on Urban Farming Workshops to train citizens in setting up zero-chemical hydroponic layers, terrace vegetable patches, and vermicompost boxes.
  * **Kissan Haat Platform:** Establishes decentralized, weekly urban farmer markets allowing small organic growers to market poison-free produce directly to local consumers without middlemen commissions.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open access given to all resident citizens and organic smallholders operating around the territory's periphery.
* **How to Apply:** Walk-in access at designated sector neighborhood hubs or by tracking training schedules on official organic networks.
* **Documents Required:** None required for baseline consumer attendance or workshop enrollment.

---

### [CH_SCHEME_008] SMART-PDS Verification and Leakage Protection Initiative
* **Category:** Digital Public Infrastructure & Logistics Monitoring
* **Target Beneficiary:** Priority cardholders, fair price shop networks, and state civil supplies trackers.
* **Benefit Details:** High-tech administrative system operating concurrently with Chandigarh's pioneering CBDC-based Digital Rupee transfers:
  * Integrates electronic Point of Sale (ePoS) terminals with nationwide biometric portability under the One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) index.
  * Uses automated digital filters to ensure that purpose-bound e-Rupee welfare transfers remain program-locked to targeted nutrition items.
  * Expands beneficiary choice beyond baseline wheat and rice to include concessional high-protein pulses.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to all registered beneficiaries enrolled on the centralized territory tracking index.
* **How to Apply:** Automatically synchronized during the digital activation of e-Rupee wallets at localized distribution points.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, digitized active Ration Card, and biometric iris/fingerprint identification logs.

---

### [CH_SCHEME_009] Chandigarh Structured Land Pooling Framework
* **Category:** Agrarian Transition & Real Estate Consolidation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional smallholder farmers and ancestral landholders located across Chandigarh's remaining 22 rural villages.
* **Benefit Details:** Urban-transition framework deployed via the Chandigarh Housing Board (CHB) to manage municipal horizontal expansion:
  * Solves land-grabbing and distress sales by allowing smallholders to optionally hand over ancestral plots to the administration in return for developed commercial and residential real estate assets.
  * Guarantees high, transparent asset valuation mapping (equivalent up to ₹16 Crore value mapping per acre).
  * Freezes private speculative resale trades upon notification of a block sector to safeguard the original farmer's appreciation equity.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Ancestral title holders owning valid land footprints across Chandigarh's 22 remaining villages.
* **How to Apply:** Property inventory details are compiled at local revenue circle offices post-public notification periods.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, ancestral mutation records, certified field revenue survey blueprints, and no-encumbrance certificates.
