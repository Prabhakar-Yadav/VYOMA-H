## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Tripura's agricultural landscape is characterized by its distinctive terrain of undulating hillocks (*Tillas*) and fertile low-lying valleys (*Lungas*), alongside a large population of indigenous tribal farmers governed under the Tripura Tribal Areas Autonomous District Council (TTAADC):
1. **Production-Linked Direct Fiscal Incentives (MKA_Y):** Replaces broad input handouts with direct per-kilogram cash rewards (₹20/kg for Ginger and Turmeric; ₹5/kg for certified Paddy, Wheat, and Maize) disbursed directly via DBT based on verified sales logs.
2. **Tribal Jhumia Rehabilitation & Cash Crop Conversion:** Transforms traditional shifting cultivation into permanent multi-tier horticulture orchards (*Jhumia Rehabilitation* - ₹1 Lakh grants + 3 years of guaranteed MGNREGA maintenance wages) and large-scale natural rubber plantations (*Hevea brasiliensis* - ₹85,000/ha asset grants linked to TFDPC latex buybacks).
3. **Renewable Lift Irrigation & Post-Harvest Cold Chains:** Eliminates water conveyance deficits between low-lying *lunga* streams and *tilla* orchards via 85% subsidized *Soura Jala Nidhi* solar pump sets and 75% subsidized solar-powered walk-in cold rooms engineered for export-grade *Queen Pineapples*.

## Section 1: Flagship Direct Income Support, Jhumia Rehabilitation & Rubber

### [TR_SCHEME_001] Mukhyamantri Krishi Atmanirbhar Yojana (MKA_Y)
* **Category:** Production-Linked Direct Financial Incentive
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual organic farmers, horticulturists, and multi-crop growers across all 8 districts of Tripura.
* **Benefit Details:** Flagship production booster rewarding farmers with direct cash transfers based on certified sales volumes:
  * **Cash Crop Incentive:** ₹20 per kilogram for high-priority cash crops (Ginger and Turmeric).
  * **Food Grain Incentive:** ₹5 per kilogram for certified food grains (Paddy/Rice, Wheat, and Maize).
  * Disbursed directly through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the farmer's validated Aadhaar-linked bank account based on verified sales receipts.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active resident landholders or tenant cultivators registered on the state agricultural registry with clear seasonal crop booking records.
* **How to Apply:** Digital entry via the unified state tracking portal or via physical validation forms processed at block-level Agri-Input Distribution Centres.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Permanent Resident of Tripura Certificate (PRTC), unique Farmer ID card, authorized Agri-Market Committee (AMC) sale receipts, and bank passbook.

---

### [TR_SCHEME_002] Tripura Jhumia Rehabilitation & Orchard Development Scheme
* **Category:** Alternative Tribal Livelihoods & Agroforestry Expansion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Indigenous tribal families (*Jhumias*) actively practicing slash-and-burn shifting cultivation in forest blocks.
* **Benefit Details:** High-impact economic migration and environmental restoration program:
  * Allocates a comprehensive capital asset grant of up to ₹1 Lakh per beneficiary family to transition to permanent, settled agriculture.
  * Fully funds the design and layout of multi-crop Horticulture Orchards on *tilla* land, distributing high-yielding fruit saplings including Pineapple (Queen and Kew varieties), Jackfruit, and Lemon.
  * Integrates direct daily cash wages via MGNREGA for three consecutive years to handle trenching, pit digging, weeding, and perimeter fencing.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active tribal households holding verified Forest Rights Act (FRA) land pattas or village-approved land access permissions.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are compiled via local TTAADC village committees and routed to the regional Block Development Officer (BDO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Scheduled Tribe (ST) Certificate, FRA Land Patta document, Village Council recommendation note, and bank passbook.

---

### [TR_SCHEME_007] Chief Minister’s Rubber Plantation & Livelihood Scheme
* **Category:** Alternative Cash Crop Plantation & Tribal Livelihoods
* **Target Beneficiary:** Forest-dwelling tribal families, rehabilitation clusters, and smallholders on underutilized *tilla* lands.
* **Benefit Details:** Flagship economic modernization initiative positioning Tripura as a premier natural rubber producer:
  * Fully funds a long-term asset setup allocation of up to ₹85,000 per Hectare spread across a 4-year gestation block.
  * Supplies elite, high-yielding *Hevea brasiliensis* rubber saplings, fencing materials, organic bio-inputs, and micro-tapping tools.
  * Direct institutional integration with the Tripura Forest Development & Plantation Corporation (TFDPC) to guarantee stable institutional latex buybacks.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent tribal residents holding Forest Rights Act (FRA) land deeds or recorded village patta clearances.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted through regional TTAADC autonomous desk cells or via block-level Forest Range Offices.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, ST Certificate, FRA Land Patta copy, linked mobile number, and land boundary sketch map.

## Section 2: Water Security, Solar Lift Irrigation & Farm Mechanization

### [TR_SCHEME_003] PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop (Tripura Module)
* **Category:** Precision Water Engineering & Micro-Irrigation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Hilly terrace vegetable growers, fruit orchardists, and rubber planters experiencing severe winter water scarcity.
* **Benefit Details:** Tailored water management support adapted to the state's undulating topography:
  * 90% capital financial subsidy to construct on-farm water harvesting and precision irrigation networks.
  * Fully covers the physical layout and installation of precision drip-irrigation networks, micro-sprinklers, high-density polyethylene (HDPE) water delivery tubes, and secondary plastic-lined storage ponds (*Jalkunds*).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators possessing clear proximity to dependable perennial streams, open gravity springs, or active rainwater catchment tanks.
* **How to Apply:** Apply physically at your regional District Agriculture Office (DAO) or submit verification profiles through line department field agents.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, PRTC copy, Land Patta ownership receipt, sketch layout design of the micro-irrigation system, and water source availability logs.

---

### [TR_SCHEME_004] Mukhyamantri Soura Jala Nidhi Yojana (Solar Pump Module)
* **Category:** Clean Energy Irrigation Infrastructure & Lift Pumping
* **Target Beneficiary:** Off-grid smallholders, vegetable clusters, and tribal farming blocks breaking dependency on expensive diesel pumps.
* **Benefit Details:** Renewable energy deployment scheme guaranteeing stable daytime irrigation power:
  * 85% upfront capital investment subsidy on the procurement and installation of standalone solar water pumping units.
  * Supports surface and submersible configurations ranging from 2 HP to 5 HP, enabling farmers to draw water from low-lying *lunga* streams up to elevated *tilla* terraces.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must have a verified open-well, borewell, or stable surface stream connection without an active electric grid line.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications open dynamically during scheduled booking windows on the state agriculture tracking portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID code, land mutation records, water yield suitability certificate, and a zero-grid electricity meter affidavit.

---

### [TR_SCHEME_005] Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM Tripura)
* **Category:** Precision Mountain Farm Automation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder farming groups, young agri-entrepreneurs, and registered agricultural cooperative societies.
* **Benefit Details:** Reduces intense manual labor and accelerates cropping windows:
  * 40% to 50% capital subsidies for individual general category farmers.
  * 80% to 90% higher subsidy rates for Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) and Farm Machinery Banks established by cooperatives.
  * Distributed equipment targets mountain terrains: narrow-track power tillers, mechanized hillside brush cutters, lightweight terraced weeders, and localized maize shellers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual farmers or village collectives owning registered land titles or recognized community lease plots.
* **How to Apply:** Profiles and dealer invoices are processed online via the central national [AgriMachinery Portal](https://agrimachinery.nic.in/) selecting the Tripura state nodal desk.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer Registration Code, dealer proforma price quote, and block verification log.

## Section 3: Soil Health, Vermicompost & Certified Sowing Security

### [TR_SCHEME_006] Certified Seed Village & High-Yielding Variety (HYV) Subsidy
* **Category:** Core Sowing Input Provision & Seed Multiplication
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders setting up seasonal sowing operations.
* **Benefit Details:** Foundational input cost reduction:
  * Supplies certified foundation and high-yielding variety (HYV) seeds of Paddy, Pulses, Maize, and Oilseeds at a 50% to 75% state-funded financial subsidy.
  * Trains designated village clusters to multiply and store certified seeds locally, creating decentralized village seed banks that reduce external input dependency.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small, marginal, and cooperative farming units holding updated land mutation profiles.
* **How to Apply:** Distributed prior to the seasonal crop cycles through regional Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies (PACS) or Block Agriculture Offices (BAO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, land revenue receipt, and active crop-booking verification log.

---

### [TR_SCHEME_010] Tripura Bio-Input & Vermicompost Infrastructure Subsidy
* **Category:** Organic Conversion Support & Biological Soil Inputs
* **Target Beneficiary:** Multi-crop cultivators, smallholders, and greenhouse vegetable groups in low-lying *lunga* plains.
* **Benefit Details:** Sustainable soil health initiative reducing chemical input costs:
  * Fixed financial subsidy grant of ₹50,000 per farming family to design and construct permanent concrete vermicompost processing units on their plots.
  * Distributes liquid bio-fertilizer starter cultures and earthworm colonies (*Eisenia fetida*) to accelerate organic waste decomposition.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators working on active farm plots who have completed a baseline soil health diagnostic test.
* **How to Apply:** Apply through regional Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) or directly via local Agriculture Supervisors.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID card, land mutation papers, and a valid Soil Health Card copy.

## Section 4: Apiculture, Solar Cold Chains & High-Yield Fisheries

### [TR_SCHEME_008] Tripura Apiculture & Multi-Pollination Expansion Scheme
* **Category:** Specialty Agroforestry & Alternative Income Streams
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholders, landless rural youth collectives, and women-led honey gatherers adjacent to dense forest grids.
* **Benefit Details:** Livelihood diversification and mountain pollination enhancement:
  * Direct capital equipment subsidy of ₹2,000 per unit for elite bee colony boxes, safety suits, and stainless-steel honey extractors.
  * Free hands-on scientific training modules via local Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) covering queen bee rearing and scientific hive division.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident cultivators operating within notified flora zones with suitable shed space for placing hives.
* **How to Apply:** Submit physical registration profiles directly at the regional District Agriculture Office (DAO) or through block ATMA extension lines.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID code, KVK training attendance certificate (if completed), and bank passbook.

---

### [TR_SCHEME_009] Chief Minister’s Micro-Cold Storage & Solar Grid Pack-houses
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Logistics & Supply Chain Security
* **Target Beneficiary:** Registered Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), village cooperative fruit unions, and rural agro-entrepreneurs handling perishable fruits.
* **Benefit Details:** Prevents post-harvest spoilage of premium horticultural cash crops:
  * 75% upfront capital investment grant to construct solar-powered, off-grid micro-cold storage rooms (5 MT to 10 MT capacities) near rural production hubs.
  * Finances automated washing, grading, and computerized weighing equipment inside village pack-houses to prepare organic *Queen Pineapples* and *Jackfruits* for direct air-cargo export.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered collective groups or individuals owning land or holding a minimum 10-year registered lease agreement.
* **How to Apply:** Processed online via the unified Tripura Single Window Portal or at the Directorate of Horticulture.
* **Documents Required:** FPO Group Registration Certificate, Land Title deeds / 10-year Lease Agreement, bank credit clearance logs, and detailed engineering DPR.

---

### [TR_SCHEME_011] Mukhyamantri Matsya Vikas Yojana (High-Yield Fishery Module)
* **Category:** Allied Sector Aquaculture Expansion & Income Diversification
* **Target Beneficiary:** Inland fish farmers, youth entrepreneurs, and rural families owning low-lying water catchment basins.
* **Benefit Details:** Leverages Tripura's high per-capita fish consumption to build profitable rural aquaculture setups:
  * 50% to 60% capital financial subsidy to construct new fish ponds or renovate old community tanks in *lunga* plains.
  * Supplies elite carp fingerlings (Rohu, Catla, Mrigal), aerator machinery units, and subsidized high-protein fish feed kits.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Tripura aged 18 to 55 years holding absolute ownership or long-term operational lease over a minimum water expanse of 0.04 Hectares.
* **How to Apply:** Submit application dossiers directly to the regional District Fisheries Officer (DFO) or during scheduled block outreach camps.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, PRTC copy, land revenue sheets proving water-body layout permissions, and bank passbook details.
