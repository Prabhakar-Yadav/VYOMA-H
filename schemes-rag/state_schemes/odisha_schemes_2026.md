## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Odisha's state agricultural policy framework addresses the specific needs of coastal delta plains, rainfed uplands, and tribal slope-land farming communities through targeted flagship interventions:
1. **Financial Inclusion for Landless & Sharecroppers:** Handled through direct cash assistance under the KALIA/CM-KISAN framework and institutional collateral-free group loans for sharecroppers under the Balaram Yojana.
2. **Crop Diversification & Climate Resilience:** Spearheaded by the globally recognized Odisha Millet Mission (OMM) promoting traditional millets (*Mandia/Ragi*) with guaranteed PACS MSP procurement.
3. **Rainfed Water Security & Farm Mechanization:** Delivered via Jalanidhi shallow lift irrigation, *Krushi Pokhari* farm pond excavation grants, and computerized lottery mechanization subsidies.

## Section 1: Direct Income Support, Financial Safety Nets & Sharecropper Credit

### [OD_SCHEME_001] KALIA Scheme (Krushak Assistance for Livelihood and Income Augmentation)
* **Category:** Direct Income Support & Financial Stabilization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders, landless agricultural laborers, and vulnerable agricultural households across all 30 districts of Odisha.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive multi-tiered financial safety net:
  * **Cultivators (Small & Marginal Farmers):** ₹25,000 per family disbursed across 5 crop seasons (₹5,000 per season) as an input support grant for certified seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, and field labor charges.
  * **Landless Agricultural Households:** Flat ₹12,500 livelihood financial grant to establish allied micro-enterprises (goat rearing, backyard poultry units, duckery clusters, or apiary/beekeeping).
  * **Vulnerable Agricultural Households:** ₹10,000 per family for sustaining disabled, elderly, or destitute farming families incapable of manual cultivation.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent resident farming families of Odisha. Landless beneficiaries must be validated through BPL/Ration Card databases and own zero cultivable land titles.
* **How to Apply:** Handled digitally through the unified KALIA Portal (`kalia.odisha.gov.in`), or assisted via Gram Panchayat Krishi Mitras and local Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVK).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, BPL Ration Card, land Patta revenue records or tenancy self-declarations, and an Aadhaar-seeded bank account passbook.

---

### [OD_SCHEME_007] CM-KISAN Yojana (Odisha Unified Income Support Upgrade)
* **Category:** Direct Income Support & Financial Safety Net
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholders, marginal cultivators, and landless agricultural families across Odisha.
* **Benefit Details:** Modernized state direct income transfer designed to complement central PM-KISAN database parameters:
  * **Small & Marginal Cultivators:** ₹4,000 per financial year disbursed in two equal installments of ₹2,000 during the Kharif and Rabi input purchasing windows.
  * **Landless Agricultural Families:** ₹12,500 one-time financial livelihood grant disbursed in tranches for micro-livestock or village trade assets.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent resident farming households in Odisha whose identity and land ownership/landless records are locked on the central Krushak Odisha farmer database.
* **How to Apply:** Automatic verification and entitlement disbursement are handled through the integrated Krushak Odisha portal (`krushak.odisha.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card (linked to an active mobile number), Land Record Patta summary, verified Ration Card ID number, and an NPCI-mapped bank passbook.

---

### [OD_SCHEME_006] Odisha Balaram Yojana (Credit Linkage for Sharecroppers)
* **Category:** Joint Liability Agricultural Credit Mobilization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landless tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers, and traditional agricultural labor collectives lacking formal land title deeds.
* **Benefit Details:** Bridges the institutional credit gap by organizing landless cultivators into Joint Liability Groups (JLGs):
  * Each JLG comprises 4 to 10 landless cultivators and receives an instant institutional crop production loan of up to ₹50,000 per individual farmer or ₹5 Lakh per JLG cluster without pledging physical land collateral.
  * The Odisha State Government acts as the primary institutional guarantor, subsidizing the effective borrowing interest rate down to 1% to 2% flat annual interest upon timely repayment.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Landless resident cultivators in Odisha forming an officially verified JLG validated by the local Krishi Mitra or Village Agriculture Worker (VAW).
* **How to Apply:** Handled through Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS), District Central Cooperative Banks (DCCB), or participating public sector commercial banks.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Cards of all JLG members, valid BPL Ration Card, signed mutual guarantee agreement bond sheet, and a validation certificate from the village Sarpanch/VAW.

## Section 2: Minor Irrigation, Rainfed Water Harvesting & Soil Moisture

### [OD_SCHEME_002] Jalanidhi Scheme (Farm Pond & Lift Irrigation Module)
* **Category:** Minor Irrigation & Groundwater Development
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual smallholders, marginal farmers, and farming clusters in drought-prone blocks.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital subsidy for installing localized private irrigation infrastructure:
  * **Farm Ponds & Dugwells:** 50% capital subsidy for general category farmers, scaled to 75% for SC, ST, women, and smallholder farmers.
  * **Pumping Units:** Capital assistance for installing shallow tubewells, open dugwells, or river lift irrigation pump sets (up to 5 HP capacities) powering micro-sprinkler and drip lines.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Minimum contiguous landholding of 0.5 Hectares with clear mutation titles and a validated water recharge/catchment point.
* **How to Apply:** Project proposals are filed online via the Jalanidhi portal (`jalanidhi.odisha.gov.in`) and physically appraised by the Assistant Agriculture Engineer (AAE).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Record Patta code, small/marginal farmer certificate, and equipment proforma invoice quote from an empanelled manufacturer.

---

### [OD_SCHEME_008] Farm Pond Excavation Mission (Krushi Pokhari Subsidy)
* **Category:** Rainfed Water Harvesting & Micro-Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rainfed and dryland farmers, slope-land cultivators, and individual smallholders across non-irrigated blocks.
* **Benefit Details:** Creates permanent on-farm rainwater storage structures:
  * Direct front-ended capital cash subsidy of ₹50,000 to excavate a standardized on-farm pond unit (*Krushi Pokhari*) to capture monsoon runoff.
  * 75% financial grant to line the pond bed with High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) sheets (minimum 300-micron thickness) to eliminate seepage and water loss in sandy/porous soils.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland with a minimum plot footprint of 0.5 Hectares. Alignment must be certified as technically viable for runoff harvesting by the Block Agriculture Officer.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted through the Jalanidhi network gateway (`jalanidhi.odisha.gov.in`) or processed through the local Watershed Development Mission office.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Passbook (Patta copy), marginal farmer category certificate, and engineering layout map.

## Section 3: Crop Diversification, Tribal Nutrition & Digital Advisory

### [OD_SCHEME_004] Odisha Millet Mission (OMM Promotion Subvention)
* **Category:** Crop Diversification & Nutrition Security Production Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Tribal cultivators, smallholders, and slope-land farmers growing traditional minor millets (Mandia/Ragi, Suan, Kodo, Gurji) in notified blocks.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive promotion package to shift land from water-intensive paddy to climate-resilient nutri-cereals:
  * Direct cash incentive grants of ₹10,000 per Hectare during Year 1, with sequential milestone-linked incentives across a 3-year adoption cycle.
  * 100% state-backed Minimum Support Price (MSP) procurement guarantee executed through local Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) and Tribal Development Cooperative Corporation (TDCCOL) procurement mandis.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland mapped on the state millet tracking registry and certified by the Agriculture Technology Management Agency (ATMA) / Block Agriculture Officer.
* **How to Apply:** Enroll through the Odisha Millet Mission portal (`odishamilletmission.com`) or register at the local Block Agriculture Office / ATMA cell.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID, Land Patta copies, and verified bank account passbook.

---

### [OD_SCHEME_003] Ama Krushai (Agri-Extension & Mobile Advisory Framework)
* **Category:** Digital Extension & Farmer Advisory Services
* **Target Beneficiary:** All registered smallholders, marginal farmers, and livestock rearers across rural Odisha.
* **Benefit Details:** State-funded, customized agro-advisory delivery system:
  * Automated interactive voice response (IVR) calls and SMS advisories customized to the farmer's registered crop, sowing date, and local block weather forecasts.
  * Real-time pest infestation outbreak alerts and daily e-mandi wholesale price discovery updates.
  * Free dedicated toll-free advisory helpline (**155333**) connecting farmers directly with agricultural scientists from Odisha University of Agriculture and Technology (OUAT).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** All resident farmers with an active profile on the central Krushak Odisha registry.
* **How to Apply:** Onboarding is completed via the Krushak Odisha portal or through village-level campaigns conducted by Krishi Mitras.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, active mobile phone number, and basic land Patta reference number.

## Section 4: High-Value Agri-Entrepreneurship, Mechanization & Drones

### [OD_SCHEME_005] Mukhyamantri Krushi Udyog Yojana (MKUY)
* **Category:** High-Value Agri-Entrepreneurship Capital Infrastructure Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Unemployed youth, agricultural graduates, Self Help Groups (SHGs), and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).
* **Benefit Details:** Capital investment subsidy (CIS) for establishing commercial agribusiness and agro-processing ventures (commercial nurseries, tissue culture labs, cold storages, sorting/grading units, modern dairies):
  * **General Category Entrepreneurs:** 40% capital investment subsidy capped at ₹50 Lakh.
  * **SC / ST / Women / Agri-Graduates:** 50% capital investment subsidy capped at ₹50 Lakh.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Odisha aged 18 to 50 years with clear land titles and non-agricultural (NA) land conversion clearance for the project site.
* **How to Apply:** Detailed Project Reports (DPR) and bank loan sanction letters are submitted through the Agricultural Promotion and Investment Corporation of Odisha Limited (APICOL) portal (`apicol.nic.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, PAN card, business/SHG registration certificate, Detailed Project Report (DPR), bank loan appraisal document, and machinery proforma invoices.

---

### [OD_SCHEME_009] Mukhya Mantri Farm Mechanization Yojana
* **Category:** Farm Mechanization & Automation Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers, women farmers, FPOs, and custom equipment operators.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidies for procuring heavy and handheld farm machinery to mitigate rural agricultural labor shortages:
  * **Tractors & Power Tillers:** 40% subsidy (capped at ₹45,000) for general category; 50% subsidy (capped at ₹60,000) for small, marginal, SC, ST, and women farmers.
  * **Paddy Transplanters, Rotavators & Power Weeders:** 50% to 60% capital grant for mechanized transplanting and weeding.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to registered farmers on Krushak Odisha. Restricted to one tractor/power tiller purchase per farming family identity once every 10 years.
* **How to Apply:** Online application via the state agriculture portal, with computerized randomized lottery draws for high-demand equipment allocations.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Krushak Odisha Farmer ID, Land Patta extract, and proforma invoice from an empanelled machinery dealer.

---

### [OD_SCHEME_010] Odisha Agri-Enterprise Drone Technology Subsidy
* **Category:** Precision Agriculture & High-Tech Automation Subvention
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers, agri-graduates, registered FPOs, and Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs).
* **Benefit Details:** Financial subventions for deploying agricultural drones for remote aerial crop health monitoring, pesticide spraying, and nano-fertilizer application:
  * **FPOs & Cooperative Societies:** 100% financial grant (capped at ₹5 Lakh) for procuring complete agricultural drone kits and ground support accessories.
  * **Individual Agri-Graduates / SC-ST Smallholders:** 50% capital subsidy (capped at ₹4 Lakh).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Drones must be sourced from DGCA-approved empanelled manufacturers. Individual operators must hold an active Remote Pilot Certificate issued by a DGCA-authorized training organization.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are filed under the high-tech mechanization module on the Krushak Odisha portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Farmer ID slip, DGCA Remote Pilot Certificate, drone Unique Identification Number (UIN), and equipment invoice quotes.
