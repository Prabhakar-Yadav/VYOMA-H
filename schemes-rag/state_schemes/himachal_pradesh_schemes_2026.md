## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Himachal Pradesh's state agricultural and horticultural policy framework is tailored to the demanding conditions of high-altitude alpine slopes, temperate apple-producing valleys, and sub-tropical foot-hill river basins:
1. **Protected Cultivation & Canopy Weather Safeguards:** Extensive state assistance to construct brand-new naturally ventilated polyhouses (85% subsidy under *Nutan Polyhouse Pariyojana*), renovate weather-damaged structures (70%–85% subsidy), and install UV-stabilized anti-hail nets (80% subsidy up to 12.5 Bighas).
2. **Zero-Chemical & Natural Agro-Ecology (PK3Y):** Statewide adoption of Subhash Palekar Natural Farming (SPNF) offering ₹8,000/acre input grants, ₹25,000 for indigenous cows, and 75% subsidies on on-farm bio-formulation pits.
3. **Mountain Water Security & Pastoral Welfare:** 90% individual and 100% community grants for solar lift irrigation from mountain streams (*khads*), paired with accident compensation for farm machinery/orchard ropeway injuries and ram breeding loans for migratory Gaddi/Gujjar shepherds.

## Section 1: Protected High-Altitude Horticulture & Canopy Weather Safeguards

### [HP_SCHEME_001] Mukhya Mantri Green House Renovation Scheme
* **Category:** High-Altitude Polyhouse Infrastructure Restoration
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal greenhouse or polyhouse horticulturists cultivating off-season vegetables (capsicum, tomato, cucumber) and exotic cut flowers (carnations, gerberas).
* **Benefit Details:** State-funded capital assistance to repair or replace polyhouse structures damaged by heavy snowfall, hailstorms, cloudbursts, or high-velocity winds:
  * Direct capital subsidy covering 70% to 85% of the total institutional replacement cost for new UV-stabilized polyethylene cladding sheets, galvanized structural steel framing, and shade-net meshes.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Applicant must hold a valid Bonafide Himachali Certificate. Farmland must possess a legally verified polyhouse setup damaged by unseasonal adverse weather events.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted offline to the local Zonal Agricultural Office (ZAO), District Horticulture Officer (DHO), or Block Development Officer (BDO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Bonafide Himachali Certificate, Land Records (recent Jamabandi & Tatima summary issued within 6 months), certified damage validation photographs countersigned by the local Patwari, and water source availability declaration.

---

### [HP_SCHEME_005] Mukhya Mantri Nutan Polyhouse Pariyojana
* **Category:** Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA) Asset Creation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Unemployed rural youth, women smallholders, and progressive horticulturists expanding commercial off-season vegetable and floriculture enterprises.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital grants to construct brand-new, modern naturally ventilated polyhouse arrays:
  * 85% capital financial subsidy to construct polyhouse domes (ranging from 100 square meters up to 500 square meters per applicant).
  * 50% matching subvention for installing integrated micro-irrigation drip kits, automated foggers, and ventilation controllers inside the structure.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of Himachal Pradesh aged 18 to 45 years. The plot must be free of legal encumbrances and clear of high-voltage overhead electric transmission lines.
* **How to Apply:** Submit form profiles and Detailed Project Reports (DPR) through the Department of Agriculture portal or directly at the Block Agriculture Development Office.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Bonafide Certificate, updated Jamabandi & Tatima extracts, manufacturer proforma invoice, and an unemployment self-declaration affidavit.

---

### [HP_SCHEME_007] Mukhya Mantri Anti-Hail Net Subsidy Yojana
* **Category:** Crop Canopy Protection & Climate Safeguards
* **Target Beneficiary:** Apple, pear, cherry, plum, and stone-fruit orchardists across hail-prone mountain valley belts.
* **Benefit Details:** Protects high-value fruit crops from severe localized hailstorms:
  * Direct capital financial subsidy of 80% of the institutional benchmark cost to purchase and erect high-durability, UV-stabilized anti-hail nets over fruit canopies.
  * Maximum physical canopy coverage ceiling is capped at 1 Hectare (approx. 12.5 Bighas) per unique farming household.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual orchardists with land records showing active fruit-bearing trees, verified by the local Horticulture Development Officer (HDO).
* **How to Apply:** Handled offline through the regional Zonal Horticulture Office (ZHO) or circle horticulture extension desks.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Krishi/Horti Card, updated Jamabandi & Tatima, and an equipment proforma invoice from a department-empanelled vendor.

## Section 2: Clean Energy Mountain Irrigation & Natural Farming

### [HP_SCHEME_004] Saur Sinchayee Yojana (Himachal Solar Pumping Scheme)
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Renewable Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholders, marginal farmers, and community agricultural clusters requiring dependable off-grid daytime irrigation in non-electrified mountain terrains.
* **Benefit Details:** Deploys standalone solar photovoltaic water pumping units (2 HP, 3 HP, and 5 HP surface or submersible packages) to lift water from mountain streams (*khads*), open springs, and borewells:
  * **Individual Small & Marginal Farmers:** 90% capital subsidy; the farmer contributes a token margin share of only 10%.
  * **Community Farming Clusters (Group Schemes):** 100% state-funded financial grant to establish joint community solar lift irrigation networks on mountain streams.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must have a verified perennial gravity stream/khad, spring, or borewell source. The plot must not hold a pre-existing grid-connected electric pump connection.
* **How to Apply:** Online application via the state agriculture portal (`hpagriculture.com`), followed by technical vetting by ZAO engineers and the Jal Shakti Vibhag.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Bonafide Himachali Certificate, Land Jamabandi extract, water availability certificate from Jal Shakti Vibhag, and an affidavit confirming zero grid-meter ownership.

---

### [HP_SCHEME_008] Prakritik Kheti Khushhal Kisan Yojana (PK3Y Core)
* **Category:** Sustainable Natural Agriculture Production Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers transitioning agricultural and orchard land from chemical fertilizers/pesticides to Subhash Palekar Natural Farming (SPNF) practices.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive transition package for chemical-free farming:
  * Direct cash incentive transfer of ₹8,000 per acre (split across three annual tranches).
  * 75% financial subsidy for constructing on-farm bio-input preparation pits (*Jeevamrit / Ghanjeevamrit* storage drums and tanks).
  * Flat cash subvention grant of ₹25,000 to purchase indigenous dairy cows (Red Sindhi, Sahiwal, or Tharparkar) whose dung and urine form the basis of natural farming formulations.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators must dedicate a minimum of 0.5 Acres to zero-chemical natural farming, validated via spot soil tests and field inspections.
* **How to Apply:** Digital registration is submitted through the dedicated SPNF portal (`spnf.hp.gov.in`) or via Block Technology Coordinators (ATMA).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique Farmer ID, baseline soil health reference card, and bank passbook.

## Section 3: Farmer Social Security, Dairy & Pastoral Livestock

### [HP_SCHEME_002] Mukhya Mantri Kisaan Evam Khetihar Mazdoor Jeevan Suraksha Yojana
* **Category:** Agricultural Social Security & Accident Compensation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers, orchardists, tenant cultivators, and manual agricultural laborers aged between 15 and 65 years.
* **Benefit Details:** State-funded accidental compensation for fatal or disabling injuries caused while operating agricultural machinery (power tillers, tractor attachments, threshers, motorized sprayers, orchard gravity ropeways) or wildlife/snake attacks:
  * **Accidental Death:** Flat statutory compensation payout of ₹3 Lakh to the registered legal nominee family.
  * **Total Permanent Disability (Loss of both limbs or both eyes):** Compensation payout of ₹2 Lakh.
  * **Partial Permanent Disability (Loss of one hand, foot, or severe structural injury):** Compensation payout of ₹50,000 to ₹1 Lakh.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Accident must occur during active farm or orchard duties within Himachal Pradesh. Claims must be submitted within a strict 3-month window from the date of the incident.
* **How to Apply:** Submit itemized claim files to the nearest Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA) or Sub-Divisional Agriculture Officer (SDAO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, certified Post-Mortem Report or Government Medical Board Disability Certificate, Police FIR / Inquest report, and a verification certificate signed by the Gram Panchayat Pradhan.

---

### [HP_SCHEME_006] Rajiv Gandhi Swarnim Rozgar Yojana (Agri-Allied Bovine Component)
* **Category:** Livestock Enterprise & Allied Livelihood Expansion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural unemployed youth, women dairy farmers, and traditional cattle breeders establishing modern dairy units.
* **Benefit Details:** Credit-linked capital investment subsidy for mini-dairy breeding farms:
  * **2 to 10 Milch Animal Units (Jersey Cross or Murrah Buffaloes):** 50% capital financial subsidy for women and SC/ST applicants; 25% subsidy for general category applicants.
  * Covers construction of modern concrete sheds, automated milking machines, and motorized chaff cutters.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent residents of HP aged 18 to 45 years holding or leasing land for green fodder cultivation.
* **How to Apply:** Submit applications online under the Animal Husbandry tab on the state portal, verified by the local Veterinary Officer.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, land availability verification layout / 5-year lease deed, dairy training completion certificate, and bank passbook.

---

### [HP_SCHEME_009] High-Altitude Sheep & Goat Breed Improvement Project
* **Category:** Allied Sector Pastoralism & Mountain Livelihood Expansion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Traditional migratory pastoralists (Gaddi and Gujjar communities) and high-altitude sheep/goat rearers.
* **Benefit Details:** Livestock genetic improvement and grazing support:
  * 60% to 75% capital grant for setting up semi-intensive breeding stations and winter shelters.
  * Free distribution of elite purebred parental rams (Rambouillet or Australian Merino crossbreds) to improve wool quality and micron count.
  * Direct linkage to the HP State Wool Procurement and Marketing Board collection centers for guaranteed floor price payouts.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Migratory pastoralists holding an active Alpine Forest Grazing Permit issued by the State Forest Department.
* **How to Apply:** Submit physical application folders to the District Animal Husbandry and Veterinary Officer.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, ST Community Certificate, active Forest Grazing Permit booklet copy, and RFID ear-tag tracking logs.

## Section 4: Precision High-Tech Orchard Drones

### [HP_SCHEME_003] Himachal Pradesh Agri-Drone Subsidy Core Module
* **Category:** Modernization & Precision High-Tech Farming
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive orchardists, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), women's SHGs, and agricultural graduates.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial subventions for deploying DGCA-certified agricultural drones for aerial orchard protective spraying (anti-fungal, micro-nutrients) across steep hillside apple terraces:
  * **FPOs, Cooperatives & KVKs:** 100% financial grant (capped at ₹5 Lakh) for acquiring agricultural drone packages.
  * **Individual Agri-Graduates / SC-ST Smallholders:** 50% capital subsidy (capped at ₹4 Lakh).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Drones must be sourced from DGCA-approved empanelled manufacturers. Individual operators must hold an active Remote Pilot Certificate.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted digitally through the high-tech mechanization tab of the state agriculture portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Bonafide Certificate, DGCA Remote Pilot License, Drone UIN, and equipment vendor proforma quotes.

## Section 5: Ground Application Workflows & Himachali Lexicon

| Regional Term | Operational Context & Significance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Jamabandi & Tatima** | Record of Rights (*Jamabandi*) paired with the certified land map (*Tatima*) proving parcel dimensions and ownership in HP. |
| **Bigha / Biswa** | Standard land measurement units in HP (1 Hectare ≈ 12.5 Bighas; 1 Bigha = 20 Biswas ≈ 800 sq meters). |
| **Bonafide Himachali** | Mandatory state domicile certificate required to qualify for land-linked agricultural and polyhouse subsidies. |
| **ZAO / ZHO** | Zonal Agricultural Officer / Zonal Horticulture Officer, responsible for field inspections and subsidy approvals. |
| **Khad** | Perennial mountain streams serving as the primary surface water source for *Saur Sinchayee* solar lift irrigation. |
| **Gaddi / Gujjar** | Traditional migratory pastoralist tribes eligible for subsidized Rambouillet rams and wool procurement floor prices. |
| **Orchard Ropeways** | Gravity or motorized ropeways used to transport apple boxes across gorges; covered under *Jeevan Suraksha* accident insurance. |
