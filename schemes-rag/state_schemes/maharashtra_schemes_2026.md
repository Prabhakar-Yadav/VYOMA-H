## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Maharashtra's state agricultural policy framework is structured around the distinct agro-climatic demands of rainfed drought-prone zones (Vidarbha and Marathwada), coastal high-rainfall belts (Konkan), and irrigated cash-crop plains (Western Maharashtra / Desh):
1. **Direct Income Security:** Delivered via the Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana (NSMNY), which provides a matching state top-up of ₹6,000/year directly over PM-KISAN, giving eligible farmers a cumulative ₹12,000 annually.
2. **Climate-Resilient Infrastructure (PoCRA & Magel Tyala Shetale):** Massive deployment of localized water-budgeting, 300-micron lined farm ponds, and saline soil reclamation kits across 5,000+ vulnerable villages.
3. **Renewable Energy & Value Chains:** Day-time farm power security through 90%–95% subsidized Mukhyamantri solar agriculture pumps, paired with ₹1 Crore agribusiness infrastructure grants for Farmer Producer Organizations under the SMART project.

## Section 1: Direct Income Support & Rural Social Security

### [MAHA_SCHEME_001] Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana (NSMNY)
* **Category:** Direct Income Support & Financial Stabilization
* **Target Beneficiary:** All landowning smallholder and marginal farming households registered within Maharashtra.
* **Benefit Details:** Operates as an identical matching cash top-up linked to the central PM-KISAN database:
  * The Maharashtra Government disburses a flat cash grant of ₹6,000 per financial year via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each.
  * When combined with PM-KISAN's ₹6,000 allocation, each registered landowning family receives a total annual income support of ₹12,000 directly into their Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active landholding farming families verified on the state's land records database who are active, verified beneficiaries of the central PM-KISAN scheme.
* **How to Apply:** Handled automatically through the state portal (`nsmny.mahait.org`). Ground validations are executed by the local Talathi (Patwari) and Circle Officer.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, updated 7/12 (Saat-Bara) and 8-A Utara extracts, unique PM-KISAN Registration ID, and NPCI-mapped bank passbook.

---

### [MAHA_SCHEME_006] Gopinath Munde Shetkari Apghat Suraksha Yojana
* **Category:** Agricultural Social Security & Accident Insurance
* **Target Beneficiary:** Registered landholding farmers and landless manual agricultural laborers aged between 10 and 75 years across Maharashtra.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive state-funded accidental death and severe disability insurance covering farming-related hazards (tractor overturns, farm machine injuries, electric shocks from tubewells, pesticide poisoning, lightning strikes, and snake bites in fields):
  * **Accidental Death:** Flat financial compensation of ₹2 Lakh paid directly to the registered legal nominee family.
  * **Total Permanent Disability (Loss of two limbs, two eyes, or one limb + one eye):** Compensation payout of ₹2 Lakh.
  * **Partial Permanent Disability (Loss of one limb or one eye):** Compensation payout of ₹1 Lakh.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** The accident must occur within Maharashtra during agricultural or allied farm operations. Formal claims must be lodged within a strict 90-day window from the date of the incident.
* **How to Apply:** Submit itemized claim files to the Taluka Agriculture Officer (TAO) or the local Revenue Department Circle Officer.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, 7/12 Utara copy, certified Post-Mortem Report or Government Medical Board Disability Certificate, Police FIR / Inquest Panchnama, and a verification certificate from the village Police Patil.

## Section 2: Climate-Resilient Agriculture, Solar Power & Water Security

### [MAHA_SCHEME_002] Nanaji Deshmukh Krishi Sanjivani Yojana (PoCRA)
* **Category:** Climate-Resilient Agriculture & Water Budgeting
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal farmers located in climate-vulnerable and drought-prone villages (covering 15+ districts primarily in Vidarbha and Marathwada).
* **Benefit Details:** World Bank-assisted project focused on climate-proofing rainfed cropping systems:
  * **Micro-Irrigation & Farm Ponds:** 75% to 85% capital subsidy (up to ₹75,000) for digging individual farm ponds, installing 300-micron plastic lining, and setting up drip/sprinkler systems.
  * **Saline Soil Reclamation:** Provision of subsidized soil amendment kits (Gypsum and agricultural lime) to restore productivity in severe salt-affected agricultural belts (*Purna river basin*).
  * **Community Water Budgeting:** 100% grant for village-level water harvesting trenches and recharge shafts.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must fall within designated PoCRA project villages. Priority is given to smallholders owning less than 2 Hectares of land.
* **How to Apply:** Farmers register and apply online through the dedicated PoCRA portal (`dbt.mahapocra.gov.in`).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, 7/12 & 8-A land Utara extracts, caste certificate (if claiming priority subsidy bands), and soil health baseline testing parameters.

---

### [MAHA_SCHEME_005] Chief Minister's Solar Powered Agriculture Pump Scheme (Mukhyamantri Saur Krishi Vahini / Pump Component)
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Renewable Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual farmers requiring dependable day-time irrigation in non-electrified pockets, load-shedding rural feeders, or forest-adjacent farms.
* **Benefit Details:** Implemented through the Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Company Limited (MSEDCL / Mahadiscom):
  * Deploys standalone DC/AC solar water pumping systems (3 HP, 5 HP, and 7.5 HP surface or submersible configurations).
  * **Subsidy Structure:** Small, marginal, and SC/ST farmers receive an elevated 90% to 95% total subsidy (combining central PM-KUSUM CFA and state matching grants). The beneficiary pays only a token 5% to 10% share (₹5,000 to ₹10,000 for a 3 HP system).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must possess an active, verified open dugwell or deep borewell water source. The plot must not have an existing grid-tied conventional electric farm connection.
* **How to Apply:** File applications on the dedicated Mahadiscom portal (`mahadiscom.in/solar`) or route through the MahaDBT gateway.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, 7/12 Land Utara, water source availability certificate signed by a Junior Line Engineer, and bank passbook.

---

### [MAHA_SCHEME_007] Magel Tyala Shetale Yojana (On-Demand Farm Pond Scheme)
* **Category:** Rainfed Water Harvesting & Micro-Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Dryland farmers, slope-land cultivators, and marginal landholders seeking dedicated on-farm water storage.
* **Benefit Details:** Operates on an on-demand asset entitlement model:
  * Direct capital cash subsidy of ₹50,000 credited to the farmer's bank account upon completion of standard on-farm pond excavation (dimensions from 30x30x3 meters down to 15x15x3 meters).
  * 75% financial subsidy for procuring UV-stabilized plastic lining sheets (minimum 300-micron thickness) to prevent seepage loss in porous basalt soils.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Individual landholders with a minimum contiguous agricultural plot of 0.6 Hectares. Alignment must allow natural monsoon runoff capture.
* **How to Apply:** Apply digitally via the MahaDBT portal (`mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in`) under the *Magel Tyala Shetale* module.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, updated 7/12 and 8-A Utara extracts, site layout map certified by the Assistant Agriculture Officer, and bank passbook.

## Section 3: Sustainable Bio-Inputs & Protected High-Tech Horticulture

### [MAHA_SCHEME_003] Dr. Punjabrao Deshmukh Krishi Kuyva Yojana (Organic Conversion)
* **Category:** Sustainable Inputs & Bio-Farming Production Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landholders and farmer clusters transitioning out of chemical inputs to certified organic farming.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct financial incentives to offset transition and bio-input costs:
  * Direct financial incentive grant of ₹20,000 per Hectare (disbursed in three annual tranches) for purchasing certified bio-fertilizers, vermicompost kits, and green manure seeds.
  * 75% capital subsidy to construct on-farm bio-input preparation pits (Jeevamrut / Dashaparni Ark preparation units).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Dedicating a minimum contiguous land parcel of 0.4 Hectares to 100% chemical-free organic farming.
* **How to Apply:** Apply through the MahaDBT portal (`mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in`) under the Organic Agriculture development tab.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, 7/12 and 8-A land extracts, organic self-declaration affidavit, and bank passbook.

---

### [MAHA_SCHEME_010] Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj High-Value Horticulture Subsidy
* **Category:** High-Tech Horticulture & Controlled Environment Agriculture
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers, rural youth, and floriculture/horticulture growers establishing protected cultivation infrastructure.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidies for building climate-controlled greenhouses, polyhouses, and shade-net structures for high-value crops (coloured capsicum, Dutch roses, carnations, cherry tomatoes):
  * 50% capital investment subsidy up to an individual financial cap of ₹25 Lakh for naturally ventilated polyhouses, GI pipe framing, UV-stabilized cladding films, and automated fogging/micro-irrigation equipment.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident farmers with clear land titles and an assured year-round irrigation source. Minimum plot footprint required is 1,000 square meters.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications and site layout blueprints are processed through `mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in` under the National Horticulture Mission (NHM) state top-up component.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, 7/12 & 8-A land extracts, engineering proforma invoices from an empanelled structure fabricator, and water quality testing reports.

## Section 4: Post-Harvest Value Chains, Farm Mechanization & Agri-Drones

### [MAHA_SCHEME_004] MahaDBT Farm Mechanization Subvention Scheme
* **Category:** Farm Mechanization & Automation Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual landholders, women cultivators, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and village cooperatives.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidies for purchasing modern agricultural machinery suited for Maharashtra's crop patterns:
  * **Tractors (up to 40 PTO HP):** 40% capital subsidy (capped at ₹45,000) for general category; 50% subsidy (capped at ₹60,000) for small, marginal, SC, ST, and women farmers.
  * **Power Tillers, Rotavators, Harvesters & Multi-Crop Threshers:** 50% to 60% direct capital subsidy to address acute seasonal labor shortages during harvesting windows.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to registered farmers on MahaDBT. Restricted to one subsidized tractor/power machine purchase per farming household every 10 years.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted on `mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in`. Beneficiary allotment is executed dynamically through a computerized randomized lottery system.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, recent 7/12 & 8-A Utara sheets, caste certificate (if claiming priority subsidy bands), and dealer proforma invoice.

---

### [MAHA_SCHEME_008] SMART Project (Hon. Balasaheb Thackeray Agribusiness & Rural Transformation)
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Infrastructure & Market Value Chain Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), women's Self Help Group (SHG) federations, and Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS).
* **Benefit Details:** World Bank-assisted project transforming post-harvest aggregation, grading, and direct market linkages for key crops (cotton, soybean, onions, turmeric, pomegranates, and grapes):
  * Matching capital investment grant of 60% (up to a massive ceiling of ₹1 Crore per project layout) for establishing centralized sorting/grading hubs, cold chains, primary processing mills, and vacuum-packaging lines.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered FPOs/federations with at least 11 active farmer shareholders and verified operational business turnover. Land must possess non-agricultural (NA) industrial setup clearance.
* **How to Apply:** Detailed Project Reports (DPR) and business plans are uploaded through the dedicated SMART portal (`smart-mh.org`).
* **Documents Required:** Commercial PAN card, FPO incorporation certificate, board resolution, audited balance sheets, bank loan approval sanction letter, and NA land title deeds.

---

### [MAHA_SCHEME_009] Maharashtra Agri-Drone Tech-Infusion Module
* **Category:** Precision Agriculture & High-Tech Automation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive cultivators, rural youth agri-entrepreneurs, Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs), and registered FPOs.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial subventions for deploying DGCA-certified agricultural drones for aerial pesticide spraying, nano-urea application, and crop health mapping:
  * **FPOs & Cooperatives:** 100% financial grant (capped at ₹5 Lakh) for acquiring agricultural drone units and technical ground battery charging stations.
  * **Individual Agri-Graduates / SC-ST Smallholders:** 50% capital subsidy (capped at ₹4 Lakh).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Drones must be sourced from DGCA-approved empanelled manufacturers. Individual operators must hold an active Remote Pilot Certificate issued by a DGCA-authorized training institution.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are processed through the high-tech mechanization section on the MahaDBT portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, 7/12 Utara, DGCA Remote Pilot License, drone Unique Identification Number (UIN), and equipment invoice quotes.
