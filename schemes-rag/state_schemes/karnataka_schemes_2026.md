# Karnataka State Government Schemes Master Database (2026 Edition)
> **Comprehensive State-Level Agricultural & Environmental Policy Framework**  
> **State Code:** `KA` / `KAR`  
> **Key State Focus Areas:** Unified Farmer Database (FRUITS / FID), Educational Social Security (*Raitha Vidya Nidhi*), Dryland Water Conservation (*Krishi Bhagya Yojana*), Input E-Vouchers (*Suvarna Krishi Nishta*), Climate-Resilient Organic Millets (*Savayava Bhagya*), Hobli-Level Custom Hiring Centers (*Krishi Yanthra Dhare*), KMF Dairy Expansion, Precision Drone Subsidies

## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Karnataka's state agricultural policy is anchored by its centralized digital infrastructure (FRUITS - Farmer Registration and Unified Beneficiary Information System) and tailored for the distinct agro-climatic demands of the arid North Karnataka drylands, South Karnataka red-loam plains, and coastal/hilly Malnad belts:
1. **Unified Digital Access (FRUITS & FID):** Every farming household is assigned a unique Farmer ID (FID) mapped directly to digital land records (*RTC / Pahani* on the Bhoomi platform), eliminating physical paperwork for scholarships, input subsidies, and crop insurance.
2. **Dryland & Rainfed Water Security (Krishi Bhagya):** Massive roll-out of 80%–90% subsidized on-farm polythene-lined rainwater harvesting ponds (*Krishi Bhagya*) paired with micro-sprinklers and diesel pump sets.
3. **Nutri-Cereals & Climate-Resilient Agriculture:** Supported through *Savayava Bhagya* cash bonuses (₹10,000/ha) for traditional organic millets (*Ragi, Jowar, Bajra, Foxtail millet*) and affordable farm machinery rental access via hobli-level *Krishi Yanthra Dhare* centers.

## Section 1: Direct Social Security, Welfare & Education

### [KAR_SCHEME_001] Raitha Vidya Nidhi Scheme
* **Category:** Agricultural Family Social Security & Welfare
* **Target Beneficiary:** Children of all registered landholding farmers, tenant cultivators, and manual agricultural laborers across Karnataka.
* **Benefit Details:** State-funded educational scholarship designed to eliminate rural student dropouts:
  * Transfers an annual direct cash scholarship ranging from ₹2,000 to ₹11,000 per student per year via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into bank accounts.
  * Covers higher secondary, ITI, diploma, general undergraduate, post-graduate, engineering, nursing, and medical degree programs.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** The parent must be a registered farmer holding an active Farmer Identification Code (FID) on the central FRUITS portal.
* **How to Apply:** Auto-entitlement checks are executed dynamically by syncing the State Scholarship Portal (SSP) directly with the FRUITS land registry database. No separate physical applications are required.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card of student and parent, verified FRUITS FID Number, and active college enrollment identity numbers.

---

### [KAR_SCHEME_004] Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY - Karnataka BeED Model Integration)
* **Category:** Crop Insurance & Yield Risk Mitigation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Landholders, tenant farmers, and registered sharecroppers cultivating notified food grains, pulses, and oilseeds.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive crop insurance linked with state-level premium absorption:
  * Farmers pay a flat premium barrier (1.5% for Rabi, 2% for Kharif, 5% for commercial crops), with the Karnataka Government funding the remaining premium balance.
  * Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) are GPS-tracked and co-managed via the *Samrakshane Portal*.
  * Claim payouts for yield loss, mid-season localized adversity, or post-harvest cyclone damages are settled directly into the farmer's bank account via automated payment bridges.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Acreage and crop sowing declarations must be logged on the state database prior to seasonal cutoff dates.
* **How to Apply:** File applications online through the integrated Samrakshane Portal (`samrakshane.karnataka.gov.in`) or via Primary Agricultural Cooperative Societies (PACS).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, FRUITS FID Code, latest RTC (Pahani) land document, and bank passbook.

## Section 2: Rainfed Water Harvesting & Soil Moisture Management

### [KAR_SCHEME_002] Krishi Bhagya Yojana (Dryland Water Conservation)
* **Category:** Rainfed Irrigation & Water Catchment Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Dryland cultivators in rain-shadow and drought-prone districts (primarily across North Karnataka blocks).
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive on-farm water harvesting and micro-irrigation package:
  * 80% to 90% capital subsidy to excavate a standardized on-farm pond (dimensions 21m x 21m x 3m) lined with high-durability UV-stabilized polythene sheets to prevent seepage.
  * Includes 100% free supply of a diesel-powered water lifting pump set and specialized drip/sprinkler piping kits to provide critical protective irrigation during dry spells.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland in state-notified rainfed zones. Minimum contiguous landholding required is 1 Hectare.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted through periodic windows on `fruits.karnataka.gov.in` or registered at the local Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, FRUITS FID Code, RTC (Pahani) extract from Bhoomi, and equipment proforma invoices from an empanelled vendor.

---

### [KAR_SCHEME_003] Karnataka Suvarna Krishi Nishta Yojana (Input Quality Subsidy)
* **Category:** Crop Input Quality & Production Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders procuring certified high-yield seeds and bio-inputs.
* **Benefit Details:** Front-ended electronic input subsidy vouchers:
  * Direct digital voucher worth ₹5,000 per farmer to purchase certified foundation seeds (paddy, ragi, jowar, maize, and pulses), liquid bio-fertilizers, and micronutrient kits.
  * Dynamic e-vouchers are redeemed instantly at local Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK) storage counters without cash handling.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent resident farmers holding an active status on Bhoomi/FRUITS. Maximum benefit boundary is capped at 2 Hectares per farming household.
* **How to Apply:** Vouchers are generated online via the FRUITS portal ahead of seasonal sowing windows and presented at local RSK counters.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, unique FRUITS FID Number, and mobile number linked for automated OTP validation.

## Section 3: Sustainable Millets, Dairy & Allied Enterprises

### [KAR_SCHEME_008] Savayava Bhagya Yojana (Organic & Millet Cultivation Bonus)
* **Category:** Sustainable Agriculture & Millet Production Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rainfed cultivators growing traditional minor millets (Ragi, Jowar, Bajra, Foxtail millet / *Navane*, Little millet / *Same*) and certified organic crops.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial and bio-input incentives for climate-resilient farming:
  * Direct cash production bonus of ₹10,000 per Hectare for farmers who register and cultivate certified organic millets.
  * 75% financial subsidy for organic bio-input composting kits, green manure seed packets, and pheromone traps to eliminate synthetic chemical inputs.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Cultivators must form or be enrolled in a registered local organic farmers' cluster (minimum 50 acres per cluster). Land details must be active on the FRUITS registry.
* **How to Apply:** Applications open seasonally ahead of the Kharif cycle via `fruits.karnataka.gov.in` or through local Raitha Samparka Kendras (RSK).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, FRUITS FID Code, Organic Cluster Membership Certificate, and NPCI-mapped bank passbook.

---

### [KAR_SCHEME_005] Mukhyamantri Anila Bhagya Yojana (Agri-Allied Dairy Component)
* **Category:** Livestock Enterprise & Allied Sector Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholders, landless pastoral laborers, and women dairy collectives affiliated with local milk unions.
* **Benefit Details:** Capital subsidies to establish clean mini-dairy units:
  * **2-Milch Animal Units (Jersey Cross or Murrah Buffaloes):** 75% capital grant for SC/ST and women applicants; 50% subsidy for general categories.
  * Includes capital funding for constructing concrete cattle sheds, installing automatic milking claws, and procuring motorized chaff cutters.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent rural residents of Karnataka holding an active membership passbook with the Karnataka Cooperative Milk Producers' Federation (KMF / Nandini).
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted through primary Dairy Cooperative Societies (DCS) or routed digitally under the Animal Husbandry tab on the state agriculture portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, KMF/DCS membership ledger card number, land availability layout sketch or stamped lease agreement, and bank passbook.

## Section 4: Farm Mechanization, Custom Hiring & Precision Drones

### [KAR_SCHEME_007] Krishi Yanthra Dhare Yojana (Hi-Tech Custom Hiring Centres)
* **Category:** Agricultural Mechanization & Equipment Rental Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholders, marginal farmers, and tenant cultivators needing affordable access to heavy farm machinery.
* **Benefit Details:** Establishes specialized *Krishi Yanthra Dhare* Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) at the hobli (sub-taluk) level:
  * The state provides an 80% capital grant to registered NGOs, trusts, or cooperatives to set up well-equipped machinery banks.
  * Farmers can rent heavy-duty tractors, laser land levelers, paddy transplanters, sugarcane harvesters, and multi-crop combine harvesters at state-regulated rental tariffs that are 30% to 40% lower than open commercial market rates.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to all farmers holding a valid FRUITS FID Number. Priority rental booking tokens are automated for smallholders owning under 2 Hectares.
* **How to Apply:** Rental slots and machine bookings are scheduled via the *Krishi Yanthra Dhare Mobile App* or directly at the local hobli CHC center kiosk.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, FRUITS FID Number, and copy of the RTC Pahani land record.

---

### [KAR_SCHEME_006] Karnataka Agri-Drone Subvention Matrix
* **Category:** Modernization & Precision High-Tech Farming
* **Target Beneficiary:** Progressive farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), Custom Hiring Centres, and unemployed agricultural graduates.
* **Benefit Details:** Financial subventions to deploy DGCA-certified agricultural drones for aerial nano-urea application and automated crop spraying:
  * **FPOs & Cooperatives:** 100% financial grant (capped at ₹5 Lakh) to acquire complete agricultural drone packages with battery charging equipment.
  * **Individual Agri-Graduates / SC-ST Smallholders:** 50% capital subsidy (capped at ₹4 Lakh).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Drones must be sourced from DGCA-approved empanelled manufacturers. Individual operators must hold an active Remote Pilot Certificate issued by a DGCA-authorized training organization.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are filed under the high-tech mechanization tab of the FRUITS portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, FRUITS FID Code, DGCA Remote Pilot License copy, Drone Unique Identification Number (UIN), and equipment invoice quotes.

## Section 5: Ground Application Workflows & Kannada Lexicon

| Regional Term | Operational Context & Significance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **FRUITS Portal** | Farmer Registration and Unified Beneficiary Information System (`fruits.karnataka.gov.in`), the single-window source of truth for all scheme entitlements. |
| **FID (Farmer ID)** | Unique digital identifier assigned to every landholder/cultivator in Karnataka, auto-linking land, bank, and social welfare profiles. |
| **RTC (Pahani) / Bhoomi** | Record of Rights, Tenancy, and Crops (*Pahani*) generated digitally through the Bhoomi software, detailing land survey numbers and active crop cultivation. |
| **Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK)** | Hobli-level grassroots agricultural extension center providing seed minikits, bio-inputs, technical advice, and scheme verification. |
| **Hobli** | Administrative revenue sub-division grouping several villages, serving as the operational unit for *Krishi Yanthra Dhare* CHCs. |
| **Samrakshane Portal** | Dedicated state crop insurance portal (`samrakshane.karnataka.gov.in`) managing PMFBY enrollment, weather risk indices, and claim settlements. |
| **KMF (Nandini)** | Karnataka Cooperative Milk Producers' Federation, managing primary dairy societies (DCS) and milk procurement subsidies. |
