## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Jharkhand's agricultural policy framework is structured around the unique terrain of the Chota Nagpur plateau, characterized by undulating terrain, rainfed mono-cropping (primarily Kharif paddy), high soil erosion, and a large concentration of tribal and marginal farmers:
1. **Direct Input Injection & Zero-Premium Crop Insulation:** 100% state-funded *Jharkhand Rajya Fasal Rahat Yojana (JRFRY)* providing ₹3,000 to ₹5,000/acre direct relief for drought/flood losses (covering both title-holding *Raiyats* and landless *Non-Raiyats* without commercial insurance fees), alongside ₹5,000/acre input grants under *MKAY* and a ₹2 Lakh institutional debt waiver (*JKRMY*).
2. **Heavy Clean Energy Irrigation Subsidies (90%):** Overcoming erratic monsoons and high diesel expenses by funding 90% capital subsidies for 5 HP fixed or 2 HP portable solar water pumps (*Kisan Samriddhi*) and 90% subsidies on micro-drip layouts (*Per Drop More Crop*).
3. **Agroforestry Convergence & Tribal Livelihoods:** *Birsa Harit Gram Yojana* pairs 300 fruit saplings with 3 years of guaranteed daily MGNREGA maintenance wages, while the World Bank-assisted *JOHAR Project* deploys ₹50,000 grants to tribal women's SHGs for apiculture, micro-fisheries, and non-timber forest produce (Lac and Tamarind value chains).

## Section 1: Flagship Direct Income Support, Risk Insulation & Debt Elimination

### [JH_SCHEME_001] Jharkhand Rajya Fasal Rahat Yojana (JRFRY)
* **Category:** Crop Loss Relief & Risk Insulation (Zero-Premium Model)
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual landowning (*Raiyat*) and landless sharecropping (*Non-Raiyat*) farmers across all 24 districts of Jharkhand.
* **Benefit Details:** 100% state-funded compensation mechanism replacing commercial crop insurance:
  * Farmers pay zero insurance premiums.
  * Delivers direct financial compensation of ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 per acre based on certified crop loss percentages triggered by drought, unseasonal rainfall, floods, or pest attacks.
  * Disbursed directly via DBT based on satellite remote sensing and block-level Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident cultivators aged 18 years or older with valid seasonal crop booking logs. Assistance is capped at a family ceiling of 5 Acres.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications are submitted seasonally on `jrfry.jharkhand.gov.in` using Aadhaar-based biometric e-KYC or at local Pragya Kendras (CSC centers).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Possession Certificate (LPC) or verified *Vanshavali* lineage sheet (for Raiyats), self-declaration form verified by the local Ward Member/Mukhiya (for Non-Raiyats), and bank passbook.

---

### [JH_SCHEME_002] Mukhyamantri Krishi Aashirwad Yojana (MKAY)
* **Category:** Direct Investment Support & Sowing Assistance
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal landholders, with focused outreach across remote tribal blocks.
* **Benefit Details:** Targeted financial assistance to support land preparation, quality seed procurement, and fertilizer purchases:
  * Provides a direct cash assistance grant of ₹5,000 per acre per year.
  * Disbursed in a single lump-sum tranche via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly before the Kharif sowing cycle.
  * Maximum financial allocation is capped at ₹25,000 per year (for landholdings up to 5 Acres).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small and marginal farmers owning up to 5 Acres of agricultural land recorded in the state land revenue database (Jharbhoomi).
* **How to Apply:** Automated data matching occurs via mapped AgriStack and land revenue registries; missing records or grievances are resolved at the Circle Officer (CO) / Block Agriculture Office.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Revenue receipt / Pattadar records, linked mobile number, and bank passbook.

---

### [JH_SCHEME_007] Jharkhand Krishi Rin Mafi Yojana (JKRMY)
* **Category:** Institutional Agricultural Debt Relief
* **Target Beneficiary:** Short-term agricultural crop loan and Kisan Credit Card (KCC) holders in Jharkhand.
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive institutional debt elimination framework:
  * Waives outstanding formal short-term agricultural crop loans up to a revised statutory ceiling of ₹2 Lakh per farming family (upgraded from the earlier ₹50,000 threshold) via a one-time settlement mechanism.
  * Funds are transferred directly from the State Exchequer to empanelled commercial banks, Regional Rural Banks (Jharkhand Rajya Gramin Bank), and cooperative banks to clear outstanding liabilities and restore fresh institutional credit access.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Resident Raiyat or Non-Raiyat farmers holding standard KCC crop loan accounts mapped under a single family ration card. Limited to one loan waiver per household.
* **How to Apply:** Applied and verified through the official JKRMY portal (`jkrmy.jharkhand.gov.in`) with mandatory biometric e-KYC authentication at local Pragya Kendras (CSCs).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Kisan Credit Card (KCC) passbook, valid Family Ration Card, and active Crop Loan Account statements.

## Section 2: Renewable Irrigation & Precision Water Infrastructure

### [JH_SCHEME_003] Jharkhand Kisan Samriddhi Yojana
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Renewable Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Marginal farmers, cooperative clusters, Water User Associations (WUAs), and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).
* **Benefit Details:** Decarbonizes irrigation by replacing costly diesel pumps with solar systems:
  * Provides a massive 90% capital financial subsidy to install standalone solar water pumping installations. The farmer contributes a token margin share of only 10%.
  * **System Configurations:** Offers two specialized setups: 5 HP fixed solar pump units (covering up to 5 acres) or highly versatile 2 HP portable solar pump packages (covering up to 1 acre on scattered plots).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered farming entities aged 18+ with verified access to an open water body, river creek, dug-well, or borewell. Limited to one solar unit per household.
* **How to Apply:** Digital applications are logged on `ksy.jharkhand.gov.in` accompanied by biometric e-KYC.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, land mutation proof near the water source, water yield certificate, and a declaration confirming access to water sources.

---

### [JH_SCHEME_004] Micro Irrigation Scheme (Per Drop More Crop Module)
* **Category:** Precision Water Management & Soil Conservation
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers cultivating horticultural crops, commercial fruit orchards, or high-value winter vegetables on undulating hill slopes.
* **Benefit Details:** Overcomes severe soil erosion and rapid water runoff across hilly terrain:
  * Extends a high 90% state and central subsidy on the complete procurement, design, and physical installation of modern micro-drip and sprinkler systems.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers holding clear land revenue titles with access to a dedicated borewell, open well, or perennial lift irrigation stream.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted via the state PMKSY-PDMC portal (`jharkhandpdmc.com`) or filed with the District Horticulture Officer (DHO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Revenue tax receipt, vendor quotation proforma, and a physical sketch map of the micro-irrigation layout.

## Section 3: Diversification, Agro-Forestry & Tribal Livelihoods

### [JH_SCHEME_005] Birsa Harit Gram Yojana
* **Category:** Agro-Forestry & Livelihood Convergence
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural landless laborers, BPL households, and smallholders owning underutilized barren upland plots (*Tand land*).
* **Benefit Details:** Long-term fruit orchard asset generation combined with immediate wage relief:
  * Allocates up to 300 fruit-bearing saplings (high-yielding Mango—Amrapali/Mallika, Guava, and Lemon varieties) per beneficiary family to plant on 0.5 to 1 acre of unutilized private land.
  * Completely converged with MGNREGA to pay guaranteed daily cash wages to the beneficiary family for three consecutive years to carry out pit digging, trenching, fencing, weeding, and watering.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Rural households possessing up to 1 acre of uncultivated land holding an active MGNREGA Job Card.
* **How to Apply:** Executed locally through Gram Panchayat planning meetings during annual village action plans.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, MGNREGA Job Card, land possession verification document, and bank passbook.

---

### [JH_SCHEME_006] JOHAR Project (Jharkhand Opportunities for Harnessing Rural Growth)
* **Category:** Value Chain & Multi-Sector Allied Subsidies
* **Target Beneficiary:** Women-led Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and marginalized tribal communities across 17 target districts.
* **Benefit Details:** World Bank co-funded comprehensive livelihood diversification project:
  * Extends an operational ₹50,000 direct seed capital grant to tribal women's collective producer groups.
  * Funds technical training, processing machinery, and storage infrastructure for multi-location micro-fisheries, commercial apiculture (honey), goat and poultry breeding, and high-value non-timber forest produce—specifically Lac cultivation (*Kusmi & Rangeeni Lac*) and Tamarind value chains.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Registered members of village-level SHGs under the Jharkhand State Livelihood Promotion Society (JSLPS).
* **How to Apply:** Handled through localized block JSLPS cluster coordinators and Community Resource Persons (CRPs).
* **Documents Required:** SHG Membership Card, Individual Aadhaar Card, ST Community Certificate, and SHG group bank logbook.

## Section 4: Capacity Building, Seed Security & Digital Extension

### [JH_SCHEME_008] Kisan Pathshala (Birsa Gram Yojana Module)
* **Category:** Modern Capacity Building & Technical Upgrades
* **Target Beneficiary:** Smallholder farming groups, young agri-entrepreneurs, and women cultivators modernizing agricultural practices.
* **Benefit Details:** Decentralized agricultural excellence and practical training network:
  * Establishes over 100 model training farms (*Kisan Pathshalas*) across the state.
  * Delivers hands-on master classes focused on protected greenhouse cultivation, organic composting (*Vermi-beds*), high-density fruit orchards, micro-fisheries, and scientific dairy management.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to all registered smallholder farmer clusters, cooperatives, and individual cultivators.
* **How to Apply:** Walk-in enrollment managed via ATMA block technology managers or regional Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVK).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, baseline Farmer ID, and land records (if claiming group demonstration kit subsidies).

---

### [JH_SCHEME_009] Jharkhand Subsidized Seed Village Scheme (Beej Gram)
* **Category:** Core Sowing Input Security & Seed Multiplication
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small, marginal, and tribal farmers preparing for primary seasonal sowing.
* **Benefit Details:** Village-level seed self-reliance:
  * Supplies certified foundation and high-yielding variety (HYV) seeds of Paddy, Maize, Wheat, and Pulses at a 50% to 75% state-funded financial subsidy.
  * Trains farmer groups to multiply and store certified seeds locally, creating decentralized village seed banks (*Beej Gram*) that lower input costs for neighboring farmers.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Small, marginal, and cooperative farming units holding updated land mutation profiles.
* **How to Apply:** Distributed prior to Kharif and Rabi cycles through local Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) and Block Agriculture Offices (BAO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land Revenue tax receipt, and seasonal crop booking log.

---

### [JH_SCHEME_010] Jharkhand Kisan Call Centre (JKCC Helpline)
* **Category:** Digital Extension & Farmer Grievance Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** All cultivators and allied livestock operators requiring real-time diagnostic advice and scheme support.
* **Benefit Details:** Centralized tele-consultation platform:
  * Connects farmers directly with agricultural scientists, agronomists, and veterinarians via a dedicated toll-free helpline number.
  * Provides real-time guidance on pest identification, chemical/organic treatment recommendations, localized weather alerts, APMC Mandi market rates, and tracking for pending subsidy DBT payments.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open universal access to all agriculturalists and livestock rearers in Jharkhand.
* **How to Apply:** Accessible anytime via voice call directly on the state-notified toll-free line.
* **Documents Required:** None.
