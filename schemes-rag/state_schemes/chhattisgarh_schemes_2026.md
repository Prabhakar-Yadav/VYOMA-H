## Executive Summary & State Operational Context

Chhattisgarh's agricultural framework addresses the "Rice Bowl of Central India" across fertile central plains (*Mahanadi Basin*) and dense tribal forest belts (*Bastar and Surguja*):
1. **Direct Input Support & Landless Social Security:** Large-scale income stabilization via *Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana* (₹9,000/acre input subsidy across four installments) and *Bhumiheen Krishi Mazdoor Nyay Yojana* (₹7,000/year for landless rural agricultural workers).
2. **Circular Livestock Economy (Godhan Nyay & Gauthans):** State procurement of raw cattle dung at ₹2/kg inside community *Gauthans*, converted by women's SHGs into organic vermicompost sold back to farmers via PACS at ₹10/kg.
3. **Tribal Agro-Forestry & Minor Millets:** Guaranteed MSP procurement floor prices for tribal minor millets (*Kodo/Kutki* at ₹3,150/qtl and *Ragi* at ₹3,578/qtl) plus ₹10,000/ha production bonuses, along with 100% buyback support for wild forest Tasar silk cocoons.

## Section 1: Direct Input Subsidies & Landless Social Security

### [CG_SCHEME_001] Rajiv Gandhi Kisan Nyay Yojana (RGKNY)
* **Category:** Direct Crop Input Subsidy & Price Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Individual landowning farmers and registered tenant cultivators growing notified crops (Paddy, Maize, Sugarcane, Ragi, Pulses, and Oilseeds) across Chhattisgarh.
* **Benefit Details:** Direct input assistance to offset operational costs:
  * Flat input cash subsidy of up to ₹9,000 per acre per year delivered directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
  * Disbursed across four equal quarterly installments aligned with agricultural sowing and harvesting cycles.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland acreage and crop types must be digitally registered on the state portal. Input assistance is capped at a maximum of 5 Acres per unique farming family. Institutional landholders are excluded.
* **How to Apply:** Mandatory digital registration on the unified integrated farmer portal (`kisan.cg.nic.in`). The local Rural Agricultural Extension Officer (RAEO) verifies field data before forwarding it to the block level.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card (linked to mobile), Land Possession Document (B1 Khasra extract from the CG Bhuinya Portal), NPCI-seeded bank passbook, and signed tenancy verification form (for tenant farmers).

---

### [CG_SCHEME_006] Rajiv Gandhi Grameen Bhumiheen Krishi Mazdoor Nyay Yojana (RGGBKMNY)
* **Category:** Livelihood Social Security for Landless Laborers
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural landless households whose primary source of livelihood is manual agricultural labor, pastoral herding, or traditional village craftsmanship (blacksmiths, carpenters, potters).
* **Benefit Details:** Targeted social safety net providing a flat annual cash grant of ₹7,000 per fiscal year transferred directly into the bank accounts of landless rural families to sustain income during agricultural lean seasons.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Permanent rural residents of Chhattisgarh who do not own any cultivable agricultural land anywhere in the state.
* **How to Apply:** Applications are submitted at the village level through the Gram Panchayat office and registered on the state's central web portal.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Resident Certificate, signed self-declaration certifying zero-landownership status, and an Aadhaar-seeded bank passbook.

## Section 2: Sustainable Inputs, Cattle Gauthans & Mechanization

### [CG_SCHEME_002] Godhan Nyay Yojana (Organic Input Value Chain Component)
* **Category:** Livestock-Agri Convergence & Sustainable Inputs
* **Target Beneficiary:** Livestock rearers, cattle owners, and village women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs) operating within community *Gauthans*.
* **Benefit Details:** Circular-economy cattle shed model:
  * The state government procures raw cow dung directly from farmers at a fixed floor rate of ₹2 per kg at village *Gauthans*.
  * Dung is handed over to women's SHGs to produce standardized, high-quality organic vermicompost.
  * The vermicompost is repackaged and sold back to farmers at a subsidized rate of ₹10 per kg through Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open to rural cattle owners and registered SHG members operating inside state-notified Gauthan community livestock premises.
* **How to Apply:** Farmers register livestock headcounts at the local Gauthan committee desk. Procurement logs are recorded via the Godhan Nyay mobile application for weekly online cash settlements.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Gauthan Membership Passbook, Animal Husbandry registry slip, and bank account passbook.

---

### [CG_SCHEME_003] Krishi Yantra Seva Kendra Yojana (CHC Subventions)
* **Category:** Agricultural Mechanization & Custom Hiring Banks
* **Target Beneficiary:** Rural agricultural entrepreneurs, progressive farmers, and primary cooperative societies (PACS).
* **Benefit Details:** Capital development grants to establish Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs):
  * 50% front-ended capital subsidy on machinery project packages valued at up to ₹10 Lakh or ₹25 Lakh to procure heavy farm equipment (tractors, rotavators, multi-crop threshers, straw reapers, power tillers).
  * Equipment is leased to local smallholders at state-regulated, affordable daily rental rates.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Rural residents aged 18 to 45 years holding verified technical credentials or diplomas in agriculture. Cooperative societies must hold active operational bank accounts.
* **How to Apply:** Digital project proposals and Detailed Project Reports (DPR) are submitted online via `agriengg.cg.nic.in`, scrutinized by the District Level Committee (DLC).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, educational qualification certificates, empanelled vendor machinery proforma invoices, PAN card, and land/shed availability blueprints.

## Section 3: Clean Energy Irrigation & Minor Pumping Infrastructure

### [CG_SCHEME_008] Saur Sujala Yojana (Solar Irrigation Expansion)
* **Category:** Clean Energy & Renewable Irrigation Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal cultivators in off-grid, non-electrified rural zones requiring dependable daytime irrigation power.
* **Benefit Details:** Implemented through the Chhattisgarh Renewable Energy Development Agency (CREDA):
  * Installs standalone solar photovoltaic water pumping systems (3 HP and 5 HP surface and submersible packages) at heavily subsidized rates.
  * **Small & Marginal Farmers:** Receive up to a 90%–95% capital subsidy, paying a token margin share of only approx. ₹7,000 to ₹10,000 for a complete 3 HP solar pump package.
  * **General Category Farmers:** Receive an 80% capital subsidy (upfront farmer contribution approx. ₹20,000).
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmland must possess an active, verified open dug-well, borewell, or surface water creek, and must lack a pre-existing grid power connection.
* **How to Apply:** Apply directly via the central CREDA portal (`creda.co.in`) or submit offline files to the block Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA) office.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Small/Marginal Farmer Certificate, updated B1 Khasra land sheet, water source feasibility certificate, and bank passbook.

---

### [CG_SCHEME_004] Chhattisgarh Shakambhari Yojana (Irrigation Pump Subsidy)
* **Category:** Minor Irrigation & Water Access Infrastructure
* **Target Beneficiary:** Small and marginal cultivators, with prioritized allocations for women and SC/ST farmers.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidizes minor irrigation equipment:
  * **Electric/Diesel Pump Sets:** 75% financial subsidy on functional irrigation pumps (up to 5 HP capacities).
  * **Open Dug-Well Construction:** 50% capital subsidy to excavate open irrigation dug-wells on smallholdings.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Open strictly to small and marginal farmers owning less than 2 Hectares (approx. 5 Acres) of cultivable land with a verified surface water source.
* **How to Apply:** Handled offline by submitting application forms to the block-level Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA) office.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Small/Marginal Farmer Certificate, updated B1 Khasra land sheet, and vendor proforma invoice.

## Section 4: Tribal Millets, Tasar Sericulture, Mandi Floor Relief & Fisheries

### [CG_SCHEME_007] Chhattisgarh Millet Mission (Ragi, Kodo, Kutki)
* **Category:** Crop Diversification & Nutrition Security Production Support
* **Target Beneficiary:** Tribal farmers, smallholders, and hill-slope cultivators growing minor millets (Ragi, Kodo, and Kutki) across 14 notified districts (primarily Bastar, Surguja, Kanker, Rajnandgaon).
* **Benefit Details:** Comprehensive price assurance and input production bonus:
  * **Guaranteed State Procurement:** Fixed floor procurement rate of ₹3,150 per quintal for Kodo and Kutki; ₹3,578 per quintal for Ragi.
  * **Input Grant Bonus:** Direct cash incentive bonus of ₹10,000 per Hectare delivered via DBT to cover certified seed minikits, organic manure processing, and bio-pesticides.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Farmers cultivating registered millets in notified tribal developmental blocks, mapped on the Kisan Portal by the local RAEO.
* **How to Apply:** Digital registration on `kisan.cg.nic.in`; physical grain deliveries are logged at nearby Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, B1 Khasra land record extract, linked mobile number for Mandi SMS tokens, and bank passbook.

---

### [CG_SCHEME_009] Resham Kukin Sahayata Yojana (Tasar Silkworm Forest Farming)
* **Category:** Agro-Forestry & Allied Sericulture Livelihood Expansion
* **Target Beneficiary:** Tribal forest-dwelling families, traditional weavers, and women sericulture self-help groups (SHGs).
* **Benefit Details:** Financial and technical assistance to rear wild Tasar and Mulberry silkworms on forest host trees (Arjun, Asan, and Saja):
  * 100% free distribution of Disease-Free Layings (DFLs) silkworm seed eggs and high-yielding saplings.
  * 100% state funding for constructing modern community silkworm rearing sheds (grant up to ₹1.2 Lakh per group).
  * Guaranteed 100% buyback of raw silk cocoons by the State Sericulture Federation at fixed floor market prices.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Tribal households or SHGs holding valid Forest Rights Act (FRA) land pattas or traditional community forest grazing rights.
* **How to Apply:** Handled offline by registering with the District Sericulture Officer or local technical sericulture expansion center.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, ST Community Certificate, Forest Rights Patta Document / Gram Sabha verification note, and bank passbook.

---

### [CG_SCHEME_005] Mukhyamantri Jinsh Suraksha Yojana (Mandi Price Fall Protection)
* **Category:** Post-Harvest Risk Management & Price Deficit Reimbursement
* **Target Beneficiary:** Farmers selling pulses, oilseeds, millets, and non-timber forest produce (NTFP) at local Krishi Upaj Mandi yards.
* **Benefit Details:** Protects growers against sudden post-harvest market price crashes:
  * The state Mandi Board directly reimburses 100% of the price deficit if wholesale prices fall below the state safety floor.
  * Provides 100% free warehouse storage at state agricultural warehouses for up to 3 months to allow farmers to delay selling until prices recover.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Produce must be traded inside authorized state Krishi Upaj Mandis with sales receipts logged on the e-Mandi network.
* **How to Apply:** Auto-enrolled upon generating an official sales agreement (*Anubandh Patra*) at any integrated state Mandi gate.
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, active Mandi Farmer Token reference number, and bank passbook.

---

### [CG_SCHEME_010] Mukhyamantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (Inland Fishery Development)
* **Category:** Allied Sector Aquaculture Optimization
* **Target Beneficiary:** Fish farmers, fisheries cooperative societies, and rural youth managing inland waterbodies and village ponds.
* **Benefit Details:** Subsidies to optimize freshwater fish production:
  * **Input Capital Grant:** 40% matching financial subsidy for general categories; 60% subsidy for SC, ST, and women collectives to procure fingerlings, pelleted feed, and surface aerators.
  * **Reservoir Cage Culture:** Capital support of up to ₹3 Lakh per cage assembly unit to install floating fish cages in large state reservoirs.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Active fish pond with a minimum area of 0.2 Hectares, or a registered multi-year lease on a panchayat village pond (minimum 5-year lease tenure).
* **How to Apply:** Submitted online through the Directorate of Fisheries portal (`agriportal.cg.nic.in/fisheries`), verified by the Assistant Fisheries Officer (AFO).
* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, Land B1 Khasra extract or registered Panchayat Pond Lease Agreement, itemized project cost estimate, and bank passbook.

## Section 5: Ground Application Workflows & Chhattisgarhi Lexicon

| Regional Term | Operational Context & Significance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **B1 Khasra (CG Bhuinya)** | Official digital land revenue record from `bhuinya.cg.nic.in` proving parcel ownership, crop sowing, and plot dimensions. |
| **Gauthan** | Village community cattle premises serving as the procurement hub for cow dung (₹2/kg) and vermicompost production under *Godhan Nyay*. |
| **RAEO** | Rural Agricultural Extension Officer, responsible for on-field crop verification and Kisan Portal approvals. |
| **PACS** | Primary Agricultural Credit Societies, acting as grassroots procurement points for paddy, millets, and subsidized vermicompost. |
| **CREDA** | Chhattisgarh Renewable Energy Development Agency, executing the *Saur Sujala* solar pumping program. |
| **Anubandh Patra** | Official sales contract receipt issued at Krishi Upaj Mandis, mandatory for Mandi price deficit claims. |
| **DFLs (Tasar Silk)** | Disease-Free Layings (silkworm eggs) distributed free of cost to tribal forest rearers under *Resham Kukin Sahayata*. |
