# Label triangle colours

Every registered pesticide sold in India carries a coloured triangle on the label.
A farmer can read this without literacy.

| Colour | Meaning | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| 🟥 **Red** | Extremely / highly toxic | Avoid if any alternative exists. Full protective covering essential. |
| 🟨 **Yellow** | Moderately toxic | Cover mouth, nose, hands. Keep others away. |
| 🟦 **Blue** | Slightly toxic | Normal precautions, still cover mouth and hands. |
| 🟩 **Green** | Comparatively safe | Basic precautions. |

# Before spraying

- Check the triangle colour on the container before buying.
- Buy only from a licensed dealer and **keep the bill**.
- Never buy loose or unlabelled chemical decanted into another bottle.
- Check the expiry date.

# While spraying

- Cover mouth and nose with a cloth; cover hands.
- **Spray with the wind at your back**, never against the wind.
- Do not eat, drink, or smoke.
- Do not blow into a blocked nozzle with your mouth — use a wire or water.
- Spray in early morning or late evening. Avoid the midday heat.
- Do not let children or animals into the field.

# After spraying

- Wash hands, face, and any exposed skin with soap and water.
- Wash the sprayed clothes separately from other clothes.
- Wash equipment away from ponds, canals, wells, and streams.
- Do not reuse empty containers for water, food, or storage. Break them and bury them.

# Pre-harvest interval

Every product has a waiting period between the last spray and harvest. Selling or
eating produce before that period has passed leaves chemical residue in the food.

**If a farmer is within the waiting period, they must not harvest — even if the
crop looks ready.**

# If someone is poisoned

1. Move the person into fresh air, away from the chemical.
2. Remove contaminated clothing. Wash the skin with plenty of water.
3. **Take the person and the pesticide container or label to a doctor immediately.**
   The label tells the doctor which antidote to use. This is the single most
   important thing to carry.
4. Do not make the person vomit unless a doctor says so.
5. National Poison Information Centre (AIIMS): **1800-116-117**
