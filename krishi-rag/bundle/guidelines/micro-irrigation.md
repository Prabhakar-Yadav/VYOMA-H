Micro-irrigation support reaches the farmer through three routes:
1. **Per Drop More Crop** under PMKSY and now RKVY — 55 percent for small and marginal farmers, 45 percent for others.
2. **State top-up** funded through the NABARD Micro Irrigation Fund — varies by state.
3. **Horticulture missions** — some horticulture crops carry drip support under MIDH in the same district.

Apply through one route at a time for the same plot. The district horticulture or agriculture officer will say which route is open this year.

⚠ Effective farmer share differs a lot between states because of the top-up.
