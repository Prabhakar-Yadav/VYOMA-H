**What the farmer gets**
An assured pension of Rupees 3,000 per month from the age of 60.
The Central Government contributes the same amount into the pension fund as the farmer pays in.
If the pensioner dies, the spouse receives 50 percent of the pension as family pension.
If the subscriber dies before 60, the spouse may continue the scheme or take the accumulated amount with interest.

**What the farmer pays**
A monthly contribution between Rupees 55 and Rupees 200, fixed by the age at entry.
The younger the farmer at entry, the smaller the monthly contribution.
Contributions continue until the age of 60.

A PM-KISAN beneficiary may allow the contribution to be deducted from the PM-KISAN benefit itself, so nothing has to be paid out of pocket.

**Who qualifies**
Small and marginal farmers aged 18 to 40 years, with cultivable landholding up to 2 hectares recorded in the state land records, qualify.
Enrolment is voluntary.

Farmers already covered by other statutory social security schemes such as EPFO, ESIC or NPS, and income tax payers, are served by those schemes instead.

**How to apply**
1. Visit the nearest Common Service Centre with documents. Or register at maandhan.in. Or contact the State PM-KISAN Nodal Officer.
2. The Village Level Entrepreneur enters Aadhaar, name, date of birth, address, mobile number, bank account and nominee details.
3. The system calculates the monthly contribution from the age.
4. Pay the first contribution in cash at the CSC.
5. Sign the enrolment-cum-auto-debit mandate form.
6. Receive the Kisan Pension Account Number and the pension card.

**Documents needed**
- Aadhaar
- Land record showing landholding up to 2 hectares
- Savings bank account or Jan Dhan account passbook with IFSC
- Mobile number
- Nominee details

**Deadline pattern**
Enrolment is open all year, for anyone aged 18 to 40.
Enrolling early keeps the monthly contribution low, since the amount is fixed by entry age.

⚠ The contribution table and the eligibility land ceiling are set in the operational guidelines and may be revised.
