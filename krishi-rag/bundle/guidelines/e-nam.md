**What the farmer gets**
An online trading platform that links APMC mandis across the country into one market.
Buyers from many mandis and many states can bid for the same lot.
The lot is quality assayed before bidding, so the buyer bids on a known quality.
Payment comes directly into the farmer's bank account.
Registration is free.

The platform covers gate entry, lot creation with a unique lot ID, quality assaying, open online bidding, live bid tracking, price discovery and electronic payment.
Quality assaying is available for a large list of commodities using Directorate of Marketing and Inspection specifications.

**Who qualifies**
Any farmer bringing produce to an e-NAM linked mandi qualifies.
FPOs may trade from the collection centre without physically moving the lot to the mandi.

**How to apply**
1. Visit the nearest e-NAM linked mandi with documents. Or self-register at enam.gov.in and complete verification at the mandi.
2. The mandi issues a farmer registration ID.
3. Bring the produce to the mandi. Gate entry is recorded and a lot ID is created.
4. A sample is drawn and assayed. The assay report is displayed with the lot.
5. Buyers bid online. The farmer sees the bids and accepts or declines the highest bid.
6. Payment is released to the bank account. Some states allow a part of the payment in cash at the mandi.

**Documents needed**
- Aadhaar
- Bank passbook or a cancelled cheque
- Mobile number
- Land record or a produce ownership proof where the state asks for it

**Deadline pattern**
Trading runs on mandi working days.
Arriving early in the day gives the lot time for assaying before the bidding session.

⚠ The list of linked mandis, assayed commodities and the cash payment limit differ by state and keep growing.
