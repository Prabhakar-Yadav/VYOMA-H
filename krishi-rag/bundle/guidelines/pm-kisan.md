**What the farmer gets**
Rupees 6,000 every year, paid straight into the bank account.
It comes in three instalments of Rupees 2,000 each.
The three instalment windows are April to July, August to November, and December to March.
The money is a direct benefit transfer. No repayment. No interest.

**Who qualifies**
A landholding farmer family — husband, wife and minor children — qualifies when farmland is recorded in their name in the village land records.
Both small and large landholding farmers qualify.
The land record must show the family as the owner.

Certain groups stay outside the scheme: institutional landholders, serving and retired government employees above Group D or Multi Tasking Staff level, income tax payers of the last assessment year, and practising doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants and architects.
Pensioners drawing a monthly pension of Rupees 10,000 or more also stay outside.

**How to apply**
1. Go to pmkisan.gov.in and open "New Farmer Registration". Or visit the nearest Common Service Centre. Or meet the village patwari, revenue officer or the State PM-KISAN Nodal Officer.
2. Enter the Aadhaar number and choose rural or urban farmer registration.
3. Fill land details exactly as they appear in the land record — survey number, khasra number, area.
4. Enter bank account and IFSC. The bank account must be linked to Aadhaar.
5. Complete e-KYC. Do this on the portal with an Aadhaar OTP, or at a CSC with a fingerprint, or through Face Authentication on the PM-KISAN Mobile App.
6. Track the application under "Know Your Status" using the registration number or mobile number.

**Documents needed**
- Aadhaar card
- Land record document — khatauni, khasra, RoR or 7/12 extract
- Bank passbook or account details with IFSC, Aadhaar-linked
- Mobile number
- Citizenship proof

**Deadline pattern**
Registration stays open all year.
The instalment a farmer receives depends on when verification finishes. A farmer verified inside a window receives that window's payment.
Completing e-KYC and Aadhaar-bank seeding keeps the payment flowing. Land seeding must also be marked "Yes" in the portal.

⚠ Instalment release dates shift. The exclusion list and the pension threshold are revised from time to time.
