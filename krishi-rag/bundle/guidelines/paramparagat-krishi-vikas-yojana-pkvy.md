**What the farmer gets**
Support of Rupees 31,500 per hectare over 3 years for farming organically in a cluster.
Of this:
- Rupees 15,000 per hectare goes straight to the farmer by direct benefit transfer for on-farm and off-farm organic inputs
- Rupees 4,500 per hectare covers marketing, packaging, branding and value addition
- Rupees 3,000 per hectare covers certification and residue analysis

Farmers also get training, exposure visits, and Participatory Guarantee System (PGS-India) organic certification.

**Who qualifies**
Farmers who join a cluster qualify. A cluster is formed of about 20 hectares, with roughly 50 farmers in the group.
Both small and large farmers may join.
The cluster commits to organic practice for the full 3-year conversion period.

**How to apply**
1. Talk to the block agriculture officer, the ATMA office or the Krishi Vigyan Kendra about the nearest PKVY cluster.
2. Join or help form a 20-hectare cluster with neighbouring farmers.
3. The cluster registers on the PGS-India portal (pgsindia-ncof.gov.in) through a Regional Council or the implementing agency.
4. Each farmer registers land parcels and keeps a field diary of inputs used.
5. Peer inspection happens within the group. The Regional Council issues the PGS green or organic certificate.
6. DBT assistance is released to the farmer's Aadhaar-linked account.

**Documents needed**
- Aadhaar
- Land record for every parcel entered into the cluster
- Bank passbook, Aadhaar-linked
- Group or cluster membership record

**Deadline pattern**
Clusters are sanctioned at the start of the financial year.
⚠ PKVY now runs under the Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana umbrella, alongside the National Mission on Natural Farming. Assistance amounts and cluster size are revised from time to time, and the North Eastern states run a separate Mission Organic Value Chain Development scheme with different rates.
