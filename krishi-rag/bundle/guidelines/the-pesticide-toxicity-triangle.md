Every registered pesticide container in India carries a coloured triangle on the label. The colour tells the toxicity class at a glance, without reading a word.

**Bright RED triangle — Extremely toxic**
Word on the label: POISON, with a skull and crossbones.
Oral LD50 of 1 to 50 mg per kg. Dermal LD50 of 1 to 200 mg per kg.
This is the most hazardous class. Handle with full protective gear, and treat any exposure as an emergency.

**Bright YELLOW triangle — Highly toxic**
Word on the label: POISON.
Oral LD50 of 51 to 500 mg per kg. Dermal LD50 of 201 to 2000 mg per kg.
Still very hazardous. Full protective gear applies.

**Bright BLUE triangle — Moderately toxic**
Word on the label: DANGER.
Oral LD50 of 501 to 5000 mg per kg. Dermal LD50 of 2001 to 20000 mg per kg.

**Bright GREEN triangle — Slightly toxic**
Word on the label: CAUTION.
Oral LD50 above 5000 mg per kg. Dermal LD50 above 20000 mg per kg.
This is the least hazardous class registered for use.

**How to use the colour**
Look at the triangle before buying and again before opening.
Prefer the green or blue class where an equally effective option exists for that pest.
Full protective gear applies to every colour. The colour tells how serious a mistake would be, and every class calls for care.

Also read on the label: the CIB&RC registration number, the crop and pest the product is approved for, the recommended dose, the waiting period before harvest, the antidote statement, and the expiry date.
