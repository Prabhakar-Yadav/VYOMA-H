# Soil and nutrition source material — Dr D. K. Shahi

Chief Scientist and Chairman, Department of Soil Science & Agricultural
Chemistry, Birsa Agricultural University, Ranchi. ~1,302 citations, 94
publications; profile at scholar.google.com/citations?user=VkZgg5MAAAAJ.

Contacted by the team; publications are publicly available.

---

## Why this source, and the gap it fills

The corpus today is 139 concepts about **things that attack plants**: 59
diseases, 12 entomology, 11 pathology, 26 pesticide records. On feeding the
crop there is exactly one file, and its job is to make the device refuse:

```
bundle/diseases/out-of-scope-nutrition.md
```

So "how much urea for my wheat" currently gets *I do not know*. Correct, since
inventing a fertiliser dose is as dangerous as inventing a pesticide dose — but
it closes off one of the commonest questions a farmer asks.

Shahi's field is exactly that gap: soil fertility, nitrogen use efficiency,
potassium dynamics, micronutrients, liming of acid soils, biofertilisers, and
pesticide residues.

---

## The constraint that shapes every concept written from this source

**His research is Jharkhand acid alfisols and red lateritic soils.** That is a
specific soil type in a specific agro-climatic zone. Potassium release in
Ranchi's acidic soils is not potassium release in Punjab's alluvium or
Maharashtra's black cotton soil, and a lime recommendation for pH 5.2 is
actively wrong advice on an alkaline soil.

This does not disqualify the source. It means every concept from it carries a
region tag, and the device says *"for acid soils like Jharkhand's"* rather than
implying national guidance. Same discipline the pesticide records got, where a
mancozeb dose is bound to its registered crop and never generalised.

**Second constraint, equally important.** These are research papers reporting
experimental findings, not extension recommendations. *"Treatment T4 gave the
highest yield at 120 kg N/ha"* is a result under one trial's conditions, not
advice to a farmer. Findings are recorded as findings, with the trial's context
attached; anything that becomes a number a farmer acts on must come from a
stated recommendation, not from us reading a results table.

## What is still needed before concepts can be written

**Full text.** Scholar gives titles, years and citation counts; it does not give
the numbers. A finding cannot be turned into a concept from its title. Needed:
the specific papers Dr Shahi pointed to, as PDFs or DOIs.

**Preference for recommendations over results.** If any of this work fed a BAU
package-of-practices or extension bulletin, that is a far better source for
farmer-facing doses than the underlying papers, because someone qualified has
already made the leap from finding to advice.

**The review question.** No agronomist has verified any of our 26 pesticide
doses. That has been the hard blocker since the corpus was built. A soil
scientist who has already spoken to the team is the best opening we have had —
his own residue work is directly adjacent to the waiting-period fields we leave
null.
