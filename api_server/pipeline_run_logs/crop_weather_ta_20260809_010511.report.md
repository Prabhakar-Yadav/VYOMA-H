**Turn 5** (crop-weather 5/5): Should I worry if my potato crop is getting attacked by more insects?

> Based on common knowledge of potato cultivation in Tamil Nadu region:
>
> Yes, you should be concerned. Potato crops are susceptible to several insect attacks that can cause significant damage. The most common threats include:
>
> Potato Aphids - Small green insects that suck sap from leaves and stems, causing yellowing, curling, and stunted growth
> Beetles (like the potato beetle) - These eat both leaves AND tubers/eyes on potatoes, creating visible holes in the plant or even damaging stored crops
>
> What to watch for:
> Yellow patches on leaves that look like webbing from aphids
> Small green bugs crawling on stems and undersides of leaves
> Holes in potato plants (especially near soil line) where beetles have fed
>
