# Farmer-facing instruction

> Mix 2 ml in one litre of water. For one acre use 405 ml in about 202 L of water. Do not harvest for 57 days after spraying.

# Safety

- The 57 day waiting period is long. Plan the spray well before the bunch is ready to cut.
- Rotate with a fungicide from a different group. Sigatoka develops resistance to strobilurins very quickly, and repeated use of this product alone will stop working.

# Non-chemical measures that carry most of the load

- Cut off and destroy infected lower leaves at every de-suckering round.
- Improve drainage and keep plant spacing wide enough for air to move.
