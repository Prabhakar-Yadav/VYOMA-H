# Farmer-facing instruction

> Mix 2.5 grams of Mancozeb 75% WP in one litre of water.
> For one acre, use 600 grams in 200 litres of water.
> Spray to cover both sides of the leaves.
> Do not pick the crop for **7 days** after spraying.

# Safety

- **Blue triangle** on the label — slightly hazardous.
- Cover mouth, nose, and hands. Wash with soap and water after spraying.
- Keep animals and children out of the field for **24 hours**.

See [handling and protective measures](/safety/spray-safety.md).

# Constraints

- **Pre-harvest interval: 7 days.**
- **Protectant only.** Mancozeb forms a barrier on the leaf surface and stops
  spores from establishing. It does **not** kill an infection already inside the
  leaf. Once lesions are visible, use a product with curative action such as
  [Cymoxanil + Mancozeb](/treatments/cymoxanil-mancozeb.md).
- Washed off by heavy rain — reapply after significant rainfall.
