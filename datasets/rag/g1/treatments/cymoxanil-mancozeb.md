# Farmer-facing instruction

> Mix 3 grams of Cymoxanil + Mancozeb in one litre of water.
> For one acre, use 600 grams in 200 litres of water.
> Spray to cover both sides of the leaves fully.
> Repeat after 7 to 10 days if the weather stays cool and wet.
> Do not pick the crop for **7 days** after spraying.

# Safety

- **Blue triangle** on the label — slightly hazardous.
- Cover mouth, nose, and hands. Do not spray against the wind.
- Wash with soap and water immediately after spraying.
- Keep animals and children out of the field for **24 hours**.

See [handling and protective measures](/safety/spray-safety.md).

# Constraints

- **Pre-harvest interval: 7 days.**
- Use when lesions are **already present** — cymoxanil gives curative action that
  a straight protectant like Mancozeb alone cannot.
- Maximum 3 sprays per season. Alternate with a different mode of action to
  avoid resistance in *Phytophthora*.
