# Farmer-facing instruction

> This is a soil drench, not a leaf spray. Mix 200 ml in 100 litres of water and pour it onto the soil around the plants. For one acre use 810 ml in about 400 litres of drench. Do not harvest for 193 days after applying.

# Safety

- The waiting period is 193 days, which is most of the crop. Apply early in the season, not near harvest.
- Registered on ginger only. Turmeric has a different registered product.

# Not registered on ginger, despite being commonly recommended

Mancozeb drench, Metalaxyl-MZ drench, and mancozeb or carbendazim rhizome seed treatment
are widely recommended for rhizome rot but **do not appear as ginger entries** in the
CIB&RC list as on 31.03.2026. Treat them as not registered on this crop.

# What actually controls rhizome rot

- Raised beds with sharp drainage. This is a waterlogging disease above all.
- Disease free seed rhizomes from a healthy field.
- Remove infected clumps together with the surrounding soil, and rotate for two to three years.
