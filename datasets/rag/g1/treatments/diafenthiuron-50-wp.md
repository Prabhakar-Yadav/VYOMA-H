# Farmer-facing instruction

> Mix 1.2 grams of Diafenthiuron 50% WP in one litre of water.
> For one acre, use 240 grams in 200 litres of water.
> Spray on the **underside** of the leaves, where the insects sit.
> Do not pick the crop for **21 days** after spraying.

# Safety

- **Yellow triangle** on the label — moderately hazardous.
- Cover mouth, nose, and hands. Do not spray against the wind.
- Do not eat, drink, or smoke while spraying.
- Wash with soap and water immediately after spraying.
- Keep animals and children out of the field for **24 hours**.
- No specific antidote. In case of poisoning, take the person and the pesticide
  container label to a doctor immediately.

See [handling and protective measures](/safety/spray-safety.md).

# Constraints

- **Pre-harvest interval: 21 days.** Do not harvest before this.
- Do not tank-mix with strongly alkaline materials such as Bordeaux mixture.
- Rotate with an unrelated mode of action; repeated use drives resistance in whitefly.
- Registered for **cotton**. Use on other crops is off-label.
