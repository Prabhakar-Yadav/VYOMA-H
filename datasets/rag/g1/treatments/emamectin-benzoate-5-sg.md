# Farmer-facing instruction

> Mix 0.4 grams of Emamectin benzoate 5% SG in one litre of water.
> For one acre, use 80 grams in 200 litres of water.
> **Direct the spray into the whorl** — the funnel at the top of the plant where
> the insect is hiding. Spraying only over the leaves will not reach it.
> Spray in the early morning or late evening, when the caterpillar is feeding.
> Do not pick the crop for **7 days** after spraying.

# Safety

- **Yellow triangle** on the label — moderately hazardous.
- Highly toxic to bees. Do not spray when the crop is flowering or bees are active.
- Toxic to fish. Do not wash equipment in ponds, canals, or streams.
- Cover mouth, nose, and hands. Wash with soap and water after spraying.
- Keep animals and children out of the field for **24 hours**.

See [handling and protective measures](/safety/spray-safety.md).

# Constraints

- **Pre-harvest interval: 7 days.**
- Maximum 2 sprays per season. Fall armyworm develops resistance quickly;
  alternate with [Chlorantraniliprole](/treatments/chlorantraniliprole-18-5-sc.md).
- Spray directed into the whorl, not broadcast over the canopy.
