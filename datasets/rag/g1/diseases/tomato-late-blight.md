# Visual symptoms

- **Water-soaked, wet-looking, greasy** dark green to brown-black patches, usually starting at leaf **tips and margins**.
- Patches are **irregular, blotchy and uniformly dark** — a plain solid dark area with an even colour throughout.
- On the **underside**, a fine **white fuzzy or downy growth** at the edge of the patch, most visible in early morning humidity.
- Patches spread **rapidly** and merge. Whole leaves collapse, blacken, and hang down.
- **Speed is the giveaway** — noticeable change from one day to the next, and a plant can collapse in 3–5 days.
- Dark brown to black greasy lesions on stems and petioles.
- On fruit: firm, greasy, brown blotches, often on the shoulder of the fruit.
- Affects leaves anywhere on the plant, not only the old ones at the bottom.

# Distinguishing from similar conditions

| Looks like | Tell it apart by |
|---|---|
| [Early blight](tomato-early-blight.md) | Early blight lesions have **concentric rings** like a target and start on **older, lower leaves**. Late blight has no rings and hits any leaf. |
| Bacterial spot | Bacterial spots are small, round, and remain separate; late blight lesions merge into large dead areas. |

# When it matters

**This is the highest-urgency condition in this bundle.** Favoured by cool
(15–22 °C) humid weather with leaf wetness. A field can be lost in under a week.
Action is warranted on **first detection** — do not wait for a threshold.

# Treatment

Do not read treatment text from this document. Retrieve the linked concepts:

- [Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP](/treatments/cymoxanil-mancozeb.md) — curative + protectant, use when lesions are already present
- [Mancozeb 75% WP](/treatments/mancozeb-75-wp.md) — protectant only, use before symptoms appear

# Non-chemical measures

- Remove and destroy infected plants; do not compost them.
- Improve air movement — wider spacing, staking, removal of lower leaves.
- Irrigate at the base, never overhead, and never in the evening.
- Do not plant tomato next to potato; the same pathogen moves between them.
