**What it is**
An umbrella scheme through which the Centre gives states money for agriculture, and states design the projects.
From 2024-25 the Ministry's centrally sponsored schemes were reorganised into two umbrellas — Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (PM-RKVY) for sustainable agriculture, and Krishonnati Yojana (KY) for food security and self-sufficiency.
PM-RKVY works as a cafeteria scheme. Each state chooses the components it needs and may move funds between components.

**What reaches the farmer**
Many familiar schemes now sit inside this umbrella, including:
- Soil Health and Fertility
- Per Drop More Crop micro-irrigation
- Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana organic farming
- Rainfed Area Development
- Agroforestry
- Crop Diversification Programme
- Agri-mechanisation and Crop Residue Management
- Agri Startup and Innovation support

So a farmer applying for a drip system, a soil test, an organic cluster or a stubble management machine is often applying under this umbrella, under the state's own scheme name.

**Who qualifies**
Farmers qualify through the individual component. Eligibility follows the component's rules.
Farmer Producer Organisations, SHGs, agri start-ups and rural entrepreneurs also access RKVY-funded projects.

**How to apply**
1. Ask the block agriculture officer or the district agriculture officer which RKVY components are running in the district this year.
2. Apply through the state agriculture department portal or the district office.
3. Follow the component's own document list and verification process.

**Deadline pattern**
⚠ Components, names and rates are decided by each state each financial year. Nothing here is fixed nationally. Always confirm the current year's state action plan at the block office.
