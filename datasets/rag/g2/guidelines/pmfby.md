**What the farmer gets**
Insurance cover on the notified crop for the full sum insured.
Cover runs from sowing right through to post-harvest.
The farmer pays a small share of the premium. The Central and State Governments pay the rest.

Farmer premium share:
- Kharif food crops and oilseeds — 2 percent of sum insured
- Rabi food crops and oilseeds — 1.5 percent of sum insured
- Annual commercial and annual horticultural crops — 5 percent of sum insured

**What is covered**
- Prevented sowing when rainfall fails or conditions turn adverse
- Standing crop loss from drought, dry spell, flood, inundation, landslide, pest attack, disease, natural fire, lightning, storm, hailstorm, cyclone and tornado
- Post-harvest loss for up to 14 days for a crop cut and left to dry in the field
- Localised calamity — hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloudburst and natural fire on an individual field

**Who qualifies**
Every farmer growing a notified crop in a notified area qualifies.
Sharecroppers and tenant farmers qualify when they can show insurable interest in the crop.
The scheme is voluntary for all farmers, including farmers with a crop loan.

**How to apply**
1. Choose the enrolment route — the pmfby.gov.in Farmer Corner, the Crop Insurance mobile app, a Common Service Centre, the bank branch that holds the crop loan account, or the empanelled insurance company agent.
2. Register with Aadhaar and mobile number.
3. Enter the season, the crop and the land details.
4. Pay the farmer premium share.
5. Collect the policy receipt. Under "Meri Policy Mere Haath", the policy document is delivered to the farmer.

Enrolment through a Common Service Centre carries no service charge for the farmer.

**Documents needed**
- Aadhaar card
- Bank passbook, Aadhaar-linked
- Land record — RoR, khasra or 7/12 extract
- Sowing certificate or self-declaration of sowing
- Tenancy agreement or a sharecropper declaration for tenant farmers

**Reporting a loss — the 72-hour rule**
When a localised or post-harvest loss happens, report it inside 72 hours.
Report through the Crop Insurance App, the pmfby.gov.in portal, the insurance company toll-free number, the bank, or the local agriculture officer.
Give the survey number and the affected area.
Helpline: **14447**.

**Deadline pattern**
⚠ Cut-off dates are notified season by season and differ by state and crop.
The common pattern is 31 July for Kharif and 31 December for Rabi.
Check the state notification each season. The notification also lists which crops are covered in which block.
