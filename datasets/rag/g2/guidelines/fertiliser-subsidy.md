**What the farmer gets**
Fertiliser at a subsidised price. The Government pays the subsidy directly to the manufacturer, so the farmer simply pays the lower shop price.

- **Urea**: sold at a statutorily notified Maximum Retail Price. A 45 kg bag costs Rupees 242, apart from neem-coating charges and applicable taxes. This price has stayed the same since March 2018.
- **Phosphatic and Potassic fertilisers (DAP, MOP, NPK complexes)**: covered by the Nutrient Based Subsidy scheme, running since 1 April 2010. A fixed subsidy is paid per nutrient, so the retail price stays affordable. Rates are approved by the Cabinet every Kharif and Rabi season.
- **DAP special package**: extra support is announced from time to time when world prices rise, to hold the DAP bag price steady.

**How the farmer buys subsidised fertiliser**
1. Go to a licensed retail outlet or a Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samruddhi Kendra (PMKSK).
2. Carry Aadhaar, or the Kisan Credit Card, or the voter ID.
3. The retailer records the sale on the Point of Sale (PoS) machine using biometric or Aadhaar authentication.
4. Collect the printed receipt showing the quantity and price.

**Buying the right quantity**
Match the fertiliser choice and quantity to the Soil Health Card recommendation for the crop.
Balanced use of N, P, K along with sulphur, zinc and boron gives a better response than nitrogen alone.
Nano urea and nano DAP are available as supplementary options through cooperative outlets.

⚠ NBS rates are revised every season. The DAP and complex fertiliser retail price can move. Urea MRP is the stable one.
