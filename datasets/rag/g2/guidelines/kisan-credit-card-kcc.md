**What the farmer gets**
A running credit limit for crop production, farm maintenance and household needs.
The card also covers allied activity — dairy, poultry, goatery, piggery, beekeeping and fisheries.

Interest terms under the Modified Interest Subvention Scheme:
- Short-term farm credit up to Rupees 3 lakh carries a concessional rate of 7 percent per year.
- Repaying on time earns a further 3 percent Prompt Repayment Incentive.
- On-time repayment brings the effective rate down to 4 percent per year.

Loans up to Rupees 2 lakh are given without collateral. This limit rose from Rupees 1.60 lakh to Rupees 2 lakh with effect from 1 January 2025.

⚠ The Union Budget 2025-26 announced raising the KCC loan limit under the interest subvention scheme from Rupees 3 lakh to Rupees 5 lakh. Confirm the current ceiling with the bank branch.

The card is valid for 5 years, with the limit reviewed each year.
A KCC holder is covered under the Personal Accident Insurance Scheme where the bank offers it.

**Who qualifies**
- Farmers who own and cultivate land, on their own or jointly
- Tenant farmers, oral lessees and sharecroppers
- Members of Joint Liability Groups and Self Help Groups, including sharecropper groups
- Farmers in animal husbandry and fisheries, including fish farmers, fishers who own a boat, and people rearing dairy animals, poultry, sheep, goats and pigs

**How to apply**
1. Visit the bank branch that holds the account — commercial bank, regional rural bank, small finance bank or cooperative bank. Or apply online on the bank's website or through the PM-KISAN portal KCC link.
2. Fill the common one-page KCC application form.
3. Attach the documents.
4. The bank works out the limit from the scale of finance for the crop and the area cultivated.
5. The bank issues the card within the timeline set by the Reserve Bank of India.

The Kisan Rin Portal (KRP) and Ghar Ghar KCC campaigns run enrolment drives at the village level. Bank Mitras and CSCs help fill forms.

**Documents needed**
- Filled application form
- Identity proof — Aadhaar, voter ID, driving licence or passport
- Address proof
- Land record or a certificate of cultivation for tenant farmers
- Passport size photographs
- Bank account details

**Deadline pattern**
Applications are accepted all year.
Applying before the sowing season leaves time for the money to arrive when inputs are bought.
Repaying inside 12 months of drawal, or by the due date the bank sets, protects the 3 percent Prompt Repayment Incentive.

⚠ Interest rates, subvention ceilings and collateral-free limits are revised by RBI and the Government from time to time.
