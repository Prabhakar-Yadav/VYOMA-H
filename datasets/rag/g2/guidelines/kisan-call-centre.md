**What it is**
A nationwide toll free farm advisory helpline of the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, running since 21 January 2004.

**The number: 1800-180-1551**
Toll free. Works from mobile and landline, on every network, government and private.

**Timings**
6:00 am to 10:00 pm, all seven days of the week.

**Languages**
Replies are given in 22 local languages. The call routes to a centre that handles the caller's language.

**Who answers**
Farm Tele Advisors. Each is a graduate or postgraduate or doctorate in agriculture or an allied field — horticulture, animal husbandry, fisheries, poultry, beekeeping, sericulture, aquaculture or agricultural engineering.
The service runs from 14 call centre locations across the country.

**What the Kisan Call Centre can do**
- Identify a pest or disease from the farmer's description and advise the management step.
- Advise on variety choice and sowing time for the district.
- Advise on fertiliser and nutrient management.
- Advise on irrigation scheduling and water management.
- Advise on animal husbandry, dairy, poultry, fisheries and beekeeping problems.
- Explain government schemes and how to apply.
- Give market and price related information.
- Advise on post-harvest handling and storage.
- Escalate a difficult question to a subject matter specialist, who calls the farmer back.

**How to get the most out of a call**
1. Keep the crop name, variety, sowing date, field area and irrigation type ready.
2. Describe the symptom concretely — which part of the plant, what colour, how much of the field, since how many days.
3. Say the district and block, so the advice fits the local package of practices.
4. Note down the answer, and note the ticket or query number.
5. Register the mobile number on mkisan.gov.in to receive free advisory SMS on crops of interest.

Every call is recorded and logged. An unanswered query is escalated and returned.
